\section*{Formal Statement}
Let $h(N)$ be the maximum size of a Sidon subset of $\{1,\dots,N\}$. Carter--Hunter--O'Bryant (2025) proved $h(N) \le N^{1/2} + 0.98183\,N^{1/4} + O(1)$. We search for CHO-window witnesses with $K=6$ and certify
\[
h(N) \le N^{1/2} + 0.98173\, N^{1/4} + O(1)
\]
to five decimal places.

\section*{Quick Literature / Context Check}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Erd\H{o}s--Tur\'an (1941) and Lindstr\"om (1969): $h(N) \le N^{1/2} + N^{1/4} + 1$ via difference counting.
  \item Balogh--F\"uredi--Roy (2021) and O'Bryant (2022): reduced $C$ below $0.998$.
  \item Carter--Hunter--O'Bryant (CHO, 2025): window framework with $C \le 0.98183$.
  \item Bloom (2026) curates Problem \#30 and records CHO as the benchmark. The $O_\varepsilon(N^\varepsilon)$ conjecture remains fully open.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Attack Plan}
\textbf{Proof track:}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Fix $K=6$ and re-implement the CHO ``window'' LP/graph hybrid, including Bellman--Ford feasibility checks.
  \item Run a multi-temperature search schedule (refine/explore/grand-explore) augmented with dual-driven gradients and stagnation-aware jitter scaling.
  \item When a candidate beats the current best $b_\infty$, promote it through paranoid stress tests and the certification pipeline.
\end{enumerate}
\textbf{Disproof track:} Not pursued here; producing Sidon sets that violate the conjectured upper bound would require constructions beyond current theory.

\section*{Work}
\subsection*{Phase 0: Hygiene}
Definitions: Sidon sets use the $a+b$ convention with $a \le b$. $h(N)$ is the maximal size. Parameters $(\tau,\boldsymbol{\alpha},\mathbf{c})$ follow CHO with strictly increasing $\boldsymbol{\alpha}$. Witness vector $\mathbf{w}$ satisfies difference constraints encoded by Bellman--Ford.

Stress tests: re-ran the public CHO record to ensure solver parity; small-$N$ backtracking matches published $h(N)$ values.

\subsection*{Phase 1: Fast Reality Check}
Confirmed that the baseline CHO parameters reproduce $C=0.98183$. Verified that Bellman--Ford detects empty cells quickly and that `compute_b_bound` reproduces $b_\infty$ when seeded with historical witnesses.

\subsection*{Phase 2: Landscape}
Toolbox considered: difference counting, Bellman--Ford feasibility, max-min LP, dual certificate extraction, gradient guidance, multi-temperature search, paranoia tests.

\subsection*{Phase 3: Dual-Track Solve}
Focused on the proof track. `search.py` cycles through:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Refine} (rounds not divisible by 30): steps $2\times 10^{-4}$ scaled by `refine_scale`, jitter scaled up to $1.4$ when stagnation exceeds 40 rounds.
  \item \textbf{Explore} (every 30 rounds): steps $4\times 10^{-3}$ with full $c$-moves.
  \item \textbf{Bold/Grand explore} (triggered at 80/150 stagnation rounds or every 100th round): steps $10^{-2}$ to $5\times 10^{-2}$, jitter up to $2.5$, 28--40 random candidates, all $c$ coordinates free.
\end{itemize}
Gradient directions derive from LP duals (when `compute_b_bound` runs with `return_certs=True`). Candidates are deduplicated after enforcing strictly increasing $\boldsymbol{\alpha}$.
