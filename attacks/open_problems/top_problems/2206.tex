\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $(A_i)$ be a family of sets with $\lvert A_i\rvert=\aleph_0$ for all $i$, such that for any $i\neq j$ we have $\lvert A_i\cap A_j\rvert$ finite and $\neq 1$. Is there a $2$-colouring of $\cup A_i$ such that no $A_i$ is monochromatic? \sref{2206}{1}

\subsection{Short English statement}

Can every family of countably infinite sets with finite pairwise intersections other than singletons be coloured with two colours without a monochromatic member?
