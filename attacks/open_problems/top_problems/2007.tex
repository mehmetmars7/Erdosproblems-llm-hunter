{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2007 · Number Theory}

Euler\textquotesingle s totient is $\phi(n)=\#\{1\leq a\leq n:\gcd(a,n)=1\}$. Distinct totient values are counted once unless the question explicitly asks for their preimage multiplicity. \sref{2007}{1} \sref{2007}{2}

\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Is $ \sum_n \frac{\phi(n)}{2^n} $ irrational? Here $\phi$ is the Euler totient function. \sref{2007}{1}

\subsection{Short English statement}

Is the binary-weighted series whose coefficients are Euler's totient values an irrational number?
