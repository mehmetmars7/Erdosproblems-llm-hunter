\section{Comparing Two Ways of Counting Totient Values}\label{2108}

{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2108 · Combinatorics}

Euler\textquotesingle s totient is $\phi(n)=\#\{1\leq a\leq n:\gcd(a,n)=1\}$. Distinct totient values are counted once unless the question explicitly asks for their preimage multiplicity. \sref{2108}{1} \sref{2108}{2}

\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $ V'(x)=\#\{\phi(m) : 1\leq m\leq x\} $ and $ V(x)=\#\{\phi(m) \leq x : 1\leq m\}. $ Does $\lim V(x)/V'(x)$ exist? Is it $>1$? \sref{2108}{1}

\subsection{Short English statement}

Does the ratio between counting totient outputs below a bound and counting outputs from inputs below that bound approach a limit greater than one?
