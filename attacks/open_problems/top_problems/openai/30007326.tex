\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} Let $\|g\|_{\mathrm{Bol},\beta}=\sum_{a\in\mathbb Z^3}\sup_{|x-a|\le1,v}e^{\beta|v|^2}|g(x,v)|$. Suppose the smooth probability density $f_0\ge0$ has $\|f_0\|_{\mathrm{Bol},2\beta}+\|\nabla_xf_0\|_{\mathrm{Bol},2\beta}<\infty$ and a nonnegative classical hard-sphere Boltzmann solution $f$ on $[0,T]$ satisfies $\sup_{t,x,v}e^{2\beta|v|^2}f<\infty$. Start diameter-$\epsilon$ Newtonian hard spheres from the grand-canonical law proportional to $\mu^N/N!\prod_if_0(z_i)\mathbf1_{|x_i-x_j|>\epsilon}\,dZ_N$, $\mu=\epsilon^{-2}$. Define \[\zeta_t^\epsilon(\phi)=\sqrt\mu\left[\mu^{-1}\sum_i\phi(z_i(t))-\mathbb E\left(\mu^{-1}\sum_i\phi(z_i(t))\right)\right].\] For every finite collection of times and compactly supported smooth real tests, their joint law is claimed to converge to a centered Gaussian field $\zeta$ with $d\zeta_t(\phi)=\zeta_t(A_t\phi)\,dt+dW_t(\phi)$. Put $B=[(v-v_*)\cdot\omega]_+$, $v^\prime=v-((v-v_*)\cdot\omega)\omega$, $v_*^\prime=v_*+((v-v_*)\cdot\omega)\omega$, and $\Delta\phi=\phi(x,v^\prime)+\phi(x,v_*^\prime)-\phi(x,v)-\phi(x,v_*)$. Then \[A_t\phi=v\cdot\nabla_x\phi+\int Bf_t(x,v_*)\Delta\phi\,dv_*\,d\omega,\qquad C_t(\phi,\psi)=\tfrac12\int Bf_tf_{t,*}\Delta\phi\Delta\psi\,dx\,dv\,dv_*\,d\omega.\] The initial covariance is $\int f_0\phi\psi$, and $\mathbb E[W_t(\phi)W_s(\psi)]=\int_0^{t\wedge s}C_r(\phi,\psi)\,dr$, with independent increments and initial field independent of $W$. The assertion is finite-dimensional convergence with exact microscopic centering, conditional on the regular kinetic lifespan.
