\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} For a characteristic-zero field \(F\), a homogeneous ideal \(I\subseteq S_F=F[x_1,\ldots,x_n]\) containing a regular sequence of degrees \(2\le a_1\le\cdots\le a_c\), \(1\le c\le n\), put \(P_F=(x_1^{a_1},\ldots,x_c^{a_c})\). In degree \(d\), define \(J_d\) by adjoining to \((P_F)_d\) the lexicographically greatest \(q_d=\dim I_d-\dim(P_F)_d\) monomials outside \(P_F\). The source claims \(J=\bigoplus_dJ_d\) is an ideal with
\[
 \dim_F(S_F/I)_d=\dim_F(S_F/J)_d\quad(d\ge0),\qquad
 \beta^{S_F}_{u,j}(S_F/I)\le\beta^{S_F}_{u,j}(S_F/J)
 \quad(u\ge0,\ j\in\mathbb Z).
\]
Here \(\beta_{u,j}\) are graded Betti numbers. The additional generators of \(I\) have no degree or number restriction. The main complex-field theorem is the full-length Artinian case \(c=n\).
