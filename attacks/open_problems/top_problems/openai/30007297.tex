\section*{Bounded recovery of modular spectral averages}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
When a faithful normal state's centralizer is scalar, positive modular-band averages are claimed to have uniformly bounded algebra witnesses, implying spectral rigidity and a separable type-III\_1 bicentralizer theorem. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} For a type-$\mathrm{III}_1$ factor $M$ with separable predual and any faithful normal state $\phi$, OpenAI claims $\operatorname{BC}(M,\phi)=\mathbb C1$. The bicentralizer consists of $a$ whose commutators with every uniformly bounded sequence $w_n$ converge strongly to zero whenever $\|w_n\phi-\phi w_n\|_{M_*}\to0$.
Its bounded-recovery theorem separately takes any von Neumann $M$ with faithful normal $\phi$ and $M_\phi=\mathbb C1$. On $H=L^2(M)$ put $\xi=\phi^{1/2}$, $D=\log\Delta_\phi$, $U_t=e^{itD}$ and fix the mean $m_tf(t)=\lim_{k\to\omega}(2k)^{-1}\int_{-k}^kf(t)\,dt$ for a free ultrafilter. Given fixed $T\in B(H)$, $s\in\mathbb R$, $\delta_n>0$ with $\delta_n\to0$ and unit vectors $h_n$ supported spectrally in $[s-\delta_n,s+\delta_n]$, it claims
\[
\limsup_nm_t\|TU_th_n\|^2>0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\exists(n_j),v_j\in M,C_*<\infty,\eta>0:\ \|v_j\|\le C_*,\ \|Tv_j\xi\|\ge\eta,
\]
with $v_j\xi$ supported in $[s-4\delta_{n_j},s+4\delta_{n_j}]$. No bicentralizer hypothesis is needed in the recovery theorem.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Manuscript 1 (family 290, 2026-09-23): Modular orbit averages and independent phases give second- and fourth-moment control for initially unbounded spectral representatives. Polar truncation produces bounded witnesses, and Fourier filtering restores their shrinking spectral support. Applying these witnesses to intertwining defects restricts the joint spectrum of modular and flow generators; scalarity of the centralizer eliminates the remaining nontrivial flow possibilities.
