\section*{Sharp nodal upper bound on smooth surfaces}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
\textit{Nodal upper length on surfaces}: The paper claims nodal length at most $C\sqrt\lambda$ for every real nonzero Laplace eigenfunction on a fixed smooth closed surface. Combined with the established lower estimate, this settles the two-dimensional smooth setting.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} Fix a smooth metric $g$ on a closed connected surface $M$. One finite $C(M,g)$ is asserted to work simultaneously for all nonzero real eigenfunctions solving $-\Delta_gu=\lambda u$, $\lambda>0$, satisfies \[\mathcal H_g^1(\{u=0\})\le C(M,g)\sqrt\lambda.\] The constant is shared by all eigenfunctions in all positive eigenspaces; the assertion is specific to surfaces.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
\textit{Nodal upper length on surfaces}: Subharmonic profile limits control local growth under affine subdivision. High-growth excursions along grid paths produce witness regions on which growth persists to terminal scale. A packing argument pays for witness regions outside their entry squares using summable collars, bounding the terminal average growth and hence nodal length.
