\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Manuscript 1.}
For an elliptic curve $E/\mathbb Q$ and any prime $q$, write $s_q(E)=\operatorname{corank}_{\mathbb Z_q}\operatorname{Sel}_{q^{\infty}}(E/\mathbb Q)$, $r(E)=\operatorname{rank}E(\mathbb Q)$ and $a(E)=\operatorname{ord}_{s=1}L(E,s)$. If $s_q(E)\in\{0,1\}$, the manuscript claims
\[
 r(E)=a(E)=s_q(E),\qquad\#\operatorname{Sha}(E/\mathbb Q)<\infty,
\]
and, with $r=r(E)$,
\[
 \frac{L^{(r)}(E,1)}{r!}
 =\frac{\Omega_E\operatorname{Reg}_E\,\#\operatorname{Sha}(E/\mathbb Q)\prod_{\ell\text{ finite}}c_{\ell}(E)}
 {(\#E(\mathbb Q)_{\rm tors})^2}.
\]
Here $\Omega_E$ is the real N\'eron period, $\operatorname{Reg}_E$ the N\'eron--Tate regulator (one in rank zero) and $c_{\ell}$ the Tamagawa numbers. No reduction, torsion, CM or residual-representation conditions are added. The low-Selmer-corank premise remains essential; this is not a claim of unrestricted BSD.
