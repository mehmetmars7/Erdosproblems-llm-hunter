\section*{Borsuk Partition Problem in Dimensions Four Through Sixty-Three}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
\textit{Nine-dimensional diameter covering}: The projector configuration of lines in $\mathbb R^4$, viewed in the nine-dimensional space of trace-one symmetric matrices, is claimed to require more than ten smaller-diameter covering sets. This compact configuration refutes Borsuk’s assertion in dimension nine.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
\textit{Nine-dimensional diameter covering}: A smaller-diameter cover is enlarged to an open cover and converted into nonnegative coordinate functions on projective three-space. Orthogonal lines must have disjoint positive supports. Extending the map to positive semidefinite matrices produces an odd sphere map; its mod-two degree and an equality analysis of the support complex contradict the possible ten-label structure.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
