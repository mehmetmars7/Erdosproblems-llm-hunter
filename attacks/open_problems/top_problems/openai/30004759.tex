\section*{Positive Scalar Curvature Obstruction for Aspherical Manifolds}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
\textit{Integral negative scalar curvature}: For each $n\ge3$, the manuscript claims a dimension-only positive lower bound on the integral of $(\mathrm{Scal}^-)^{n/2}$ in terms of real simplicial volume; the bound is uniform over closed oriented manifolds and their metrics.

\textit{Positive scalar curvature and inessentiality}: OpenAI claims that positive scalar curvature forces the rational fundamental class to vanish under the classifying map, without spin or group restrictions; consequences include the aspherical obstruction, flatness rigidity at nonnegative scalar curvature, and zero real simplicial volume in the nonnegative case.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
\textit{Integral negative scalar curvature}: The companion vanishing result forces a negative Yamabe minimizer, which is normalized to scalar curvature minus one; Hölder then reduces the desired integral estimate to a volume bound. A bounded cocycle is represented through an equivariant probability map from the cover. Weighted graph deformations increase map energy while retaining the volume budget, and a coordinate-by-coordinate degree estimate pays for the detecting cocycle, yielding the uniform comparison.

\textit{Positive scalar curvature and inessentiality}: An essential rational class is detected on an auxiliary cover at a finite word-metric scale. Compactly supported cohomology and relative duality produce a finite-dimensional comparison with a nonzero local degree. Transverse realization and scalar-curvature reduction contradict that degree; the nonnegative aspherical rigidity statement then uses splitting after the positive-curvature obstruction is established.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
\textit{Integral negative scalar curvature}: The integral estimate rules out nonnegative scalar curvature when real simplicial volume is nonzero. It does not obstruct positive scalar curvature on every aspherical manifold with zero simplicial volume.

\textit{Positive scalar curvature and inessentiality}: The source explicitly proves rational inessentiality for positive scalar curvature and its corollary excludes positive scalar curvature on every closed aspherical manifold; orientation issues pass to the orientable cover.
