\section*{Alperins Weight Conjecture for Modular Representations of Finite Groups}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. For each prime and each block of a finite group, the claimed count of simple modular characters equals the count of block weights up to conjugacy.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} For a finite group \(G\), prime \(p\), and \(p\)-block \(B\), let \(l(B)\) count its irreducible Brauer characters and \(W_p(B)\) its conjugacy classes of block weights \((Q,\phi)\), with \(Q\) a \(p\)-subgroup and \(\phi\) a defect-zero character of \(N_G(Q)/Q\) inducing the block \(B\). The claimed identity is
\[
 l(B)=|W_p(B)|.
\]
If \(D\) is a defect group and \(b\) its Brauer-correspondent block in \(N_G(D)\), the corollary gives \(l(B)\ge l(b)\), with equality when \(D\) is abelian. For \(P\in\operatorname{Syl}_p(G)\), it gives \(|\operatorname{IBr}_p(G)|\ge|\operatorname{IBr}_p(N_G(P))|\). Writing \(B_0\) for the principal block and \(k_p(G)\) for the number of \(p\)-power-order classes including the identity, it also gives
\[
 k_p(G)=3\Rightarrow l(B_0)\ge(p-1)/2,\qquad
 p\mid|G|\Rightarrow l(B_0)\ge2\sqrt{p-1}+1-k_p(G).
\]
