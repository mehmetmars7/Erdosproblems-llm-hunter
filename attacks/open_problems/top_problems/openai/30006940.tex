\section*{Shub entropy conjecture}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
A noninvertible $C^1$ self-map of a closed smooth manifold is claimed to have zero topological entropy although its second-homology action has eigenvalue two, disproving the general $C^1$ entropy inequality. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Manuscript 1 (family 151, 2026-09-25): A slowing circle clock controls a main sphere map and a collection of signal registers. A uniformly differentiable infinite prediction series forces every orbit toward two zero-entropy invariant sets; a direct separated-set argument localizes entropy there, while projection retains degree two on homology without providing a dynamical factor map.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
Manuscript 1 (family 151, 2026-09-25): The selected C1 self-map conjecture does not require invertibility. The paper’s compact-manifold example has entropy zero and a real homology eigenvalue two, directly contradicting its logarithmic spectral-radius lower bound.
