\section*{Smooth Realization of Finite-Entropy Ergodic Transformations}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
A zero-entropy invertible ergodic nonatomic probability system is claimed to have no realization by any finite-dimensional smooth diffeomorphism preserving a strictly positive smooth density. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} OpenAI claims an ergodic invertible probability-preserving transformation $(X,\mu,T)$ on a standard nonatomic probability space with
\[
h_\mu(T)=0,
\]
which is not conjugate measurably after deleting invariant sets of measure zero to any smooth diffeomorphism of a compact manifold of finite dimension whose preserved probability measure has smooth everywhere-positive density. The exclusion covers nonorientable models as well as manifolds with a smooth boundary. The measure class is positive smooth volume; arbitrary singular invariant measures are not covered.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Manuscript 1 (family 152, 2026-09-25): For smooth positive volume, a dyadic switching estimate joins the future of one nearby point to the past of another with controlled output measure and summable cell complexity. Derivative-log bounds and finite boundary-value calculations establish that estimate; the constructed system violates the resulting measure-theoretic switching constraint in every finite dimension.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
The asserted transformation is invertible and ergodic on a standard nonatomic space, and has zero entropy, hence finite entropy. The claimed nonexistence of every compact finite-dimensional smooth positive-density model includes the smooth-volume-form models requested by this problem. Allowing smooth boundary or nonorientability on the forbidden model enlarges the excluded class. The obstruction is specifically to smooth positive volume; singular-measure models remain outside this target.
