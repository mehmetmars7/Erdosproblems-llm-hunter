\section*{Finitely presented infinite simple amenable groups}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. An infinite finitely presented amenable simple group is claimed to exist.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} The existence claim is
\[
 \exists G:\quad |G|=\infty,\quad
 G\text{ finitely presented},\quad G\text{ simple},\quad G\text{ amenable}.
\]
Here simplicity excludes proper nontrivial normal subgroups. Both ordinary finite presentability and infinitude are part of the assertion.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Boolean polygons over a quadratic ring define a Stone space and piecewise translation tables. Track and slot comparisons give perfection and simplicity, while finite presentation is obtained from the controlled polygon moves and the translation geometry supplies amenability.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
The claimed group simultaneously has infinitude, simplicity, finite presentation, and amenability. These are precisely the four existence properties requested, with no separate construction method imposed on the answer.
