\section*{Uniqueness of percolation on graphs roughly isometric to lattices}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a mathematical result relevant to this question in the selected manuscript. Critical bond percolation is claimed to have no infinite component on every infinite locally finite quasi-transitive graph with $p_c<1$.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} Suppose \(G\) is infinite, connected, locally finite, undirected, and has finitely many vertex orbits under automorphisms. For Bernoulli bond percolation, define \(p_c(G)\) as the infimum of parameters with positive probability of an infinite cluster. The claimed implication is
\[
 p_c(G)<1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \mathbb P_{p_c(G)}(\exists\text{ infinite open cluster})=0.
\]
Equivalently, every fixed vertex \(x\) satisfies \(\mathbb P_{p_c}(|C_x|=\infty)=0\). The hypothesis \(p_c<1\) is essential to the stated scope.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
The proof separates growth regimes and uses finitary structural reduction in the polynomial-growth case. Two coordinate moves are glued through fresh corridors using a conditional connection inequality; adaptive exploration would then produce subcritical percolation if an infinite critical cluster existed.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
The cited theorem gives zero infinite clusters at pc<1 on the quasi-transitive members of our coarse-lattice class; our question quantifies every parameter and allows arbitrary bounded-degree nontransitive roughly-isometric graphs.
