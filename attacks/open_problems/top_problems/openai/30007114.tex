\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Manuscript 1.}
For a $d$-dimensional convex body, define its projection body by $h_{\Pi K}(u)=\operatorname{vol}_{d-1}(\operatorname{proj}_{u^{\perp}}K)$ on unit vectors, and set $R_d(K)=|\Pi K|/|K|^{d-1}$ and $c_d=(d+1)d^d/d!$. Take the standard simplex $T_{10}=\operatorname{conv}(0,e_1,\ldots,e_{10})$ and the product $K=T_{10}\times T_{10}\subset\mathbb R^{20}$. The exact asserted comparison is
\[
 \frac{R_{20}(K)}{c_{20}}=\frac{121\binom{20}{10}}{21\cdot2^{20}}
 =\frac{22\,355\,476}{22\,020\,096}>1.
\]
Thus a simplex does not maximize projection-body volume normalized by $|K|^{d-1}$ in every dimension.
