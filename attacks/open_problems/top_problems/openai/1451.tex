\section*{Eilenberg-Ganea Conjecture}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a counterexample in the selected manuscript. A finitely generated residually finite group is claimed to have integral cohomological dimension two but no two-dimensional classifying space.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Degree estimates on conjugation-invariant parts of word fibers obstruct arbitrary aspherical presentations. Exact boundary-chain counts and quadratic bounds compare any candidate low-dimensional model with the constructed group, forcing geometric dimension three despite the shorter cohomological resolution.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
The example has cd\_Z=2 but gd=3, excluding every two-dimensional K(G,1), not merely one chosen cellular model. Within these assumptions, this is a claimed negative answer to the stated question.
