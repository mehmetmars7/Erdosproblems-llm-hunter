\section*{CAT(0) Actions of Artin Groups}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a mathematical result relevant to this question in the selected manuscript. A specific Artin group on 116 generators is claimed to have no proper cocompact isometric action on a proper CAT(0) space.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} For an Artin matrix \(M\) on 116 generators with off-diagonal entries only in \(\{2,3,\infty\}\), the constructed Artin group \(A_M\) is claimed to have no action that is simultaneously isometric, proper, and cocompact on any nonempty proper CAT(0) metric space. In quantified form,
\[
 \nexists X,\ A_M\curvearrowright X:\quad
 X\text{ proper CAT}(0),\quad\text{action proper and cocompact}.
\]
The metric space is required to be proper, and the exclusion is independent of dimension.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Stable translation lengths and centralizer characters impose quantitative braid constraints. Six asymptotic sectors and labeled triangular diagrams yield a local winding count incompatible with those constraints; a finite Artin matrix realizes the contradictory configuration.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
The paper disproves the assertion for every proper CAT(0) target. The question does not explicitly require the target metric space itself to be proper; This entry records partial support; complete coverage of the question is not established by this catalogue.
