\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Manuscript 1 (family 034, 2026-10-05): Nonvanishing is obtained by fibrations when they exist and by minimal singular metrics in algebraic dimension zero. Spherical transverse structures, reduced-boundary models, gluing of pluricanonical sections, and multiplier-ideal extension address the remaining zero-dimensional-algebraic case before returning to the assumed nef endpoint. Manuscript 2 (family 034, 2026-09-27): Minimal metrics and moving centers reduce the problem to compatible jet-order and rank estimates on two factors. Frobenius diagonal filtrations convert ordinary jets to truncated monomials, while instability control keeps the numerical separation needed to force nonvanishing. Manuscript 3 (family 034, 2026-09-27): Finite-cover invariants lift successive neighborhood layers into divisorial sheaves. Their positive twists provide increasing section counts while the cohomological loss stays bounded, forcing growth without assuming convergence of an infinite formal neighborhood.

Manuscript 4 (family 034, 2026-10-04): The induction retains both the nef line and the effective negative part through a restricted birational program. Boundary generation, signed-support root constructions, Hodge-module lifting, and compact cycle deformations produce nonvanishing; affine changes in negative multiplicities make the selected wall crossings finite. Manuscript 5 (family 034, 2026-09-24): A dimension induction establishes nonvanishing before constructing good models in that same dimension. Cyclic-cover eigenspaces and logarithmic Hodge modules control the boundary lifting obstruction, while the companion subadditivity result supplies the fibration step; descent of the evaluation map recovers the actual line bundle over the original field. Manuscript 6 (family 034, 2026-09-24): The total-space canonical model, with its good-model property, supplies the geometric reduction. Cartier intersection theory and proper pushforward compare the base with the canonical image without requiring either map to factor through the other; transfer of sections then yields the Kodaira and fiber formulas.

Manuscript 7 (family 034, 2026-09-26): Marked curve chains control degrees of rational quotient leaves with compatibility under projections and finite quotients. Scalar bounds and Frobenius estimates handle the index step; a semiample contraction and the relative big-base theorem then bring uniform floor systems back to the original pair. Manuscript 8 (family 034, 2026-10-05): The normal components first receive a uniform pluricanonical exponent. Residues along conductor strata turn descent into a scalar-gluing problem, whose possible characters are bounded using Hodge-norm rank estimates and moving-subvariety fibrations; this produces a common exponent for the glued pair. Manuscript 9 (family 036, 2026-09-24): Positive-boundary programs first reduce the possible exceptional loci. Algebraic codimension-two cycle classes give a finite homological decrease after discrepancy control; a final surface criterion eliminates the remaining obstruction without assuming general minimal-model existence.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
Manuscript 1 (family 034, 2026-10-05): The fourfold good-model and nef-endpoint nonvanishing theorems explicitly assume Campana, MMP and effective-abundance inputs and strong Q-factoriality; neither settles the unrestricted unconditional catalogue statement.

Manuscript 2 (family 034, 2026-09-27): The smooth canonical theorem proves first-section existence in dimension four and the lc corollary treats nef adjoints in dimension at most four; the catalogue asks for arbitrary-dimensional pseudo-effective lc adjoints.

Manuscript 3 (family 034, 2026-09-27): The general lifting reduction assumes smooth canonical nonvanishing in every dimension and needs specified supported-boundary data; it is a conditional reduction, not the catalogue unconditional first-section assertion.

Manuscript 4 (family 034, 2026-10-04): The main Kähler theorem explicitly assumes logarithmic Iitaka subadditivity; it gives semiampleness and hence nonvanishing only for nef adjoints under that additional assumption, whereas our catalogue assertions are unconditional.

Manuscript 5 (family 034, 2026-09-24): Log abundance gives a nonzero Cartier-multiple section for nef lc adjoints; our nonvanishing target assumes only pseudo-effectivity, so the nef hypothesis is an additional restriction.

Manuscript 6 (family 034, 2026-09-24): The smooth canonical-good-model theorem covers zero-boundary smooth complex canonical divisors, including nonvanishing and abundance there. It does not cover arbitrary log-canonical boundaries or all slc varieties.

Manuscript 7 (family 034, 2026-09-26): The log-Iitaka main theorem gives a nonempty system for every pseudo-effective lc rational adjoint on a complex fourfold, which is the desired conclusion only in dimension four.

Manuscript 8 (family 034, 2026-10-05): Uniform trivial multiples are obtained for the log-Calabi–Yau subclass. This does not cover general nef slc adjoints, or all pseudo-effective projective lc adjoints.

Manuscript 9 (family 036, 2026-09-24): A good minimal model gives canonical nonvanishing for smooth projective varieties of numerical dimension one; our target ranges over all pseudo-effective lc adjoints and numerical dimensions.
