\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} For $n\ge3$, define the closed capacity-$R$ ball $B^{2n}(R)=\{z\in\mathbb C^n:\pi\sum_j|z_j|^2\le R\}$ with the standard symplectic form. For positive $R,R_1,\ldots,R_k$, the claim is \[\bigsqcup_{i=1}^k B^{2n}(R_i)\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Int}B^{2n}(R)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\sum_iR_i^n<R^n\ \text{ and }\ R_i+R_j<R\ (i<j).\] The embedding is symplectic on a neighborhood of each closed ball; compact images must be pairwise disjoint. The parameters are capacities, not Euclidean radii.
