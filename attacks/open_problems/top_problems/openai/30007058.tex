\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} Let $C$ be a complex curve, smooth and projective, with genus $g\ge2$, composite $n\ge4$, $\gcd(n,d)=1$, and $L\in\operatorname{Pic}^d(C)$. Let $X$ parametrize stable rank-$n$, determinant-$L$, trace-free Higgs bundles, with Hitchin map $h:X\to A=\bigoplus_{i=2}^nH^0(C,K_C^i)$ and $R=(n^2-1)(g-1)$. The corresponding Betti variety is
\[
X^B=\{(A_j,B_j)\in\operatorname{SL}_n(\mathbb C)^{2g}:\prod_j[A_j,B_j]=e^{2\pi id/n}I\}\mathbin{/\mkern-6mu/}\operatorname{SL}_n.
\]
Transport the perverse filtration $P_kH^m(X)=\operatorname{im}[H^{m-R}(A,{}^p\tau_{\le k}Rh_*\mathbb Q_X[R])\to H^m(X)]$ by nonabelian Hodge theory. OpenAI claims
\[
P_kH^m(X^B,\mathbb Q)=W_{2k}H^m(X^B,\mathbb Q)=W_{2k+1}H^m(X^B,\mathbb Q)
\]
for every $m,k\ge0$, where $W$ is Deligne's weight filtration. The determinant is fixed and the composite-rank coprime-degree restrictions are part of the theorem.
