\section*{Singular Free Boundaries in the Critical Dimension}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
\textit{Critical Bernoulli dimension}: The claimed first nonflat minimizing Bernoulli cone occurs in dimension seven; all homogeneous global solutions that minimize in lower dimensions are flat. Dimensions at most six have smooth local free boundaries, with the announced sharp singular-set bounds in higher dimensions.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} For nonnegative $u\in H^1_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathbb R^d)$, call it a global one-phase minimizer if on every ball it minimizes \[J(u;B)=\int_B(|\nabla u|^2+\mathbf1_{\{u>0\}})\,dx\] against all nonnegative Sobolev competitors with the same boundary trace. The claim states that every nonzero one-homogeneous global minimizer, $u(rx)=ru(x)$, in $1\le d\le6$ is $(x\cdot e)_+$ with $|e|=1$, and some nonflat one-homogeneous global minimizer exists in $\mathbb R^7$. Thus the least nonflat minimizing-cone dimension is $d_*=7$. This concerns global minimizers rather than all stable critical points.
