\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Manuscript 1.}
For explicitly encoded strings $x,y$ of total length $N$ over a finite alphabet, let $D=\operatorname{ED}(x,y)$ with insertions, deletions and substitutions each of unit cost. Given rational $0<\varepsilon<1$, the asserted uniform randomized algorithm outputs an integer $\widehat D\ge0$ satisfying
\[
 \Pr[D\le\widehat D\le(1+\varepsilon)D]\ge2/3.
\]
For each fixed rational $\varepsilon$, the worst-case expected time is $N^{1+o(1)}$ on a logarithmic-word RAM, including all input, preprocessing, arithmetic and random-sampling costs. Equal strings yield zero deterministically; the time bound is an expectation, not a bound on every random path.
