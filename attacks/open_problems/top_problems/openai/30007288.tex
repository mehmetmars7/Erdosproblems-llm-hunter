\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Manuscript 1.}
Let $E/\mathbb Q_2$ be finite and let $r:G_{\mathbb Q}\to\operatorname{GL}_2(E)$ be continuous, absolutely irreducible, unramified outside finitely many primes, and odd, meaning $\det r(c)=-1$ for complex conjugation. No de Rham or Hodge--Tate condition is imposed. The claim provides an odd integer $N$, divisible by every odd ramified prime, and a continuous $\mathbb Z_2$-algebra homomorphism
\[
 \lambda:\mathbb T_2(N)\longrightarrow\mathcal O_E,
 \qquad\lambda(T_{\ell})=\operatorname{tr}r(\operatorname{Frob}_{\ell}),\quad
 \lambda(\ell S_{\ell})=\det r(\operatorname{Frob}_{\ell})\quad(\ell\nmid2N).
\]
The full algebra includes Eisenstein forms. Its definition is
\[
 \mathbb T_2(N)=\varprojlim_k\bigl(\mathbb Z_2\otimes_{\mathbb Z}T_{\le k}^{(2)}(N)\bigr),
\]
where $T_{\le k}^{(2)}(N)$ is the $\mathbb Z$-algebra of operators on $\bigoplus_{i=1}^kM_i(\Gamma_1(N))$ generated by $T_\ell$ and $\ell S_\ell$ for primes $\ell\nmid2N$. Here $M_i$ denotes all holomorphic modular forms of weight $i$, and on that weight $S_\ell=\ell^{i-2}\langle\ell\rangle$. Each stage has the two-adic topology and the limit has its inverse-limit topology. The conclusion is pro-modularity, expressed by this compatible completed-Hecke eigensystem.
