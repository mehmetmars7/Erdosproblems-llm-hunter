\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} For rooted FK--Ising map/subset pairs with \(n\) edges and weight \(2^{\ell/2}\), let \(d_n\) be all-edge graph distance, \(\mu_n(v)=\deg(v)/(2n)\), and \(L_n=P_n-I\) the rate-one simple-walk generator. Let \(\lambda_{n,j}\) be the eigenvalues of \(-L_n\), extended by infinity beyond the vertex count. On the ordinary unit-area \(\sqrt3\)-LQG sphere, close
\[
 \mathcal E_h(f,f)=\tfrac12\int_{S^2}|\nabla f|^2\,d\mathrm{vol}
\]
on \(L^2(\mu_h)\); the claim gives compact resolvent, one-dimensional constant kernel, and finite \(H_h(t)=\sum_je^{-t\Lambda_j(h)}\) for \(t>0\). For deterministic spatial normalization \(a_n\), it asserts
\[
 ((V_n,a_nd_n,\mu_n),(n\lambda_{n,j})_{j\ge0},H_n)
 \Longrightarrow((S^2,D_h,\mu_h),(\Lambda_j(h))_{j\ge0},H_h),
\]
where \(H_n(t)=\sum_je^{-nt\lambda_{n,j}}\). The respective topologies are Gromov--Hausdorff--Prokhorov, coordinatewise spectral convergence, and uniform convergence on compact positive-time intervals. All limits use the same sphere. The deterministic scale is fixed by
\[
 a_n=\exp\{m(\log\operatorname{diam}(S,D_h))-m(\log\max(1,\operatorname{diam}(V_n,d_n)))\}.
\]
For a real random variable $Y$, $m(Y)$ is the unique real $r$ solving $\mathbb E\arctan(Y-r)=0$; both expectations here use unconditional laws.
