\section*{Falconer Distance Conjecture in the Plane}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
\textit{Falconer distances}: For every $d\ge2$, the manuscript claims positive one-dimensional measure for the distance set of a compact subset of $\mathbb R^d$ with dimension exceeding $d/2$. Both the ambient dimension and the set itself are unrestricted within these hypotheses.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
\textit{Falconer distances}: Auxiliary projections first provide angular nonconcentration and filters on pairs of points. A fractal Fourier estimate and a graph estimate with separate angular variables control shell approximations after small additional deletions. The filters are brought back to distances of the original pairs, and monotone shell summation produces a nonzero absolutely continuous distance measure.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
\textit{Falconer distances}: The manuscript’s result proves the compact-set assertion in every dimension, including d=2. For a Borel planar set of dimension >1, the compact-subset characterization of Borel Hausdorff dimension supplies a compact subset of dimension >1, and its positive-measure distance set lies in the original distance set.

The claimed result implies the stated question and also supplies the additional strength described above.
