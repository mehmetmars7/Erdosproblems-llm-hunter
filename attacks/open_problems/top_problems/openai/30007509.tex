\section*{Thomason structures in all strict categorical dimensions}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
Strict globular n-categories are claimed to admit proper combinatorial Thomason model structures for every positive finite n and n=infinity, Quillen equivalent to spaces with fibrations detected by \(\operatorname{Ex}^2\) of the Street nerve. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} For every positive integer $n$ and $n=\infty$, let $n\mathrm{Cat}$ be strict higher categories and $N_nX$ the Street nerve with simplices $\operatorname{Hom}(\mathcal O_m,X)$ from orientals. Let $c_n$ be its left adjoint and set $L_n=c_n\operatorname{Sd}^2$, $R_n=\operatorname{Ex}^2N_n$. OpenAI claims a proper combinatorial cofibrantly generated model structure with
\[
W_n=R_n^{-1}(\text{weak homotopy equivalences}),\quad
F_n=R_n^{-1}(\text{Kan fibrations}),\quad C_n={}^{\perp}(F_n\cap W_n).
\]
The generators are $L_n(\partial\Delta[m]\hookrightarrow\Delta[m])$ and $L_n(\Lambda^k[m]\hookrightarrow\Delta[m])$, and $L_n\dashv R_n$ is a Quillen equivalence with Kan--Quillen simplicial sets. Here left orthogonality is the lifting property, and subdivision and extension are the usual simplicial functors.
