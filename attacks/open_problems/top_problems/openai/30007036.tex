\section*{Hodge classes on powers of split Weil sixfolds}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
Algebraicity is claimed for Hodge classes on powers of split Weil sixfolds and of abelian varieties of dimension at most five carrying an imaginary-quadratic action, including special periods. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} Let $E=\mathbb Q(u)$ be imaginary quadratic with $u^2=-d$, $d>0$, and let $A$ be a complex abelian variety with a unital $E$-action. OpenAI claims rational cycle-class surjectivity
\[
\operatorname{CH}^p(A^m)_{\mathbb Q}\longrightarrow H^{2p}(A^m,\mathbb Q)\cap H^{p,p}(A^m)
\quad(m\ge1,\ p\ge0)
\]
in either of these two cases: $\dim A\le5$; or $\dim A=6$ and the rational Hermitian form associated to a compatible polarization is hyperbolic with signature $(3,3)$. In the second case, with rational Riemann form $e$, this means $h_E(x,y)=e(x,uy)+u e(x,y)$ on $H_1(A,\mathbb Q)$ has a totally isotropic three-dimensional $E$-subspace. Compatibility means $e(ax,y)=e(x,\bar a y)$ for all $a\in E$.
