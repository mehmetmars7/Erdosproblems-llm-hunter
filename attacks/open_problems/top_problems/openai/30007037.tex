\section*{Rational Hodge classes on products of K3 surfaces}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
Rational Hodge classes on any finite product of projective complex K3 surfaces are claimed to be algebraic, even when the factors have unrelated periods or extra endomorphisms. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} Choose any integer $m\ge1$ and any projective complex K3 surfaces $S_1,\ldots,S_m$, allowing repeated factors. For $X=\prod_{j=1}^m S_j$, OpenAI claims
\[
\operatorname{CH}^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}\xrightarrow{\operatorname{cl}}
H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Q)\cap H^{p,p}(X)
\quad\text{is surjective for every }0\le p\le2m.
\]
There are no Picard-number, period or endomorphism-field restrictions. The quantified varieties are products of K3 surfaces, so this statement has smaller scope than the Hodge conjecture for arbitrary smooth projective complex varieties.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}

Clifford-algebra calculations then identify its transcendental cohomology component; algebraic Kuga--Satake tensors and their contractions supply the classes needed on products.
