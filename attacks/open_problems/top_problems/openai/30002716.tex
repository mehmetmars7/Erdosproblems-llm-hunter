\section*{The mod-\$\textbackslash{}ell\$ Bogomolov–Pop conjecture}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 009; 2026-10-05), the asserted result is that degree-one and degree-two Milnor $K$-theory modulo a prime different from the field characteristic recover the perfect closure of a function field of transcendence degree at least two, with a common scalar and Frobenius ambiguity.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Manuscript 1.}
Fix any prime $\ell$, and for fields of characteristic different from $\ell$ put $V_F=F^{\times}/(F^{\times})^{\ell}$ and $R_F=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb F_{\ell}}\{[x]\otimes[1-x]:x\ne0,1\}$. Compatible linear isomorphisms carry $R_K$ onto $R_L$. For finitely generated $K/k,L/l$ with algebraically closed constants and transcendence degrees at least two, the claim is
\[
 \operatorname{Isom}_{\rm F}^{i}(K,L)\xrightarrow{\sim}
 \operatorname{Isom}_{\rm M}(V_K,V_L)/\mathbb F_{\ell}^{\times}.
\]
The left side uses isomorphisms of perfect closures carrying $k$ to $l$, identified in positive characteristic under postcomposition by any integral power of Frobenius; in characteristic zero there is no such quotient. If characteristics differ it is empty. Thus a compatible mod-$\ell$ invariant isomorphism is claimed to force equal characteristics and relative transcendence degrees. Perfect closures and the Frobenius ambiguity are essential here.
