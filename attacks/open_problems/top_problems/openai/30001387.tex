\section*{Mahler Volume Product Conjecture}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 087; 2026-09-22), the asserted result is that the interior polar product of an origin-symmetric body in dimension at least two has Gromov width four, including ball embeddings of every smaller capacity; In manuscript 2 (family 087; 2026-09-22), the asserted result is that origin-symmetric convex bodies in dimension $n$ have volume product at least $4^n/n!$, with equality precisely for linear images of Hanner polytopes.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Manuscript 1.}
For every origin-symmetric convex body $K\subset\mathbb R^n$, $n\ge2$, let $K^{\circ}=\{p:q\cdot p\le1\ (q\in K)\}$ and $U_K=\operatorname{int}K\times\operatorname{int}K^{\circ}$ in standard symplectic space $(\mathbb R^{2n},\sum_jdq_j\wedge dp_j)$. The claim asserts
\[
 c_G(U_K)=4,\qquad
 \forall0<c<4:\quad\{(q,p):\pi(|q|^2+|p|^2)<c\}\hookrightarrow U_K
\]
by a smooth symplectic embedding. The Gromov width is the supremum of these ball capacities; the endpoints are not an assertion that capacity four is itself attained.

\paragraph{Manuscript 2.}
In each dimension $n\ge1$, take any convex body $K\subset\mathbb R^n$ with $K=-K$. Define $K^{\circ}=\{y:x\cdot y\le1\text{ for all }x\in K\}$. The manuscript claims
\[
 |K|\,|K^{\circ}|\ge\frac{4^n}{n!},\qquad
 |K|\,|K^{\circ}|=\frac{4^n}{n!}\ \Longleftrightarrow\
 K\text{ is a linear Hanner body}.
\]
Here Hanner bodies are built from one-dimensional symmetric intervals by successive $\ell_1$ and $\ell_{\infty}$ sums, and a linear Hanner body is an invertible linear image of one. The bound and the complete equality classification hold in every dimension.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
For manuscript 1 (family 087; 2026-09-22), a planar holomorphic coordinate gives a uniform slice estimate that can be realized inside a polar product. Smooth approximation extends the construction to nonsmooth convex bodies; volume preservation then yields the symmetric volume-product bound.

For manuscript 2 (family 087; 2026-09-22), a functional inequality is obtained from a conformal-lens construction and a real simplex identity. Feasible bases and a common equality witness identify the extremizers, while approximation transfers the sharp estimate from polytopes to arbitrary bodies.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
The claimed inequality $|K||K^\circ|\ge4^n/n!$ is exactly the cube's volume-product bound for all origin-symmetric convex bodies in all dimensions. The theorem further characterizes equality as the invertible linear images of Hanner bodies.
