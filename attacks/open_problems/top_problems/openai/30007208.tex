\section*{Single-letter classical capacity of generalized amplitude damping}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. Every generalized amplitude-damping qubit channel is claimed to have an explicit one-variable unassisted capacity formula achieved by binary pure states, with the listed universal partner-channel additivities.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} For \(\gamma,\nu\in[0,1]\), define the qubit channel
\[
 \mathcal A_{\gamma,\nu}\begin{pmatrix}1-q&z\\\overline z&q\end{pmatrix}
 =\begin{pmatrix}1-t&\sqrt{1-\gamma}z\\\sqrt{1-\gamma}\overline z&t\end{pmatrix},
 \quad t=(1-\gamma)q+\gamma\nu.
\]
Put \(h(t)=-t\log t-(1-t)\log(1-t)\), \(g(u)=h((1+\sqrt{1-4u})/2)\), and \(v_{\gamma,\nu}(p)=\gamma\nu(1-\nu)+\gamma(1-\gamma)(p-\nu)^2\). The claimed Holevo-information formula in bits is
\[
 \chi(\mathcal A_{\gamma,\nu}^{\otimes n})
 =n\chi(\mathcal A_{\gamma,\nu})
 =\frac n{\log2}\max_{0\le p\le1}
 [h((1-\gamma)p+\gamma\nu)-g(v_{\gamma,\nu}(p))]
\]
for every \(n\ge1\), so its unassisted classical capacity is single-letter. Independent products of the two equiprobable states \(\sqrt{1-p}|0\rangle\pm\sqrt p|1\rangle\), at a maximizing \(p\), attain this information.
