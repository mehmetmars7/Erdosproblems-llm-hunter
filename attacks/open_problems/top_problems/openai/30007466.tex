\paragraph{Manuscript 1.}
For finite rational metric $k$-median with clients $D$, facilities $F$, and $1\le k\le|F|$, define $\operatorname{cost}(S)=\sum_{j\in D}d(j,S)$ and $\operatorname{opt}_k$ as its minimum over $|S|\le k$. The claim gives an absolute $\sigma>0$ such that for every fixed $a>0$ a randomized polynomial-bit-time algorithm always returns a feasible $S$ and satisfies
\[

\]
It can additionally achieve $\mathbb E\operatorname{cost}(S)\le(2-\sigma)\operatorname{opt}_k$. Polynomial constants may depend on fixed parameters.
