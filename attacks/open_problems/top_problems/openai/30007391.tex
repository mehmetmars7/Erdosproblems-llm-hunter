\section*{A size-independent Gibbs Poincaré constant throughout high-temperature SK}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. Throughout fixed SK temperatures $0<\beta<1$, the rate-one heat-bath gap is claimed bounded below by a positive constant with high disorder probability.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} For independent standard Gaussian $J_{ij}$, $i<j$, with $J_{ji}=J_{ij},J_{ii}=0$, use the zero-field SK measure
\[
 \pi_{n,\beta}^J(x)=Z^{-1}\exp\left(\frac\beta{\sqrt n}\sum_{i<j}J_{ij}x_ix_j\right),\qquad x\in\{-1,1\}^n.
\]
Continuous heat-bath dynamics refreshes every site at rate one; a discrete step refreshes one uniformly selected site, including attempts that retain its spin. For fixed $0<\beta<1$, a finite deterministic $C_\beta$ satisfies
\[
 \mathbb P_J\left\{\operatorname{Var}_\pi(f)\le C_\beta\sum_{i=1}^n
 \mathbb E_\pi(f-\mathbb E_\pi[f\mid x_{-i}])^2
 \text{ for all }f:\{-1,1\}^n\to\mathbb R\right\}\longrightarrow1.
\]
The continuous-time spectral gap is at least $C_\beta^{-1}$ on this event; the uniform-site discrete gap is at least $(nC_\beta)^{-1}$. This theorem uses zero external field.
