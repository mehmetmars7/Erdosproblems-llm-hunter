\section*{Higher Finiteness Embeddings and TDLC Analogues}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
The linked manuscripts present the following claims by OpenAI: One group of type $F_\infty$ is claimed to contain every finitely presented group, with finitely generated subgroups exactly the recursively presented ones; A group with finitely many generators and a decidable identity-word test is claimed to embed into a nontrivial simple group of type $F_\infty$. Together they provide the following support within the scopes described below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} The claimed group \(H\) has type \(F_\infty\), meaning a classifying space with finite skeleton in every dimension, and satisfies
\[
 \forall G\text{ finitely presented},\qquad\exists\iota:G\hookrightarrow H.
\]
The corollary characterizes the finitely generated subgroups embeddable in this same \(H\):
\[
 G\text{ finitely generated}:\qquad G\hookrightarrow H
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad G\text{ recursively presented}.
\]
This does not assert embedding of every countable group.

\paragraph{Result 2.} Consider a group \(G\) generated by finitely many elements, with an algorithm deciding its word problem. The assertion supplies a nontrivial simple group \(H\) of type \(F_\infty\) and an embedding
\[
 \iota:G\hookrightarrow H.
\]
Type \(F_\infty\) requires finite skeleta of a classifying space in all dimensions. The decidability condition on \(G\) is part of this theorem.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
A universal finite presentation is inserted into faithful finite diagram envelopes using two systems of idempotents. An ascending-torus construction supplies uniform fillings with finitely many simplex types in each dimension, establishing the claimed higher finiteness of the universal envelope.

Encoded finite diagrams carry faithful actions with controlled stabilizers. A homological device for ascending tori provides fillings in every dimension, and stabilizers of finite type in a highly transitive action assemble the simple overgroup while retaining higher finiteness.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
The universal-group theorem embeds every finitely presented group into an $F_\infty$ group, hence into an $F_3$ group. This resolves the first clause of the recorded question. It does not establish the analogous embedding for every group of type $\mathrm{FP}_2$, which need not be finitely presented, or supply the requested totally disconnected locally compact analogue. The simple-overgroup companion assumes decidable word problem and adds no answer to those missing clauses. The bundle remains partial.
