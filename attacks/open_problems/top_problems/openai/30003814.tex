\section*{Distances in Decorated Random Planar Maps}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a mathematical result relevant to this question in the selected manuscript. For each fixed FK parameter $q>4$, finite spherical maps are claimed to converge after an $n^{-1/2}$ distance rescaling to the Brownian continuum random tree.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} Fix \(q>4\). Among rooted planar maps with \(n\) edges and marked edge subsets \(A\), sample with weight proportional to
\[
 q^{k_M(A)+(|A|-|V(M)|)/2}.
\]
Loops and parallel edges are allowed. Let \(d_n\) use all map edges and \(\mu_n(v)=\deg(v)/(2n)\). The claim provides deterministic \(c(q)>0\) such that, through every positive integer size,
\[
 (V(M_n),c(q)n^{-1/2}d_n,\mu_n)
 \Longrightarrow(\mathcal T_e,d_e,\mu_e)
\]
in Gromov--Hausdorff--Prokhorov topology. The limit is the Brownian continuum random tree coded by a normalized excursion \(e\), with \(d_e(s,t)=e(s)+e(t)-2\min_{[s\wedge t,s\vee t]}e\) and \(\mu_e\) the pushforward of Lebesgue probability measure.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
The contour encoding is reorganized into an exact tree of marked blocks. A critical finite-variance offspring law gives the conditioned tree limit, while uniform bounds along ancestral chains show that distances through the actual map blocks have the same scaling.
