\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Manuscript 1.}
Fix $k\ge4$ and put $t=k+1$ for joint correlated-replacement $k$-WL, or $t=k$ for separate-coordinate refinement. A choice system has exactly $t$ finite domains $D_1,\ldots,D_t$ and pairwise label maps $\lambda_{ij}^i:D_i\to L_{ij}$, $\lambda_{ij}^j:D_j\to L_{ij}$. A successful choice satisfies $\lambda_{ij}^i(d_i)=\lambda_{ij}^j(d_j)$ for every pair. The claimed explicit uncolored simple graphs of equal order satisfy
\[
 X_0\equiv_kX_{\star}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\text{there is no successful choice}.
\]
Both common-color separate comparison and pure-tuple comparison within a disjoint union are allowed. If all domain and label sets have size at most $A\ge1$, the common graph order is bounded by
\[
 A\left(t2^{t-2+3\binom{t-1}{2}}+12\binom t3\right).
\]
If a successful choice exists, the histograms disagree by round two in the joint convention and by round one in the separate convention.
