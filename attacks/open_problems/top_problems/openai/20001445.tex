\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Manuscript 1.}
For a full-dimensional origin-symmetric convex body $K\subset\mathbb R^n$, write $h_K(u)=\sup_{x\in K}x\cdot u$, and for a positive function $g$ on the unit sphere define $W[g]=\{x:x\cdot u\le g(u)\text{ for every unit }u\}$. For every integer $n\ge1$, every such pair $K,L$, and every $\lambda\in[0,1]$, the claim is
\[
 |W[h_K^{1-\lambda}h_L^{\lambda}]|\ge |K|^{1-\lambda}|L|^{\lambda}.
\]
Its stated consequence, for every $0<p<1$, is
\[
 \left|W[((1-\lambda)h_K^p+\lambda h_L^p)^{1/p}]\right|^{p/n}
 \ge(1-\lambda)|K|^{p/n}+\lambda|L|^{p/n}.
\]
Volumes are Lebesgue volumes, and central symmetry is an assumption in both assertions.
