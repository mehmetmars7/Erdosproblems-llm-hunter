\section*{Strong Ulam stability characterizes amenability}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. For countable discrete groups, uniform stability of all unitary almost-representations is claimed equivalent to amenability, allowing infinite-dimensional spaces.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} For countable discrete \(G\), define the defect of \(\mu:G\to\mathcal U(\mathcal H)\), \(\mu(1)=I\), by
\[
 \operatorname{def}(\mu)=\sup_{g,h\in G}\|\mu(gh)-\mu(g)\mu(h)\|.
\]
Strong Ulam stability means: for every \(\varepsilon>0\), some \(\delta>0\) works for all complex Hilbert spaces and all \(\mu\) of defect at most \(\delta\), supplying a genuine unitary representation \(\pi\) on the same space with \(\sup_g\|\mu(g)-\pi(g)\|\le\varepsilon\). The claimed characterization is
\[
 G\text{ strongly Ulam stable}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad G\text{ amenable}.
\]
The quantifier includes infinite-dimensional Hilbert spaces.
