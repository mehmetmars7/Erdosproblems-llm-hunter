\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} Let $X$ be a smooth connected projective complex curve and $G$ a connected simple complex algebraic group, with Langlands dual $G^\vee$ of the full dual root datum. Fix dual pinnings and a theta characteristic, and let $r\in\{1,2,3\}$ be the lacing number. OpenAI claims for every $c\in\mathbb C\setminus\mathbb Q$ an equivalence
\[
D_c(\operatorname{Bun}_G(X))\simeq D_{-1/(rc)}(\operatorname{Bun}_{G^\vee}(X))

of presentable complex-linear DG categories. The assertion retains the actual global group forms and all connected components, and includes compatibility with localization--Whittaker comparison under moving and colliding marked points, and with central torsor actions and their transgression local systems.
