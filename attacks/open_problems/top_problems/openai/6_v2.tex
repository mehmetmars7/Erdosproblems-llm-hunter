\textbf{Withdrawn on October 6, 2026.}
OpenAI withdrew \emph{The rational Hodge conjecture for products of K3 surfaces}.
Its argument uses a stabilization-trace construction from the split abelian
eightfold paper; a sign error invalidates the required cancellation of signed
double points. The asserted K3-product theorem therefore remains unproved
by this argument. The notice withdraws the proof without claiming a disproof.
