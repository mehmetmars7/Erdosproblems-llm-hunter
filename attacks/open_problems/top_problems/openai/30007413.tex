Approximate it from inside by regions bounded by simple honeycomb cycles, containing every enclosed vertex and edge, with uniformly converging boundary parametrizations and eventual containment of every compact subset of $D$. On enclosed faces, sample integer heights with boundary value zero and $|h(u)-h(v)|\le1$ for adjacent faces, using weight
\[
 \mathbb P_{\delta,x}(h)=Z_{\delta,x}^{-1}x^{N_\delta(h)},\qquad
 N_\delta(h)=\sum_{\{u,v\}:u\sim v}\mathbf1_{\{h(u)\ne h(v)\}}.
\]
Each unordered adjacency is counted once. Extend $h_\delta$ constantly over faces and by zero elsewhere, so $h_\delta(f)=\int_Dh_\delta f$. For each fixed $x\in[1/\sqrt2,1]$, there is a domain-independent $\sigma(x)>0$ such that
\[
 h_\delta/\sigma(x)\Longrightarrow\Phi_D\quad\text{in }\mathcal D'(D),
\]
where the zero-Dirichlet GFF has covariance $(-\Delta_D)^{-1}$. All finite joint test-function distributions and mixed moments converge.
