\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. For diluted even-arity Ising interactions in the Panchenko--Talagrand class, the limiting pressure is claimed to equal the infimum of finite hierarchical cavity trial values under first-moment integrability.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} Fix even \(p\ge2\), \(\alpha>0\), independent \(M_N\sim\operatorname{Pois}(\alpha N)\), interaction copies \(\theta_k\), field copies \(h_i\), and uniformly sampled vertex indices with replacement. Set
\[
 -H_N(\sigma)=\sum_{k=1}^{M_N}\theta_k(\sigma_{i_{1,k}},\ldots,\sigma_{i_{p,k}})+\sum_i h_i\sigma_i,
 \quad F_N=N^{-1}\mathbb E\log\sum_{\sigma\in\{\pm1\}^N}e^{-H_N(\sigma)}.
\]
Assume \(\mathbb E\max_s|\theta(s)|+\mathbb E|h|<\infty\), and a factorization
\[
 e^{\theta(s)}=a\left(1+b\prod_{\ell=1}^pf_\ell(s_\ell)\right),\quad
 a>0,\quad|b\prod_\ell f_\ell(s_\ell)|<1,\quad
 \mathbb E(-b)^n\in[0,\infty)\quad(n\ge1),
\]
where the \(f_\ell\) are iid random functions and independent of \(b\). The claimed exact pressure is
\[
 \lim_{N\to\infty}F_N=\inf_{r\ge0}\Phi_r.
\]
To define $\Phi_r$, put $q_x(s)=e^{xs}/(2\cosh x)$ and
\[
 C_\theta(x_1,\ldots,x_p)=\sum_{s\in\{\pm1\}^p}e^{\theta(s)}\prod_{\ell=1}^pq_{x_\ell}(s_\ell),\quad
 U_\theta(x_1,\ldots,x_{p-1};\epsilon)=\log\sum_{s\in\{\pm1\}^{p-1}}e^{\theta(s,\epsilon)}\prod_{\ell<p}q_{x_\ell}(s_\ell).
\]
Let $\mathcal M_1=\mathcal P(\mathbb R)$ and $\mathcal M_{j+1}=\mathcal P(\mathcal M_j)$, with $\mathcal P$ denoting Borel probability laws. For depth $r\ge1$, choose $\zeta\in\mathcal M_{r+1}$ and $0<m_1<\cdots<m_r<1$. Every independent message label follows $\eta_0\sim\zeta$, $\eta_{d+1}\mid\eta_d\sim\eta_d$ for $0\le d\le r-2$, then $x\mid\eta_{r-1}\sim\eta_{r-1}$. These variables are independent of physical disorder. With independent $k\sim\operatorname{Pois}(\alpha p)$, copies $\theta_j$ and field $h$, set
\[
 V_{\mathrm s}=\tfrac12\sum_{\epsilon=\pm1}\exp\left(h\epsilon+\sum_{j=1}^kU_{\theta_j}(x_1^j,\ldots,x_{p-1}^j;\epsilon)\right),\quad
 V_{\mathrm e}=C_\theta(x_1,\ldots,x_p).
\]
Define $\mathcal F_d$ to contain physical disorder and all message ancestors through depth $d$. Starting at $T_rV=V$, recurse
\[
 T_dV=\left(\mathbb E[(T_{d+1}V)^{m_{d+1}}\mid\mathcal F_d]\right)^{1/m_{d+1}},\quad
 \Phi_r=\inf_{\zeta,\boldsymbol m}\left(\log2+\mathbb E\log T_0V_{\mathrm s}-\alpha(p-1)\mathbb E\log T_0V_{\mathrm e}\right).
\]
At depth zero take iid real messages with law $\zeta$ and omit the power means. Interaction and field laws remain fixed as $N$ varies. Distinct interactions, field samples, Poisson counts and sampled indices are independent; message samples are also independent of this physical disorder.
