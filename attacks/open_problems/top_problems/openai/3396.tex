\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Manuscript 1.}
For positive integers $a_1,\ldots,a_n$, nonnegative target $t$ and $n\ge2$, ask whether $\sum_{i\in I}a_i=t$ for some subset. Put $b=\max\{1,\lceil\log_2(1+\max(t,a_1,\ldots,a_n))\rceil\}$ and use the stated word-RAM with read-only input and $w=\lceil4(n+b+\log_2(n+2))\rceil$-bit words; all preprocessing, addressing, word arithmetic and random generation are charged. The claimed single uniform randomized algorithm halts on every execution, is correct with probability at least $2/3$ on every input, and obeys
\[
 \forall c\ge1\ \exists C_c,N_c\ \forall n\ge N_c,\ b\le n^c:
 \quad T\le C_c2^{0.49n}
\]
word operations on every random path. The program is independent of the fixed polynomial bit-length exponent $c$.
