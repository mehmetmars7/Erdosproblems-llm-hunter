\section*{Projectivity from simultaneous self-Ext and algebra-Ext vanishing}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a counterexample in the selected manuscript. Over $\mathbb F_2(q,H_1,H_2)$, an explicit finite algebra and nonprojective Gorenstein-projective module are claimed to have all positive self-Ext and Ext into the algebra equal to zero.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} Over \(k=\mathbb F_2(q,H_1,H_2)\), with algebraically independent indeterminates, the source claims a finite-dimensional algebra \(\Lambda\) and nonprojective finite-dimensional left module \(Z\) with
\[
 \operatorname{Ext}^i_\Lambda(Z,Z)=0=
 \operatorname{Ext}^i_\Lambda(Z,\Lambda)\qquad(i\ge1).
\]
The module is Gorenstein-projective, \(\Lambda/\operatorname{rad}\Lambda\cong k^8\), and \((\operatorname{rad}\Lambda)^4\ne0\). All these conclusions persist after any extension of the coefficient field.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
A parameterized complete resolution starts from a small algebra and is transformed by cone and triangular-module constructions. Powers of the transcendental parameters prevent unwanted recurrence, while uniform kernel calculations give total acyclicity and remove positive self-extensions; scalar extension preserves those calculations.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
The finite-dimensional nonprojective module Z has Ext\textasciicircum{}i(Z,Z)=Ext\textasciicircum{}i(Z,Lambda)=0 for every i>0 over the explicit finite-dimensional field algebra. Additivity gives the requested Ext\textasciicircum{}i(Z,Z plus Lambda)=0, a counterexample to the universal implication. lean/docs/199.md documents precisely this counterexample and its ancillary properties. Within these assumptions, this is a claimed negative answer to the stated question.
