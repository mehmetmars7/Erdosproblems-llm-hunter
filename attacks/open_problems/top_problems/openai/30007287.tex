\paragraph{Manuscript 1.}
Let $F=\mathbb F_q(X)$ for a smooth projective geometrically connected curve, $G/\mathbb F_q$ split connected semisimple, and $D$ any effective divisor. Write $K_D=\ker(G(\mathcal O_{\mathbb A})\to G(\mathcal O_D))$ and $C_{D,\mathbb Q}$ for compactly supported rational cuspidal functions on $G(F)\backslash G(\mathbb A_F)/K_D$. For each dual-group nilpotent orbit $\mathcal O$, choose its Jacobson--Morozov $\phi_{\mathcal O}:\operatorname{SL}_2\to\widehat G$. Let $C_{D,\mathcal O,\mathbb Q}$ consist of functions whose spherical Hecke spectra, at every good point $x$, have semisimple class
\[
 [\phi_{\mathcal O}(\operatorname{diag}(q_x^{1/2},q_x^{-1/2}))c],
\]
where $c$ centralizes $\phi_{\mathcal O}$ and is algebraic and compact under every complex embedding. The same orbit is required at every good point. The claimed rational and $\ell$-adic decompositions are
\[
 C_{D,\mathbb Q}=\bigoplus_{\mathcal O} C_{D,\mathcal O,\mathbb Q},\qquad
 C_{D,\ell}=\bigoplus_{\mathcal O} C_{D,\mathcal O,\ell},
\]
with $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}\mathbb F_q$ and scalar extensions from $\mathbb Q$. For split adjoint absolutely simple $G/F$, a cuspidal representation generic and unramified at one place is further claimed tempered at every unramified place. Ramified temperedness and a full multiplicity formula are not asserted here.
