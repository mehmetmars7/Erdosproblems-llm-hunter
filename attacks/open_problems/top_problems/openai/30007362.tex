\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} On the \(d\)-regular tree, \(d\ge3\), the free zero-field Ising law has edge correlation \(\theta=\tanh\beta\). A factor of iid is a measurable map of independent uniform vertex labels to spins commuting with automorphisms. The claimed threshold is
\[
 \mu_{d,\beta}\text{ factor of iid}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \tanh\beta\le(d-1)^{-1/2}\qquad(\beta\ge0).

In that range the map can be Borel and equivariant on every label input. Finitary dependence or a coding-radius estimate is not asserted.
