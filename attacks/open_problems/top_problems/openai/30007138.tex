\section*{Uniform bi-Hölder transport under weak MTW}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
\textit{Uniform bi-Hölder transport under weak MTW}: For a fixed compact weak-MTW manifold, the manuscript claims one Hölder modulus for optimal maps and their inverses over all density pairs with common positive lower and finite upper bounds. No density smoothness or absence of conjugate cut points is assumed.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} On a smooth connected compact boundaryless $n$-manifold, $n\ge2$, use $c=d^2/2$ and the weak MTW assumption $-\tfrac32\partial_s^2\partial_t^2c(\exp_x(t\xi),\exp_x(v+s\eta))|_0\ge0$ for every $v$ in the open pre-cut domain and $\xi\perp\eta$. Fix $0<\lambda\le\Lambda<\infty$ such that normalized densities $\lambda\le\rho_i\le\Lambda$ exist. There are $0<\alpha\le1,C<\infty$, depending only on $(M,g),\lambda,\Lambda$, such that the almost-everywhere optimal map for $c$ from $\rho_0dV$ to $\rho_1dV$ has a homeomorphic representative $T$ with \[d(Tx,Tx^\prime)\le Cd(x,x^\prime)^\alpha,\qquad d(T^{-1}y,T^{-1}y^\prime)\le Cd(y,y^\prime)^\alpha.\] The two densities are merely measurable, have total mass one and obey the bounds almost everywhere. Its geometric convexity result also asserts convexity of every open tangent injectivity domain.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
\textit{Uniform bi-Hölder transport under weak MTW}: Lifted gap sections of transport potentials are studied in the tangent injectivity domain. Convexity turns a uniform power estimate on dual-potential oscillation into a diameter estimate on their endpoint images. Taking the supremum over all admissible densities gives one class-wide bound, and comparison at nearby poles supplies Hölder continuity in both transport directions.
