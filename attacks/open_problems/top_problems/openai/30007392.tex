\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} Fix $\beta>1$ and independent standard Gaussian $g_{ij}$. Put
\[
 F_n(\beta)=\log\sum_{\sigma\in\{-1,1\}^n}
 \exp\left(\frac\beta{\sqrt n}\sum_{i<j}g_{ij}\sigma_i\sigma_j\right),\quad
 s_n^2=\operatorname{Var}F_n,\quad X_n=(F_n-\mathbb EF_n)/s_n.
\]
Along the entire integer sequence $n\to\infty$, $X_n\Longrightarrow\nu_\beta$ for a nondegenerate probability law of mean zero and variance one. There is a positive $c$ with $\int e^{c|x|}\nu_\beta(dx)<\infty$; its finite-system limiting moments uniquely specify the law. No external field or temperature varying with $n$ is included.
