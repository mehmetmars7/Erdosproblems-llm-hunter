\section*{Virasoro conjecture}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
Manuscript 1 (family 065, 2026-09-24): Ordinary descendant Virasoro constraints are claimed for smooth projective complete intersections in every genus and curve class, retaining primitive and odd cohomology without semisimplicity. Manuscript 2 (family 065, 2026-10-05): Full descendant Virasoro constraints are claimed to pass from any smooth projective base to projectivizations of arbitrary nonsplit algebraic bundles, including all genera, integral curve classes, and odd insertions.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} Let $X\subset\mathbb P^N_{\mathbb C}$ be any smooth complete intersection. Let $F_g^X$ be its connected ordinary, unreduced genus-$g$ descendant potential, with cotangent classes at stable-map markings, arbitrary cohomology insertions and separate Novikov symbols for actual integral curve classes. Set $Z_X=\exp(\sum_{g\ge0}\hbar^{g-1}F_g^X)$. OpenAI claims the formal coefficientwise identities
\[
L_k^X Z_X=0\qquad(k\ge-1).
\]
Here $L_k^X$ are the manuscript's normally ordered Virasoro operators built from the Poincaré pairing, first Hodge grading $\mu|_{H^{p,q}}=p-\dim(X)/2$ and $R=c_1(TX)\cup$, with its stated scalar normalization. The quantifiers include all genera, individual curve classes and primitive or odd insertions; these are descendant rather than ancestor constraints.

\paragraph{Mathematical claim 2.} For a smooth connected projective complex variety $B$, an algebraic vector bundle $E$ of rank $r\ge2$, and $X=\mathbb P_B(E)$ parametrizing lines in the fibers, OpenAI claims
\[
\bigl[L_k^B Z_B=0\text{ for all }k\ge-1\bigr]\ \Longrightarrow\
\bigl[L_k^X Z_X=0\text{ for all }k\ge-1\bigr].
\]
For each target $Y$, $Z_Y$ is its ordinary descendant partition function $\exp(\sum_g\hbar^{g-1}F_g^Y)$ with all cohomology classes, stable-map cotangent classes and actual effective integral curve labels. The normally ordered $L_k^Y$ use $\mu_Y|_{H^{p,q}}=p-\dim(Y)/2$, $R_Y=c_1(TY)\cup$, and zero-mode scalar $\chi(Y)/16-\operatorname{str}(\mu_Y^2)/4$; other modes have no scalar correction. The conclusion is coefficientwise in every genus, curve class and descendant list.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Manuscript 1 (family 065, 2026-09-24): Relative caps create and detect bounded seam-contact states with coefficientwise finite inversions. Pairing each primitive error tensor with its conjugate realizes a squared norm through these tests; Hodge--Riemann positivity then kills the full tensor without limiting the number of primitive insertions.

Manuscript 2 (family 065, 2026-10-05): Localization on the completion by a trivial summand separates the base and projective-bundle blocks. The logarithm of an equivariant shift cancels coefficient differentiation in the grading, giving quantizable modes; ancestor bounds continue their identities coefficientwise, and quantum Riemann--Roch identifies the ordinary constraints on both components.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
Manuscript 1 (family 065, 2026-09-24): The main theorem has all genera, curve classes and cohomology insertions, but only smooth projective complete intersections; our Virasoro target is unrestricted in the smooth projective variety.

Manuscript 2 (family 065, 2026-10-05): Virasoro constraints are propagated only to projective bundles over a base already satisfying them; the catalogue target includes every smooth projective variety.
