\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
Manuscript 1 (family 065, 2026-09-24): Ordinary descendant Virasoro constraints are claimed for smooth projective complete intersections in every genus and curve class, retaining primitive and odd cohomology without semisimplicity. Manuscript 2 (family 065, 2026-10-05): Full descendant Virasoro constraints are claimed to pass from any smooth projective base to projectivizations of arbitrary nonsplit algebraic bundles, including all genera, integral curve classes, and odd insertions.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Manuscript 1 (family 065, 2026-09-24): Relative caps create and detect bounded seam-contact states with coefficientwise finite inversions. Pairing each primitive error tensor with its conjugate realizes a squared norm through these tests; Hodge--Riemann positivity then kills the full tensor without limiting the number of primitive insertions.

Manuscript 2 (family 065, 2026-10-05): Localization on the completion by a trivial summand separates the base and projective-bundle blocks. The logarithm of an equivariant shift cancels coefficient differentiation in the grading, giving quantizable modes; ancestor bounds continue their identities coefficientwise, and quantum Riemann--Roch identifies the ordinary constraints on both components.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
Manuscript 1 (family 065, 2026-09-24): The main theorem has all genera, curve classes and cohomology insertions, but only smooth projective complete intersections; our Virasoro target is unrestricted in the smooth projective variety.

Manuscript 2 (family 065, 2026-10-05): Virasoro constraints are propagated only to projective bundles over a base already satisfying them; the catalogue target includes every smooth projective variety.
