\section*{A deterministic linear walk clock on tree-weighted planar maps}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. On tree-weighted planar maps, the stationary walk is claimed to converge to Brownian motion of a $\sqrt2$-Liouville sphere with exactly the edge count as its time factor.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} Choose $(M_n,T_n)$ uniformly from rooted planar maps with $n$ edges and a distinguished spanning tree. Loops and multiple edges are permitted. On its vertex set $V_n$, let $d_n$ be graph distance and $\mu_n=\sum_v\deg(v)/(2n)\,\delta_v$, counting a loop twice in the degree. Start a rate-one walk $X^n$ from $\mu_n$ and choose each incident half-edge uniformly at a jump. Its generator is
\[
 L_nf(v)=\deg(v)^{-1}\sum_{e\text{ incident half-edge}}[f(\operatorname{other}(e))-f(v)].
\]
Let $(S,D_h,\mu_h)$ have the ordinary Liouville quantum sphere law at parameter $\sqrt2$, normalized to area one. Its stationary Liouville Brownian motion $B^h$ has closed form $\mathcal E_h(f,g)=\frac12\int_{S^2}\langle\nabla f,\nabla g\rangle\,d\operatorname{vol}$ on $L^2(\mu_h)$. For a random real $Y$, define $m(Y)$ by $\mathbb E\arctan(Y-m(Y))=0$, and set
\[
 a_n=\exp\{m(\log\operatorname{diam}(S,D_h))-m(\log\max(1,\operatorname{diam}(V_n,d_n)))\}.
\]
With $Q_n^T=\operatorname{Law}((X^n_{nt})_{0\le t\le T}\mid M_n,T_n)$ and $Q_h^T=\operatorname{Law}((B_t^h)_{0\le t\le T}\mid h)$,
\[
 (V_n,a_nd_n,\mu_n,Q_n^T)\Longrightarrow(S,D_h,\mu_h,Q_h^T)\quad(T>0).
\]
The topology embeds both spaces into a common metric space and combines Hausdorff distance, Prokhorov distance of the area measures, and Prokhorov distance of the path laws in Skorokhod $J_1$ topology. The geometric metric-measure and contour reconstruction results are inputs; the walk clock is exactly $nt$, with multiplier one.
