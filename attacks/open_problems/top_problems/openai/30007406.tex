\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} Assume that critical Poisson--Voronoi crossing probabilities converge to Cardy's value in every bounded Jordan quadrilateral. Couple colours at all densities by independent uniform site marks, declaring a site black when its mark is at most $p$. Compare full-plane Poisson--Voronoi cells of intensity $\varepsilon^{-2}$ with triangular-lattice sites of mesh $\delta$ and their hexagonal cells. For the unit-square left-to-right crossing, let $N_e^M$ count colour-pivotal sites at $p=1/2$, with $M\in\{V,\triangle\}$ denoting the model and $e$ its mesh. Set
\[
 r_e^M=(\mathbb EN_e^M)^{-1},\qquad
 p_e^M(\lambda)=\max(0,\min(1,1/2+\lambda r_e^M)),\qquad
 \tau_e^M(Q)=\inf\{\lambda:C_Q\text{ occurs at }p_e^M(\lambda)\}.
\]
Crossings stay within the closed quad, without wiring. For a fixed enumeration $(Q_j)$ of rational polygonal quads, including the unit square, use
\[
 \mathcal K=[-\infty,\infty]^{\{Q_j\}},\quad
 d_{\mathcal K}(s,t)=\sum_{j\ge1}2^{-j}|\theta(s(Q_j))-\theta(t(Q_j))|,\quad
 \theta(t)=2\arctan(t)/\pi.
\]
Expected pivotal counts are finite and positive, and both $r_e^M\to0$. There is a probability law $\nu_{\triangle}$ on $\mathcal K$ such that
\[
 \operatorname{Law}(\tau_\delta^{\triangle})\Longrightarrow\nu_{\triangle},\qquad
 d_{\mathrm{BL}}(\operatorname{Law}(\tau_\varepsilon^V\mid\eta_\varepsilon),\nu_{\triangle})
 \xrightarrow{\mathbb P}0.
\]
The second convergence is in Poisson-geometry probability; the conditional law averages colours while keeping all geometry fixed.
