\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} Use balanced-torus six-vertex weights \((a,b,c)=(1,1,c)\), \(0<c\le2\), conditioning each vertical column to equal left/right arrow counts. Take first horizontal size to infinity, then even vertical size, and height steps of one. The source claims that the resulting plane law exists and, modulo constants,
\[
 h_\delta\Longrightarrow\sigma(c)\Gamma,\quad
 \sigma(c)^2=\frac1{\arcsin(c/2)},\quad
 G(x,y)=-\frac1{2\pi}\log|x-y|.
\]
For compactly supported zero-mass finite-energy signed measures \(\mu_i\), pairings converge to centered Gaussian covariance \(\sigma(c)^2\iint G\,d\mu_i\,d\mu_j\). On any bounded open \(U\), convergence and tightness also hold in \(B^\alpha_{\infty,\infty}(U)\), \(B^\alpha_{p,q}(U)\), and \(W^{\alpha,p}(U)\), for \(-1<\alpha<0\), \(1\le p<\infty\), \(1\le q\le\infty\), with common pinning. For disjoint endpoint pairs, increment moments converge uniformly on compact configurations to
\[
 \sigma(c)^k\sum_{\pi\text{ pairing}}\prod_{\{i,j\}\in\pi}
 [G(u_i',u_j')-G(u_i',u_j)-G(u_i,u_j')+G(u_i,u_j)],
\]
with odd moments zero. No uniqueness among all plane Gibbs states or slopes is asserted.
