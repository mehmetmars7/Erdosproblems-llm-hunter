\subsection{OpenAI's claim}

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} For a complete separable metric space $(M,d)$, integer $n\ge2$ and $\kappa\in\mathbb R$, the claim equates being an $n$-dimensional Alexandrov space of curvature $\ge\kappa$ with the following analytic conditions: $m=\mathcal H_d^n$ has full support, $(M,d,m)$ is unreduced $\operatorname{RCD}((n-1)\kappa,n)$, and \[R(X,Y,Y,X)(f)\ge\kappa\int f(|X|^2|Y|^2-\langle X,Y\rangle^2)\,dm\] for every test field pair $X,Y$ and nonnegative test $f$. The test functions lie in the $L^2$ Laplacian domain, have bounded globally Lipschitz representatives and $\Delta f\in W^{1,2}$; test fields are finite sums $a\nabla b$ of such functions. With $[X,Y]=\nabla_XY-\nabla_YX$, the distributional tensor is \[\begin{aligned}R(X,Y,Z,W)(f)=\int\bigl(&-\langle\nabla_X(fW),\nabla_YZ\rangle-f\langle\nabla_YZ,W\rangle\operatorname{div}X\\&+\langle\nabla_Y(fW),\nabla_XZ\rangle+f\langle\nabla_XZ,W\rangle\operatorname{div}Y-f\langle\nabla_{[X,Y]}Z,W\rangle\bigr)\,dm.\end{aligned}\] The curvature sign is $\nabla_X\nabla_Y-\nabla_Y\nabla_X-\nabla_{[X,Y]}$. RCD here combines the unreduced curvature-dimension condition with quadratic Cheeger energy; the reference measure is exactly Hausdorff measure.
