\section*{Entanglement Area Law for 2D Gapped Quantum Systems}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. Unique ground states of gapped finite-range square-lattice Hamiltonians are claimed to have entropy bounded by a constant times the boundary size for every site subset.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} On a finite induced square-lattice domain \(\Lambda\), place \(\mathbb C^q\) at each site and consider
\[
 H=\sum_{\varnothing\ne X\subseteq\Lambda,\ \operatorname{diam}_{d_\Lambda}X\le R}h_X,
 \qquad h_X=h_X^*,\quad\|h_X\|\le J,
\]
with one term per support, fixed integers \(q\ge1,R\ge0\), and \(J>0\). If the normalized ground vector \(\Omega\) is unique up to phase and
\[
 H-E_0I\ge\Delta(I-|\Omega\rangle\langle\Omega|),\qquad\Delta>0,
\]
the claimed constant \(C(q,R,J,\Delta)<\infty\) satisfies
\[
 -\operatorname{Tr}\rho_A\log\rho_A\le C|\partial_\Lambda A|
 \qquad(A\subseteq\Lambda),\quad
 \rho_A=\operatorname{Tr}_{\Lambda\setminus A}|\Omega\rangle\langle\Omega|.
\]
The boundary counts nearest-neighbor edges crossing from \(A\) to its complement, and \(C\) is independent of domain, subset, and admissible interactions.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Marginal tail estimates start with boxes, and positive constraints truncate states near finite sets. Conditional operator bounds and symmetric-replica entropy metrics are transported through finite metric trees, converting the global gap into a uniform area estimate for arbitrary induced domains.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
The area theorem treats all finite induced square-lattice domains, arbitrary finite-range uniformly bounded interactions, fixed finite site dimension and a unique ground vector with a global gap Delta. Its entropy bound uses exactly the number of lattice edges crossing A and one size-independent constant depending only on those fixed parameters. Within these assumptions, this is a claimed affirmative answer to the stated question.
