\section*{Massive interface limits under thermal FK–Ising perturbation}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a mathematical result relevant to this question in the selected manuscript. At fixed nonzero thermal mass, FK--Ising interfaces on angle-controlled isoradial lattices are claimed to converge to a lattice-independent massive $\mathrm{SLE}_{16/3}$ law.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} Fix a bounded simply connected $\Omega\subset\mathbb C$, interior basepoint $z_*$, different boundary prime ends $a,b$, and $m\ne0$. Approximate it by finite simply connected isoradial domains of circumradius $\delta$, with rhombus half-angles $\eta\le\theta_e\le\pi/2-\eta$ for fixed $\eta>0$. Assume kernel convergence from $z_*$ and convergence of the two marks under normalized maps $\chi_\delta:\Omega^\delta\to\mathbb D$ and $\chi:\Omega\to\mathbb D$. Choose nomes $r_\delta$ with $r_\delta/\delta\to|m|/2$ and elliptic parameters $k_\delta$ satisfying
\[
 r_\delta=\exp[-\pi K(\sqrt{1-k_\delta^2})/K(k_\delta)],\quad
 U_k(t)=\int_0^t(1-k^2\sin^2s)^{-1/2}\,ds,\quad K(k)=U_k(\pi/2).
\]
Let $A_\delta(\theta)$ solve $U_{k_\delta}(A_\delta(\theta))=2K(k_\delta)\theta/\pi$, and set $\widehat\theta_e=A_\delta(\theta_e)$ for $m>0$, or $\pi/2-A_\delta(\pi/2-\theta_e)$ for $m<0$. Along the counterclockwise boundary, wire $(b^\delta a^\delta)$ primally and $(a^\delta b^\delta)$ dually. The interface is oriented from $a^\delta$ to $b^\delta$. An open-edge configuration $E$, with $\ell(E)$ closed medial loops, has probability proportional to
\[
 (\sqrt2)^{\ell(E)}\prod_{e\in E}\sin(\widehat\theta_e/2)
 \prod_{e\notin E}\sin(\pi/4-\widehat\theta_e/2).
\]
For its oriented interface $\gamma_\delta$, the full-sequence limit is
\[
 \operatorname{Law}(\chi_\delta\circ\gamma_\delta)\Longrightarrow\chi_*\mu_{\Omega,a,b,m}
\]
in uniform curve distance modulo increasing reparametrization. The physical prime-end law is independent of the admissible lattices and nome sequence. It is the uniquely characterized thermal massive $\mathrm{SLE}_{16/3}$ law for $m>0$; for $m<0$, reverse the positive-$|m|$ law with exchanged endpoints and primal/dual labels. No regularity of the physical boundary beyond the stated prime-end hypotheses is imposed. The conformal maps satisfy $\chi_\delta(z_*)=\chi(z_*)=0$, with both derivatives at $z_*$ positive and real.
