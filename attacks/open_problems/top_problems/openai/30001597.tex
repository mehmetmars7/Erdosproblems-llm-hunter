Manuscript 3 (family 039, 2026-10-05): The same eventual volume-bound equality for ordinary multipoint Seshadri constants is claimed in positive characteristic at geometric generic tuples, in dimensions two and higher; Manuscript 4 (family 039, 2026-09-23): At $r\ge10$ very general complex plane points, every effective curve is claimed to have total assigned multiplicity below its degree times $\sqrt r$, including reducible curves and unequal assignments.

Full density forces every sufficiently deep interior lattice point to occur, allowing torus interpolation to separate the needed jets; very-general tuples inherit all the finite tests. Manuscript 2 (family 039, 2026-09-23): Finite jet tests are transferred to an algebraic torus through nodal-curve degeneration. A varying exponent region is tuned to a primitive lattice direction and a subgroup with exactly $r$ points, giving injectivity for all required orders rather than only a fixed multiplicity.

Manuscript 3 (family 039, 2026-10-05): Formal substitutions replace the analytic degeneration used in the complex companions. A nodal equation with two distinct branches even in characteristic two permits the graded-support reshaping and finite torus interpolation to proceed over the ground field. Manuscript 4 (family 039, 2026-09-23): Incidence loci and cyclic products reduce a hypothetical failure to equal multiplicities at arbitrary configurations. Degeneration toward a cubic produces finite jet constraints; colliding the remaining points and taking limits of elliptic theta bases turns the contradiction into a full-rank polynomial evaluation problem.
