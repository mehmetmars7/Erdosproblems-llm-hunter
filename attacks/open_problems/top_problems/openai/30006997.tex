\section*{Integral block finiteness over a fixed complete valuation ring}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. Bounded defect is claimed to yield finitely many integral Morita classes over Witt vectors, and over any fixed complete valuation coefficient ring of mixed characteristic, provided its residue field is algebraically closed.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} For \(\mathcal O=W(\overline{\mathbb F}_p)\) and integer \(M\ge1\), the source claims finitely many \(\mathcal O\)-linear Morita classes among \(\mathcal O[G]b\), as \(G\) ranges over finite groups and blocks \(b\) have defect-group order at most \(M\). Its complete-ring corollary states more generally: fix a complete discretely valued ring \(\mathcal R\), whose fraction field has characteristic zero and whose residue field \(\ell\) has characteristic \(p\) and is algebraically closed. Then
\[
 \#\{\mathcal R[G]b:\ G\text{ finite},\ b\text{ primitive central idempotent},\ |D_{\bar b}|\le M\}/\mathcal R\text{-linear Morita equivalence}<\infty.
\]
Only the block and finite group vary; the coefficient ring remains fixed.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
The proof combines integral bounds for basic orders with a bound on their Morita--Frobenius behavior. Rationality and Cartan controls feed the integral finiteness criterion, and lifting from the residue field handles fixed ramified coefficient rings as well as Witt vectors.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
The fixed-complete-coefficient-ring corollary states exactly O-linear bounded-residue-defect Morita finiteness for every fixed complete characteristic-zero DVR with algebraically closed characteristic-p residue field. Within these assumptions, this is a claimed affirmative answer to the stated question.
