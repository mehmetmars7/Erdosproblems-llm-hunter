\section*{Rational injectivity of reduced coarse assembly}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
For a uniformly discrete bounded-geometry union of finite graphs, an infinite-order degree-one coarse K-homology class is claimed to have zero ordinary reduced assembly image, contradicting coarse Novikov injectivity. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} For some uniformly discrete bounded-geometry coarse disjoint union $X$ of finite connected uniformly bounded-degree graphs, OpenAI claims an infinite-order $\alpha\in KX_1(X)$ with
\[
\mu_X(\alpha)=0\in K_1(C^*(X)).
\]
The target is the ordinary reduced Roe algebra and $KX$ is coarse topological $K$-homology. Consequently ordinary coarse assembly is not injective integrally or after rational tensoring. Bounded geometry means each fixed-radius ball has uniformly bounded cardinality.
