\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} Let $X$ be smooth connected projective complex with $H^0(X,\Omega_X^q)=0$ for $q>0$ and smoothly semipositive $L=-K_X$. Equip an algebraic torus action by $T$ with the differential linearization on the anticanonical bundle. Take a dominant $T$-invariant rational $f:X\dashrightarrow Z$ to smooth connected projective $Z$, with $\mathbb C(Z)$ relatively algebraically closed in $\mathbb C(X)$. On a resolution $\pi:Y\to X$, $f_Y:Y\to Z$, put $L_Y=\pi^*L$, $E_Y=K_{Y/X}$ and $\eta=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb C(Z)$. Assume
\[
H^0(Y_\eta,pL_Y)^T\ne0\text{ for some }p>0,\qquad
\dim_{\mathbb C(Z)}H^0(Y_\eta,E_Y+mL_Y)^T=1\text{ for every }m\in a\mathbb Z_{>0}
\]
for some $a>0$. OpenAI claims either a global invariant section $H^0(X,rL)^T\ne0$ for some $r>0$, or a dominant rational $Z\dashrightarrow W$ to a smooth connected projective $W$ with $\dim W<\dim Z$ and relatively algebraically closed $\mathbb C(W)\subset\mathbb C(X)$, such that the composite again satisfies these two generic-fiber conditions, with possibly different positive indices.
