\section*{Higher bounded Hochschild cohomology of von Neumann algebras}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
Every bounded Hochschild cocycle of degree two or higher, with self-coefficients on any complex von Neumann algebra, is claimed to admit an actual bounded primitive, settling Kadison--Ringrose cohomology vanishing. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} For every complex von Neumann algebra $M$ and integer $k\ge2$, let $C_b^k(M,M)$ be bounded complex multilinear maps. Use the Hochschild differential
\[
(df)(a_1,\ldots,a_{k+1})=a_1f(a_2,\ldots,a_{k+1})+\sum_{i=1}^k(-1)^if(a_1,\ldots,a_ia_{i+1},\ldots,a_{k+1})+(-1)^{k+1}f(a_1,\ldots,a_k)a_{k+1}.
\]
OpenAI claims $df=0$ implies $f=dg$ for some bounded $(k-1)$-cochain, hence $H_b^k(M,M)=0$. The quotient uses the actual coboundary image, without closure; degree one is not the new headline range.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Manuscript 1 (family 295, 2026-09-23): A carefully weighted unitary random walk makes averaging in one cocycle variable harmonic. This module identity constructs primitives, after which classical normal-reduction and type-decomposition results extend the argument to all algebras.
