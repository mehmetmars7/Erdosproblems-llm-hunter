\paragraph{Manuscript 1.}
Let $k=\overline{\mathbb F}_q$ have characteristic $p$, $E=\overline{\mathbb Q}_{\ell}$ with $\ell\ne p$, and let $X_0/\mathbb F_q$ be smooth projective geometrically connected of genus at least two with rational point $x_0$. Set $X=(X_0)_k$, $x=(x_0)_k$ and $U=X\setminus\{x\}$. Let $G_0$ be split simple simply connected, with Borel $B_0$, and write $G,B$ for base changes. The characteristic hypotheses are: a nondegenerate invariant symmetric form on $\mathfrak g$ whose restriction to the central Lie algebra of each Levi is nondegenerate; the Chevalley-restriction isomorphism $k[\mathfrak m]^M\simeq k[\operatorname{Lie}(T_M)]^{W_M}$ for every Levi; semisimple Lie-algebra centralizers are Levi subgroups; and over every extension field each nilpotent Lie-algebra element lies in a parabolic unipotent-radical Lie algebra.

For a continuous Zariski-dense parameter $\rho:\pi_1^{\rm et}(U)\to\widehat G(L)$, $L/\mathbb Q_{\ell}$ finite, require trivial wild inertia and tame monodromy
\[
 \rho(\gamma)=\exp(t_{\ell}(\gamma)N),\qquad
 \dim Z_{\widehat G}(N)=\operatorname{rank}(\widehat G),
\]
with nilpotent $N$ and $t_{\ell}:I_x\to\mathbb Z_{\ell}$. On the stack $\mathcal A=\operatorname{Bun}_{G,B,x}(X)$, the claim gives a nonzero locally constructible perverse $E$-adic sheaf $M$ with nilpotent parabolic singular support and
\[
 \operatorname{Hecke}_{I,(V_i)}(M)\simeq
 M\boxtimes\mathop{\boxtimes}_{i\in I}(V_i)_{\rho}
 \quad\hbox{on }\mathcal A\times U^I
\]
for every finite set $I$ and representations $V_i$ of $\widehat G$. The eigenisomorphisms respect units, convolution, permutations and collision-diagonal fusion. No Frobenius descent of $\rho$ is assumed. The nilpotent cone consists of Higgs fields with nilpotent generic value and residue in the Borel unipotent radical.
