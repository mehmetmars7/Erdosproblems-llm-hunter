\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
Manuscript 1 (family 068, 2026-09-26): A globally strict anticanonical metric with controlled boundary integrability is claimed to make every pseudo-effective rational divisor effective after a correction on that boundary, and to remove fixed errors from unbounded anticanonical twists. \subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} Let $Y$ be a complex projective variety that is smooth and has positive dimension and $C\ge0$ integral. Assume $-K_Y+C$ carries a singular Hermitian metric $h$ with curvature at least a Kähler form and whose multiplier ideal satisfies $\mathcal J(h)\supset\mathcal O_Y(-C)$. OpenAI claims
\[
\forall\text{ pseudo-effective rational }M\ \exists C_0\ge0\text{ supported on }C:\quad M+C_0\sim_{\mathbb Q}E\ge0.
\]
The multiplier condition means local squared integrability of the canonical section of $C$ in the metric. A separate conversion theorem says that if $X$ is smooth connected projective complex, $L=-K_X$ smoothly semipositive and $P$ pseudo-effective Cartier, then effective integral $N_j\sim m_jL-P$ for unbounded positive $m_j$ imply $H^0(X,mL)\ne0$ for some $m>0$.

\paragraph{Mathematical claim 2.} Consider a holomorphic action by a compact real torus $T$ on a compact complex manifold $F$, with natural differential linearization on $L_F=-K_F$. Define
\[
I_T(m)=\sum_{q\ge0}(-1)^q\dim H^q(F,L_F^m)^T.
\]
OpenAI claims some integer $a>0$ and $P\in\mathbb Q[t]$ with $I_T(m)=P(m)$ for every positive multiple of $a$, and $P(0)=I_T(0)$.
Its independent conversion theorem takes smooth connected projective complex $S$, smoothly semipositive $L=-K_S$ and pseudo-effective Cartier $D$; sections $H^0(S,m_jL-D)\ne0$ for a strictly increasing positive sequence $m_j$ are claimed to imply $H^0(S,kL)\ne0$ for some $k>0$. This conversion statement has no group-action hypothesis.
