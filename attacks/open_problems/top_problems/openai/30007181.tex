\section*{Superreflexivity from nontrivial Markov type}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 327; 2026-09-23), the asserted result is that Markov type with exponent $p>1$ on a real Banach space forces superreflexivity and hence the existence of an equivalent uniformly smooth norm.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Manuscript 1.}
A real Banach space $X$ has Markov type $p$ with constant $K$ if every finite stationary reversible chain $(Z_t)$, map $f$ of its state space into $X$, and integer $t\ge1$ satisfy
\[
 \mathbb E\|f(Z_t)-f(Z_0)\|^p\le K^pt\,\mathbb E\|f(Z_1)-f(Z_0)\|^p.
\]
The asserted implication is
\[
 (\exists p>1,K<\infty:\ X\text{ has Markov type }p)
 \Longrightarrow X\text{ admits an equivalent uniformly convex norm}.
\]
The conclusion is superreflexivity and imposes no separability or finite-dimensionality assumption.
