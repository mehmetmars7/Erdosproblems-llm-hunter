\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} Let \(q=3(N-1)\), \(U_q\) be the spin-\(q/2\) lowest Landau level on a sphere of area \(2\pi q\), and
\[
 H_{N,q}=\sum_{i<j}P_{ij}^{(1)}\quad\text{on }\bigwedge^NU_q,
\]
where each projector selects pair total spin \(q-1\). Perturb by \(\lambda\sum_iT_q(\varphi)^{(i)}\), with \(T_q(\varphi)=\Pi_qM_\varphi\Pi_q|_{U_q}\). The source claims constants \(\lambda_*,\Delta_*>0\), \(N_*<\infty\), such that
\[
 E_1(N,\lambda,\varphi)-E_0(N,\lambda,\varphi)\ge\Delta_*
\]
for every \(N\ge N_*\), bounded real measurable \(\varphi\) with \(\|\varphi\|_\infty\le1\), and real \(|\lambda|\le\lambda_*\). Eigenvalues count multiplicity, so the ground state is unique.
