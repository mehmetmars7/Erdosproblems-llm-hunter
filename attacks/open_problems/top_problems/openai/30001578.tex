\section*{Strict Cost–\$\textbackslash{}ell\textasciicircum{}2\$-Betti Inequalities for Free Group Actions}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. A finitely generated amalgam is claimed to have free probability-preserving actions of different costs, refuting fixed price.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Finite-height extensions of the amalgam yield graphings with costs approaching one. A relative graphing-compression inequality and Bernoulli finite-permutation models give a separate cost lower bound, supported by a planar word lemma; the two action constructions therefore have unequal costs.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
The cost lower bound beta\_1\textasciicircum{}(2)(G)+1 applies to every free p.m.p. action of the infinite main group. The higher-cost action is strictly above its other action’s cost and thus strictly above that lower bound, giving the record’s requested existence. Within these assumptions, this is a claimed affirmative answer to the stated question.
