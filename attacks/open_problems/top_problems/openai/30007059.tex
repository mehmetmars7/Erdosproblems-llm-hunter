\section*{Split tangents on rationally connected projective manifolds}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
Both summands of a specified two-part tangent splitting on a rationally connected projective complex manifold are claimed to be integrable, yielding a compatible product decomposition. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} Let $X$ be smooth connected projective complex of dimension at least two and rationally connected, meaning a morphism $\mathbb P^1\to X$ can contain two general points in its image. Specify any holomorphic decomposition into subbundles of positive rank
\[
T_X=E_1\oplus E_2,
\]
OpenAI claims both summands are integrable: $[s,t]$ is a local section of $E_i$ whenever $s,t$ are local sections of $E_i$. The embeddings into the tangent bundle are fixed data, so this concerns their actual distributions.

The manuscript's product corollary also gives smooth projective factors $X_1,X_2$ and an isomorphism $X\simeq X_1\times X_2$ taking each specified tangent summand to the tangent direction of its corresponding factor. It derives this global conclusion by applying the cited earlier product theorem after establishing integrability.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Manuscript 1 (family 052, 2026-09-23): A formal diagonal solution separates derivatives through the two holomorphic projectors. Positive normal-bundle section counts force the formal projective ratios to be rational, and tangency contracts the exceptional curves of a resolution, giving a global holomorphic map whose factor directions certify integrability.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
Automatic integrability proves the first requested conclusion for the specified splitting. The same manuscript's compatible-product corollary then gives the two smooth projective factors and identifies their tangent directions with the original embedded summands. Together these results cover both assertions on this page.
