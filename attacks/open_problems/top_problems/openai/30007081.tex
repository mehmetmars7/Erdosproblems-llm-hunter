\section*{Bounded-degree coboundary expanders in each dimension at least three}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
\textit{Bounded-degree coboundary expanders}: For each $d\ge3$, the paper claims arbitrarily large finite $d$-complexes with bounded vertex incidence and positive $\mathbb F_2$ coboundary expansion in every lower degree. The constants may depend on the fixed dimension.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} For each integer $d\ge3$ there are finite connected pure $d$-dimensional simplicial complexes $X_m$ with $|X_m(0)|\to\infty$ and constants $D<\infty$, $\epsilon>0$ such that each vertex belongs to at most $D$ top faces. Over $\mathbb F_2$, put $w_i(\sigma)=\#\{\tau\in X_m(d):\sigma\subset\tau\}/(\binom{d+1}{i+1}|X_m(d)|)$ and $\|f\|_i=\sum_{f(\sigma)\ne0}w_i(\sigma)$. With $\delta_i f(\tau)=\sum_{\sigma\subset\tau,\ |\sigma|=i+1}f(\sigma)$, $B^i=\operatorname{im}\delta_{i-1}$ and $B^0$ the constants, the claimed inequality is \[\|\delta_i f\|_{i+1}\ge\epsilon\min_{b\in B^i}\|f-b\|_i\quad(0\le i<d).\] Constants are uniform in $m,i,f$, while depending on the fixed dimension.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
\textit{Bounded-degree coboundary expanders}: An infinite coset complex is built from a congruence subgroup of a polynomial special linear group over a fixed finite field. Its growing finite congruence quotients have fixed local groups, hence bounded degrees. Acyclicity and quotient cohomology vanishing are combined with local spectral expansion; taking the required skeleton preserves the normalized expansion inequalities.
