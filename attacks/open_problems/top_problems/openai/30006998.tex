\section*{Integral K3 injectivity for regular local domains}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a counterexample in the selected manuscript. A different two-dimensional ramified ring in mixed characteristic $(0,5)$ is claimed to violate integral Gersten injectivity in degree three.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
A divisor on a cyclotomic quintic supplies a Bott-symbol class whose coefficient boundary vanishes by a Steinberg relation. The resulting integral lift is generically zero by support, and a norm-valuation test detects it using the different residue-degree behavior of closed points.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
The specified two-dimensional ramified regular local domain A and integral class c have c nonzero (detected after inverting the uniformizer modulo 5) and restriction zero to Frac A. This directly refutes universal integral K\_3 injectivity. Within these assumptions, this is a claimed negative answer to the stated question.
