\section*{Stanley–Stembridge Chromatic Symmetric Function Conjecture}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
\textit{Elementary positivity for unit interval graphs}: OpenAI claims nonnegative $\mathbb N[q]$ coefficients in the elementary-basis expansion of chromatic quasisymmetric functions of natural unit interval graphs. This concerns the elementary-positivity portion of the Shareshian--Wachs conjecture.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} For weakly increasing integers $i\le h(i)\le n$, define $G$ on $[n]$ by $\{i,j\}\in E(G)$ exactly when $i<j\le h(i)$. Its chromatic quasisymmetric function is $\chi_G(X;q)=\sum_{f\text{ proper}}q^{\#\{ij\in E:i<j,\ f(i)<f(j)\}}\prod_i x_{f(i)}$. The claim is a formal identity \[\chi_G(X;q)=\sum_{\sigma\in D_G^0}q^{\operatorname{inv}_G(\sigma)}e_{\theta_G(\sigma)}(X),\] where $D_G^0$ consists of permutations whose adjacent descents are graph edges, $\operatorname{inv}_G$ counts inversions whose values form edges, and a deterministic terminating procedure supplies a partition $\theta_G(\sigma)\vdash n$. Since $e_\lambda=\prod_i e_{\lambda_i}$, all elementary-basis coefficients are polynomials in $q$ with nonnegative integer coefficients.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
\textit{Elementary positivity for unit interval graphs}: Positive wall transformations produce sections indexed by exponent-lattice elements. Interval rotations identify the relevant graph functions with single positive sections, and a support argument forces the contributing exponents onto distinguished anchors. A packet bijection then extracts a nonnegative combinatorial expansion whose powers of $q$ record the accumulated jumps.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
\textit{Elementary positivity for unit interval graphs}: The theorem proves coefficientwise elementary positivity in q for all natural unit interval graphs G(m). At q=1 this is the complete recorded chromatic-symmetric-function assertion; the stated Guay-Paquet reduction also covers all (3+1)-free posets.

The claimed result implies the stated question and also supplies the additional strength described above.
