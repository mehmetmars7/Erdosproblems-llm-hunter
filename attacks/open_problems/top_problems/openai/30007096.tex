\section*{Degree-uniform power saving for intersective polynomial differences}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
\textit{Degree-uniform intersective differences}: For an intersective polynomial with a positive leading term and degree $k\ge2$, the claimed bound for difference-avoiding subsets is $O_h(N^{1-c_k})$. The exponent depends only on $k$, although the implicit constant depends on the polynomial.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} For each degree $k\ge2$ there is $0<c_k<1$ such that every intersective $h\in\mathbb Z[x]$ of degree $k$ with positive leading coefficient has constants $C_h\ge1,N_h$ satisfying \[(A-A)\cap h(\mathbb N_{>0})\subseteq\{0\},\quad A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\},\quad N\ge N_h\quad\Longrightarrow\quad|A|\le C_hN^{1-c_k}.\] Intersective means having a root modulo every positive integer. The saving exponent depends only on the degree; the prefactor and threshold may depend on all coefficients.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
\textit{Degree-uniform intersective differences}: Local roots are normalized until their first nonzero Taylor coefficients are units, without changing their multiplicities. Auxiliary polynomials are chosen to remain compatible under passage to progressions. A multilinear density lower bound and a distribution-comparison argument then produce contraction with numerical choices controlled by the degree rather than the coefficients.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
\textit{Degree-uniform intersective differences}: The main theorem has one c\_k>0 for every k>=2, then for every positive-leading degree-k intersective h constants C\_h,N\_h, and the exact avoidance (A-A) intersect h(N\_+) subset \{0\}. It allows all signed nonzero polynomial values as differences and an h-dependent threshold; it is not limited to squares or to the exponent depending arbitrarily on h.
