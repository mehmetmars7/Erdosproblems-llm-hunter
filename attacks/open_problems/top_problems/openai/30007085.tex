\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
\textit{Deterministic nonbipartite Ramanujan graphs}: For each fixed $d\ge3$, the claimed deterministic polynomial-time construction gives a simple nonbipartite $d$-regular graph at every sufficiently large even order. All nonconstant eigenvalues lie strictly inside the tree spectral interval.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} For every fixed integer $d\ge3$ there are $n_0(d),k_d$ and a deterministic algorithm which, on every even $n\ge n_0(d)$, outputs an adjacency list using $O_d(n^{k_d})$ bit operations. Its $n$-vertex graph $G$ is simple and has degree $d$ at every vertex. The asserted adjacency eigenvalue condition is \[-2\sqrt{d-1}<\lambda<2\sqrt{d-1}\quad\text{for every eigenvalue of }A_G|_{\mathbf1^\perp}.\] These strict inequalities imply that $G$ is connected and nonbipartite, and in particular imply the requested non-strict Ramanujan bound.
