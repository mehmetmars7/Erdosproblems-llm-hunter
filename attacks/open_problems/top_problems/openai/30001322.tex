\section*{Transience Implies Ballisticity for Uniformly Elliptic RWRE}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. Iid uniformly elliptic nearest-neighbor walks in dimensions $d\geq2$ are claimed to have positive directional speed whenever escape in that direction is almost sure.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Regeneration is split into spatial-radius and temporal estimates. Controlling radii first yields quantitative crossing information without assuming nearby directional transience, and the resulting tail estimates make regeneration duration integrable, producing a deterministic positive speed.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
The main theorem treats every iid uniformly elliptic nearest-neighbor environment on Zd in every d>=2 and every deterministic direction with almost-sure directional transience, and gives a deterministic limiting velocity whose projection in that same direction is positive. This is exactly the transience-to-ballisticity implication in the problem statement. Within these assumptions, this is a claimed affirmative answer to the stated question.

\subsection{Formal verification}
