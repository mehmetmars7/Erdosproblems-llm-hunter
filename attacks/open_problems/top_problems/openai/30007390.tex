\section*{Glauber cutoff throughout the high-temperature SK phase}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. Worst-start SK heat-bath dynamics is claimed to cut off at $(2\lambda(\beta))^{-1}\log n$ for each fixed $0\leq\beta<1$ in per-site rate-one time.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} For independent standard Gaussian $J_{ij}$, $i<j$, with $J_{ji}=J_{ij},J_{ii}=0$, use the zero-field SK measure
\[
 \pi_{n,\beta}^J(x)=Z^{-1}\exp\left(\frac\beta{\sqrt n}\sum_{i<j}J_{ij}x_ix_j\right),\qquad x\in\{-1,1\}^n.
\]
Continuous heat-bath dynamics refreshes every site at rate one; a discrete step refreshes one uniformly selected site, including attempts that retain its spin. For every fixed $0\le\beta<1$, there is a deterministic $\lambda(\beta)>0$. Set $t_n=\log n/[2\lambda(\beta)]$, let $d_n(t)$ be the maximum continuous-time distance over initial states, and define $d_n^{\mathrm{disc}}(k)$ analogously. For every $0<\epsilon<1$, in disorder probability,
\[
 d_n((1-\epsilon)t_n)\to1,\quad d_n((1+\epsilon)t_n)\to0,
\]
\[
 d_n^{\mathrm{disc}}(\lfloor(1-\epsilon)nt_n\rfloor)\to1,\quad
 d_n^{\mathrm{disc}}(\lceil(1+\epsilon)nt_n\rceil)\to0.
\]
The rate obeys $\lambda(0)=1$ and $0<\lambda(\beta)\le(1-\beta)^2$ for $0<\beta<1$; its definition through an equilibrium gradient operator is implicit. Temperature is fixed before $n\to\infty$.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Finite-path Gaussian calculus and projected likelihoods identify the equilibrium spin spectral decay. The proof makes one sufficiently long comparison block uniform over initial configurations before iterating it, allowing equilibrium information to control logarithmic-time worst-start total variation.
