\textit{Uniform hypergraph influence}: For fixed uniformity $r\ge3$, the paper claims a variance-to-influence inequality with denominator $(\log n)^{r/(r-1)}$ for all biases and all relabeling-invariant Boolean properties. Increasing properties therefore have the corresponding sharp threshold width.
