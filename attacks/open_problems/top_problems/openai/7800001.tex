\section*{Soft Phases in Two-Dimensional O(N) Models}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a mathematical result relevant to this question in the selected manuscript. Square-lattice classical $O(n)$ spin correlations for $n\geq3$ are claimed to decay exponentially at every finite positive inverse temperature.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} On any finite nearest-neighbor square-lattice subgraph \(G=(V,E)\), put spins \(\sigma_x\in S^{n-1}\) and give their free-boundary law density proportional to
\[
 \exp\!\left(\sum_{\{x,y\}\in E}b_{xy}\sigma_x\cdot\sigma_y\right)
 \prod_{x\in V}d\omega_{n-1}(\sigma_x),\qquad 0\le b_e\le\beta.
\]
For every integer \(n\ge3\) and finite \(\beta>0\), the asserted constants \(A(n,\beta)<\infty\), \(m(n,\beta)>0\) work for every such graph, coupling array, and pair of vertices:
\[
 0\le\langle\sigma_x\cdot\sigma_y\rangle\le A e^{-m\|x-y\|_2}.
\]
There is no fixed boundary spin, applied field, or added interaction.
