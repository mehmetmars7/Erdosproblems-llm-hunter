\section*{Long-Range Order for the Quantum Heisenberg Model}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
The linked manuscripts present the following claims by OpenAI: Finite-range three-dimensional Heisenberg ferromagnets are claimed to obey the exact $T^{3/2}$ spontaneous-magnetization deficit law, with the dispersion determinant fixing the coefficient; For every positive quantum spin in dimensions $d\geq3$, low-temperature nearest-neighbor Heisenberg ferromagnets are claimed to admit zero-field KMS states with magnetization at least $S/4$; For the nearest-neighbor model, magnetization deficit is claimed equal to ideal-magnon density up to $o(\beta^{-5/2})$, including the stated $\zeta(5/2)$ lattice correction; At low fixed temperature, symmetric zero-field magnetization is claimed to approach a uniformly oriented vector of deterministic positive magnitude equal to the right pressure derivative. Together they provide the following support within the scopes described below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} Fix \(S\in\{1/2,1,3/2,\ldots\}\). Let \(J:\mathbb Z^3\to[0,\infty)\) have finite support, \(J(0)=0\), \(J(z)=J(-z)\), and support generating \(\mathbb Z^3\). For the free-boundary Hamiltonian \(H=-\tfrac12\sum_{x,y}J(y-x)\boldsymbol S_x\cdot\boldsymbol S_y\), let \(m_{S,J}(\beta)\) be obtained by differentiating the limiting pressure from positive fields and evaluating at field zero. With
\[
 D_{S,J}=\frac S2\sum_zJ(z)zz^{\mathsf T},
\]
the claimed low-temperature law is
\[
 \lim_{\beta\to\infty}\beta^{3/2}(S-m_{S,J}(\beta))
 =\frac{\zeta(3/2)}{8\pi^{3/2}\sqrt{\det D_{S,J}}}.
\]
The thermodynamic limit precedes removal of the positive field.

\paragraph{Result 2.} For the coupling-one nearest-neighbor spin-\(S\) quantum ferromagnet on \(\mathbb Z^d\), with
\[
 H_\Lambda=-\sum_{\{x,y\}\subset\Lambda,\ |x-y|_1=1}\boldsymbol S_x\cdot\boldsymbol S_y,
\]
the source claims that every integer \(d\ge3\) and \(S\in\{1/2,1,3/2,\ldots\}\) admit a finite \(\beta_0(d,S)>0\). At every finite \(\beta\ge\beta_0\), the induced infinite-volume dynamics has a translation-invariant \(\beta\)-KMS state \(\omega\) satisfying
\[
 \omega(S_0^z)\ge S/4.
\]
This chooses an ordered equilibrium state; it does not assign a nonzero one-site mean to the rotation-invariant state.

\paragraph{Result 3.} For the three-dimensional coupling-one nearest-neighbor spin-\(S\) quantum ferromagnet, define spontaneous magnetization \(m_S(\beta)\) by the thermodynamic pressure's right field derivative at zero. Put
\[
 \varepsilon_S(k)=2S\sum_{j=1}^3(1-\cos k_j),\qquad
 n_S^{\mathrm{sw}}(\beta)=\int_{[-\pi,\pi]^3}
 \frac{d^3k}{(2\pi)^3(e^{\beta\varepsilon_S(k)}-1)}.
\]
For each fixed $S\in\{1/2,1,3/2,\ldots\}$, the assertion is
\[
 \lim_{\beta\to\infty}(\beta S)^{5/2}
 [S-m_S(\beta)-n_S^{\mathrm{sw}}(\beta)]=0.
\]
Thus the full lattice spin-wave integral captures the magnetization deficit through the stated scale.

\paragraph{Result 4.} On the even-side torus \(\Lambda_L=(\mathbb Z/L\mathbb Z)^3\), set \(V=L^3\), \(H_{S,L}=-\sum_{\{x,y\}\text{ nearest neighbors}}\boldsymbol S_x\cdot\boldsymbol S_y\), and \(M_L^a=\sum_xS_x^a\). For each fixed $S\in\{1/2,1,3/2,\ldots\}$, the claim provides finite \(\beta_0(S)\) so that any finite \(\beta\ge\beta_0\) has positive spontaneous magnetization \(m=p'_{S,\beta}(0+)\). With zero-field Gibbs expectation,
\[
 \lim_{L\to\infty,\ L\text{ even}}
 \left\langle\exp\!\left(V^{-1}\sum_{a=1}^3t_aM_L^a\right)\right\rangle_{\beta,L}
 =\frac1{4\pi}\int_{S^2}e^{m t\cdot n}\,d\sigma(n)
 =\frac{\sinh(m|t|)}{m|t|}\qquad(t\in\mathbb R^3).
\]
The value at \(t=0\) is one. The same limit gives \(V^{-2}\sum_{x,y}\langle\boldsymbol S_x\cdot\boldsymbol S_y\rangle\to m^2\).
