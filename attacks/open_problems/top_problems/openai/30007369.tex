\section*{Critical honeycomb polygon diameter and length exponents}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
The linked manuscripts present the following claims by OpenAI: Critical polygons of diameter at most $H$ are claimed to have squared-length mass at most $H^{2/3+o(1)}$, with quantitative two-mark and unequal-arc bounds; Critical unrooted polygon diameter tails are claimed to have exponents $-2$ and $-2/3$ without and with length weighting, leading to diameter exponent $3/4$ under large-length conditioning. Together they give a claimed affirmative answer in the matching scope.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} For simple unoriented honeycomb polygons, modulo primitive triangular-lattice translations and with no distinguished vertex, let \(|P|\) count vertices and diameter be Euclidean. The source claims
\[
 \forall\eta>0\ \exists C_\eta<\infty:\quad
 \sum_{[P]:\operatorname{diam}P\le H}(2+\sqrt2)^{-|P|/2}|P|^2
 \le C_\eta H^{2/3+\eta}\qquad(H\ge1).
\]
The translation convention retains the two honeycomb vertex types.

\paragraph{Result 2.} On the honeycomb lattice with edge length one, count finite simple unoriented polygons once modulo lattice translations. For a polygon $\gamma$, let $L(\gamma)$ count edges, let $D(\gamma)$ be its Euclidean diameter, and put $w(\gamma)=(2+\sqrt2)^{-L(\gamma)/2}$. The three sums
\[
 A(r)=\sum_{D(\gamma)\ge r}w(\gamma),\quad
 P(r)=\sum_{D(\gamma)\ge r}L(\gamma)w(\gamma),\quad
 M_2(r)=\sum_{D(\gamma)\le r}L(\gamma)^2w(\gamma)
\]
are finite and satisfy
\[
 A(r)=r^{-2+o(1)},\qquad P(r)=r^{-2/3+o(1)},\qquad M_2(r)\le r^{2/3+o(1)}\quad(r\to\infty).
\]
The exponent notation means bounds with every fixed positive error in the exponent, for sufficiently large $r$.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Finite cylinder vacua yield an exact marked-pair identity. A denominator estimate tolerates unbalanced multiplicities, and coercive magnetic trial actions handle unequal walls; the rare central scale then gives the uniform polynomial one-arc bound.

Two cylinder pressures and angular color-sum asymptotics are passed to a radial series. Determinant normalization and a one-mark Hilbert--Schmidt plateau bound control tails and moments; unequal-scale two-mark estimates complete the length-conditioned consequences.
