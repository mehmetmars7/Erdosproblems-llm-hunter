\section*{Hahn–Wilson thick generation at fp-type two}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
For sufficiently large primes, a connective p-complete spectrum of exact fp-type two is claimed not finitely constructible from standard BP<2>, despite satisfying both finite-localization and telescope-to-Morava comparison equivalences. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} For every sufficiently large prime $p$, OpenAI claims a connective completed spectrum $X$ with finitely presented mod-$p$ cohomology over the Steenrod algebra, of exact fp-type two, satisfying
\[
X\notin\operatorname{Thick}(BP\langle2\rangle_p^\wedge),\qquad
L_2^fX\simeq L_2X,\qquad L_{T(2)}X\simeq L_{K(2)}X.
\]
Fp-type at most $n$ means $\pi_*(V\wedge X)$ has finitely many nonzero finite groups for some, equivalently every, finite type-$(n+1)$ spectrum $V$; exact type two excludes type at most one. Thick closure permits finite sums, shifts, cofibers and retracts, not arbitrary colimits. Here $L_2=L_{E(2)}$, $L_2^f$ is finite localization away from type greater than two, and $T(2)$ a height-two periodic telescope. The Brown--Peterson generator uses the specified standard form with coefficients $\mathbb Z_p[v_1,v_2]$.
