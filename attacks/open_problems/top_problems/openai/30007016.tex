\section*{Bounded denominators in low-dimensional log Calabi–Yau fibrations}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
Uniform moduli denominators, trivializing degrees, and effective rounded systems are claimed for the stated log Calabi--Yau fibrations of total dimension at most four. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} Fix a finite $\Phi\subset[0,1]\cap\mathbb Q$ and $1\le d\le4$. For every projective complex log canonical $d$-fold pair $(X,B)$ with coefficients in $\Phi$ and a contraction $f:X\to Z$, $\dim Z>0$, with $K_X+B\sim_{\mathbb Q}f^*D$ for a rational Cartier divisor $D$ on $Z$, OpenAI claims uniform positive integers $p_0\mid p$ and a rational descending-chain-condition set $\mathcal B\subset[0,1]$, depending only on $(d,\Phi)$, such that
\[
K_X+B+p_0^{-1}\operatorname{Div}(\psi)=f^*D_Z,\qquad
D_Z\sim_{\mathbb Q}D,\quad D_Z=K_Z+B_Z+M_Z
\]
for some rational function $\psi$. The integer $p_0$ clears $\Phi$, $B_Z\ge0$ has coefficients in $\mathcal B$, and the moduli b-divisor $\mathbf M$ is b-nef with $p\mathbf M$ b-Cartier. The resulting generalized pair is log canonical, and klt when the original pair is klt. This is the denominator result in dimensions at most four, not a bound in unrestricted dimensions.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Manuscript 1 (family 034, 2026-09-27): Reduction to curves measures monodromy characters using the index of the entire reduced special fiber. Bounded generalized pairs, smooth-locus topology, Kummer theory, and finitely generated divisor classes provide a common torsion exponent, after which positive base divisors yield effective field-generating systems.

The source treats projective complex log canonical pairs, with a rational Cartier divisor in the pullback hypothesis. With those conventions, its finite coefficient set, dimensions $1\le d\le4$ and positive-dimensional base agree with this question. It asserts the exact rational-function presentation with uniform $p_0\mid p$, effective discriminant in a rational DCC set, and b-nef moduli with $p$ times it b-Cartier.
