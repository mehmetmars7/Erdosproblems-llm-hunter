\section*{Rigidity of Completely Close \$C\textasciicircum{}*\$-Algebras}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
For any prescribed small unitary displacement, the paper claims one uniform Kadison--Kastler tolerance guaranteeing conjugacy of close unital von Neumann algebras on any complex Hilbert space. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} For every $\varepsilon>0$, OpenAI claims a single $\delta(\varepsilon)>0$ valid for every complex Hilbert space $H$ and all unital von Neumann algebras $M,N\subset B(H)$ with common identity: if their norm-unit-ball Hausdorff distance $d(M,N)<\delta$, then
\[
\exists u\in B(H):\quad u^*u=uu^*=I_H,\quad uMu^*=N,\quad\|u-I_H\|<\varepsilon.
\]
No separability, type or injectivity hypothesis is imposed on these von Neumann algebras.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Manuscript 1 (family 289, 2026-09-23): For finite factors, free-word estimates control amplification while boundary averaging corrects nearby unitaries into a measurable cocycle. Double ergodicity makes the resulting correspondence multiplicative and yields a spatial implementation. Properly infinite algebras are handled through matrix amplification and modular preparation; alignment of centers and limits over separable restrictions assemble the universal conclusion.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
Manuscript 1 (family 289, 2026-09-23): The primary manuscript establishes uniform near-identity unitary conjugacy of sufficiently two-sided close unital von Neumann algebras. The theorem gives spatial isomorphism and near-identity conjugacy for the von Neumann, common-identity subcase. This problem also quantifies over general C*-algebras and asks about their structural invariants at completely bounded distance; that full class is not covered.
