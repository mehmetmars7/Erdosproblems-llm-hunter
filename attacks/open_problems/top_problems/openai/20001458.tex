\section*{An ultrafilter-free local criterion for O2-ultrapower embeddability}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
One explicit separable unital full group C*-algebra is claimed to embed into no norm ultrapower of a nonzero unital nuclear algebra, giving a negative instance for O\_2 as well. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Manuscript 1 (family 292, 2026-09-23): An ascending HNN construction supplies a property-T base with finite-index self-embeddings. A nuclear tensor-product estimate restricts the finite-dimensional representation types approximated by arbitrary unitary tuples, uniformly in dimension. Applying this to coordinate lifts of a proposed ultrapower embedding and then inducing through the self-embedding produces incompatible finite sets of representation types.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
Manuscript 1 (family 292, 2026-09-23): The separable unital counterexample cannot embed unitally into \(\mathcal O_2^\omega\); any nonunital embedding sends its unit to a nonzero projection, which is equivalent to 1 coordinatewise in O2, so passing to that corner would give a forbidden unital embedding.

\subsection{Formal verification}
For family 292, the scope page documents the following selections. The selected full-group algebra has no unital embedding in a norm ultrapower of any nonzero unital nuclear target. In particular, the Cuntz-algebra ultrapower target is excluded. The documented statement covers the main claim of manuscript 1. Its selected declaration also covers the clause used for this problem.
