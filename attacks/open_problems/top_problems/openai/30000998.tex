\section*{Convexity of Injectivity Loci Under MTW}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
\textit{Weak MTW support and injectivity-domain convexity}: On a compact smooth manifold, weak MTW curvature is claimed to imply convexity of every tangent injectivity domain; the paper also gives global supporting mountains for ordinary subgradients of squared-distance potentials, without a density assumption.

\textit{Uniform bi-Hölder transport under weak MTW}: For a fixed compact weak-MTW manifold, the manuscript claims one Hölder modulus for optimal maps and their inverses over all density pairs with common positive lower and finite upper bounds; no density smoothness or absence of conjugate cut points is assumed.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
\textit{Weak MTW support and injectivity-domain convexity}: Proper radial shortening gives smooth distance supports and strict time monotonicity of the branch Hessian. The full ordinary-subgradient graph is represented locally by convex combinations of all active minimizing logarithms. At the first scale where support could fail, limiting convex hulls remain minimizing; a conjugacy-kernel analysis and the radial Hessian identity rule out the alleged failure.

\textit{Uniform bi-Hölder transport under weak MTW}: Lifted gap sections of transport potentials are studied in the tangent injectivity domain. Convexity turns a uniform power estimate on dual-potential oscillation into a diameter estimate on their endpoint images. Taking the supremum over all admissible densities gives one class-wide bound, and comparison at nearby poles supplies Hölder continuity in both transport directions.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
\textit{Weak MTW support and injectivity-domain convexity}: Every compact connected boundaryless manifold in dimension at least two satisfying weak MTW has convex tangent injectivity domains.

\textit{Uniform bi-Hölder transport under weak MTW}: The original source resolves the catalogue statement shorthand: OWR31/2008 p.1762 uses the weak cost-orthogonal MTW condition, and Figalli–Gallouët–Rifford arXiv:1404.0141v1 pp.1–3 states Villani’s conjecture for smooth compact manifolds of dimension n>=2, with exactly the open pre-cut I(x), no nonfocality, and weak MTW as Definition1.1. The manuscript geo:convexity supplies exactly this source-level convexity conclusion. Its compactness is the original conjecture scope rather than an unsupported restriction of the intended record.
