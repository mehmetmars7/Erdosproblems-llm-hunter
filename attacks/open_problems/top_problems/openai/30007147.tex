\section*{Uniformization with positive bisectional curvature}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
\textit{Kähler uniformization}: For a complete noncompact Kähler metric whose bisectional curvature is strictly positive, the claimed conclusion identifies the complex manifold with Euclidean complex space by a biholomorphism. No complex-dimension bound is imposed.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} Fix a complex dimension $n\ge1$ and a complex manifold $M$ which is connected but not compact. If it has a smooth complete K\"ahler metric whose holomorphic bisectional curvature is strictly positive, then the claimed uniformization is \[M\simeq_{\mathrm{bihol}}\mathbb C^n.\] Positivity is pointwise; no bounded-curvature or maximal-volume-growth assumption appears in the theorem.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
\textit{Kähler uniformization}: Complete Hermitian approximations on an exhaustion produce a global Kähler--Ricci flow without an initial global curvature bound. A weighted scalar comparison is proved before the completeness needed later is available. Holomorphic-disc minimization on the cotangent bundle then gives a viscosity inequality and a Harnack mechanism, supplying the flow geometry used to construct the global holomorphic uniformization.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
The uniformization theorem applies to every connected noncompact complex $n$-manifold, $n\ge1$, with a smooth complete K\"ahler metric of strictly positive holomorphic bisectional curvature. It claims biholomorphism with $\mathbb C^n$ without extra curvature bounds or volume-growth conditions.
