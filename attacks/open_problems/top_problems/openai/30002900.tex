\section*{Injectivity of the Baum–Connes Assembly Map}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
For one finitely generated group without torsion, the coefficient-free reduced assembly map annihilates an infinite-order degree-zero K-homology class; this is claimed to refute rational injectivity. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} OpenAI claims a discrete group, torsion-free and finitely generated, $G$, an injection $i:\mathbb Z^2\hookrightarrow G$, and $c\in H^2(BG;\mathbb Z)$ such that
\[
\langle(Bi)^*c,[T^2]\rangle=1,\qquad
 i_*\beta=0\text{ in }K_0(C_r^*(G)),
\]
here $\beta\in K_0(C_r^*(\mathbb Z^2))$ is the Bott generator chosen to have rank zero. The class $h=(Bi)_*[D_{T^2}]\in K_0(BG)$ has infinite order but satisfies $\mu_G^r(h)=0$. Here $\mu_G^r:K_*(BG)\to K_*(C_r^*(G))$ is reduced coefficient-free analytic assembly and $[D_{T^2}]$ is the oriented torus spin-Dirac class. This asserts failure of reduced assembly injectivity without coefficients, even rationally.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Manuscript 1 (family 285, 2026-09-23): The construction embeds a torus subgroup with an integral cohomology detector while arranging disappearance of its Bott class analytically. Operators assembled on almost disjoint Cayley-graph sheets retain controlled spectral supports; estimates for the leftover forest permit the required homotopies in the reduced completion. A finite witness then restricts the construction to a finitely generated subgroup.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
Manuscript 1 (family 285, 2026-09-23): The infinite-order degree-zero assembly-kernel class for a finitely generated torsion-free discrete group directly refutes the universal coefficient-free reduced injectivity and isomorphism assertions, with no coefficient or maximal-completion substitution.
