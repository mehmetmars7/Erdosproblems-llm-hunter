\section*{Least volume of nongeodesic minimal hypersurfaces in spheres}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
\textit{Least volume of nongeodesic minimal hypersurfaces}: The announced lower bound for a non-totally-geodesic closed minimal immersion into a round sphere is the volume of the smallest Clifford product in the same dimension. Domain volume counts any covering multiplicity.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} For an integer $m\ge2$, let $a_m=\min_{1\le k<m}\operatorname{Vol}_m\bigl(S^k(\sqrt{k/m})\times S^{m-k}(\sqrt{(m-k)/m})\bigr)$. For every closed connected smooth $m$-manifold and immersion $F:M\to S^{m+1}$ which is smooth and minimal, with unit curvature on the target sphere, if its image is not totally geodesic, the claimed sharp volume bound is \[\operatorname{Vol}(M,F^*g_{\mathrm{round}})\ge a_m.\] Volume is measured on the domain and includes immersion multiplicities. A minimizing Clifford product attains the threshold.
