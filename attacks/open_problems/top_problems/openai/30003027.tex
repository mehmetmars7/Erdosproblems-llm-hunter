\subsection{OpenAI's claim}

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
\textit{Unrestricted infinite matroid obstruction}: An ultrafilter carries an ordinal-valued rank on large subsets. Alternating copies along an infinite bundle path would turn a supposed covering into an infinite descending ordinal chain.
