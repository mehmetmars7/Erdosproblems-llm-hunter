\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Manuscript 1.}
For $d\ge2$, let $V\in C^2(\mathbb R^d)$ satisfy $V(0)=0$, $\nabla V(0)=0$ and $I_d\preceq\nabla^2V(x)\preceq2I_d$ everywhere. The target is $\pi_V(dx)\propto e^{-V(x)}dx$. Exact adaptive first-order queries return $(V(x),\nabla V(x))$; algorithms are measurable and have unrestricted real computation and randomness between queries. Let $Q(d)$ be the minimum worst-execution query budget for total-variation error at most $1/10$ uniformly over this class. The claim gives
\[
 \forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists C_{\varepsilon}:\quad Q(d)\le C_{\varepsilon} d^{\varepsilon},
 \qquad Q(d)\ge c\log d\quad(d\ge d_0)
\]
for absolute $c,d_0$. Thus the optimal dimension exponent is zero. These are oracle-query bounds, without a polynomial bit-time conclusion.
