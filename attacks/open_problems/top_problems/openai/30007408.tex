\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} On square-lattice bonds let $J_e=1+\varepsilon\xi_e$, with independent fair signs $\xi_e$. Define the physical critical inverse temperature by
\[
 \beta_c(\varepsilon)=\inf\left\{\beta\ge0:
 \mathbb E_{\mathrm{env}}\lim_{m\to\infty}\langle\sigma_0\rangle^+_{[-m,m]^2,\beta,J}>0\right\},
\]
normalizing each Gibbs law before environmental averaging. In any bounded Jordan domain $(D;a,b)$, take deterministic filled square-lattice approximations with mesh tending to zero, boundary homeomorphisms converging uniformly to the identity, and corresponding endpoints tending to $a,b$. Conditional on the bonds, sample FK configurations with cluster weight two, one boundary arc wired and the other free, and edge probabilities $p_e=1-e^{-2\beta_c(\varepsilon)J_e}$. If $\mu_{n,\xi}$ is the physical medial exploration law, there is an absolute $\varepsilon_0>0$ such that, for each fixed $0<\varepsilon<\varepsilon_0$,
\[
 d_{\mathrm{BL}}\bigl(\mu_{n,\xi},\operatorname{Law}(\mathrm{SLE}_{16/3}(D;a,b))\bigr)
 \xrightarrow{\mathbb P_{\mathrm{env}}}0.
\]
The curve topology is uniform distance modulo increasing reparametrization; the disorder bound is independent of the chosen domain.
