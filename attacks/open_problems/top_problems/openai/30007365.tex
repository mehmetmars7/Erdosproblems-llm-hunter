\section*{BKT scaling and the endpoint stiffness for finite-range integer heights}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI claims that finite-range integer heights have a Gaussian-free-field scaling limit at all rough-phase parameters and also at the phase boundary. In the parameter convention used here, the endpoint effective temperature is $8\pi v_J^2$, so its normalized ratio to $v_J^2$ is $8\pi$. The paper also gives spin-field limits for the Villain and XY models in its specified low-temperature regime.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} Let \(J\subset\mathbb Z^2\setminus\{0\}\) be finite, square-symmetric, and contain nearest neighbors. Define \(\Delta_Ju(x)=|J|^{-1}\sum_{y\in J}(u(x+y)-u(x))\) and \(v_J^2=(2|J|)^{-1}\sum_{y\in J}y_1^2\). For heights in \(2\pi\mathbb Z\) with Gaussian weight \(\exp[-(\sigma,-\Delta_J\sigma)/(2\beta)]\), the zero-boundary second-moment threshold \(\beta_c\) is claimed finite and positive, with bounded variance below it and unbounded variance at/above it. On tori of side \(L^N\), the centered distribution \(H_N=(L^N)^{-2}\sum_x(\sigma_x-\bar\sigma)\delta_{x/L^N}\) satisfies
\[
 H_N\Longrightarrow\sqrt{\beta_{\mathrm{eff}}(J,\beta)/v_J^2}\,\Phi_{\mathbb T^2}
 \quad\text{in }H^{-3}\qquad(\beta\ge\beta_c).
\]
For mean-zero smooth \(f\), log Laplace transforms tend to \(\beta_{\mathrm{eff}}(f,(-\Delta)^{-1}f)/(2v_J^2)\). The asserted endpoint and asymptotics are
\[
 \beta_{\mathrm{eff}}(J,\beta_c)=8\pi v_J^2,\quad
 \lim_{\beta\downarrow\beta_c}\frac{\beta_{\mathrm{eff}}(J,\beta)-\beta_{\mathrm{eff}}(J,\beta_c)}{\beta-\beta_c}=+\infty,
 \quad\beta_{\mathrm{eff}}=\beta+O_J(e^{-c_J\beta}).
\]
The effective parameter is differentiable above threshold; for square interaction ranges \(J_\rho\), \(\beta_c(J_\rho)/(8\pi v_{J_\rho}^2)\to1\). Nearest-neighbor \(v_J^2=1/4\) gives critical effective temperature \(2\pi\). For each interaction range $J$, the torus side base is selected as an integer $L=L(J)\ge2$. With zero-boundary expectation in growing boxes of radius $R$, the threshold convention is
\[
 \beta_c=\inf\{\beta>0:\sup_R\mathbb E_{R,\beta}^{0}\sigma_0^2=\infty\}.
\]

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
The height analysis controls renormalized covariance and the universal endpoint stiffness across finite square-symmetric interaction sets. Spin--height duality and Green-function normalization then identify the corresponding spin observables as imaginary exponentials of the Dirichlet Gaussian field.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
The height theorem supplies each requested assertion for the admissible interaction $J$: the positive finite threshold and roughness at equality, the field and Laplace-functional limits, the endpoint value $\beta_{\rm eff}(b_c)=8\pi v_J^2$, the infinite right slope, the high-temperature estimate and the spread-out asymptotic. The normalized endpoint is $8\pi$, while the unnormalized nearest-neighbor value is $2\pi$ because $v_J^2=1/4$.
