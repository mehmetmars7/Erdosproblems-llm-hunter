\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Manuscript 1.}
For all integers $d,N\ge1$ and vectors $v_1,\ldots,v_N\in\mathbb R^d$ of Euclidean norm at most one, one absolute $C$ is claimed to permit signs with
\[
 \max_{0\le k\le N}\|\sum_{i=1}^k\varepsilon_iv_i\|_2\le C\sqrt d.
\]
The prescribed vector order is retained and the same signs control every prefix. If additionally $\sum_iv_i=0$, the asserted Steinitz--Bergstr\"om consequence instead gives a permutation $\pi$ with
\[
 \max_{0\le k\le N}\|\sum_{i=1}^kv_{\pi(i)}\|_2\le C\sqrt d.
\]
The constant is independent of dimension, vector count and the particular family.
