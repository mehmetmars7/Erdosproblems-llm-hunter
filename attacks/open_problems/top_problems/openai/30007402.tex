\section*{Oracle-free computation of the limiting random 3-SAT threshold}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. The limiting random 3-SAT threshold is claimed computable to any requested accuracy by one finite deterministic procedure.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} For uniform proper signed random 3-SAT clauses sampled independently with replacement, write \(p(n,m)\) for satisfiability probability. The source asserts \(\alpha_3\in(0,\infty)\) with
\[
 p(n,\lfloor an\rfloor)\to1\ (a<\alpha_3),\qquad
 p(n,\lfloor an\rfloor)\to0\ (a>\alpha_3).
\]
It also claims a single deterministic Turing machine that, on unary input \(1^r\), halts with rational \(q_r\) satisfying
\[
 |q_r-\alpha_3|\le2^{-r}\qquad(r\ge1).
\]
This machine has no oracle, advice, or noncomputable constant as input; no polynomial running-time bound is asserted. The clause-density parameter is restricted to $a\ge0$.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Deletion bounds certify rational lower brackets, and finite hierarchical soft-pressure trials certify upper brackets. A fair enumeration of both certificate families halts at every accuracy, using existence of the limiting threshold without needing an effective finite-size convergence rate.
