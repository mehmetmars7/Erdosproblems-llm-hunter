\section*{Small spatial embeddings from one-way near-inclusions}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
On separable Hilbert spaces, one-sided near inclusions can have arbitrarily small gaps while every spatial embedding requires a unitary bounded away from the identity, despite embeddings existing. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
