\section*{The Generalized Star Height Problem}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 134; 2026-09-25), the asserted result is that for an arbitrary finite alphabet and a regular language on it, a generalized expression needs at most thirteen nested star levels; In manuscript 2 (family 134; 2026-09-25), the asserted result is that every regular language has generalized star height at most four without changing its alphabet; In manuscript 3 (family 134; 2026-09-25), the asserted result is that three nested star levels suffice to express any regular language using Boolean operations and complementation in its original free monoid.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Manuscript 1.}
A generalized regular expression may use union, concatenation, complementation and Kleene star. Its star height is the greatest nesting depth of star, and $h_{\Sigma}(L)$ is the least such height among expressions for $L$. The claim is the uniform bound
\[
 \forall\text{ finite }\Sigma\ \forall\text{ regular }L\subseteq\Sigma^*:\quad h_{\Sigma}(L)\le13.
\]
The alphabet and language may vary; the numerical bound stays fixed.

\paragraph{Manuscript 2.}
If $h_{\Sigma}(L)$ is the minimum Kleene-star nesting depth for expressions formed using union, concatenation, complement and star, the claimed universal result is
\[
 \forall\text{ finite }\Sigma\ \forall\text{ regular }L\subseteq\Sigma^*:\quad h_{\Sigma}(L)\le4.
\]
The bound concerns generalized expressions, for which complement is permitted.

\paragraph{Manuscript 3.}
For a finite alphabet $\Sigma$ and regular language $L$, minimize star nesting depth over expressions using union, concatenation, complementation and Kleene star. The manuscript's claimed bound is
\[
 h_{\Sigma}(L)\le3\qquad\text{for every such }\Sigma,L.
\]
Thus a single integer bounds the generalized star height across all regular languages and finite alphabets.
