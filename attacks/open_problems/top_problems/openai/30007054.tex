\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} Put $g=10^{60}$, $D=\binom g3$ and $n=3D$. In the formal decorated stable-graph strata algebra $\mathcal S_{g,n}$, OpenAI constructs a degree-$2D$ class $Y$ as follows. Concatenate all increasing triples of colors $1,\ldots,g$ into a color word $\mathbf i$ of length $n$, with three-slot blocks $J=(a,b,c)$. Let $\overline D_{ab}$ sum all rational-tail divisors carrying $a,b$. Let $\beta_J$ sum one-edge unmarked genus-$g$ graphs with the edge subdivided by a genus-zero vertex carrying $J$, weighted by inverse automorphism orders and pulled back by forgetting other labels. Then
\[
F_J=\overline D_{ab}\overline D_{ac}-\tfrac12\beta_J,\qquad
Y=\sum_{\tau\mathbf i=\mathbf i}\operatorname{sgn}(\tau)\,\tau\left(\prod_JF_J\right).
\]
OpenAI claims $q_{g,n}(Y)=0$ but $Y\notin\mathcal P_{g,n}$, where $q$ realizes graph classes in rational Chow of $\overline{\mathcal M}_{g,n}$ and $\mathcal P_{g,n}$ is Pixton's original relation span. Thus that span is strictly smaller than the Chow kernel; the same class also lies in the rational-cohomological kernel outside the span.
