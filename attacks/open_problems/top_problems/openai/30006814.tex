\section*{Kadison Similarity Problem for C*-Algebras}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
Every bounded, unital, complex-linear algebra map on a complex C*-algebra is claimed to become a *-representation after similarity, with a universal hyperreflexivity bound for arbitrary von Neumann algebras. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} For any unital complex $C^*$-algebra $A$, any complex Hilbert space $H$ and bounded complex-linear unital algebra homomorphism $\pi:A\to B(H)$, OpenAI claims a bounded invertible $S$ with bounded inverse such that
\[
\rho(a)=S\pi(a)S^{-1},\qquad\rho(a^*)=\rho(a)^*\quad(a\in A).
\]
Thus $\rho$ is a $*$-homomorphism. No initial preservation of involution, separability or nuclearity is assumed; the theorem concerns bounded unital representations rather than arbitrary unbounded algebra maps.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Manuscript 1 (family 288, 2026-09-23): A free-product corner test combines invariant matrix tensors with a Hankel lower bound to control derivations uniformly. Independence in tracial ultrapowers transfers this estimate to finite factors, and representation and central-decomposition arguments remove separability restrictions. Uniform local implementations yield complete boundedness of derivations; the similarity and derivation equivalences then give the stated homomorphism conclusion.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
The main theorem accepts a unital complex $C^*$-algebra and a bounded unital complex-linear algebra map into operators on an arbitrary Hilbert space. It produces a bounded invertible similarity to a $*$-homomorphism. This is exactly the recorded unital/nondegenerate convention; the standard unitized formulation extends it to the equivalent nonunital convention. No separability or nuclearity restriction remains.
