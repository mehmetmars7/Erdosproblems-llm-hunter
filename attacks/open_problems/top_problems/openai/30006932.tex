\section*{Stein's Conjecture on the Hilbert Transform along Lipschitz Vector Fields}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
\textit{Hilbert transforms in Lipschitz directions}: The claimed $L^2$ bound is uniform for inner truncations of a Hilbert transform following a planar Lipschitz unit field over a short outer interval. Its length is an absolute multiple of the reciprocal Lipschitz constant, and the field may vary in both coordinates.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} For a Lipschitz $v:\mathbb R^2\to S^1$ with $\operatorname{Lip}(v)\le1$, put $H_{v,a}^{\epsilon}f(x)=\int_{\epsilon<|t|<a}f(x-tv(x))\,dt/t$. The claim gives absolute $0<a_*<1/2$ and $C_*<\infty$ with \[\sup_{0<\epsilon<a_*}\|H_{v,a_*}^{\epsilon}f\|_2\le C_*\|f\|_2\quad(f\in\mathcal S(\mathbb R^2)).\] Thus the symmetric principal value has the same $L^2$ bound and $|\{|H_{v,a_*}f|>\lambda\}|\le C_*^2\lambda^{-2}\|f\|_2^2$. The supremum above is outside the norm; it is not a maximal-truncation norm assertion.
