\section*{Linear hitting-time variance for random clause size at least four}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. The proposed first-unsatisfiable-prefix variance is $\Theta(n)$ for $k\geq4$, and between order $n$ and order $n\log n$ for $k=3$.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} Sample independent clauses, with replacement, uniformly among the $2^k\binom nk$ signed clauses on $k$ distinct variables. Write $F_m=\bigwedge_{j=1}^mC_j$ and $H_n=\min\{m\ge1:F_m\text{ is unsatisfiable}\}$. For each fixed $k\ge3$, put $\ell_3(n)=\log(en)$, $\ell_k(n)=1$ for $k\ge4$, and $U_k=\log2/[-\log(1-2^{-k})]$. Constants depending only on $k$ satisfy
\[
 \operatorname{Var}(H_n)\le C_kn\ell_k(n)\quad(n\ge k),\qquad
 \operatorname{Var}(H_n)\ge c_kn\quad(n\text{ sufficiently large}).
\]
For fixed $B>0$, $T_{n,B}=\min(H_n,\lfloor Bn\rfloor)$ obeys $\operatorname{Var}(T_{n,B})\le C_{k,B}n\ell_k(n)$. Its matching lower bound $c_kn$ applies when $B>U_k$.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Assignment-set survival estimates and clause omission express deletion delays through independent root tests. Occupation and collision moments bound squared delays and hence variance, with matching linear lower bounds from transition-width estimates; clipping preserves the upper bounds.
