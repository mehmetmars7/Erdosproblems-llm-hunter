\section*{Ellipse rigidity from a continuous physical collar of smooth caustics}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
A smooth positively curved convex billiard table is claimed to be an ellipse whenever its near-boundary region has a continuous full foliation by smooth convex caustics, with no transverse differentiability required. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} Let $\Omega$ be a bounded strictly convex planar domain with a smooth regular simple closed boundary of positive curvature. Assume a relatively open collar $A\subset\overline\Omega$ of the boundary and a homeomorphism $H_0:S^1\times[0,1)\to A$ whose level zero is $\partial\Omega$ and whose positive levels are smooth closed convex caustics. A caustic means a curve to which tangency of a billiard segment is preserved by reflection $v\mapsto v-2(v\cdot n)n$, with outward unit normal $n$. OpenAI claims
\[
\Omega=\{x:(x-c)^TQ(x-c)<1\},\qquad Q=Q^T>0.
\]
The statement assumes a continuous collar of caustics and includes circles.
