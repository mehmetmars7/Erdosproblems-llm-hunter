\section*{Amenability of Thompson Group and Interval Homeomorphism Groups}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a counterexample in the selected manuscript. Thompson group $F$ is claimed to be nonamenable.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Recursive colors on dyadic partitions are designed to behave under affine transport. Finite averages of these colors give a uniform boundary discrepancy under a fixed set of group moves, contradicting the existence of Følner sets.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
