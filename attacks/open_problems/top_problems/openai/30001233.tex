\section*{SHGH Conjecture for Linear Systems on Blown-Up Planes}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
At $r\ge10$ very general complex plane points, every effective curve is claimed to have total assigned multiplicity below its degree times $\sqrt r$, including reducible curves and unequal assignments. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} For each $r\ge10$, OpenAI claims a nonempty very-general locus of ordered distinct points $(p_1,\ldots,p_r)$ in $\mathbb P^2_{\mathbb C}$ such that every nonzero effective plane curve $C$ of degree $d\ge1$ and integers $m_i\ge0$ with $\operatorname{mult}_{p_i}C\ge m_i$ satisfy
\[
d\sqrt r>\sum_{i=1}^r m_i.
\]
Very general means excluding a union, indexed by a countable set, of proper algebraic closed subsets of the distinct-point configuration space. Multiplicities may be unequal, curves may be reducible, and the strict inequality starts at ten points.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Manuscript 1 (family 039, 2026-09-23): Incidence loci and cyclic products reduce a hypothetical failure to equal multiplicities at arbitrary configurations. Degeneration toward a cubic produces finite jet constraints; colliding the remaining points and taking limits of elliptic theta bases turns the contradiction into a full-rank polynomial evaluation problem.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
Manuscript 1 (family 039, 2026-09-23): Nagata’s inequality forces emptiness for the restricted curve systems violating its strict slope bound; it leaves the SHGH exact dimension formula for arbitrary Cremona-reduced systems undecided.
