\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} For every proper CAT$(0)$ metric space $X$ and integer $n\ge2$, each compactly supported integral $n$-current $T$ with $\partial T=0$ is claimed to bound a compactly supported integral $(n+1)$-current $S$ with \[\partial S=T,\qquad\mathbf M(S)\le\frac{\mathbf M(T)^{(n+1)/n}}{(n+1)^{(n+1)/n}\omega_{n+1}^{1/n}}.\] No ambient-dimension or multiplicity restriction is imposed, and the constant is claimed optimal. Its manifold corollary gives $\operatorname{area}(\partial\Omega)\ge d\omega_d^{1/d}\operatorname{vol}(\Omega)^{(d-1)/d}$ for smooth bounded domains in a smooth Cartan--Hadamard $d$-manifold, $d\ge3$. For relatively compact measurable $E$ of finite perimeter the assertion instead uses the ambient variation $P_g(E)=|D\mathbf1_E|_g(M)$: \[P_g(E)\ge d\omega_d^{1/d}\operatorname{vol}_g(E)^{(d-1)/d}.\] For smooth $\Omega$, $P_g(\Omega)=\operatorname{area}_g(\partial\Omega)$.
