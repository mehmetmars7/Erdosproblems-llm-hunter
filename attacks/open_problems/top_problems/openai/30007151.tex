\section*{Nonnegative Einstein curvature classification in dimension four}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
For closed Einstein four-manifolds with $\operatorname{Ric}=3g$ and nonnegative sectional curvature, OpenAI claims that one zero-curvature plane forces the universal cover to be the product of two round two-spheres of radius $1/\sqrt3$. Its classification corollary claims that every such universal cover is this product, the round four-sphere, or normalized Fubini--Study projective two-space.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} For a connected smooth closed four-manifold with $\operatorname{Ric}_g=3g$ and sectional curvature $\ge0$, the existence of one zero-curvature tangent two-plane is claimed to force its universal Riemannian cover to be \[S^2(1/\sqrt3)\times S^2(1/\sqrt3).\] Its corollary classifies every such universal cover as this product, round $S^4$ of curvature $1$, or Fubini--Study $\mathbb{CP}^2$ with $\operatorname{Ric}=3g$. This is an Einstein four-dimensional assertion, not a classification of all nonnegatively curved manifolds.
