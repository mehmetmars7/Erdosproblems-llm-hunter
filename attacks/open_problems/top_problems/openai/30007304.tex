\section*{Kaplansky quasitrace additivity conjecture}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
A separable unital algebra is claimed to have normalized 2-quasitraces but no traces; consequently, minimal tensoring can turn two simple unital stably finite C*-algebras into a properly infinite algebra. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Manuscript 1 (family 294, 2026-09-23): Twenty-four bundle-based generators have unit column sum and a strictly smaller row sum, excluding tracial states. Continuous operator scaling preserves this imbalance while nonzero Chern classes obstruct every finite matrix test for proper infiniteness. A bounded-product construction retains all tests simultaneously; established quasitrace and tensor-product implications give the claimed consequences, including a reduced-free-group tensor factor.
