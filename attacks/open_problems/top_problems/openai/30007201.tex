\section*{Coefficient injectivity for unimodular one-variable equations}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. For every nontrivial group $G$, the free product $G*\mathbb Z$ is claimed not normally generated by a single element.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} In the free product \(H=A*\langle t\rangle\), a word \(w\) is unimodular when its total exponent of \(t\) equals \(1\) or \(-1\). For any coefficient group \(A\) and such \(w\), the source claims
\[
 A\longrightarrow H/\langle\!\langle w\rangle\!\rangle_H\text{ injective}.
\]
The Kervaire corollary drops the exponent assumption for a different conclusion:
\[
 A\ne1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 H/\langle\!\langle w\rangle\!\rangle_H\ne1\qquad(w\in H).
\]
