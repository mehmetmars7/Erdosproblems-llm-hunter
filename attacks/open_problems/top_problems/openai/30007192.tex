\section*{Simple overgroups of type F infinity for decidable word problems}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. Such a group is also claimed to embed in a simple group of type $F_\infty$.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Encoded finite diagrams carry faithful actions with controlled stabilizers. A homological device for ascending tori provides fillings in every dimension, and stabilizers of finite type in a highly transitive action assemble the simple overgroup while retaining higher finiteness.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
Decidability of the word problem for a finite generating set is the input hypothesis. The claimed simple overgroup has a classifying complex with only finitely many cells in each dimension; this is the requested $F_\infty$ strengthening. This is presented as a claimed affirmative answer.

\subsection{Formal verification}
For family 250, the documented entries cover Boone--Higman equivalence, simple $F_\infty$ overgroups for decidable word problems, and one universal $F_\infty$ group. The converse characterization of all its recursively presented subgroups is extra manuscript scope.
