\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} Start with a normal irreducible globally Weil rational-factorial compact Kähler fourfold $X_0$, effective rational boundary $B_0$ of coefficients below one, and a fixed rational nef b-divisor $\mathbf M$ represented on a projective birational compact Kähler model. Require generalized log discrepancies positive for every prime and at least one for exceptional primes. For strict transforms $B_i$, traces $M_i$ and rational Cartier $D_i=K_{X_i}+B_i+M_i$, OpenAI claims every sequence of projective small nonisomorphic bimeromorphic diagrams with connected fibers
\[
X_i\xrightarrow{f_i}Z_i\xleftarrow{f_i^+}X_{i+1},\qquad
-D_i\text{ relatively ample for }f_i,\quad D_{i+1}\text{ relatively ample for }f_i^+,
\]
is finite, provided every $Z_i$ is normal compact Kähler, all models are normal globally Weil rational-factorial compact Kähler fourfolds, and canonical representatives are compatible. Small means no exceptional prime divisor. This theorem has a generalized-canonical discrepancy hypothesis, not unrestricted generalized log canonicity.
