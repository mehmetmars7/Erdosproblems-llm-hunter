\section*{All groups are good: the four-dimensional disc-embedding conjecture}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
Manuscript 1 (family 305, 2026-09-24): A compact smooth four-manifold is claimed to contain disc maps with framed algebraic duals whose boundary circles admit no disjoint locally flat fillings, even without replacement framings or relative classes prescribed. Manuscript 2 (family 305, 2026-09-24): The unrestricted four-dimensional disk-embedding assertion is claimed false: algebraically suitable immersed disks lack disjoint locally flat replacements preserving boundaries and induced framings, even when relative homotopy classes may change.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} OpenAI claims a smooth oriented four-manifold $M$ that is compact and connected, with boundary nonempty, proper generically immersed disks $f_i:(D^2,S^1)\to(M,\partial M)$ with disjoint embedded boundary circles, and framed sphere maps $g_i:S^2\to M$, for some $k\ge1$, with
\[
\lambda(f_i,g_j)=\delta_{ij}\,1,\qquad\lambda(g_i,g_j)=0,\qquad\widetilde\mu(g_i)=0,
\]
but with no pairwise disjoint proper locally flat embedded disks filling these circles. Here $\lambda$ is the equivariant intersection pairing in $\mathbb Z[\pi_1M]$ and $\widetilde\mu$ is the reduced equivariant self-intersection invariant. Each sphere can individually be smoothly embedded, although the spheres may intersect one another. The nonexistence conclusion imposes neither output framings nor dual spheres. Further corollaries assert that the rank-two free group $F_2$, and every group containing it, is not good in the Freedman--Quinn disk-embedding sense, and that unrestricted four-dimensional topological surgery and five-dimensional $s$-cobordism cannot both hold.

\paragraph{Mathematical claim 2.} For some smooth oriented four-manifold $M$ that is compact, connected and has boundary and integer $k\ge1$, OpenAI claims proper generically immersed disks $f_i$ with disjoint embedded boundaries and framed generically immersed spheres $g_i$ satisfying
\[
\lambda(f_i,g_j)=\delta_{ij}\,1,\qquad\lambda(g_i,g_j)=0,\qquad\widetilde\mu(g_i)=0.
\]
The pairing $\lambda$ takes values in $\mathbb Z[\pi_1M]$; the reduced self-intersection invariant takes values in its additive quotient by $h-h^{-1}$ and integral multiples of $1$. Nevertheless no pairwise disjoint locally flat embedded disks have the same boundary maps and the normal boundary framings induced by the $f_i$. The preserved framings are part of this nonexistence statement.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Manuscript 1 (family 305, 2026-09-24): A cut construction retains boundary holonomy data from hypothetical fillings and converts them into formal gradient identities. Finite algebraic specialization passes these identities to positive characteristic, where truncated complexes expose an incompatible tensor pairing. Because the obstruction concerns the boundaries themselves, it also rules out the weaker unframed filling demand and implies that the rank-two free group is not good.

Manuscript 2 (family 305, 2026-09-24): Independently conjugated Brunnian relations are installed before cutting the hypothetical embedded images. Based flat SL\_2 connections give Chern--Simons potentials, and algebraization preserves their full marked critical schemes, including nilpotents. After positive-characteristic truncation, transverse graph classes pair nontrivially, whereas triple relations and independently lifted parameters force every corresponding pairing to vanish.
