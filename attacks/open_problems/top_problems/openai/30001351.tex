\section*{Weak Zariski Decompositions of Pseudo-Effective Divisors}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
Manuscript 1 (family 036, 2026-09-24): Projective generalized log-canonical rational pairs are claimed to admit minimal models in the pseudo-effective case and Mori fiber spaces otherwise, with the chosen nef b-divisor retained. Manuscript 2 (family 036, 2026-10-03): For projective characteristic-zero klt pairs, a nef ordinary adjoint plus a nef rational divisor is claimed to be numerically equivalent to a semiample divisor whenever the ordinary adjoint is pseudo-effective.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Manuscript 1 (family 036, 2026-09-24): The argument extends positive-boundary programs by selecting valuations with controlled normalized volume and character congruences. Equivariant regrading and finitely generated Rees constructions combine with complements and polarized boundedness to obtain the endpoint while keeping the generalized nef data fixed.

Manuscript 2 (family 036, 2026-10-03): Ordinary log abundance and terminating programs supply the birational reductions. The remaining full-nef-dimension Calabi--Yau case is treated with jet and Frobenius estimates, after which the positive part descends as a semiample numerical representative; flat nontorsion bundles explain why numerical equivalence is the conclusion.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
Manuscript 1 (family 036, 2026-09-24): A generalized minimal model gives a nef positive part and effective exceptional negative part for the rational adjoints satisfying this manuscript's data assumptions. The problem weak-decomposition question concerns any pseudo-effective real Cartier divisor, not merely these adjoints.

Manuscript 2 (family 036, 2026-10-03): For the specified projective rational klt nef adjoints, the numerical semiample representative is already nef, hence gives a weak numerical Zariski decomposition with zero negative part. Arbitrary pseudo-effective real divisors on arbitrary normal projective morphisms are absent.
