\section*{Equivariant Jiang–Su absorption for amenable actions}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
Amenable countable-group actions on simple unital separable nuclear stably finite Jiang--Su-stable algebras are claimed to absorb the trivial Jiang--Su action, without fixing the tracial-state simplex. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} Let $A$ be a complex $C^*$-algebra, nuclear and stably finite, as well as simple, separable, unital and infinite-dimensional with $A\simeq A\otimes_{\min}\mathcal Z$, and let $\alpha$ be any action of a countable discrete amenable $G$. Write $B=A\otimes_{\min}\mathcal Z$, $\beta_g=\alpha_g\otimes\mathrm{id}$. OpenAI claims a unital $*$-isomorphism $\Phi:A\to B$ and unitaries $u_g\in B$ with
\[
u_e=1,\qquad u_{gh}=u_g\beta_g(u_h),\qquad
\Phi(\alpha_g(a))=u_g\beta_g(\Phi(a))u_g^*.
\]
Thus the action absorbs the trivial Jiang--Su action up to cocycle conjugacy. No freeness assumption on $\alpha$ is stated.
