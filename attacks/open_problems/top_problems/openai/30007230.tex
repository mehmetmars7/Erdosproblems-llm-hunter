\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} The source constructs smooth source-free axisymmetric electrovacuum data on \(\Omega=\{x\in\mathbb R^3:|x|\ge1\}\), with connected future-MOTS boundary \(S\), no interior weakly future-trapped enclosing surface, and every full enclosing cut of area at least \(A=|S|\). The examples are maximal, \(\operatorname{tr}_gK=0\), with \(g-\delta=O_4(r^{-1})\), \(K=O_3(r^{-3})\), and electromagnetic fields \(O_3(r^{-2})\). They obey
\[
 R_g-|K|^2=2(|\mathcal E|^2+|\mathcal B|^2),\quad
 \operatorname{div}K=2(\mathcal E\times\mathcal B)^\flat,\quad
 \operatorname{div}\mathcal E=\operatorname{div}\mathcal B=0.
\]
With $P_{\mathrm{ADM}}=0$, $m=E_{\mathrm{ADM}}>0$, gravitational ADM angular momentum $J$, and zero total electric/magnetic charge, both fields are nevertheless nonzero. One example satisfies
\[
 A>8\pi|J|,\qquad m^2<\frac A{16\pi}+\frac{4\pi J^2}{A}.
\]
Another satisfies equality in that formula but admits no smooth spacelike Kerr--Newman embedding inducing its metric, second fundamental form, and electromagnetic fields. The missing electromagnetic-tail controls are therefore material. The angular momentum convention is
\[
 J=\frac1{8\pi}\lim_{R\to\infty}\int_{S_R}(K-(\operatorname{tr}_gK)g)(\nu_R,\eta)\,dA_g.
\]
The symmetry generator $\eta$ has period $2\pi$ and agrees with standard axial rotation at infinity. This flux excludes an electromagnetic surface contribution. The counterexample does not assert failure for the corrected conserved total angular momentum.
