\subsection{OpenAI's claim}

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} Fix $J>0$ and a signed finite-range potential $U$ on finite dual-edge sets, with $U(\varnothing)=0$ and square-lattice translation, rotation and reflection invariance. In a finite square-lattice spin domain $\Omega$ whose union of unit cells is the closure of a Jordan domain, choose two dual boundary marks and exterior stubs. Let $E_\Omega$ be dual edges crossing bonds with an endpoint in $\Omega$. Interior contours $P\subset E_\Omega$ have odd degrees at precisely the two marks; a finite exterior completion $P_o$ contains the stubs and has the same odd-degree marks. Sample contours with probability proportional to
\[
 \exp\left[-2\beta J|P|-\beta\lambda
 \sum_{\varnothing\ne X\subseteq P}\sum_{Y\subseteq P_o}U(X\cup Y)\right].
\]
Thus interactions joining interior and exterior occupied edges remain present. Resolve a degree-four contour vertex by pairing north with east and south with west, obtaining the oriented chord $\gamma(P)$ between stub midpoints. There is $\lambda_0(J,U)>0$ and a domain-independent $\beta_c(J,U,\lambda)>0$, with $\beta_c(J,U,0)=\log(1+\sqrt2)/(2J)$, such that for $|\lambda|<\lambda_0$, deterministic marked approximations of any bounded Jordan $(D;a,b)$ with uniformly converging boundary homeomorphisms satisfy
\[
 \delta_n\gamma(P_n)\Longrightarrow\mathrm{SLE}_3(D;a,b)
\]
at $\beta=\beta_c$. The topology is uniform distance modulo increasing reparametrization. Exterior completions may vary with the mesh subject to the stated parity and geometric conditions. The exterior completion is a finite subset $P_o\subset E\setminus E_\Omega$ of the full dual-edge set. The allowed set of interior contours with the prescribed odd-degree marks must be nonempty.
