\[

 \mu_m\ge|J_m|_g,\quad\operatorname{div}_gE=0.
\]
Assume $\mu_m,|J_m|\in L^1$, $g-\delta=O_2(r^{-q})$, $K=O_1(r^{-1-q})$ for $q>(n-2)/2$, and $E=O_1(r^{-k})$. A full cut consists of all of its intrinsic boundary $\Gamma=\partial D$; require this boundary to be compact, smoothly embedded and two-sided. All components and portions coinciding with $S=\partial\Omega$ count. Put $a_g(S)=\inf_\Gamma\operatorname{Area}_g(\Gamma)$. Put $r_A=(a_g(S)/\omega)^{1/k}$ and $X_A=r_A^{k-1}$.
