\section*{An irreducible tensor square containing every symmetric-group representation}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. For each symmetric-group degree other than $2,4,9$, the authors claim an irreducible representation whose square contains every irreducible constituent.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} For every positive integer \(n\notin\{2,4,9\}\), the source claims a partition \(\lambda\vdash n\) satisfying
\[
 \forall\nu\vdash n,\qquad g(\lambda,\lambda,\nu)>0.
\]
Thus one irreducible complex representation of \(S_n\) has every irreducible as a constituent of its tensor square. The three exceptional sizes are excluded from this theorem.
