\section*{A new-Lefschetz defect reduction for rational Hodge pairings}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 001; 2026-09-23), the asserted result is that reduction of rational Hodge classes on abelian varieties preserves rational intersection numbers with divisor products, in crystalline as well as prime-to-characteristic cohomology, including characteristic two; In manuscript 2 (family 032; 2026-09-30), the asserted result is that rational Hodge classes on CM abelian varieties in all dimensions are algebraic, with the stated Tate and Hodge-standard consequences; In manuscript 3 (family 032; 2026-09-30), the asserted result is that the rational Hodge assertion holds on every self-power of the stated hyperbolic Weil sixfolds of signature $(3,3)$ and on every self-power of imaginary-quadratic-action varieties of dimension at most five.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Manuscript 1.}
Let an abelian variety $A/\overline{\mathbb Q}$ of dimension $d$ have good reduction $A_0/\overline{\mathbb F}_p$ at a place $w$. For any rational Hodge class $\gamma\in H^{2r}(A(\mathbb C),\mathbb Q(r))$, $0\le r\le d$, and Cartier divisors $D_1,\ldots,D_{d-r}$ on $A_0$, the claim is existence of one rational number $q$ with
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}_{\ell}(\gamma_{0,\ell}\prod_i c_{1,\ell}(D_i))=q\quad(\ell\ne p),\qquad
 \operatorname{Tr}_p(\gamma_{0,p}\prod_i c_{1,\rm cris}(D_i))=q.
\]
The first specialization is smooth-proper \'etale specialization; the second is de Rham--crystalline specialization in the completed $p$-adic comparison field. The trace normalization is in the highest Tate twist, with the trace of a zero-cycle equal to its degree. For $r=d$ the divisor product is one. This is a common rational intersection-number assertion, not itself a full algebraicity assertion for every Hodge class.

\paragraph{Manuscript 2.}
If $A$ is any complex abelian variety of CM type, then for every integer $p\ge0$ the claim is
\[
 \operatorname{cl}_B:\operatorname{CH}^p(A)\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb Q
       \twoheadrightarrow H^{2p}(A,\mathbb Q)\cap H^{p,p}(A).
\]
The intersection is inside complex cohomology, so this asserts algebraicity of every rational Hodge class, rather than merely classes with extra symmetry. A CM abelian variety has a commutative semisimple algebra of rational endomorphisms of dimension $2\dim A$. Finite products and all powers of CM abelian varieties are included in the assertion.

\paragraph{Manuscript 3.}
Take a complex abelian variety $A$, with $\dim A\le5$, and an imaginary quadratic field embedded in $\operatorname{End}(A)\otimes\mathbb Q$. For every positive integer $m$ and every codimension $p\ge0$, the claim is
\[
 \operatorname{CH}^p(A^m)\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb Q
 \twoheadrightarrow H^{2p}(A^m,\mathbb Q)\cap H^{p,p}(A^m)
\]
under the Betti cycle-class map, with the intersection inside complex cohomology. It includes all rational Hodge classes on every power, rather than only the distinguished Weil classes.
