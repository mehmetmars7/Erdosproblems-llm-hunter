\section*{Campana–Peternell Conjecture for Fano Manifolds}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
Manuscript 1 (family 062, 2026-09-23): Every complex contact Fano manifold is claimed to be an adjoint variety with its given contact distribution; the quaternionic-Kähler consequence identifies positive manifolds of real dimension $4m$, $m\ge2$, with symmetric Wolf models up to scale. Manuscript 2 (family 067, 2026-09-25): Among smooth complex Fano varieties in dimension six, those with nef tangent are claimed to be rational homogeneous, with a universal-cover consequence when dimension minus maximal étale irregularity is at most six.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} For every smooth connected projective complex contact Fano manifold $(X,F)$ of dimension $2n+1$, $n\ge1$, with contact line $L=T_X/F$, OpenAI claims a simple complex Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$ and an algebraic contact isomorphism
\[
X\simeq\mathbb P(\mathcal O_{\min}(\mathfrak g)),\qquad
L\simeq\mathcal O_{\mathbb P(\mathfrak g)}(1)|_X.
\]
The target is the projectivized least-dimensional nonzero nilpotent adjoint orbit, with its canonical contact distribution. The Riemannian corollary says that every closed connected $4m$-manifold, $m\ge2$, with holonomy contained in $\operatorname{Sp}(m)\operatorname{Sp}(1)$ and positive scalar curvature has $\nabla\operatorname{Rm}=0$ and agrees up to constant metric scaling with a Wolf space, which is compact, symmetric and positive quaternionic-Kähler.

\paragraph{Mathematical claim 2.} OpenAI claims rational homogeneity for each smooth connected complex projective Fano manifold of dimension six whose tangent bundle is nef (a quotient of a complex semisimple group by a parabolic subgroup).
Its Kähler uniformization corollary considers a compact connected Kähler $X$ with analytically nef tangent bundle and
\[
0\le d=\dim X-\widetilde q(X)\le6,\qquad
\widetilde q(X)=\max_{X'\to X\text{ finite étale}}h^1(X',\mathcal O_{X'}).
\]
It claims a connected finite étale cover whose Albanese morphism is a holomorphically locally trivial bundle with rational homogeneous fiber $F$ of dimension $d$, and
\[
\widetilde X\simeq F\times\mathbb C^{\widetilde q(X)}.
\]
Thus the universal cover is quasi-projective and holomorphically convex, with Remmert reduction to the affine factor. In particular the corollary applies in complex dimension at most six.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Manuscript 1 (family 062, 2026-09-23): Two contact lines with nonzero Levi pairing are smoothed into very free conics while tracking both endpoint velocities. This produces a symmetric line-valued section on the square with the prescribed diagonal jet, forcing global contact fields to span; homogeneity follows, and the twistor correspondence supplies the Riemannian conclusion.

Manuscript 2 (family 067, 2026-09-25): Known contractions reduce the new case to Picard number one and pseudoindices four or five. The universal minimal-curve family has two smooth fibrations whose projection identities give linear intersection constraints; characteristic-number and integrality comparisons rule out the remaining models and identify the homogeneous possibilities.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
Manuscript 1 (family 062, 2026-09-23): Contact-Fano homogeneity implies the Campana–Peternell conclusion for the subset of nef-tangent Fano manifolds admitting a contact structure; this problem imposes no contact hypothesis.

Manuscript 2 (family 067, 2026-09-25): The homogeneity theorem treats exactly complex dimension six; the catalogue Campana–Peternell assertion ranges over all Fano dimensions.
