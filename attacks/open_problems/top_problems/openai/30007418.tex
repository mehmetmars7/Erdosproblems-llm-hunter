\section*{The full critical XY spin-field limit with fixed boundary}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a mathematical result relevant to this question in the selected manuscript. With zero boundary angles, the normalized critical XY spin field is claimed to approach the imaginary exponential of a zero-Dirichlet Gaussian field of stiffness $2/\pi$.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} For angles $\theta_x\in\mathbb R/(2\pi\mathbb Z)$ on $\Lambda_R=[-R,R]^2\cap\mathbb Z^2$, use the free-boundary cosine law proportional to $\exp[b\sum_{\{x,y\}\subset\Lambda_R,\,|x-y|=1}\cos(\theta_x-\theta_y)]$, relative to product uniform angles. Count each edge once and omit exterior edges. Define
\[
 C_b(r)=\lim_{R\to\infty}\mathbb E_{R,b}^{\mathrm{free}}\cos(\theta_0-\theta_{re_1}),\quad
 m(b)=\lim_{r\to\infty}-r^{-1}\log C_b(r),\quad b_c=\inf\{b>0:m(b)=0\}.
\]
The thermodynamic limit precedes the separation limit. Set $D=(-1,1)^2$, $D_n=[-1,1]^2\cap n^{-1}\mathbb Z^2$ and fix boundary angles to zero at $b_c$, retaining every nearest-neighbour bond in $D_n$. Let $a_n=\mathbb E_n^0e^{i\theta_0}=\mathbb E_n^0\cos\theta_0>0$. Let $G_n$ be the inverse of the unscaled graph Laplacian $L_nu(x)=\sum_{|x-y|=1/n}(u(x)-u(y))$ with zero boundary. With $K_*=2/\pi$, define
\[
 S_n=\frac1{n^2}\sum_{x\in D_n\cap D}a_n^{-1}
 \exp\left(\frac{G_n(x,x)-G_n(0,0)}{2K_*}\right)e^{i\theta_x}\delta_x.
\]
For the zero-Dirichlet GFF $\Phi_D$ with covariance $(-\Delta_D)^{-1}$, its circle averages define the full-variance imaginary chaos
\[
 V_*=\lim_{\epsilon\downarrow0}\exp\left(\frac{i\Phi_{D,\epsilon}}{\sqrt{K_*}}
 +\frac{\mathbb E\Phi_{D,\epsilon}^2}{2K_*}\right).
\]
Then $S_n\Longrightarrow V_*$ along all integers in $H^{-3}_{\mathrm{loc}}(D)$. For every finite set $f_j\in C_c^\infty(D;\mathbb C)$ and integers $p_j,q_j\ge0$,
\[
 \mathbb E_n^0\prod_jS_n(f_j)^{p_j}\overline{S_n(f_j)}^{q_j}
 \longrightarrow\mathbb E\prod_jV_*(f_j)^{p_j}\overline{V_*(f_j)}^{q_j}.
\]
The joint laws also converge. The square geometry, aligned fixed boundary, centre normalization and diagonal Green correction are all part of this claim.
