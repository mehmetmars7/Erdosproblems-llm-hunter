\section*{Decidable word problem and embedding in finitely presented simple groups}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. Every group with finite generators and a decidable word problem is claimed to embed in a finitely presented simple group.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} For any finitely generated group \(G\), the claimed equivalence is
\[
 G\text{ has decidable word problem}
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \exists H\text{ finitely presented and simple}:\ G\hookrightarrow H.
\]
Both finite presentability and simplicity concern the containing group \(H\).

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Finite algebraic data encode the input inside an affine envelope. The construction removes the Steinberg kernel and builds a two-transitive envelope, while a decreasing dependency bound makes the coding finite; simplicity and embedding follow from the resulting action.
