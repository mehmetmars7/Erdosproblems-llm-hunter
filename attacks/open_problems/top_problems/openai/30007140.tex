\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} On the unit cubic flat torus $\mathbb R^3/\mathbb Z^3$, for $0<V<1$ set $v=\min(V,1-V)$. The claimed finite-perimeter isoperimetric profile is \[I(V)=\min\{(36\pi)^{1/3}v^{2/3},\ 2\sqrt{\pi v},\ 2\}.\] For $V\le1/2$ every minimizer, up to isometry and a null set, is a radius-$(3V/(4\pi))^{1/3}$ ball when $V\le4\pi/81$, a radius-$\sqrt{V/\pi}$ tube about a shortest closed geodesic when $4\pi/81\le V\le1/\pi$, or a coordinate-torus slab of width $V$ when $1/\pi\le V\le1/2$. Adjacent types both minimize at common endpoints; no other equality types occur. Larger-volume minimizers are complements.
