\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Manuscript 1.}
Let $\Lambda\subset\mathbb R^2$ be locally finite with no multiplicities and bounded density in centered disks. Admit locally integrable fields $E$ satisfying, distributionally,
\[
 \operatorname{div}E=2\pi(\sum_{p\in\Lambda}\delta_p-1),\qquad
 \operatorname{curl}E=0.
\]
For $K_R=[-R,R]^2$, take smooth $0\le\chi_R\le1$ supported in $K_R$, equal to one on $K_{R-1}$ and with uniformly bounded gradients. Define
\[
 W(E,\chi)=\lim_{\eta\downarrow0}
 \left[\frac12\int_{\mathbb R^2\setminus\bigcup_{p\in\Lambda}B(p,\eta)}\chi|E|^2
       +\pi\log\eta\sum_{p\in\Lambda}\chi(p)\right],
 \qquad W(E)=\limsup_{R\to\infty}\frac{W(E,\chi_R)}{|K_R|}.
\]
For the unit-density triangular lattice
\[
 \Lambda_{\triangle}=\sqrt{2/\sqrt3}\,[\mathbb Z(1,0)+\mathbb Z(1/2,\sqrt3/2)],
\]
let $E_{\triangle}=-\nabla h$ with the periodic mean-zero solution $-\Delta h=2\pi(\delta_0-1)$. The claim, for every allowed field and every such cutoff family, is
\[
 W(E)\ge W(E_{\triangle})<\infty,
 \qquad\inf_E W(E)=W(E_{\triangle}).
\]
The cited spherical consequence concerns the ordered-pair logarithmic energy on the unit sphere:
\[
 \min_{y_1,\ldots,y_n\in S^2}-\sum_{i\ne j}\log\|y_i-y_j\|
 =\left(\frac12-\log2\right)n^2-\frac n2\log n+C n+o(n),
\]
\[
 C=2\log2+\frac12\log(2/3)
           +3\log\left(\frac{\sqrt\pi}{\Gamma(1/3)}\right).
\]
The norm is Euclidean, collisions have infinite energy, and $n\to\infty$ through integers.
