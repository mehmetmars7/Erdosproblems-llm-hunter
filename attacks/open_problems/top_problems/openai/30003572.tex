\section*{Universal-Cover Decomposition for Nef Anticanonical Manifolds}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
Among smooth complex Fano varieties in dimension six, those with nef tangent are claimed to be rational homogeneous, with a universal-cover consequence when dimension minus maximal étale irregularity is at most six. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} OpenAI claims rational homogeneity for each smooth connected complex projective Fano manifold of dimension six whose tangent bundle is nef (a quotient of a complex semisimple group by a parabolic subgroup).
Its Kähler uniformization corollary considers a compact connected Kähler $X$ with analytically nef tangent bundle and
\[
0\le d=\dim X-\widetilde q(X)\le6,\qquad
\widetilde q(X)=\max_{X'\to X\text{ finite étale}}h^1(X',\mathcal O_{X'}).
\]
It claims a connected finite étale cover whose Albanese morphism is a holomorphically locally trivial bundle with rational homogeneous fiber $F$ of dimension $d$, and
\[
\widetilde X\simeq F\times\mathbb C^{\widetilde q(X)}.
\]
Thus the universal cover is quasi-projective and holomorphically convex, with Remmert reduction to the affine factor. In particular the corollary applies in complex dimension at most six.
