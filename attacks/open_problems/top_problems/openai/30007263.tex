\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Manuscript 1.}
Let $X/\mathbb F_q$ be smooth projective geometrically connected, $G/\mathbb F_q$ split connected semisimple, $D$ an effective divisor, $U=X\setminus\operatorname{supp}D$ and $K_D=\ker(G(\mathcal O_{\mathbb A})\to G(\mathcal O_D))$. Fix $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}\mathbb F_q$, $k=\overline{\mathbb Q}_{\ell}$, an embedding $j:\overline{\mathbb Q}\to k$ and $a=j(\sqrt q)$. For an occurring character $\nu$ of the excursion algebra on cuspidal functions at full level $K_D$, write $\sigma_{\nu}:\pi_1(U)\to\widehat G(k)$ for its parameter and $\Gamma=W_U$ for the Weil subgroup.

The theorem assumes the companion finite-level decomposition: one nilpotent orbit $\mathcal O_{\nu}$ works at all good points, and each semisimple Frobenius class has the form $[\phi_{\mathcal O_{\nu}}(\operatorname{diag}(a^{d_x},a^{-d_x}))c_x]$, with $c_x$ commuting, algebraic and compact at every complex embedding. Under this assumption it claims a global algebraic $\phi_{\nu}:\operatorname{SL}_2\to\widehat G$ and continuous $\tau_{\nu}:\Gamma\to Z_{\widehat G}(\phi_{\nu}(\operatorname{SL}_2))(k)$, defined over a finite $\ell$-adic extension, with reductive Zariski closure, such that after one fixed conjugation
\[
 \sigma_{\nu}(w)=\tau_{\nu}(w)\phi_{\nu}(\operatorname{diag}(a^{\deg w},a^{-\deg w}))\quad(w\in\Gamma).
\]
The differential of $\phi_{\nu}$ sends the standard upper nilpotent matrix into $\mathcal O_{\nu}$. For every rational-form algebraic representation and closed $x\in U$, eigenvalues of $\tau_{\nu}(\operatorname{Frob}_x)$ are algebraic and have absolute value one at every complex embedding. The full centralizer may be disconnected. Ellipticity, packet classification and a multiplicity formula are not asserted.
