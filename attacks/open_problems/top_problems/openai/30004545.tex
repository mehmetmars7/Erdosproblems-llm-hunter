\section*{The Tamed-to-Compatible Symplectic Conjecture}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
\textit{Taming versus compatibility}: The manuscript claims that an almost-complex structure on a closed smooth four-manifold admits a compatible symplectic form whenever it admits a taming one. It keeps the almost-complex structure fixed.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} Fix a smooth almost complex structure $J$ on a smooth real four-manifold $X$, assumed connected and closed. If some closed real two-form $\omega$ satisfies $\omega(v,Jv)>0$ for all nonzero tangent $v$, the claim produces a closed real two-form $\eta$ with \[\eta(v,Jv)>0\quad(v\ne0),\qquad\eta(Ju,Jv)=\eta(u,v).\] Thus a symplectic form taming $J$ can be replaced by one compatible with $J$. No equality of their cohomology classes or prescribed-volume assertion is included.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
\textit{Taming versus compatibility}: Failure of compatibility yields a positive current annihilating closed invariant forms. An elliptic correction makes it annihilate all closed two-forms, with resolvent estimates controlling its anti-invariant part and nearby complex-line directions. Splitting off the positive-density region and refining the pairing removes the negative error, contradicting the existence of the original tamer.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
\textit{Taming versus compatibility}: Keeping the tamed almost complex structure fixed, the theorem supplies a smooth compatible symplectic form on every closed connected four-manifold. The compact boundaryless convention is the closed-manifold convention of this conjecture; no prescribed class or integrability is required.

With the hypotheses and conventions above, the claimed result addresses the whole mathematical assertion on this page.
