\section*{Topological right-triviality of constant-Milnor surface families}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
A one-parameter isolated surface-hypersurface family with constant Milnor number is claimed to admit a jointly continuous ambient trivialization preserving both parameter and function value. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} For a holomorphic family $F:(\mathbb C^3\times\Delta,\{0\}\times\Delta)\to(\mathbb C,0)$, put $f_t(x)=F(x,t)$. Assume each $f_t(0)=0$ has an isolated critical point and constant Milnor number
\[
\mu(f_t,0)=\dim_{\mathbb C}\mathcal O_{\mathbb C^3,0}/(\partial_1f_t,\partial_2f_t,\partial_3f_t)=\mu(f_0,0).
\]
OpenAI claims, after shrinking the disk and representatives, a parameter-preserving homeomorphism germ $\Phi(x,t)=(\phi_t(x),t)$ with $\phi_t(0)=0$, $\phi_0=\mathrm{id}$ and $f_t(\phi_t(x))=f_0(x)$. Both $\Phi$ and its inverse are jointly continuous along the origin section. This is topological right-triviality, including defining functions rather than only zero sets.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}

Constant Milnor number cancels the main contribution, and nonnegative smoothing excesses plus the orbifold surface inequality force their log squares to vanish; resolution transport then extends to the ambient function levels.
