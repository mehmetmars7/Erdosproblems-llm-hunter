\section*{Algebraic K-Theory and Rigidity Conjectures for Discrete Groups}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
Two closed aspherical topological four-manifolds are claimed homotopy equivalent but not homeomorphic, sharing a word-hyperbolic fundamental group; one also admits a self-equivalence outside every homeomorphism homotopy class. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} OpenAI claims closed connected aspherical topological four-manifolds $M,N$ with a common word-hyperbolic fundamental group such that
\[
M\simeq N\quad\text{but}\quad M\not\cong N\text{ by any homeomorphism}.
\]
Aspherical means that the universal cover is contractible; $\simeq$ denotes homotopy equivalence. A second theorem claims a topological four-manifold that is aspherical, closed and connected $M$ and a self homotopy equivalence $f:M\to M$ for which no homeomorphism is homotopic to $f$. These are four-dimensional topological counterexamples; the first excludes homeomorphism existence in any homotopy class.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Manuscript 1 (family 320, 2026-10-04): Reflected marked chambers retain an odd-cover filling obstruction, and carefully chosen binary label relations eliminate unwanted chamber symmetries. Hyperbolicity and Poincare duality make the outer automorphism group finite, with a controlled normalizer of the realized chamber action. A hypothetical homeomorphism would transport another finite action into that realized subgroup and force an excluded conjugacy.
