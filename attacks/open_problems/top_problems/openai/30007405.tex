Let $x\in(0,1)$ be the image of $b$ under the conformal map taking $(a,c,d)$ to $(0,1,\infty)$ in $\mathbb H$. Averaging both cell geometry and colours, the claim is
\[
 \lim_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}\mathbb P(\mathcal C_\varepsilon(D;a,b,c,d))

 =\frac{\Gamma(2/3)x^{1/3}}{\Gamma(1/3)\Gamma(4/3)}
