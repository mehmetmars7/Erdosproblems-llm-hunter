\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} For every closed smooth Riemannian surface $(M,g)$ there is a smooth immersion $F:M\to\mathbb R^4$ with \[F^*\Bigl(\sum_{i=1}^4dx_i^2\Bigr)=g.\] Closedness means compactness without boundary. The assertion is an isometric immersion, which allows self-intersections; it does not assert a globally injective isometric embedding.
