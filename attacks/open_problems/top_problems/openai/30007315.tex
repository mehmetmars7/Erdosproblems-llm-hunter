\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} Let $\mathscr K$ consist of ICC groups that are countably infinite and abstractly commensurable with lattices in products, with a positive finite number of factors, of $\mathbf H_i(k_i)^+$, where $k_i$ is real, complex or a finite extension of a $p$-adic field, $\mathbf H_i$ is connected adjoint absolutely simple, and each factor is noncompact with property $(T)$. The plus group is generated by rational points of unipotent radicals of proper parabolics; ICC means all nonidentity conjugacy classes are infinite, and abstract commensurability means isomorphic finite-index subgroups. No irreducibility, cocompactness or torsion premise is imposed. For $\Gamma\in\mathscr K$, any countably infinite ICC $\Lambda$ and normalized scalar cocycles $\mu,\omega$, OpenAI claims every separable bifinite $L_\mu(\Gamma)$--$L_\omega(\Lambda)$ correspondence is a closed bimodule summand of a finite sum of induced models
\[
E=L^2(L_\mu(\Gamma))\ \overline\otimes_{L_{\mu|_A}(A)}\ (V_\sigma\otimes L^2(L_\omega(\Lambda))),
\]
for finite-index $A\le\Gamma$, $B\le\Lambda$, actual isomorphism $\delta:A\to B$ and finite-dimensional projective unitary $\sigma$ with
\[
\sigma(a)\sigma(b)=\frac{\mu(a,b)}{\omega(\delta(a),\delta(b))}\sigma(ab).
\]
Conversely these models and summands are bifinite. For $n=\dim V_\sigma$ it claims left dimension $n[\Lambda:B]$ and right dimension $n[\Gamma:A]$. Twisted factors have canonical unitaries multiplying by the displayed cocycles; bifinite means finite module dimension on both sides, not mutual-commutant actions.
