\section*{Generic weak future inextendibility near a fixed rotating Kerr datum}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
The linked manuscripts present the following claims by OpenAI: Near each fixed rotating subextremal two-ended Kerr bridge, a dense $G_\delta$ class of smooth vacuum data is claimed to have no future $C^1$ extension; In the same near-Kerr setting, data permitting a continuous nondegenerate future extension with locally square-integrable weak connection are claimed meagre; Near every fixed rotating subextremal Kerr bridge, data with future $C^2$-extendible full developments are claimed meagre, without a symmetry restriction. Together they give a claimed affirmative answer in the matching scope.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} Fix a complete two-ended Kerr bridge datum \(d_*=(h_*,K_*)\) on \(\Sigma\cong\mathbb R\times S^2\), of mass \(M>0\) and subextremal nonzero rotation \(\mathfrak a\). With a smooth end-radius weight \(\rho\ge1\), define
\[
 p_m(u,v)=\sum_{j=0}^m\left(\sup_\Sigma\rho^{1+j}|\nabla_*^ju|_*+
 \sup_\Sigma\rho^{2+j}|\nabla_*^jv|_*\right).
\]
The data space \(\mathcal D\) consists of smooth complete positive-definite \(h\), with all \(p_m(d-d_*)\) finite, satisfying
\[
 R(h)+(\operatorname{tr}_hK)^2-|K|_h^2=0,\qquad
 \operatorname{div}_hK-d(\operatorname{tr}_hK)=0.
\]
Use the relative topology of all these seminorms and \(\mathcal U_\varepsilon=\{d:p_{10}(d-d_*)<\varepsilon\}\). An extension has a smooth time-oriented isometric embedding of the full maximal globally hyperbolic development with image open and strictly contained in a connected Lorentzian four-manifold, and a future timelike \(C^1\) curve that exits its image at its endpoint. The ambient extension need not satisfy the vacuum equations or global hyperbolicity. For \(0<\mathfrak a<M\), the theorem claims some \(\varepsilon_0>0\) and a dense \(G_\delta\) subset \(\mathcal G\subset\mathcal U_{\varepsilon_0}\) whose data have no such future extension with nondegenerate \(C^1\) metric. This is Baire genericity in the stated near-Kerr topology.
