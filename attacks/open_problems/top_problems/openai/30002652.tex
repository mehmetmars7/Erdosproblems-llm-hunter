\section*{Rational Curves on Compact Kähler Manifolds}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
Every positive-dimensional compact Kähler manifold without nonconstant entire curves is claimed to be projective with ample canonical bundle. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} For a connected Kähler manifold $X$, assumed compact and having positive complex dimension, OpenAI claims
\[
[\text{every holomorphic }\mathbb C\to X\text{ is constant}]\quad\Longrightarrow\quad K_X\text{ ample}.
\]
Consequently some positive tensor power of $K_X$ gives a holomorphic embedding into projective space. The hypothesis is absence of nonconstant entire curves, with no initial projectivity assumption.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Manuscript 1 (family 051, 2026-09-23): Hyperbolicity bounds derivatives of holomorphic discs, and a Hardy-space frame realizes center-fixed variations with an area-Hessian identity. Disc envelopes give nef canonical weights, while the complementary variation estimate forces a volume lower bound; these positivity controls lead to canonical ampleness.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
Manuscript 1 (family 051, 2026-09-23): For the hyperbolic compact Kähler subclass the theorem gives ample K and excludes rational curves. It does not prove the general equivalence or existence of a rational curve when a pseudo-effective K is non-nef.
