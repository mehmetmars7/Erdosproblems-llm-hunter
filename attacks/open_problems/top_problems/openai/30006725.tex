\section*{Deligne's Absolute Hodge Conjecture}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
Manuscript 1 (family 032, 2026-09-30): For tensor-Hodge-generic abelian-cover Jacobians and the specified very general diagonal complete intersections, OpenAI claims the rational Hodge assertion on every self-power; Manuscript 2 (family 032, 2026-09-30): On the specified quadratic-lattice locus of projective K3 surfaces, all rational Hodge classes on every power are claimed to be algebraic; an exact algebraic Kuga--Satake tensor also gives a conditional criterion beyond that locus; Manuscript 3 (family 032, 2026-10-03): For every projective complex K3 surface, the precisely normalized Kuga--Satake embedding into the full even-Clifford abelian target is claimed to come from an algebraic cycle. Manuscript 4 (family 032, 2026-09-18): Every rational Weil class of a split eight-dimensional abelian variety is claimed to have an algebraic representative, uniformly over imaginary-quadratic fields and compatible polarizations; Manuscript 5 (family 032, 2026-09-30): The manuscripts claim algebraicity of rational Hodge classes on CM abelian varieties, with consequences for generalized Hodge classes and finite-field Tate classes; Manuscript 6 (family 032, 2026-10-04): Rational Hodge classes on any finite product of projective complex K3 surfaces are claimed to be algebraic, even when the factors have unrelated periods or extra endomorphisms. Manuscript 7 (family 032, 2026-09-30): Algebraicity is claimed for Hodge classes on powers of split Weil sixfolds and of abelian varieties of dimension at most five carrying an imaginary-quadratic action, including special periods.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} Fix $n\ge2$, $d\ge0$, $c\in\{0,1,2\}$ and $m=d+c+1$. For a rank-$c$ complex matrix $A=(a_{\alpha i})$ with every maximal coordinate minor nonzero, define the smooth diagonal complete intersection
\[
X_A=\{[x_1:\cdots:x_m]\in\mathbb P^{m-1}:\ \sum_i a_{\alpha i}x_i^n=0\quad(1\le\alpha\le c)\}.
\]
OpenAI asserts surjectivity of $\operatorname{CH}^p(X_A^u)_{\mathbb Q}\to H^{2p}(X_A^u,\mathbb Q)\cap H^{p,p}(X_A^u)$ for every $u\ge1$ and every $p$, outside a union, indexed by a countable set, of proper algebraic closed subsets of this smooth parameter space. The exceptional set is unnecessary when $c\le1$ or $d=0$.
A second assertion concerns a fixed connected finite abelian topological cover of a compact oriented surface with specified branch monodromy, allowing unbranched and trivial covers. Vary the full marked complex structure and branch points. At any parameter $s$ for which each rational tensor of type $(a,a)$ in $H^1(C_s,\mathbb Q)^{\otimes 2a}$ keeps its Hodge type at every point of the marked family, the same cycle-class surjectivity holds on
\[
(\operatorname{Jac}(C_s)\times M)^N\quad(N\ge1),\qquad C_s^a\times M^b\quad(a,b\ge0),
\]
for every complex CM abelian variety $M$. CM means that its degree-one Hodge group is a torus. This is a Hodge-generic assertion, with the generic locus chosen independently of $M$.

\paragraph{Mathematical claim 2.} For a projective complex K3 surface $S$, write $T_S=\operatorname{NS}(S)_{\mathbb Q}^{\perp}$ with cup-product quadratic form $q$. Assume a rational isometric embedding
\[
(T_S,q)\hookrightarrow\mathbb U_{\mathbb Q}^{\oplus2}\perp\langle-1\rangle^4,
\]
where $\mathbb U$ is the hyperbolic plane with Gram matrix $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$. Choose any full even-Clifford realization $H^1(A,\mathbb Q)=C^+(T_S,q)$, any $w\in T_S$ with $q(w)\ne0$, and any rational weight-one polarization. The Kuga--Satake map is formed from $v\mapsto(a\mapsto vaw)$ and this polarization. OpenAI asserts existence of $\Gamma\in\operatorname{CH}^2(S\times A^2)_{\mathbb Q}$ inducing this exact prescribed map on $T_S$, also after transporting it to an isogenous realization. It further claims rational Hodge and generalized Hodge conjectures in all degrees of $S^m$ for every $m\ge1$. The quadratic embedding is a hypothesis, so this theorem alone is restricted to that locus.

\paragraph{Mathematical claim 3.} Let $S$ be any smooth projective complex K3 surface and let $T(S)=\operatorname{NS}(S)_{\mathbb Q}^{\perp}$ in $H^2(S,\mathbb Q)$. Fix the full even-Clifford Kuga--Satake construction, including the rational quadratic polarization, parity identification and rational weight-one polarization, with associated abelian variety $A_S$. These choices specify $\kappa_S:T(S)\to H^2(A_S^2,\mathbb Q)$. OpenAI claims
\[
\exists\Gamma_S\in\operatorname{CH}^2(S\times A_S^2)_{\mathbb Q}\quad
(p_{A_S^2})_*\bigl(\operatorname{cl}(\Gamma_S)\smile p_S^*x\bigr)=\kappa_S(x)
\quad\text{for all }x\in T(S).
\]
Here $\operatorname{CH}^2$ is the group of rational codimension-two cycles modulo rational equivalence and $p_S,p_{A_S^2}$ are the projections. The assertion includes every isogenous realization with the prescribed map transported by its rational Hodge isomorphism.

\paragraph{Mathematical claim 4.} Let $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-d})$ be imaginary quadratic, $A$ a complex abelian variety of dimension eight with a $K$-action, and $\lambda$ a compatible polarization of Weil type, with eigenspace multiplicities $(4,4)$. Put $V=H_1(A,\mathbb Q)$, let $\psi$ be the rational Riemann form and $J$ multiplication by $\sqrt{-d}$. Assume the associated Hermitian form
\[
h(x,y)=\psi(Jx,y)+\sqrt{-d}\,\psi(x,y)
\]
vanishes on a four-dimensional $K$-subspace of $V$ (the split condition). Define the rational Weil space $W_K(A)=\bigwedge_K^8H^1(A,\mathbb Q)\subset H^8(A,\mathbb Q)$ using the two conjugate $K$-eigenspace exterior powers. OpenAI claims
\[
W_K(A)\subset\operatorname{im}\bigl(\operatorname{CH}^4(A)_{\mathbb Q}\xrightarrow{\operatorname{cl}}H^8(A,\mathbb Q)\bigr).
\]
The field and compatible polarization type are arbitrary; extra endomorphisms are allowed. The statement concerns these Weil classes, rather than all Hodge classes on all varieties.

\paragraph{Mathematical claim 5.} A complex abelian variety $A$ is CM if $\operatorname{End}(A)\otimes\mathbb Q$ contains a commutative semisimple algebra of dimension $2\dim A$. For every such $A$ and every $p\ge0$, OpenAI claims
\[
\operatorname{CH}^p(A)_{\mathbb Q}\longrightarrow H^{2p}(A,\mathbb Q)\cap H^{p,p}(A)
\quad\text{is surjective}.
\]
This also applies to arbitrary finite products of CM abelian varieties, including their powers. If $W\subset H^k(A,\mathbb Q)$ is a rational Hodge substructure and $W(r)$ is effective (all Hodge bidegrees are nonnegative), it claims a closed algebraic $Z\subset A$ of codimension at least $r$ with $W\subset\ker[H^k(A,\mathbb Q)\to H^k(A\setminus Z,\mathbb Q)]$.
The manuscript additionally asserts that, for every abelian variety $A/\mathbb F_q$, every $r\ge0$ and prime $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}\mathbb F_q$,
\[
\operatorname{CH}^r(A)\otimes\mathbb Q_\ell\longrightarrow
H^{2r}_{\mathrm{\acute et}}(A_{\overline{\mathbb F}_q},\mathbb Q_\ell(r))^{\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb F}_q/\mathbb F_q)}
\quad\text{is surjective}.
\]
Numerical and $\ell$-adic homological equivalence are claimed to agree on rational cycles. Over any algebraically closed field, for an abelian $g$-fold and rational ample class $h$, the form $(-1)^r\deg(xyh^{g-2r})$ is claimed positive definite on the real extension of $\ker[h^{g-2r+1}:A^r_{\mathrm{num}}\to A^{g-r+1}_{\mathrm{num}}]$ for $0\le r\le g/2$.

\paragraph{Mathematical claim 6.} Choose any integer $m\ge1$ and any projective complex K3 surfaces $S_1,\ldots,S_m$, allowing repeated factors. For $X=\prod_{j=1}^m S_j$, OpenAI claims
\[
\operatorname{CH}^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}\xrightarrow{\operatorname{cl}}
H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Q)\cap H^{p,p}(X)
\quad\text{is surjective for every }0\le p\le2m.
\]
There are no Picard-number, period or endomorphism-field restrictions. The quantified varieties are products of K3 surfaces, so this statement has smaller scope than the Hodge conjecture for arbitrary smooth projective complex varieties.

\paragraph{Mathematical claim 7.} Let $E=\mathbb Q(u)$ be imaginary quadratic with $u^2=-d$, $d>0$, and let $A$ be a complex abelian variety with a unital $E$-action. OpenAI claims rational cycle-class surjectivity
\[
\operatorname{CH}^p(A^m)_{\mathbb Q}\longrightarrow H^{2p}(A^m,\mathbb Q)\cap H^{p,p}(A^m)
\quad(m\ge1,\ p\ge0)
\]
in either of these two cases: $\dim A\le5$; or $\dim A=6$ and the rational Hermitian form associated to a compatible polarization is hyperbolic with signature $(3,3)$. In the second case, with rational Riemann form $e$, this means $h_E(x,y)=e(x,uy)+u e(x,y)$ on $H_1(A,\mathbb Q)$ has a totally isotropic three-dimensional $E$-subspace. Compatibility means $e(ax,y)=e(x,\bar a y)$ for all $a\in E$.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Manuscript 1 (family 032, 2026-09-30): Deck-character projections and equivariant period calculations identify the necessary monodromy invariants of curve covers. Correspondences dominated by CM sources supply determinant tensors, while Gale duality transfers the character-level construction to diagonal complete intersections with the required supports and degree shifts. Manuscript 2 (family 032, 2026-09-30): Clifford multiplication carries exterior powers of the transcendental space into degree-two cohomology of an abelian target. Transposed cycles and a polynomial inverse on the finite-dimensional image recover these summands, so divisor algebraicity supplies the required classes without an inverse Lefschetz correspondence. Manuscript 3 (family 032, 2026-10-03): The construction engineers a Lagrangian whose second cohomology has a nineteen-dimensional deficit, yielding a corresponding bound on self-extensions after mirror realization. The resulting characteristic class is transported through K3 moduli by semiregularity and specialization, retaining the prescribed Clifford tensor.

Manuscript 4 (family 032, 2026-09-18): An embedded spin Lagrangian detects the exceptional middle-cohomology component and makes two homological obstruction maps injective. Finite-cutoff weighted Floer operations are assembled into algebraic module identities; cancellation in alternating specialization then transports the desired Chern character to an algebraic cycle. Manuscript 5 (family 032, 2026-09-30): The argument realizes four-factor balanced tensors using unitary theta constructions. Elementary switches between equal-sum lists, followed by algebraic polarization contractions and descent of coefficients, build arbitrary balanced CM tensors from these elementary cycle classes. Manuscript 6 (family 032, 2026-10-04): A Lagrangian immersion is used to produce prescribed Euler pairings while bounding the second self-extension space of the associated complex. Clifford-algebra calculations then identify its transcendental cohomology component; algebraic Kuga--Satake tensors and their contractions supply the classes needed on products.

Manuscript 7 (family 032, 2026-09-30): A coherent seed and its Fourier--Mukai transform produce the split-sixfold Weil plane through an obstruction calculation and deformation. A determinant description of the actual Hodge group, combined with the CM result, extends these algebraic generators to powers and to the stated low-dimensional cases.
