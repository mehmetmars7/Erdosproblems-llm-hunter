\section*{Linear variance of the random 3-SAT unsatisfiability time}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. For proper random 3-SAT clauses sampled with replacement, the uncapped first-unsatisfiable hitting time is claimed to have variance $\Theta(n)$.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} Sample independent clauses, with replacement, uniformly among the $2^k\binom nk$ signed clauses on $k$ distinct variables. Write $F_m=\bigwedge_{j=1}^mC_j$ and $H_n=\min\{m\ge1:F_m\text{ is unsatisfiable}\}$. For $k=3$, an absolute $C<\infty$ gives
\[
 \operatorname{Var}(H_n)\le Cn\quad(n\ge3).
\]
There are also absolute $c>0,n_0$ with $\operatorname{Var}(H_n)\ge cn$ for $n\ge n_0$. Thus $\operatorname{Var}(H_n)=\Theta(n)$.
