\section*{Log-squared sharp threshold for monotone graph properties}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
\textit{Sharp thresholds for graph properties}: The claimed threshold interval for a vertex-invariant increasing graph property has width at most a universal multiple of $\log(1/(2\varepsilon))/(\log n)^2$. This comparison uses the probabilities $\varepsilon$ and $1-\varepsilon$.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
\textit{Sharp thresholds for graph properties}: A random block of vertices is retained while all edges disjoint from it are conditioned upon, preserving a useful permutation symmetry. A biased Fourier estimate bounds conditional low-degree mass linearly in influence. A small-degree vertex in each indexing graph transfers that bound to a larger original degree range, and integration of the probability derivative gives the quantile width.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
