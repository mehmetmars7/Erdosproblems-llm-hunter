\section*{Algorithms for the word problem in Artin groups}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. Every intersection, including an infinite intersection, of parabolic subgroups in a finite-rank Artin group is claimed parabolic.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Relative categorical layers track positive divisors and caps. Two-point uniqueness and a relative frame control null layers, while harmonic heights produce connected minima and bounded positive-height representatives; these give the parabolic carrier of the intersection.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
Corollary 1.3 (the cited result) gives an algorithm taking an arbitrary finite Coxeter matrix and standard-generator words, deciding membership in a conjugate parabolic. The empty parabolic yields an explicit uniform word-problem algorithm. Within these assumptions, this is a claimed affirmative answer to the stated question.

\subsection{Formal verification}
