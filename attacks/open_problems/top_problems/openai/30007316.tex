\section*{Stable recovery of twisted lattice factors}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
Stable isomorphisms of arbitrarily twisted factors from the specified property-T lattice class are claimed to recover the group and cocycle, preserve amplification scales, and fit an explicit arithmetic correspondence category. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} Let $\mathscr K$ consist of ICC groups that are countably infinite and abstractly commensurable with lattices in products, with a positive finite number of factors, of $\mathbf H_i(k_i)^+$, where $k_i$ is real, complex or a finite extension of a $p$-adic field, $\mathbf H_i$ is connected adjoint absolutely simple, and each factor is noncompact with property $(T)$. The plus group is generated by rational points of unipotent radicals of proper parabolics; ICC means all nonidentity conjugacy classes are infinite, and abstract commensurability means isomorphic finite-index subgroups. No irreducibility, cocompactness or torsion premise is imposed. For $\Lambda\in\mathscr K$, any countably infinite ICC $\Pi$, normalized scalar cocycles $\omega,\nu$ and positive amplification scales $t,s$, OpenAI claims
\[
L_\omega(\Lambda)^t\simeq L_\nu(\Pi)^s\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 t=s\text{ and }\exists\delta:\Lambda\simeq\Pi,\ b:\Lambda\to S^1:\
\omega(g,h)=b(g)b(h)b(gh)^{-1}\nu(\delta g,\delta h).
\]
Amplification $Q^t$ is a matrix corner of unnormalized trace $t$. Every specified unit-scale isomorphism is claimed to have $u_g\mapsto wb(g)v_{\delta g}w^*$ for a target unitary $w$. At matrix corners it is implemented by a rectangular partial isometry after the canonical entrywise map, with the initial and final corner projections. It also claims the recovered comparison group belongs to $\mathscr K$ and every such factor has fundamental group $\{1\}$.
