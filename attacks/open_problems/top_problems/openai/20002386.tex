\section*{Polynomial-time lifted LMI representations of convex cones}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 095; 2026-10-05), the asserted result is that the explicit twenty-three-variable nonspectrahedral cone nonetheless has an exact lift with a size-$100$ pencil and $307$ auxiliary variables, including singular boundary points; In manuscript 2 (family 095; 2026-10-05), the asserted result is that some hyperbolicity cones remain outside the class of projections of finite real semidefinite systems, whatever real coefficients and auxiliary variables are used.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Manuscript 1.}
For cyclic $i\in\{1,2,3\}$ set $Q(y)_{ii}=y_i^2+y_{i+1}^2$, $Q(y)_{ij}=-y_i y_j$ otherwise, and
\[
 \Phi_y\begin{pmatrix}a&r^{\mathsf T}\\r&T\end{pmatrix}
 =\begin{pmatrix}\operatorname{tr}(Q(y)T)&-r^{\mathsf T}Q(y)\\-Q(y)r&aQ(y)\end{pmatrix},
 \quad p(X,Z,y)=\det((\det X)Z-\Phi_y(\operatorname{adj}X)).
\]
Let $K$ be the closed hyperbolicity cone of $p$ in direction $(I_4,I_4,0)$ on the real $23$-dimensional space of symmetric $X,Z$ and $y\in\mathbb R^3$. The claim gives fixed real symmetric $100\times100$ matrices $A_1,\ldots,A_{23},B_1,\ldots,B_{307}$ with
\[
 x\in K\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \exists v\in\mathbb R^{307}:\quad
 \sum_{\nu=1}^{23}x_{\nu} A_{\nu}+\sum_{j=1}^{307}v_jB_j\succeq0.
\]
Coordinates list upper-triangular entries of $X$ then $Z$, followed by $y$. Singular boundary points are included. Auxiliary variables make this a lift; it does not assert direct spectrahedrality.

\paragraph{Manuscript 2.}
The existence claim is a real homogeneous polynomial $p$ and a direction $e$ with $p(e)>0$ such that every univariate $t\mapsto p(te-x)$ is real-rooted, but no finite semidefinite lift represents the resulting closed hyperbolicity cone $K$. Precisely,
\[
 \nexists N,r<\infty,\ A_i,B_j\in\operatorname{Sym}_N(\mathbb R):\quad
 K=\{x:\exists y\in\mathbb R^r,\ A_0+\sum_ix_iA_i+\sum_jy_jB_j\succeq0\}.
\]
This excludes representations even with auxiliary variables, and is stronger than excluding a direct linear matrix inequality.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
For manuscript 1 (family 095; 2026-10-05), the three-variable parameter is covered by rotating two-dimensional planes. Each angular slice has a trigonometric Gram factorization and an exact pencil representation; a finite matching identity combines these slices into the full homogeneous lift.

For manuscript 2 (family 095; 2026-10-05), a positive semidefinite Hankel seed with controlled multiplication kernel produces finitely many inverse-limit directions. Separation from Hankel images yields a polynomial patch whose moment deformation contradicts analytic data that any semidefinite lift would require.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
The exact lift gives one explicit positive example, and the nonliftable construction defeats a universal hyperbolicity inclusion. Neither result classifies all cones or gives the requested general polynomial-time representation criterion.
