\section*{Sharp Upper Bound in Yau's Conjecture on Nodal Sets of Laplace Eigenfunctions}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
\textit{Power-law failure of nodal upper bounds}: The manuscript claims a fixed smooth metric on $S^4\times S^1$ with eigenfunctions whose nodal four-volume exceeds the square-root scale by a fixed positive power of the eigenvalue; this rules out even an arbitrary-small-power-loss version of the proposed general upper bound.

\textit{Nodal upper length on surfaces}: The paper claims nodal length at most $C\sqrt\lambda$ for every real nonzero Laplace eigenfunction on a fixed smooth closed surface; combined with the established lower estimate, this settles the two-dimensional smooth setting.

\textit{Nodal upper-bound failures in dimensions three and four}: The companion claims fixed smooth metrics on $S^3$ and $S^2\times\mathbb T^2$ with unbounded nodal-measure-to-$\sqrt\lambda$ ratios along exact eigenfunction sequences; the three-sphere metric may be chosen as close to round as prescribed in the smooth topology.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
\textit{Power-law failure of nodal upper bounds}: A logarithmic envelope on the four-sphere is corrugated through a number of generations proportional to the logarithm of frequency, producing a power gain in its gradient integral. Complex phase and transport jets create highly accurate localized waves beneath that envelope. Exact metric and spectral corrections then realize genuine eigenfunctions while preserving the growing nodal score on one limiting metric.

\textit{Nodal upper length on surfaces}: Subharmonic profile limits control local growth under affine subdivision. High-growth excursions along grid paths produce witness regions on which growth persists to terminal scale. A packing argument pays for witness regions outside their entry squares using summable collars, bounding the terminal average growth and hence nodal length.

\textit{Nodal upper-bound failures in dimensions three and four}: Each construction starts with explicit high-frequency background modes and an untouched region that anchors spectral control. Corrugated logarithmic envelopes and finite complex-wave jets create quasimodes with a stable lower score from opposite-sign tests. Metric corrections make the waves exact, and a persistence argument passes to one fixed smooth metric without losing the score.

\textit{Power-law failure of nodal upper bounds}: One fixed smooth connected closed metric and eigenfunctions of unbounded positive eigenvalue have unbounded nodal-measure-to-square-root-eigenvalue ratio. This directly negates the universal metric-dependent-constant assertion in our definition.

\textit{Nodal upper length on surfaces}: The sharp nodal upper bound holds for all smooth closed connected surfaces, while the recorded target ranges over all smooth closed dimensions n at least two.
