\section*{Bounded coordinates for negatively pinched Kähler manifolds}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
\textit{Pinched Kähler uniformization obstruction}: A contractible complex threefold is claimed to have a complete Kähler metric with sectional curvature between two negative constants but no bounded holomorphic coordinate system. In particular, it is not biholomorphic to a bounded domain.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
\textit{Pinched Kähler uniformization obstruction}: Disk fibers over the complex two-ball have radii prescribed by a smooth base function. The Hessian of a logarithmic fiber potential gives the Kähler metric, with explicit control of completeness and two-sided real sectional curvature. The chosen radius behavior imposes a holomorphic obstruction preventing a bounded map with nonvanishing Jacobian.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
\textit{Pinched Kähler uniformization obstruction}: Two-sided negative sectional pinching and completeness are retained in the threefold example. Its failure of bounded coordinates directly prevents a biholomorphism to a bounded domain, without needing the stronger assertion that every bounded holomorphic function is constant.

The claimed counterexample lies within the question’s hypotheses and contradicts its universal conclusion.
