\section*{The generalized Mukai pseudoindex assertion}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
For a smooth complex Fano $n$-fold, the generalized Mukai bound $\rho(\iota-1)\le n$ is claimed, with equality exactly for the corresponding product of projective spaces. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} For a smooth connected projective complex Fano manifold $X$ of dimension $n>0$, let $\rho_X$ be its Picard number and $\iota_X=\min\{-K_X\cdot C:C\text{ an irreducible rational curve}\}$ its pseudoindex. OpenAI claims
\[
\rho_X(\iota_X-1)\le n,
\qquad\text{equality}\ \Longleftrightarrow\ X\simeq(\mathbb P^{\iota_X-1})^{\rho_X}.
\]
The pseudoindex is a minimum curve degree, not divisibility of the anticanonical line bundle. There is no dimension restriction.
