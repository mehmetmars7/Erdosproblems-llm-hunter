\section*{Full support of every zero-temperature SK minimizing order parameter}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. Every integrable zero-temperature Parisi minimizer for pure zero-field SK is claimed to have full relative Stieltjes support below overlap one.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} Let \(\mathcal U\) contain nonnegative, nondecreasing right-continuous \(\gamma:[0,1)\to[0,\infty)\) with \(\int_0^1\gamma<\infty\), and define \(\mu_\gamma([0,t])=\gamma(t)\). For covariance \(\xi(t)=t^2/2\), let
\[
 \Phi_t=-\tfrac12(\Phi_{xx}+\gamma(t)\Phi_x^2),\quad\Phi(1,x)=|x|,
 \qquad\mathcal P(\gamma)=\Phi_\gamma(0,0)-\tfrac12\int_0^1t\gamma(t)\,dt.
\]
Every minimizing \(\gamma\) is claimed to satisfy
\[
 \operatorname{supp}\mu_\gamma=[0,1),\qquad
 \gamma(b)>\gamma(a)\quad(0\le a<b<1).
\]
For \(dX_t=\gamma(t)\Phi_{\gamma,x}(t,X_t)dt+dB_t\), \(X_0=0\), the source also gives \(\mathbb E\Phi_{\gamma,x}(t,X_t)^2=t\) and \(\mathbb E\Phi_{\gamma,xx}(t,X_t)^2=1\) for \(t<1\). The optimization corollary, for \(H_n(\sigma)=n^{-1/2}\sum_{i<j}J_{ij}\sigma_i\sigma_j\), gives a randomized \(C(\varepsilon)n^2\)-operation algorithm whose value is at least \(\max H_n-\varepsilon n\) with probability tending to one. This operation model charges one unit for real sums/products and uses prescribed scalar functions; no separate finite-precision preprocessing bound is claimed.
