\section*{Hitting lists for noncommutative rational formulas}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 116; 2026-09-24), the asserted result is that polynomial-size rational matrix lists hit every nonzero admissible noncommutative rational formula of bounded tree size at a defined invertible value.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Manuscript 1.}
Given unary $1^n,1^s$, $n,s\ge1$, a fixed deterministic Turing machine is claimed to construct a list $\mathcal H_{n,s}$ whose entries are tuples of $n$ square matrices over $\mathbb Q$, using polynomial bit time and with polynomial total output length, such that
\[
 \forall0\ne\Phi\text{ admissible noncommutative rational formula of size}\le s:
 \quad\exists\mathbf T\in\mathcal H_{n,s}:\ \Phi(\mathbf T)\text{ is defined and invertible}.
\]
Formulas use rational constants, noncommuting variables and arithmetic/inversion gates and represent elements of the free skew field; admissibility requires each inverted subexpression nonzero there. The list is independent of the formula, of its constant bit lengths and of its inverse depth; no advice, oracle or randomness is given to the generator.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
For manuscript 1 (family 116; 2026-09-24), each formula and its domain are certified by affine pencils. Rectangular maps that expand all small subspaces detect their ranks; deformation inside a division algebra and rational specialization give a finite hitting list with polynomially controlled entries.
