\section*{Holomorphic Convexity of Universal Covers}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
Manuscript 1 (family 046, 2026-10-05): A smooth complex fourfold is claimed to be projective, to have large fundamental group, and to have a non-Stein universal cover lacking positive-dimensional compact analytic subvarieties; Manuscript 2 (family 046, 2026-09-23): OpenAI claims a smooth projective complex surface whose universal cover fails holomorphic convexity, giving a two-dimensional counterexample. Manuscript 3 (family 058, 2026-09-24): A normal projective universal cover is claimed to have a semialgebraic projective model exactly when it decomposes into a symmetric bounded-domain factor, a complex affine-space factor, and a simply connected normal projective factor; Manuscript 4 (family 058, 2026-09-24): With cocompact biholomorphisms, a connected semialgebraic bounded open set in a complex affine variety is claimed to be smooth and biholomorphically equivalent to a symmetric domain that is bounded. Manuscript 5 (family 067, 2026-09-25): Among smooth complex Fano varieties in dimension six, those with nef tangent are claimed to be rational homogeneous, with a universal-cover consequence when dimension minus maximal étale irregularity is at most six.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Manuscript 1 (family 046, 2026-10-05): An arrangement in an arithmetic ball quotient supplies a rank-three lattice-cover obstruction on a simple abelian surface. Compatible local covers and a path criterion show that its infinite cyclic image survives the global relations; the absence of compact analytic fibers then turns failure of Steinness into failure of holomorphic convexity. Manuscript 2 (family 046, 2026-09-23): The construction arranges a connected lift of rational curves with compact components but a noncompact incidence graph, so every holomorphic function is constant on the resulting infinite string. A multiplication cover and weighted Golod--Shafarevich estimates ensure that the relevant graph loop remains infinite after the imposed monodromy relations.

Manuscript 3 (family 058, 2026-09-24): Boundary windows produce compact projective fibers, and bounded plurisubharmonic parameter traces show that intersecting fibers coincide. Functions controlling moving chart boundaries globalize the quotient; inverse-chart compactness makes it a biholomorphism, and special-manifold abelianity controls the remaining deck action. Manuscript 4 (family 058, 2026-09-24): Holomorphic supports and analytic discs select a noncollapsing boundary model despite possible ambient singularities. Joint normal-offset limits and weighted dilation give a quadratic domain; convergence of both maps and inverses identifies the original domain with it, and the homogeneous-domain theorem supplies symmetry.

Manuscript 5 (family 067, 2026-09-25): Known contractions reduce the new case to Picard number one and pseudoindices four or five. The universal minimal-curve family has two smooth fibrations whose projection identities give linear intersection constraints; characteristic-number and integrality comparisons rule out the remaining models and identify the homogeneous possibilities.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
Manuscript 1 (family 046, 2026-10-05): The projective fourfold’s universal cover is non-Stein and has no positive-dimensional compact analytic subvariety; holomorphic convexity would then have a zero-dimensional Remmert reduction fiber and force Steinness, so this is a counterexample.

Manuscript 2 (family 046, 2026-09-23): The main theorem constructs a smooth projective complex surface with a noncompact holomorphic hull in its universal cover, directly negating the catalogue holomorphic-convexity requirement.

Manuscript 3 (family 058, 2026-09-24): The Remmert-reduction corollary proves holomorphic convexity under a semialgebraic projective-open presentation of the universal cover; the catalogue requires no semialgebraicity.

Manuscript 4 (family 058, 2026-09-24): For a projective manifold whose universal cover meets the bounded semialgebraic-domain and compact-quotient hypotheses, symmetry makes that cover Stein and hence holomorphically convex; general projective universal covers need not satisfy these hypotheses.

Manuscript 5 (family 067, 2026-09-25): The nef-tangent uniformization corollary yields a compact homogeneous factor times affine space and hence holomorphic convexity in the stated dimension-at-most-six or bounded Albanese-fiber class; the catalogue allows arbitrary projective varieties.
