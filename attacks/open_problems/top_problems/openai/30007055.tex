\section*{Prescribed large-volume stability charges on threefolds}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
On a smooth projective threefold with trivial canonical bundle, the ordinary and Todd-corrected large-volume charges are claimed to define full numerical stability conditions uniformly in a real twist and ample-direction sector. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} Let $X$ be a smooth projective complex threefold, $H$ ample integral and $B_0\in N^1(X)_{\mathbb Q}$. With $\operatorname{ch}^B=e^{-B}\operatorname{ch}$, define $\mathcal B_{H,B}$ by tilting coherent sheaves at slope $H^2\operatorname{ch}_1^B/(H^3\operatorname{ch}_0)$, using positive versus nonpositive Harder--Narasimhan slopes. Put
\[
\nu_{a,b}(E)=\frac{H\operatorname{ch}_2^{B_0+bH}(E)-a^2H^3\operatorname{ch}_0(E)/2}{H^2\operatorname{ch}_1^{B_0+bH}(E)}.
\]
OpenAI claims constants $k\in\mathbb Q_{\ge0}$ and $R_*>0$, depending only on $(X,H,B_0)$, such that, simultaneously for all $a>0$ and $b\in\mathbb R$, every finite-slope $\nu_{a,b}$-semistable object of slope zero satisfies
\[
\operatorname{ch}_3^{B_0+bH}(E)\le(a^2/6+k)H^2\operatorname{ch}_1^{B_0+bH}(E),
\]
and the same inequality with $k=0$ holds whenever $a>R_*$, uniformly in real $b$.
If $K_X\simeq\mathcal O_X$, then for each $(B_*,H_*)\in N^1(X)_{\mathbb R}\times\operatorname{Amp}(X)$ it claims open neighborhoods $V$ of $B_*$ and $W$ of $H_*$, together with a single $T>0$, such that all $B\in V$, $H\in W$ and $t\ge T$ admit geometric numerical Bridgeland stability conditions supported on the full real numerical Grothendieck space, with exact charges
\[
Z_{B,tH}(E)=-\int_Xe^{-B-itH}\operatorname{ch}(E),\qquad
Z^{\mathrm{LV}}_{B,tH}(E)=-\int_Xe^{-B-itH}\operatorname{ch}(E)(1+c_2(X)/24).
\]
For the first charge the heart is the second tilt at slope $[tH\operatorname{ch}_2^B-t^3H^3\operatorname{ch}_0/6]/[t^2H^2\operatorname{ch}_1^B]$ with the same positive/nonpositive cuts. Geometric means point sheaves are stable of phase one.
