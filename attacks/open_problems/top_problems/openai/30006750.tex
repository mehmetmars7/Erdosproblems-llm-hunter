\section*{Residual Finiteness of Hyperbolic Groups}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a counterexample in the selected manuscript. A torsion-free hyperbolic group is claimed not residually finite and consequently not linear over any field.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Punctured-line blocks with few short cycles give triangular relations meeting a geometric filling criterion. Real and prime-field rank comparisons produce a persistent finite-quotient obstruction, while local factorization certificates and planar metric bounds establish hyperbolicity and exclude torsion.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
The torsion-free hyperbolic example has one fixed nonidentity element killed by every finite quotient, exactly refuting residual finiteness. Within these assumptions, this is a claimed negative answer to the stated question.
