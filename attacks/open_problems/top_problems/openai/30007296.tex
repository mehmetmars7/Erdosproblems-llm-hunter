\section*{Quadratic strong-operator paving for every MASA}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
Strong-operator paving over an arbitrary MASA is claimed with at most 500000000 \(\varepsilon^{-2}\) projections for each self-adjoint element, uniformly over representations and without separability or expectation assumptions. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} For $0<\varepsilon<1$, put $r_\varepsilon=\lceil400000000\varepsilon^{-2}\rceil+\lceil4/\varepsilon\rceil+1\le500000000\varepsilon^{-2}$. For every maximal abelian $A\subset M\subset B(H)$ and self-adjoint $x\in M$, OpenAI claims that for any finite $F\subset H$ and $\delta>0$ there are a unit partition $p_1,\ldots,p_{r_\varepsilon}\in A$, self-adjoint $a\in A$ with $\|a\|\le\|x\|$, and a projection $q\in M$ such that
\[
\|(1-q)\xi\|<\delta\ (\xi\in F),\qquad
\left\|q\left(\sum_ip_ixp_i-a\right)q\right\|\le\varepsilon\|x\|.
\]
The number is fixed before the inclusion and strong neighborhood, with no separability or conditional expectation premise.
