\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
\textit{A topological Einstein-energy gap}: A sufficiently small scale-invariant $L^2$ trace-free Ricci energy is claimed to restrict a simply connected closed nonnegatively curved four-manifold to the diffeomorphism types $S^4$, $\mathbb {CP}^2$, or $S^2\times S^2$; no extra geometric bounds are assumed.

\textit{Positive-Einstein four-manifold classification}: The paper claims that closed connected positively curved Einstein four-manifolds are, up to scaling, the round sphere, Fubini--Study projective plane, or round real projective space; the last possibility accommodates nonorientability.

\textit{Zero-plane Einstein rigidity}: The claim assumes a positive Einstein constant on a closed four-manifold; if sectional curvature is nonnegative and one plane is flat, the universal cover is the product of two round constant-curvature surfaces; the radii are fixed by the Einstein normalization.
