\section*{Finiteness of Finitely Presented Periodic Groups}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
The linked manuscripts present the following claims by OpenAI: An infinite ordinarily finitely presented periodic group and an infinite-dimensional finitely presented nil algebra over $\mathbb F_2$ are claimed to exist; The periodic group of the companion is claimed residually finite, with an infinite finite-index finitely presented subgroup whose element orders are finite powers of two and unbounded. Together they give a claimed negative answer in the matching scope.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Finite local rules encode a hierarchy and a faithful computation tableau in admissible words. The resulting graded algebra has nil behavior without global nilpotence; a Steinberg-type group construction then transports these finite relations into an infinite group while forcing each element to have finite order.

Phases of long graded words make finite-degree truncations detect group elements. Faithful Steinberg strips, cyclic-shift comparisons, and long-entry commutator calculations treat the central kernel as well, so residual finiteness survives passage to the periodic two-group subgroup.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
