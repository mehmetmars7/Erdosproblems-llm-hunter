\subsection{OpenAI's claim}

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} For any complex K3 surface $X$, any smooth Hermitian distance $d_X$, any $m\ge1$, any nonempty compact convex $K\subset\mathbb C^m$, neighborhood $U$ of $K$ and holomorphic $f:U\to X$, OpenAI claims
\[
\forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists F:\mathbb C^m\to X\text{ holomorphic}:\quad\sup_{z\in K}d_X(F(z),f(z))<\varepsilon.
\]
This convex approximation property characterizes the Oka condition. It includes nonprojective K3 surfaces. A corollary claims, for every $x\in X$ and $0\ne v\in T_xX$, a holomorphic immersion $g:\mathbb C\to X$ with $g(0)=x$, $g'(0)=v$ and ordinary-topology-dense image; for projective $X$ the image is also Zariski dense.
