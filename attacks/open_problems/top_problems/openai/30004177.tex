\section*{Birkhoff Rigidity Conjecture for Convex Billiards}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
Manuscript 1 (family 147, 2026-09-24): A merely continuous invariant foliation of the full grazing phase annulus of a smooth positively curved convex billiard is claimed to create an analytic caustic collar and hence an elliptical table. Manuscript 2 (family 147, 2026-09-24): A smooth positively curved convex billiard table is claimed to be an ellipse whenever its near-boundary region has a continuous full foliation by smooth convex caustics, with no transverse differentiability required.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} Let $\Omega\subset\mathbb R^2$ be bounded and strictly convex, with smooth regular simple closed boundary and positive curvature. In boundary arclength $s\in\mathbb R/L\mathbb Z$ and outgoing angle $\delta\in(0,\pi)$, let $T$ be the billiard reflection map on $M=(\mathbb R/L\mathbb Z)\times(0,\pi)$. Assume a $T$-invariant open annulus $U$ contains $(\mathbb R/L\mathbb Z)\times(0,\eta)$ for some $\eta>0$, and admits a homeomorphism $H:S^1\times(0,1)\to U$ for which each circle leaf is simple, closed, essential and individually $T$-invariant. OpenAI claims
\[
\Omega=\{x\in\mathbb R^2:(x-c)^TQ(x-c)<1\}
\]
for some center $c$ and symmetric positive-definite real matrix $Q$. The assumed phase-space foliation near grazing is essential; circles are included.

\paragraph{Mathematical claim 2.} Let $\Omega$ be a bounded strictly convex planar domain with a smooth regular simple closed boundary of positive curvature. Assume a relatively open collar $A\subset\overline\Omega$ of the boundary and a homeomorphism $H_0:S^1\times[0,1)\to A$ whose level zero is $\partial\Omega$ and whose positive levels are smooth closed convex caustics. A caustic means a curve to which tangency of a billiard segment is preserved by reflection $v\mapsto v-2(v\cdot n)n$, with outward unit normal $n$. OpenAI claims
\[
\Omega=\{x:(x-c)^TQ(x-c)<1\},\qquad Q=Q^T>0.
\]
The statement assumes a continuous collar of caustics and includes circles.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Manuscript 1 (family 147, 2026-09-24): Strict twist barriers and area recurrence rule out vertical leaf pairs and rational rotation plateaus, yielding unique periodic graph leaves. Stationary-mesh, Fourier, and buffered-division estimates then promote the continuous phase data to a physical analytic collar before the companion ellipse-rigidity theorem is applied.

Manuscript 2 (family 147, 2026-09-24): Periodic rational caustics give a Fourier action system whose near-identity derivative supports analytic Newton inversion. Values and parameter jets produce a joint glancing conjugacy; finite reflected-chain jet determination then controls complex continuation and rules out a first meromorphic wall, yielding global ellipse rigidity.
