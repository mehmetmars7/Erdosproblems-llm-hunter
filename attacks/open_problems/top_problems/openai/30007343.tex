\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} Fix \(d\ge3\), and take uniform simple labeled \(d\)-regular graphs, independent iid \(\omega_i\sim\operatorname{Unif}[-1,1]\), and \(H_{n,d,w}=A_{n,d}+w\operatorname{diag}(\omega_i)\). Let \(\nu_{d,w}\) be the expected root spectral measure of the same operator on the infinite \(d\)-regular tree. For each \(0<\kappa<2\sqrt{d-1}\), the source gives \(w_0(d,\kappa)>0\). At every fixed \(0<w<w_0\), \(\nu_{d,w}\) has a continuous positive density \(\rho_{d,w}\) near \(I=[-2\sqrt{d-1}+\kappa,2\sqrt{d-1}-\kappa]\). For \(E\in I\) and every nonnegative \(f\in C_c(\mathbb R)\),
\[
 \left|\mathbb E e^{-\int f\,d\Xi_{n,E}}-
 \mathbb E e^{-\int f\,d\Xi_n^{\mathrm{GOE}}}\right|\to0,
 \quad\Xi_{n,E}=\sum_i\delta_{n\rho_{d,w}(E)(\lambda_i-E)}.
\]
GOE uses variances \(1/n\) off diagonal and \(2/n\) on diagonal, unfolded at zero by \(n/\pi\). Convergence holds through \(n>d\) with \(nd\) even.
