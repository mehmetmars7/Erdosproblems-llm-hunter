\section*{The Dixmier Unitarisability Problem}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a mathematical result relevant to this question in the selected manuscript. Discrete-group amenability is claimed equivalent to unitarizability of all uniformly bounded complex Hilbert representations.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} A uniformly bounded representation \(\pi:G\to\operatorname{GL}(\mathcal K)\) is unitarizable when it is similar to a unitary representation. The source claims for every discrete group
\[
 G\text{ amenable}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad G\text{ unitarizable}.
\]
Its strengthened assertion says that for every nonamenable \(G\) and every \(\varepsilon>0\), there is a nonsimilar-to-unitary representation satisfying
\[
 \sup_{g\in G}\|\pi(g)\|\le1+\varepsilon.
\]
For countable \(G\), the witnessing complex Hilbert space may be separable; countability is not required for the equivalence.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Nonamenability supplies sparse assignments that are turned into weighted block operators. Their invariant-kernel obstruction rules out a similarity to unitaries for the constructed bounded representation, giving the converse to the established amenable implication.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
The source equates amenability and unitarizability for all discrete groups, hence proves the target implication. Its construction of nonunitarizable representations with arbitrarily small excess uniform bound strengthens the converse direction.
