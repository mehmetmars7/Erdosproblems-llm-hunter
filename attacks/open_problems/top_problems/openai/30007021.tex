\section*{The cohomological Hikita isomorphism for quivers}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
The equivariant cohomological Hikita correspondence is claimed for arbitrary finite quivers under the stated free stable-action hypothesis, as an algebra isomorphism preserving nilpotent structure. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} Take any finite oriented quiver, allowing loops and parallel arrows, vector spaces $V_i,W_i$, and
\[
G=\prod_i\operatorname{GL}(V_i),\qquad
N=\bigoplus_{a:i\to j}\operatorname{Hom}(V_i,V_j)\oplus\bigoplus_i\operatorname{Hom}(V_i,W_i).
\]
Let a flavor torus $F$ act on $N$ commuting with $G$. Choose a character $\nu$ for which the cotangent moment-map semistable locus at zero equals the stable locus and $G$ acts freely, and set $X=\mu^{-1}(0)^{\nu\text{-stable}}/G$. Put $\mathcal O=\mathbb C[[t]]$, $\mathcal K=\mathbb C((t))$ and $\mathcal R=\{[g,s]\in G(\mathcal K)\times_{G(\mathcal O)}N(\mathcal O):gs\in N(\mathcal O)\}$. Let $A_F=H_*^{G(\mathcal O)\times F}(\mathcal R;\mathbb C)$ be its commutative BFN convolution algebra, without loop-rotation parameter. The character $\nu$ gives a topological-torus grading $A_F=\bigoplus_m(A_F)_m$.
OpenAI claims that the Kirwan and Coulomb coefficient maps from $R=H^*_{G\times F}(\mathrm{pt};\mathbb C)$ are surjective with identical kernels, giving
\[
H_F^*(X;\mathbb C)\simeq A_F/\langle(A_F)_m:m\ne0\rangle
\]
as graded $R$-algebras, with cohomological versus BFN homological degree and linear coefficient parameters of degree two. The quotient is scheme-theoretic: any nilpotents are retained, with no reduction applied; empty stable locus gives zero rings.
