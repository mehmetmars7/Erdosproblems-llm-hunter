\section*{Monge Minimizers for Multimarginal Coulomb Transport}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
\textit{Three-marginal Monge nonattainment}: The manuscript claims smooth compactly supported identical marginals in three dimensions for which no preserving pair of maps attains the Coulomb transport minimum. The Monge and relaxed infima nevertheless agree, and inverse-power analogues in other dimensions are also announced.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
\textit{Three-marginal Monge nonattainment}: Two local families of triples share a supporting potential near a central component, while strict cost gaps exclude all other center patterns. Marginal mass imbalance then prevents a deterministic preserving map from selecting the required split between the two families. Approximate preserving maps approach the optimum, proving nonattainment without a gap between the infima.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
