\subsection{Outline of the argument}

Pure-state compression produces norm-visible matrix cones, while localized nuclear approximations produce trace-visible ones. Manuscript 2 (family 291, 2026-09-23): Localized order-zero approximations control ranks even when traces are infinite on some ideals. A supporting map and comparison yield two-color approximations that survive stabilization and hereditary passage. Conversely, Gaussian sign tensors and pure-state excision construct two full orthogonal central elements from the absence of characters, supplying the absorption criterion.

Manuscript 3 (family 302, 2026-09-25): Finite-support comparison witnesses are converted to bundle embeddings, which feed the companion's boundary-preserving compression theorem. Packet reconstruction translates that compression into a smaller cover without introducing absent colors. Applying the construction on aligned orbit blocks contradicts any larger cover-growth rate, providing the missing lower bound; established upper bounds and regularity results finish the equality and zero-dimensional consequences.
