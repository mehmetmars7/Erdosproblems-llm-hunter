\section*{Logarithmic Kodaira additivity under stratum smoothness}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
For reduced normal-crossing projective pairs that are smooth on every boundary stratum over the open base, OpenAI claims logarithmic Kodaira additivity, including the cases with negative-infinite dimension. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} Take a connected-fiber surjective morphism $f:X\to Y$ of smooth connected projective complex varieties, reduced simple-normal-crossing divisors $E,D$ with $\operatorname{Supp}(f^*D)\subseteq\operatorname{Supp}E$, and $V=Y\setminus D$. Require $X$ and each nonempty boundary intersection stratum of $E$, restricted over $V$, to be smooth over $V$. For a very general fiber $F$ and $E_F=E|_F$, OpenAI claims
\[
\kappa(X,K_X+E)=\kappa(Y,K_Y+D)+\kappa(F,K_F+E_F).
\]
The independent main theorem proves the upper inequality; equality uses the companion logarithmic subadditivity result. The zero divisors and $-\infty$ Kodaira dimensions are included. Smoothness is required over the original open base, with no assertion of smoothness above $D$.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Manuscript 1 (family 033, 2026-09-26): Iterated logarithmic cotangent and Higgs maps turn a selected pluriform into base sections with controlled coefficients. Period-image positivity and a canonical bundle formula supply the reverse inequality; combining it with subadditivity yields equality under the stated stratum-smoothness assumptions.
