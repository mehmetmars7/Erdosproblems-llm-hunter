\section*{Every irreducible occurs in the staircase tensor square}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. Every irreducible constituent is claimed to occur in the tensor square of the staircase representation of the appropriate symmetric group.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Row and column alternation on a triangular set of tensor positions gives one prescribed cyclic vector. Nonzero pairings show that its orbit already supports all required constituents; band decompositions and induction maintain the generator information rather than merely proving positivity somewhere in an ambient tensor product.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
The Saxl theorem states g(rho\_m,rho\_m,lambda)>0 for every m>=1 and every partition lambda of N\_m. This exactly matches the complete question. lean/docs/205.md covers staircase positivity but not the stronger cyclic-orbit-span theorem. Within these assumptions, this is a claimed affirmative answer to the stated question.
