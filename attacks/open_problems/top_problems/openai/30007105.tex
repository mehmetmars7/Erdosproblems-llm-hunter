\section*{Talagrand discrete convexity conjecture}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
\textit{Talagrand discrete convexity}: The paper claims a universal number of family members whose unions leave only a small containment-cover obstruction, provided the original Bernoulli measure is sufficiently close to one. The cover cost is bounded at the original density.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} Let $k=2^{75}$ and $\mu_p$ be product Bernoulli measure on $2^{[N]}$. For every $N\ge1$, $0<p<1$, and arbitrary family $\mathcal D$, define $E_k(\mathcal D)=\{S:S\nsubseteq D_1\cup\cdots\cup D_k\text{ for every }D_1,\ldots,D_k\in\mathcal D\}$. Repetitions among the $D_i$ are allowed. The theorem claims \[\mu_p(\mathcal D)\ge1-1/k\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\exists\mathcal G:\ E_k(\mathcal D)\subseteq\{S:\exists I\in\mathcal G,\ I\subseteq S\},\quad\sum_{I\in\mathcal G}p^{|I|}\le1/2.\] No increasingness or disjointness hypothesis is imposed on $\mathcal D$.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
\textit{Talagrand discrete convexity}: A biased orthogonal expansion produces nonnegative subset weights with a global budget. A signed many-row identity turns failure of a union cover into a large higher-power sum of those weights. Large weights give cheap generators directly, while weighted-degree selection and random unions cover the remaining classes; a final density transfer gives the universal union count.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
The explicit $k=2^{75}$ theorem supplies the requested containment cover with budget one half for every product-measure parameter and arbitrary family, allowing repeated tuple members. Relabelling handles all finite nonempty ground sets; for the empty ground set the exceptional family is empty.
