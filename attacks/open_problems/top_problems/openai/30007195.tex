\section*{Virtual compact specialness of hyperbolic one-relator groups}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. Every hyperbolic one-relator group is claimed virtually compact special and, by the cited theorem, virtually free-by-cyclic with possibly infinitely generated free kernel.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} For a finite set \(X\) and relator \(r\in F(X)\), suppose
\[
 G=F(X)/\langle\!\langle r\rangle\!\rangle
\]
is word-hyperbolic. The claim supplies a finite-index subgroup \(H\le G\), a finite nonpositively curved cube complex \(C\) with \(\pi_1C\cong H\), and a cubical local isometry from \(C\) to a right-angled Artin Salvetti complex. This is virtual compact specialness. The second theorem applies to any injective endomorphism \(\phi\) of a finitely generated free group \(F\):
\[
 F*_{\phi}=\langle F,t\mid t^{-1}ft=\phi(f),\ f\in F\rangle
 \text{ word-hyperbolic}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad F*_{\phi}\text{ virtually compact special}.
\]
Word-hyperbolicity is assumed in both assertions.
