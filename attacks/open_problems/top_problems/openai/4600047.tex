\section*{Surjunctive groups}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
The linked manuscripts present the following claims by OpenAI: A finite characteristic-two field and a finitely presented group are claimed to produce a scalar one-sided inverse pair; the resulting shift is injective without being onto and the group is nonsofic; For a particular odd prime $p$, the authors claim a one-sided inverse defect over a field with $p^4$ elements, together with a nonsurjective injective shift; Another construction claims a torsion-free, finitely presented $G$ for which $\mathbb F_2[G]$ contains $a,b$ satisfying $ab=1$ and $ba\ne1$. Together they provide the following support within the scopes described below.

\subsection{Outline of the argument}
Incidence data and polynomial pairings first give a defective inverse pair of matrices. A geometric group-embedding construction preserves the defect, and character packing realizes the matrix algebra inside a larger group algebra; dual convolution gives the cellular-automaton consequence.

The construction uses translation representations of finite groups: a high-degree subspace is killed on one side and a weighted pairing supplies the other annihilation. Finite group identifications and a quadratic field tower package these local relations into the scalar inverse pair; the claim concerns the specified prime rather than all odd characteristics.

An unequal-side parity pattern supplies the one-sided inverse relation in labeled graphs. Weighted counts handle repeated edge traversals, and a separator reduces arbitrarily large planar arrangements to bounded path collections; cone attachment and root separation then preserve the inverse defect while proving torsion-freeness.

\subsection{Scope relative to this problem}
Theorem 1.1 and its finite-field convolution transfer supply a countable nonsurjunctive group and settle the universal subquestion, but do not characterize all countable surjunctive groups. This entry records partial support; complete coverage of the question is not established by this catalogue.
