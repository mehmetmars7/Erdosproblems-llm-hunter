\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Result 1.} Let a smooth connected orientable three-dimensional exterior be complete including its nonempty compact boundary $S$, with one hyperboloidal end. Use $16\pi\mu=R_g+(\operatorname{tr}_gK)^2-|K|^2$, $8\pi J=\operatorname{div}_g(K-(\operatorname{tr}_gK)g)$ and $\mu\ge|J|$. Put $h=(1+r^2)^{-1}dr^2+r^2\sigma$, $g=h+a$, $K=h+b$. The precise CKS asymptotics are
\[
 a_{rr}=r^{-5}m^r+O_3(r^{-6}),\quad a_{rA}=O_3(r^{-3}),\quad
 a_{AB}=r^{-1}m^g_{AB}+O_3(r^{-2}),
\]
\[
 b_{rr}=O_2(r^{-5}),\quad b_{rA}=O_2(r^{-3}),\quad
 b_{AB}=r^{-1}m^K_{AB}+O_2(r^{-2}),
\]
with smooth angular leading fields and differentiated component bounds using angular derivatives and $r\partial_r$. For the sphere inclusion $n:\mathbb S^2\to\mathbb R^3$, define
\[
 M=\operatorname{tr}_\sigma(m^g+2m^K)+2m^r,\quad
 (E_B,P_B)=\frac1{16\pi}\int_{\mathbb S^2}(1,n)M\,dA_\sigma,\quad E_B>|P_B|.
\]
These are the initial-data CKS charges, without assuming a spacetime development. Choose a connected smooth end-side region $D\subset\Omega$, closed in $\Omega$, containing the distant end and having its manifold interior in $\operatorname{int}\Omega$. A full cut consists of all of its intrinsic boundary $\Gamma=\partial D$; require this boundary to be compact, smoothly embedded and two-sided. All components and portions coinciding with $S=\partial\Omega$ count. Put $a_g(S)=\inf_\Gamma\operatorname{Area}_g(\Gamma)$. There is a balanced end chart, with $P_B=0$, in which for every sufficiently large $R$ there are complete DEC-preserving asymptotically flat replacements $(G_R,k_R)$ agreeing with $(g,K)$ on the compact side of $r=R$, satisfying $G_R\ge(1-\varepsilon_R)g$, $\varepsilon_R\to0$, and having $P_R=0$, $E_R=m_B+\eta_R$, $0\le\eta_R\le2R^{-1/2}$, where $m_B=\sqrt{E_B^2-|P_B|^2}$. They preserve every cut area up to $1-\varepsilon_R$. If additionally $H+\operatorname{tr}_SK\le0$ and $a_g(S)>0$, the corollary gives
\[
 m_B\ge\sqrt{a_g(S)/(16\pi)}.
\]
The boundary may be disconnected. For each $m>0$, horizon-regular Schwarzschild hyperboloidal data attain $m_B=m$, $a_g(S)=16\pi m^2$.
