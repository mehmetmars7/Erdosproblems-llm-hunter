\section*{Conditional good models for strongly Q-factorial Kähler fourfolds}

\subsection{OpenAI's claim}
Under explicit orbifold-subadditivity, fourfold-program, and abundance assumptions, good minimal models are claimed for the specified compact Kähler klt fourfold pairs. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

\subsection{Mathematical statement of the claimed result}
\paragraph{Mathematical claim 1.} This is explicitly conditional on three global premises: (1) orbifold Iitaka subadditivity in all dimensions for smooth compact manifolds in Fujiki class $\mathcal C$, namely $\kappa(X,K_X+\Delta)\ge\kappa(F,K_F+\Delta|_F)+\kappa(f\mid\Delta)$ for connected-fiber holomorphic fibrations with rational simple-normal-crossing boundary; $\kappa(f\mid\Delta)$ is the Kodaira dimension of the neat orbifold base, using infimum fiber multiplicities; (2) existence and termination of every negative-extremal-ray minimal model program for pseudo-effective rational klt adjoints on globally strongly rational-factorial compact Kähler fourfolds, with projective divisorial contractions or log-canonical-positive flips and a nef endpoint; (3) semiampleness of a nef rational klt fourfold adjoint already having nonnegative Iitaka dimension.
Given these premises, take a normal connected compact Kähler fourfold $X$ with effective rational $B$, klt pair $(X,B)$, and rational Cartier analytically pseudo-effective $D=K_X+B$. Assume every global rank-one coherent reflexive sheaf has an invertible positive reflexive power. OpenAI claims a bimeromorphic contraction $\phi:X\dashrightarrow Y$ to a fourfold of the same category such that, writing $B_Y=\phi_*B$,
\[
D_Y=K_Y+B_Y\text{ is nef and semiample},\qquad
 a(E;Y,B_Y)\ge a(E;X,B)
\]
for every prime divisor over the models, strictly for every prime on $X$ contracted by $\phi$. The map extracts no divisor. The three premises are part of the theorem, rather than conclusions proved without assumptions.
