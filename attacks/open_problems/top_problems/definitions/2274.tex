\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $f(n,m)$ be minimal such that in $(m,m+f(n,m))$ there exist distinct integers $a_1,\ldots,a_n$ such that $k\mid a_k$ for all $1\leq k\leq n$. Prove that $ \max_m f(n,m) \leq n^{1+o(1)} $ and that $ \max_m (f(n,m)-f(n,n))\to \infty. $ \sref{2274}{1}
