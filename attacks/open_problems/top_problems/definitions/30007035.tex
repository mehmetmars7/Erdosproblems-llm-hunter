\section*{Hodge classes on diagonal complete-intersection powers}\label{30007035}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Fix $n\geq 2$, $d\geq 0$ and $c\in {0,1,2}$, set $m=d+c+1$, and let A be a rank-c matrix all of whose maximal coordinate minors are nonzero. In $P^{m-1}$, define $X_A$ by $\Sigma _i$ $a_{\alpha i}x_i^n=0$ for $1\leq \alpha \leq c$. Outside a countable union of proper algebraic subsets of this smooth labelled parameter space, must every rational Hodge class on every $X_A^u$, $u\geq 1$, be algebraic? For $c\leq 1$ or $d=0$, the assertion is required at every smooth parameter.
