\section{Open Problems in Integrable Systems — Nijenhuis operators: singular points and global properties}\label{2100512}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2100512 · Dynamical Systems}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


For $\sigma=(\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_n)$, define the companion matrix $C(\sigma)$ by
\[ C_{i1}=-\sigma_i\quad(1\leq i\leq n),\qquad C_{i,i+1}=1\quad(1\leq i<n), \]
with all remaining entries zero. For a map $\Phi=(\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_n)$ in coordinates $x_1,\ldots,x_n$, its Jacobian is $J_{ij}=\partial\sigma_i/\partial x_j$.\par Let $U$ be a neighborhood of $0$ in $\mathbb R^n$ and $\Phi:U\to\mathbb R^n$ be smooth, with $\operatorname{rank}J(0)<n$ and $\det J\neq0$ almost everywhere on $U$. On the regular set define
\[ L(x)=J(x)^{-1}C(\sigma(x))J(x). \]
This is the reconstruction of the Nijenhuis field from characteristic coefficients in equation (5.5).
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Find necessary and sufficient conditions on $\Phi$ for every entry of $L$, initially defined where $\det J\neq0$, to extend to a smooth function on all of $U$. Describe all such maps, including their behavior at the singular point $0$. \sref{2100512}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Which generically regular coefficient maps yield a Nijenhuis tensor whose companion-matrix reconstruction stays smooth across all singular points?
