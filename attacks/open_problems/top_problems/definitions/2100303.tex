\section{Open Problems in Integrable Systems — Polynomial in momenta integrals in higher dimensions.}\label{2100303}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2100303 · Dynamical Systems}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


For a smooth Riemannian metric $g$ on $M$, a natural Hamiltonian on the canonical symplectic cotangent bundle $T^*M$ is \[H(x,p)=\tfrac12\sum_{i,j}g^{ij}(x)p_ip_j+U(x),\] with smooth potential $U$ and inverse metric coefficients $g^{ij}$. A polynomial integral of degree $d$ is a smooth function $F(x,p)$ polynomial of degree $d$ in the cotangent variables $p$, satisfying $\{H,F\}=0$. Following the source, integrals which are functions of $H$ alone are trivial. A geodesic flow is the case $U=0$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Construct and describe natural Hamiltonian systems on manifolds of dimension $n>2$ that have $n$ integrals with linearly independent differentials almost everywhere, pairwise Poisson commuting $F_1=H,F_2,\ldots,F_n$, each polynomial in momenta. The source asks for further examples, particularly systems on closed manifolds or arising in mathematical physics. \sref{2100303}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Construct further higher-dimensional natural Hamiltonian systems with a full commuting family of polynomial integrals.
