\section*{Kirby Problem 2.7}\label{2755}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2755 · KP-2.7 · Topology · Kirby's Problems in Low-Dimensional Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For $n\geq2$, the braid group has presentation
\[
B_n=\left\langle\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_{n-1}\ \middle|\
\begin{array}{ll}
\sigma_i\sigma_j=\sigma_j\sigma_i&(|i-j|\geq2),\\
\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_i=\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1}&(1\leq i<n-1)
\end{array}\right\rangle .
\]
Geometrically its elements are isotopy classes of $n$ strands in a disk times
an interval, each transverse to the interval direction; endpoints may be
permuted. The operation stacks braids. Put $B_1=\{1\}$.

Classify all homomorphisms $\phi:B_n\to B_m$ for arbitrary positive integers
$n,m$. Such a homomorphism is determined by images $b_i=\phi(\sigma_i)$ in
$B_m$ satisfying the displayed braid and commuting relations. The target is a
structural classification of these tuples, or equivalently of all such maps,
rather than merely restating those relations.

A useful classification may be given up to simultaneous conjugation of the
images, provided the equivalence is stated and all maps can be recovered from
the described families. It must include maps with cyclic image, noninjective
maps, and injective maps; no restriction comparing $m$ and $n$ is imposed.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Describe every homomorphism between two braid groups, for all numbers of source and target strands.
