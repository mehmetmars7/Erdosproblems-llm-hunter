\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Fix a prime $p$, an algebraically closed field $k$ of characteristic $p$, and a finite $p$-group $P$. Among all finite groups $G$, are there only finitely many Morita-equivalence classes of blocks of $kG$ with defect group isomorphic to $P$?
