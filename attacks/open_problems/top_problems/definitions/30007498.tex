\section*{Parity witnesses for Weisfeiler–Leman equivalence}\label{30007498}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For fixed $k\geq 4$, let $t=k+1$ under joint WL refinement or $t=k$ under separate refinement. Given finite domains $D_i$ and pairwise label maps $\lambda ^i_ij:D_i\to L_ij$, can one explicitly produce two equal-order uncolored simple graphs that are k-WL-equivalent exactly when no choice $d_i\in D_i$ satisfies $\lambda ^i_ij(d_i)=\lambda ^j_ij(d_j)$ for all $i<j$, with common graph order at most $A[t\cdot 2^{t-2+3binom(t-1,2)}+12binom(t,3)]$ when all domain and label cardinalities are $\leq A$?
