\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Stanley–Stembridge conjecture: if $G=G(m)$ is the incomparability graph associated with a Hessenberg function $m$, then its chromatic symmetric function $X_G(x)$ is a nonnegative sum of elementary symmetric functions.
