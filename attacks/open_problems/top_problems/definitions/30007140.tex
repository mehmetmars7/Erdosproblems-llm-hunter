\section*{Isoperimetric profile of the cubic flat three-torus}\label{30007140}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
On $\mathbb R^3/\mathbb Z^3$, let $V\in(0,1)$ be the prescribed volume and put $v=\min(V,1-V)$. Is the least perimeter
\[\min\bigl\{(36\pi)^{1/3}v^{2/3},\;2\sqrt{\pi v},\;2\bigr\}?\]
Are all minimizers, modulo isometry and null sets, balls for $v\leq4\pi/81$, round tubes along shortest geodesics for $4\pi/81\leq v\leq1/\pi$, coordinate slabs for $v\geq1/\pi$, and their complements for $V>1/2$, with exactly both adjacent types at transition volumes?
