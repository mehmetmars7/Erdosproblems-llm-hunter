\section*{Weak mixing of every triangular billiard with an irrational angle}\label{30007164}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every Euclidean triangle having at least one angle whose ratio to pi is irrational, is its billiard flow weakly mixing for normalized Liouville measure, after deleting trajectories that hit a vertex?
