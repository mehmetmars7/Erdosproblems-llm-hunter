\section*{Capacitated packing of k-arborescences}\label{3000009}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 3000009 · AMR-029-0009 · Combinatorics · AMR Open Problem Lists}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $D=(V,A)$ be a finite loopless digraph, with specified root $r$ and
positive integers $k,\ell$. Parallel arcs, when present, are distinct
members of $A$. A spanning out-arborescence rooted at $r$ is a directed
spanning tree in which $r$ has indegree zero, each other vertex has
indegree one, and every vertex is reachable from $r$.

Give each arc an integer capacity $c(a)$ with $0\le c(a)\le\ell$.
A capacity-respecting packing of arborescences is a list in which any
fixed arc $a$ is used at most $c(a)$ times. Suppose there is such a
packing of $k\ell$ spanning out-arborescences rooted at $r$.

Must there exist $\ell$ subgraphs $F_1,\ldots,F_\ell$, each itself the
union of $k$ pairwise arc-disjoint spanning out-arborescences rooted at
$r$, such that
\[
                         |\{i:a\in F_i\}|\le c(a)
                                     \quad(a\in A)?
\]
The original packing need not merely be regrouped; a new packing may
be chosen. The extra demand is that no arc occur twice inside a single
$k$-arborescence, even when its capacity permits repeated use across
different subgraphs. Replacing an arc by several indistinguishable copies
therefore does not remove this constraint. All arborescences span the
same vertex set and have the same prescribed root.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can a capacitated packing of $k\ell$ rooted spanning trees be
reorganized into $\ell$ groups of $k$ internally arc-disjoint rooted trees?
