\section*{Logarithmic total-variation mixing of the physical Thorp shuffle}\label{30007400}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For the physical Thorp shuffle on $n=2^d$ cards from a deterministic deck, is the worst-start mixing time at total-variation distance 1/4 Theta(d)? Require a universal linear-in-d upper bound, uniformly over starts, together with the support lower bound $||q_d^{*t}-Uniform||_TV\geq 1-2^{tn/2}/n!$ for every integer $t\geq 0$.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For the physical Thorp shuffle on $n=2^d$ cards from a deterministic deck, is the worst-start mixing time at total-variation distance 1/4 Theta(d)? Require a universal linear-in-d upper bound, uniformly over starts, together with the support lower bound $||q_d^{*t}-Uniform||_TV\geq 1-2^{tn/2}/n!$ for every integer $t\geq 0$.
