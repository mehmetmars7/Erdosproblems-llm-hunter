\section{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 4.4}\label{2304004}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2304004 · Analysis}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


For a nonzero polynomial $P(z)=\sum_{j\ge0}a_jz^j\in\mathbb C[z]$, let
\[\tau(P)=\#\{j:a_j\ne0\}\]
be its number of nonzero terms. Cancellation in the coefficients of $P^2$ is allowed. A term-count threshold is a function $f:\mathbb N\to\mathbb N$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Does there exist such an $f$ with
\[\tau(P)\ge f(k)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\tau(P^2)\ge k
\]
for every $k\ge1$ and every nonzero complex polynomial $P$? If it exists, determine bounds for the least possible thresholds. \sref{2304004}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can a bound on the number of terms in a square force a bound on the number of terms in the original polynomial, uniformly over all coefficients and exponents?
