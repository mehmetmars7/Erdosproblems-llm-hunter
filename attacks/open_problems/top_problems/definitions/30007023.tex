\section*{Kähler log abundance assuming logarithmic Iitaka}\label{30007023}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Assume that for every connected-fiber surjective morphism $f:U\to V$ of smooth connected projective complex varieties with reduced effective SNC $D_U,D_V$ and $Supp(f*D_V)\subset Supp(D_U)$, the very general fiber F with $D_F=D_U|F$ satisfies $\kappa (U,K_U+D_U)\geq \kappa (V,K_V+D_V)+\kappa (F,K_F+D_F)$, taking $-\infty $ as absorbing. For every normal irreducible compact Kähler space X with an effective rational lc boundary $\Delta $ and Q-Cartier analytically nef $K_X+\Delta $, must some positive Cartier multiple of the actual adjoint line be globally generated?
