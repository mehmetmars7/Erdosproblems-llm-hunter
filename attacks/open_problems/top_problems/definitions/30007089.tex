\section*{Graph decomposition at the integral expectation threshold}\label{30007089}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Are there universal constants k and L such that every graph H on at most n vertices has an edge partition into k fixed pieces $H_i$ whose random-graph containment thresholds $p_c(H_i;n)$ are at most L times the integral expectation threshold q(H;n)?
