\section*{Quasilinear robust PCP for PPAD}\label{30007479}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Are there fixed constants $\varepsilon ,\delta >0$ and a deterministic polynomial-time reduction taking every binary End-of-Line instance of length N to an $\varepsilon$-approximate generalized circuit of full encoding length O(N(log $N)^b)$, for one fixed b, such that every assignment satisfying all but a $\delta $ fraction of its gates has polynomial encoding and can be decoded in polynomial time to an End-of-Line solution?
