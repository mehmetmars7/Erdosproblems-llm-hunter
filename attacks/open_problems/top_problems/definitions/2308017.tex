\section{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 8.17}\label{2308017}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2308017 · Analysis}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


For $n\ge1$, let $\Omega=\mathbb B^n=\{z\in\mathbb C^n:\sum_{j=1}^n|z_j|^2<1\}$ or $\Omega=\mathbb D^n=\{z:\max_j|z_j|<1\}$. Write $H^{\infty}(\Omega)$ for the commutative unital ring of bounded holomorphic functions. A finitely generated ideal has the form \[I(f_1,\ldots,f_m)=\left\{\sum_{j=1}^mf_jh_j:h_j\in H^{\infty}(\Omega)\right\}.\]
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every such $\Omega$, is the intersection of any two finitely generated ideals of $H^{\infty}(\Omega)$ itself finitely generated? \sref{2308017}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Are intersections of finitely generated ideals finitely generated in the bounded-holomorphic-function rings of balls and polydisks?
