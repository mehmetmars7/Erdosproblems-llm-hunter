\section*{Integral-point density on curve character varieties}\label{30007257}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $X$ be a smooth connected complex algebraic curve and let $r\ge1$. Write $M_r(X)$ for the character variety of semisimple representations $\pi_1(X)\to\mathrm{SL}_r(\mathbb C)$, with its integral model obtained from a finite presentation of $\pi_1(X)$ and conjugation invariants. Representations have determinant one. If $\overline X\setminus X=\{x_1,\ldots,x_s\}$, choose meridians and quasi-unipotent conjugacy classes $C_i\subset\mathrm{SL}_r(\mathbb C)$; all their eigenvalues are roots of unity. Let $Y\subset M_r(X)_{\mathbb C}$ be the reduced locus where those meridians have the specified conjugacy classes, allowing nonsemisimple boundary matrices.

For a number field $K\subset\mathbb C$ containing the boundary eigenvalues, does there exist one finite extension $L/K$ for which
\[
 M_r(X)(\mathcal O_L)\cap Y(\mathbb C)
\]
is Zariski dense in $Y$, and hence in every irreducible component? Integrality means extension to the ambient character variety over the full ring of integers $\mathcal O_L$; it does not require membership in a strict locally closed integral boundary model at every finite prime. Ask also for the version fixing only the boundary characteristic polynomials with roots of unity, and for density in the entire character variety without boundary conditions. In the latter version take $K=\mathbb Q$. The same extension must work throughout the selected locus and all its components.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Are integral characters of determinant-one local systems Zariski dense in every component of a curve character variety after one finite number-field extension, also when quasi-unipotent boundary data are fixed?
