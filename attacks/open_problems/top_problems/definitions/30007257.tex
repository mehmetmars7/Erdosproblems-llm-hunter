\section*{Integral-point density on curve character varieties}\label{30007257}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every smooth connected complex algebraic curve X and every rank $r>=1$, are integral points over the ring of integers of some number field Zariski dense in every component of its rank-r character variety? The same question includes fixing boundary monodromy conjugacy classes that are quasi-unipotent, or fixing their characteristic polynomials with all roots roots of unity.
