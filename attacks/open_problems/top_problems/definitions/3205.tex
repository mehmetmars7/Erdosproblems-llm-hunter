\section{Strong edge colouring conjecture}\label{3205}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 3205 · Graph Theory}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


In a finite simple graph $G$, an induced matching is a set of edges with disjoint endpoints and no edge joining endpoints of distinct members. A strong edge coloring partitions $E(G)$ into induced matchings. Let $s\chi'(G)$ be the smallest number of color classes and $\Delta=\Delta(G)$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Must every $G$ satisfy \[s\chi'(G)\le\begin{cases}5\Delta^2/4,&\Delta\text{ even},\\(5\Delta^2-2\Delta+1)/4,&\Delta\text{ odd}?\end{cases}\] The source also asks the stronger bipartite bound: if $G$ has bipartition $(A_1,A_2)$ with degrees at most $\Delta_i$ on $A_i$, is $s\chi'(G)\le\Delta_1\Delta_2$? In particular this would give $s\chi'(G)\le\Delta(G)^2$ for every bipartite graph. \sref{3205}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Do strong edge colorings satisfy the sharp piecewise quadratic bound, and the stronger product-of-degrees bound for bipartite graphs?
