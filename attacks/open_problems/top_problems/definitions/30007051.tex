\section*{The ordinary-double-point normalized-volume gap}\label{30007051}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For any singular point x on a normal complex algebraic n-fold, $n\geq 2$, with zero boundary, Q-Cartier $K_X$ and klt singularities near x, must the normalized volume be at most $2(n-1)^n$, attaining that value exactly for the analytic germ of $\Sigma _{i=0}^n$ $z_i^{2}=0$?
