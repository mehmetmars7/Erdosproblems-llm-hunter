\section*{Makienko conjecture}\label{3700005}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 3700005 · AMR-036-0005 · Dynamical Systems · AMR Open Problem Lists}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $f:\widehat{\mathbb C}\to\widehat{\mathbb C}$ be a rational
map of degree at least two, and let $f^m$ denote its $m$-fold
iterate. Its Fatou set $F(f)$ is the open set on which the
family $\{f^m:m\geq0\}$ is normal in the spherical metric.
Its Julia set is $J(f)=\widehat{\mathbb C}\setminus F(f)$.
A Fatou component is a connected component of $F(f)$.

Suppose that some Fatou component $D$ has the entire Julia
set as its boundary:
\[
                    \partial D=J(f).
\]
Must $D$ be completely invariant under the second iterate,
in the precise sense that
\[
 (f^2)^{-1}(D)=D?
\]
The preimage is the full set of points of the sphere whose
second forward iterate belongs to $D$, not a chosen inverse
branch. The assertion therefore requires both that points
of $D$ remain in $D$ after two steps and that no point
outside $D$ enters it after two steps.

No initial periodicity or simple connectivity assumption is
made on $D$, and no local connectedness assumption is made
on $J(f)$. The use of the second iterate permits a two-cycle
of Fatou components. The question is whether the strong
boundary equality alone forces this complete invariance for
every rational map of every degree. A counterexample must
have exactly the stated boundary equality, rather than only
a boundary containing a substantial subset of the Julia set.
\subsection{Short English statement}
If one Fatou component has the whole Julia set as its boundary, must its full two-step preimage equal itself?
