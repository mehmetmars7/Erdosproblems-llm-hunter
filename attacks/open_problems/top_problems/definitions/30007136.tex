\section*{Bounded scalar curvature extends closed four-dimensional Ricci flow}\label{30007136}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let g(t), $0\leq t<T<\infty $, be a smooth Ricci flow from a smooth metric on a closed connected smooth four-manifold. If $sup_{M×[0,T})|Scal_g(t)|$ is finite, must g(t) extend as a smooth Ricci flow on the same manifold to an interval $[0,T+\varepsilon )$ for some $\varepsilon >0$?
