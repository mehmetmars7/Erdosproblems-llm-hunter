\section*{Numerical semiampleness of Kähler adjoints}\label{30007040}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let X be smooth connected compact Kähler, B effective rational SNC with coefficients below one, and M a nef rational holomorphic line. If $K_X+B$ is pseudo-effective and $D=K_X+B+M$ is nef, must some semiample rational holomorphic line L have $c_{1}(L)=c_{1}(D)$ in real Bott–Chern cohomology? Actual semiampleness of D is not required.
