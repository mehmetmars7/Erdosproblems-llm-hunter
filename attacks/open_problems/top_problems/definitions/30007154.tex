\section*{Euclidean sharp filling bounds in proper CAT(0) spaces}\label{30007154}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every proper CAT(0) space X and integer $n\geq 2$, does every compactly supported Ambrosio–Kirchheim integral n-current T with $\partial T=0$ bound a compactly supported integral (n+1)-current S satisfying $M(S)\leq [(n+1)^((n+1)/n)\omega _{n+1}^{1/n}]^{-1}$ $M(T)^((n+1)/n)$, where $\omega _j$ is unit Euclidean j-ball volume?
