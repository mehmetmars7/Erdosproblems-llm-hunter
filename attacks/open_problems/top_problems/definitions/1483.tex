\section{Novikov Conjecture}\label{1483}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 1483 · Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let \(G\) be a discrete group and \(BG\) a classifying space:
it has fundamental group \(G\) and contractible universal cover.
Let \(M\) be a closed oriented smooth \(n\)-manifold, with fundamental
class \([M]\in H_n(M;\mathbb Q)\). Its total Hirzebruch class
\(L(TM)=1+L_1(TM)+L_2(TM)+\cdots\) is the rational characteristic
class of the tangent bundle determined by the power series \(z/\tanh z\);
for example \(L_1=p_1/3\) and \(L_2=(7p_2-p_1^2)/45\), where
\(p_j\in H^{4j}(M;\mathbb Q)\) are the Pontryagin classes.
For a continuous map \(u:M\to BG\) and
\(\alpha\in H^*(BG;\mathbb Q)\), define the higher signature
\[
 \sigma_\alpha(M,u)=\bigl\langle
       [L(TM)\smile u^*\alpha]_n,[M]\bigr\rangle\in\mathbb Q.
\]
Here \(\smile\) is cup product, the subscript selects degree \(n\),
and the bracket evaluates cohomology on the fundamental class.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every discrete group \(G\), every pair of closed oriented smooth
\(n\)-manifolds \(M,N\), every orientation-preserving homotopy equivalence
\(f:M\to N\), every map \(v:N\to BG\), and every
\(\alpha\in H^*(BG;\mathbb Q)\), is
\[
 \sigma_\alpha(M,v\circ f)=\sigma_\alpha(N,v)?
\] \sref{1483}{2} \sref{1483}{3}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Are all higher signatures preserved by orientation-preserving homotopy equivalences, for every discrete group and every rational cohomology class of its classifying space?
