\section{Kirby Problem 1.67}\label{2726}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2726 · Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Put $\alpha=dz-y\,dx$ on $\mathbb R^3$, $\xi_{\mathrm{std}}=\ker\alpha$, and equip the symplectization $\mathbb R_t\times\mathbb R^3$ with $\omega=d\lambda$, where $\lambda=e^t\alpha$. A Legendrian link is tangent to $\xi_{\mathrm{std}}$. An exact Lagrangian cobordism $F$ from $\Lambda_-$ to $\Lambda_+$ is a properly embedded surface with $\omega|_F=0$ and $\lambda|_F=df$, cylindrical over these links outside a compact region; there are constants $c_-,c_+\in\mathbb R$ such that the primitive equals $c_-$ on the entire negative cylindrical end and $c_+$ on the entire positive cylindrical end. Each constant is common to all link components at its corresponding end. A filling has $\Lambda_-=\varnothing$. Compactly supported Hamiltonian isotopy means an ambient isotopy generated by a time-dependent Hamiltonian with compact support, taking one filling to another while leaving the cylindrical end unchanged.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For a given Legendrian link in $(\mathbb R^3,\xi_{\mathrm{std}})$ other than the standard Legendrian unknot and standard Hopf link, determine all its exact Lagrangian fillings up to compactly supported Hamiltonian isotopy. \sref{2726}{2} \sref{2726}{3}

\subsection{Short English statement}
Classify exact fillings of each Legendrian link beyond the standard unknot and Hopf link, using compactly supported Hamiltonian isotopy as the equivalence.
