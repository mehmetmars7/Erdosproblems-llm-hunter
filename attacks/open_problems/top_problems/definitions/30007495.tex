\section*{Factor-two hardness of Vertex Cover}\label{30007495}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For each fixed $0<\varepsilon\le1$, is it NP-hard, under deterministic polynomial-time reductions, to return a vertex cover of a finite simple unweighted graph whose size is at most $(2-\varepsilon)$ times the minimum? A vertex cover meets every edge. The graph is explicitly encoded; no degree bound or indistinguishability promise is imposed.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Is every fixed improvement of the factor-two approximation for Vertex Cover NP-hard on simple unweighted graphs?
