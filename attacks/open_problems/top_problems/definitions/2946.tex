\section*{Kirby Problem 4.70}\label{2946}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2946 · KP-4.70 · Topology · Kirby's Problems in Low-Dimensional Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a closed smooth $4$-manifold $X$, let $\operatorname{Diff}(X)$
be its full diffeomorphism group with the $C^\infty$ topology, and
let $\operatorname{Homeo}(X)$ be its full homeomorphism group with
the compact-open topology. Forgetting differentiability defines a
continuous homomorphism $i:\operatorname{Diff}(X)\hookrightarrow
\operatorname{Homeo}(X)$.

For $k\ge1$, use the identity map as basepoint when taking
$\pi_k$; a representative is a continuous based map from $S^k$
into the relevant transformation group, modulo based homotopy.
For $k=0$, $\pi_0$ is the group of path components, with
multiplication induced by composition. Thus these degree-zero groups
are smooth and topological mapping class groups respectively.

Do there exist a closed smooth $4$-manifold $X$ and an integer
$k\ge0$ for which
\[
 i_*:\pi_k(\operatorname{Diff}(X))
      \longrightarrow\pi_k(\operatorname{Homeo}(X))
\]
is an isomorphism?
Both the manifold and the single degree may be chosen. It is not
required that the map be an isomorphism in all degrees or that the
inclusion be a homotopy equivalence.

Injectivity would mean that a family which becomes nullhomotopic
topologically was already nullhomotopic smoothly; surjectivity would
mean every topological family has a smooth representative up to
homotopy. Both conditions are required in the chosen degree. The
source does not restrict $X$ to be orientable or simply connected.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Is the inclusion of diffeomorphisms into homeomorphisms an isomorphism on some homotopy group for some closed smooth four-manifold?
