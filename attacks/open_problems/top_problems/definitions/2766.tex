\section*{Kirby Problem 2.18}\label{2766}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2766 · KP-2.18 · Topology · Kirby's Problems in Low-Dimensional Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $S=S_{g,n}$ be an oriented finite-type surface of genus $g$ with $n$
punctures and negative Euler characteristic, supporting a pseudo-Anosov, and let
$\phi:S\to S$ be pseudo-Anosov: it preserves a pair of transverse measured
singular foliations, multiplying their transverse measures by $\lambda$ and
$\lambda^{-1}$ for some $\lambda>1$. Its mapping torus
$M_\phi=(S\times[0,1])/((x,1)\sim(\phi(x),0))$ has a complete finite-volume
hyperbolic metric of curvature $-1$; write $V(\phi)$ for its volume.

Two relevant metric spaces are the Teichmüller space $\mathcal T(S)$ with its
Weil--Petersson metric (the $L^2$ metric on harmonic Beltrami differentials),
and the pants graph $\mathcal P(S)$. Vertices of the latter are maximal
disjoint essential multicurves. An edge replaces one curve by one meeting it
minimally in the complementary one-holed torus or four-holed sphere.
For either space $Y$, define stable translation length by
\[
 \tau_Y(\phi)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{d_Y(y,\phi^n y)}n,
\]
which is independent of $y$.

Find strong explicit lower bounds for $V(\phi)$ in terms of these translation
lengths. In particular, determine effective constants, and their dependence
on $(g,n)$, in inequalities $V(\phi)\geq c_{g,n}\tau_Y(\phi)$ valid for every
pseudo-Anosov $\phi$. The problem seeks quantitative bounds, beyond a
comparison with unspecified constants for each fixed surface.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Give effective lower bounds on the volume of a hyperbolic surface bundle from how far its monodromy moves points in Weil--Petersson space or the pants graph.
