\section*{Generalized-canonical flip termination on Kähler fourfolds}\label{30007026}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Fix a normal globally Weil-Q-factorial compact Kähler fourfold, rational boundary coefficients in [0,1), and nef rational b-Cartier data on a projective birational Kähler model. Assume generalized discrepancies are positive for all primes and at least one for exceptional primes. Must every sequence of nontrivial projective small rational-adjoint flips, preserving these canonical and b-data, be finite?
