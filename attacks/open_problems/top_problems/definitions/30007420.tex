\section*{Finite conditional resolution of critical planar Ising stopping bands}\label{30007420}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For the critical nearest-neighbor zero-field Ising model on square lattices of mesh delta, with plus boundary in a fixed larger square $Q_L={z:||z||_infinity<L}$, fix $0<u<v<L$. In the inward case choose a compact target K inside $Q_u$; in the outward case choose K inside $Q_L$ minus the closure of $Q_v$. Require strictly positive distances from K to the band and fixed square boundary. Allow the two contour levels to be chosen in fixed open windows with disjoint closures, preserving these clearances. Fix a triangulation of each lattice face, linearly interpolate spins, and explore zero-level strands meeting the starting contour until their first return to that contour or first hit of the other contour, revealing the traversed triangle spins and starting-contour spins. Initially reveal the outside of the outer contour for an inward exploration, or the inside of the inner contour for an outward exploration. Is it true that for every $\epsilon ,\eta >0$ one can choose the levels and a finite list of deterministic regular Jordan barriers with finitely many plus/minus boundary arcs, all independent of the mesh, and a finite-valued descriptor of the band spins, so that for every sufficiently small delta the exceptional descriptor has probability at most epsilon, while for every realizable nonexceptional transcript inserting its descriptor-specific plus pins and then minus pins changes the entire conditional target spin law by summed total variation at most eta and yields the same final target-side law for every transcript with that descriptor? Require the descriptors of any fixed finite list of such bands to have convergent joint laws. Clearances and barriers are fixed before the mesh threshold, and the approximation is uniform over all bounded, including mesh-dependent, target observables.
