\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For a separable Hilbert space $H$, is there $\delta>0$ such that every pair of separable $C^*$-subalgebras $A,B\subseteq B(H)$ at Kadison--Kastler distance less than $\delta$ are spatially isomorphic? This distance is the Hausdorff distance between their closed unit balls in operator norm; spatial isomorphism means conjugacy by a unitary on $H$.
