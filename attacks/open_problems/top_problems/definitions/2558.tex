\section*{Kourovka Notebook Problem 21.49}\label{2558}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2558 · KOU-21.49 · Group Theory · Kourovka Notebook - New Problems, Issue 21}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A geodesic metric space is hyperbolic if some $\delta\ge0$ makes each
side of every geodesic triangle lie in the $\delta$-neighbourhood of the
other two sides. An isometric action of a group $K$ on a metric space
$X$ is acylindrical if for every $\varepsilon>0$ there exist $R>0$ and
an integer $N\ge1$ such that, whenever $d(x,y)\ge R$,
\[
 |\{g\in K:d(x,gx)\le\varepsilon,
                  \ d(y,gy)\le\varepsilon\}|\le N.
\]
The constants are uniform over all such pairs of points.

A group is virtually cyclic if it has a cyclic subgroup of finite
index. In the source convention, $K$ is acylindrically hyperbolic if
it is not virtually cyclic and admits an acylindrical isometric action
on a hyperbolic geodesic space with an unbounded orbit.
The space need not be proper or locally finite, and faithfulness of
the action is not part of the definition.

Let $G$ be a finitely generated acylindrically hyperbolic group. Is its
full automorphism group $\operatorname{Aut}(G)$ also acylindrically
hyperbolic? This group consists of all bijective homomorphisms $G\to G$,
with composition as multiplication.

The requested action for $\operatorname{Aut}(G)$ may use a different
hyperbolic space from the one supplied for $G$. Neither finite generation
of $\operatorname{Aut}(G)$ nor finite outer automorphism group is assumed.
The target is the full automorphism group, not just its subgroup of
inner automorphisms or a selected finite-index subgroup.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Is the full automorphism group of every finitely generated acylindrically hyperbolic group itself acylindrically hyperbolic?
