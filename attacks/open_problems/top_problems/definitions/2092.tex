\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $f(n)$ be the minimal $m$ such that $ n! = a_1\cdots a_k $ with $n< a_1<\cdots <a_k=m$. Is there (and what is it) a constant $c$ such that $ f(n)-2n \sim c\frac{n}{\log n}? $ \sref{2092}{1}

{\small\emph{Transcription note.} Only unambiguous escape/formatting damage and nonmathematical serialization debris were repaired in the displayed source statement.}

\subsection{Short English statement}

When a factorial is split into distinct factors all larger than its argument, does the smallest achievable largest factor have the proposed second-order asymptotic?
