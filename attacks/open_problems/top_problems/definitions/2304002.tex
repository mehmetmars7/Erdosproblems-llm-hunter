\section{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 4.2}\label{2304002}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2304002 · Analysis}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Write $\mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}$ and $\mathbb T=\partial\mathbb D$. Let $p(z)=\sum_{k=0}^na_kz^k$ be a nonconstant complex polynomial of degree $n\ge1$ whose zeros all lie on $\mathbb T$. Put
\[A(p)=\max_{0\le k\le n}|a_k|,\qquad M(p)=\max_{|z|=1}|p(z)|.
\]
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Is $M(p)\ge2A(p)$ for every such polynomial? \sref{2304002}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does a polynomial with all zeros on the unit circle have maximum modulus there at least twice its largest coefficient?
