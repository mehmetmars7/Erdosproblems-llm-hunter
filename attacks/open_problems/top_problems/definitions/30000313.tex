\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For a $C^*$-algebra $A$ in the stated infinite-tensor-power setting, is the infinite tensor product $A^{\otimes\infty}$ isomorphic to its tensor product with the Jiang--Su algebra $\mathcal Z$? This tensorial absorption property is called $\mathcal Z$-stability.
