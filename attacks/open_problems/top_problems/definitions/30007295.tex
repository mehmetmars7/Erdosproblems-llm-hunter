\section*{Approximation paving for every MASA}\label{30007295}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Is there a universal C such that, for each $0<\varepsilon <1$, every self-adjoint x in a complex von Neumann algebra M and every MASA $A\subseteq M$ can be strongly approximated by self-adjoint y of norm $\leq 3||x||$, with a partition $(p_i)$ in A into at most $C\varepsilon ^-6$ pieces and a self-adjoint $a\in A$ of norm $\leq ||y||$ satisfying $||\sum p_iyp_i-a||\leq \varepsilon ||y||$? No separability or expectation is assumed.
