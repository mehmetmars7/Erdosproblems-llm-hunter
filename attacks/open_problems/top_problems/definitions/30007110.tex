\section*{Half-minimum-area bound for cylinder covers}\label{30007110}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
If finitely many cylinders $B_i+R$ $u_i$ cover a convex body $K\subset R^{3}$, with unit axes $u_i$ and measurable perpendicular bases $B_i\subset u_i\perp $ of finite area, must $\Sigma _i$ $area(B_i)\geq \tfrac12 $ $min_{|u|=1}area(proj_{u\perp }K)$? Costs are counted with multiplicity and base convexity is not required.
