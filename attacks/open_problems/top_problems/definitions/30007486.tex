\section*{Low-space worst-case Subset Sum at half-exponential time}\label{30007486}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For positive-integer Subset Sum with n items and bit length $b\leq n^c$ for any fixed c, can one uniform randomized classical word algorithm use $n^{O_c(1)}2^{n/2}$ operations and $O_c((n+b+2)^{15}2^{0.199n})$ writable words on every path, never falsely answering YES and answering YES instances correctly with probability $\geq 2/3$, without being given c?
