\section*{Annular variation of the triangular Hilbert transform}\label{30007438}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For F,G in $L^3(R^2)$, write $B_{a,b}(F,G)(x,y)=integral_{a<|t|<b}$ F(x+t,y) G(x,y+t) dt/t. For every $r>2$, is the $L^{3/2}$ norm of the supremum of $(sum_j$ $|B_{a_j,b_j}(F,G)|^r)^{1/r}$, over finite disjoint ordered positive annuli, at most $C_r||F||_3||G||_3$?
