\section{Crouzeix's Conjecture}\label{1300}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 1300 · Algebra}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.


\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For a complex square matrix $A$, its numerical range is $W(A)=\{\langle Av,v\rangle:\|v\|=1\}$. If $f$ is analytic on a neighborhood of $W(A)$, must
\[\|f(A)\|\leq2\max_{z\in W(A)}|f(z)|\]
hold in the Euclidean operator norm? The proposed constant is independent of matrix size.
\par\smallskip\textit{Formulation references:} \sref{1300}{1}, \sref{1300}{2}.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does the numerical range control every analytic matrix function with universal constant two?
