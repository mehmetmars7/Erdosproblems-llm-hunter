\section*{Integral scalar-curvature control of simplicial volume}\label{30007157}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every integer $n\geq 3$, is there a positive constant a(n) such that every closed connected oriented smooth n-manifold M and every smooth metric g satisfy $a(n)\Vert M\Vert \leq \int _M$ $max(0,-Scal_g)^{n/2}$ $dvol_g$, with $\Vert M\Vert $ the real simplicial volume?
