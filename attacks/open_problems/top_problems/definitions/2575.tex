\section*{Kourovka Notebook Problem 21.66}\label{2575}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2575 · KOU-21.66 · Group Theory · Kourovka Notebook - New Problems, Issue 21}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A finite group is soluble if its derived series reaches $1$, where
the next term is the subgroup generated by all commutators in the
previous term. For a finite soluble group $G$, the Fitting height
$h(G)$ is the least number of factors in a normal series from $1$
to $G$ with nilpotent factors, and $h(1)=0$.
A group $L$ is nilpotent if its lower central series
$\gamma_1(L)=L$, $\gamma_{i+1}(L)=[\gamma_i(L),L]$ reaches $1$.

Let $A\le\operatorname{Aut}(G)$ be a nilpotent group of automorphisms
of a finite soluble group $G$. Since $G$ is finite, $A$ is finite.
Its common fixed-point subgroup is
\[
 C_G(A)=\{g\in G:a(g)=g\text{ for every }a\in A\}
        =\bigcap_{a\in A}C_G(a).
\]
Is there a function $f:\mathbb N\times\mathbb N\to\mathbb N$
such that
\[
 h(G)\le f\bigl(|A|,|C_G(A)|\bigr)
\]
for every such pair $(G,A)$?

The same function must control all ambient groups with the same
orders of the automorphism group and its common fixed subgroup.
There is no assumed bound on $|G|$, and no coprimality condition
between $|A|$ and $|G|$. The parameter counts elements fixed by every
automorphism in $A$ simultaneously, not a sum of individual fixed-point
counts. The conclusion bounds the height of $G$ itself, rather than
only the height of some subgroup of bounded index.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Is the Fitting height of a finite soluble group bounded by the order of a nilpotent automorphism group and the number of its common fixed points?
