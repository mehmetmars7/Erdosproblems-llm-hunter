\section{Algebraic Stories — Ideals of generic forms}\label{1500008}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 1500008 · Algebra}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $S=\mathbb C[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$ with its usual total-degree grading. For a homogeneous quotient $R$, define
\[\operatorname{Hilb}_R(t)=\sum_{e\geq0}(\dim_{\mathbb C}R_e)t^e.
\]
A generic tuple of forms has its coefficient vector in a suitable nonempty Zariski open subset of the product of the prescribed homogeneous pieces.For a series $A(t)=\sum_{e\geq0}a_et^e$, use $[A(t)]_+$ for its truncation at the first non-positive coefficient: if $e_0=\min\{e:a_e\leq0\}$, retain the terms with $e<e_0$ and replace all later terms by zero; if no such $e_0$ exists, retain the entire series.Fix positive integers $n,r,d_1,\ldots,d_r$, and choose generic $f_i\in S_{d_i}$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Does $R=S/(f_1,\ldots,f_r)$ have the Hilbert series
\[\operatorname{Hilb}_R(t)=\left[\frac{\prod_{i=1}^r(1-t^{d_i})}{(1-t)^n}\right]_+?\] \sref{1500008}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does the proposed truncated product give the Hilbert series for every ideal generated by generic complex forms?
