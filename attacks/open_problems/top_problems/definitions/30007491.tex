\section*{Uniform quasipolynomial worst-case trace reconstruction}\label{30007491}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For binary strings of known length $n\ge2$ and known rational retention probability $p\in(0,1]$, seek one randomized multitape Turing machine that reconstructs every input with probability at least $2/3$. At each fixed $p$, its independent ordinary deletion-trace budget should be
\[
 M(n,p)=\exp\bigl(O_p((\log n)^3(1+\log\log(2n))^6)\bigr).
\]
Its bit time must be polynomial in $n$, the binary encoding length of $p$, and the prescribed trace budget, on every internal random-bit sequence and every trace stream consisting of words of length at most $n$. Preprocessing, finite-precision computation, received bits and output are charged. Only external generation of independent traces is an oracle operation.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Can one uniform binary trace-reconstruction algorithm achieve the specified quasipolynomial trace and bit budgets at every fixed rational retention probability?
