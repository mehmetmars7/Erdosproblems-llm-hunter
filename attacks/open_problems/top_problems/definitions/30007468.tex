\section*{Hitting lists for noncommutative rational formulas}\label{30007468}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Can polynomial bit time in unary n,s produce a polynomial-size list of rational matrix n-tuples such that every nonzero admissible size-s free rational formula has a defined evaluation with invertible value at some list member, uniformly over arbitrary rational constants and inverse depth, without receiving the formula?
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can polynomial bit time in unary n,s produce a polynomial-size list of rational matrix n-tuples such that every nonzero admissible size-s free rational formula has a defined evaluation with invertible value at some list member, uniformly over arbitrary rational constants and inverse depth, without receiving the formula?
