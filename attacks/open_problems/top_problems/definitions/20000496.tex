\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $\Omega$ be a bounded planar simply connected domain, or a bounded convex domain in $\mathbb R^n$ for $n\geq3$. Must every first nonconstant Neumann eigenfunction of its Laplacian attain its global maximum and minimum on the boundary rather than in the interior? This is the stated eigenfunction form of the hot-spots question.
