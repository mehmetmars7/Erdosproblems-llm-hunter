\section*{Arithmetic classification of twisted lattice correspondences}\label{30007315}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $\Gamma $ be a countably infinite ICC group commensurable with a property-(T) lattice in a finite nonempty product $H_i(k_i)^+$, where $k_i$ is R, C or a finite extension of $Q_p$ (with primes allowed to differ), each $H_i$ is a connected adjoint absolutely simple algebraic group, $H_i(k_i)^+$ is generated by the $k_i-points$ of unipotent radicals of proper parabolic subgroups, and each such topological group is noncompact and has property (T), and $\Lambda $ any countably infinite ICC group. For arbitrary normalized circle-valued cocycles $\mu $ and $\omega $, is every bifinite $L_\mu (\Gamma )–L_\omega (\Lambda )$ correspondence a closed summand of finitely many models obtained by induction from actual isomorphisms between finite-index subgroups and finite-dimensional projective representations with multiplier $\mu /\delta *\omega $?
