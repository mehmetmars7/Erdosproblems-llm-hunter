\section*{Algebraicity of split Weil-type eightfold classes}\label{30007008}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every imaginary quadratic field K and complex abelian eightfold A of split Weil type, with K acting with multiplicities (4,4) and a compatible split Hermitian polarization, is the Weil-class subspace $\wedge ^{8}_K$ $H^{1}(A,Q)\subset H^{8}(A,Q)$ contained in the image of $CH^{4}(A)\otimes Q$? All compatible polarization types and members with extra endomorphisms are included.
