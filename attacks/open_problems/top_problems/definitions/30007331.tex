\section*{The exact critical dimension for homogeneous one-phase minimizers}\label{30007331}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For the one-phase Bernoulli energy integral(|grad $u|^2+1_{u>0})$, is every nonzero degree-one-homogeneous global minimizer in dimensions 1 through 6 a half-space solution, while a nonflat degree-one-homogeneous global minimizer exists in $R^7$? Thus is the first nonflat homogeneous minimizing dimension exactly seven?
