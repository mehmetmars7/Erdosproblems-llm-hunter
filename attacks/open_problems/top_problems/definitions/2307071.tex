\section{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 7.71}\label{2307071}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2307071 · Analysis}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Fix $n\ge2$. A domain of holomorphy $\Omega\subset\mathbb C^n$ is a connected open set that is a domain of existence for a holomorphic function. A holomorphic function $p$ has no continuation through a boundary point $a$ if there is no neighborhood of $a$ carrying a holomorphic function agreeing with $p$ on an adjoining open part of $\Omega$. The complex Laplace operator is
\[\Delta_{\mathbb C}p=\sum_{j=1}^n\frac{\partial^2p}{\partial z_j^2},
\]
which is different from the real Laplacian or the sum of mixed $z_j,\bar z_j$ derivatives.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Find necessary and sufficient conditions on $\Omega$ for the existence of $p\in\mathcal O(\Omega)$ satisfying $\Delta_{\mathbb C}p=0$ and admitting no analytic continuation through its boundary. The question is for arbitrary domains of holomorphy in every dimension $n\ge2$. \sref{2307071}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Which domains of holomorphy admit a solution of the complex Laplace equation whose analytic continuation stops at the boundary?
