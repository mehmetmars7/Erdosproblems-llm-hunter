\section*{Kirby Problem 2.11}\label{2759}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2759 · KP-2.11 · Topology · Kirby's Problems in Low-Dimensional Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $S$ be a connected orientable finite-type hyperbolic surface and $\gamma$ an
essential nonperipheral closed curve, considered up to free homotopy.
Its simple lifting degree $d_S(\gamma)$ is the least degree of a connected
finite-sheeted cover $p:S'\to S$ in which the map $\gamma:S^1\to S$ lifts to
a simple closed curve. The lift must project to the specified curve itself;
replacing it by a power is not allowed.
Let $i(\gamma,\gamma)$ be its minimal self-intersection number, and let
$\ell_\rho(\gamma)$ be its geodesic length for a fixed complete hyperbolic
metric $\rho$.

Find sharp bounds for the extremal functions
\[
f_S(n)=\sup_{i(\gamma,\gamma)\leq n}d_S(\gamma),\qquad
f_\rho(L)=\sup_{\ell_\rho(\gamma)\leq L}d_S(\gamma).
\]
Seek analogous bounds using conjugacy word length in a fixed finite generating
set of $\pi_1(S)$. Determine also typical or average growth, specifying the
sampling model, for example uniform sampling from the finite set of closed
geodesic classes of length at most $L$. The surface, metric, and generating
set must remain fixed when taking the relevant asymptotic limit. Sharp average
bounds need not coincide with extremal ones.
\subsection{Short English statement}
How large a finite cover is needed to turn a closed surface curve into a simple curve, in the worst case and on average?
