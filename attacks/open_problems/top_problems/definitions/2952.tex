\section*{Kirby Problem 4.76}\label{2952}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2952 · KP-4.76 · Topology · Kirby's Problems in Low-Dimensional Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $X$ be a closed oriented simply connected smooth $4$-manifold
and fix an integer $k>0$. For $n\ge0$, write
$X_n=X\#_{n}(S^2\times S^2)$, with $X_0=X$.
Connected sum removes an interior smooth $4$-ball from each
oriented summand and identifies their boundary $3$-spheres by an
orientation-reversing diffeomorphism.

Let $\operatorname{Diff}^+(X_n)$ have its $C^\infty$ topology
and $\operatorname{Homeo}^+(X_n)$ its compact-open topology.
Both consist of orientation-preserving self-maps of the indicated
kind. Inclusion gives a homomorphism on homotopy groups, based
at the identity:
\[
 i_{n,*}:\pi_k(\operatorname{Diff}^+(X_n))
       \longrightarrow\pi_k(\operatorname{Homeo}^+(X_n)).
\]
Does there exist an integer $N=N(X,k)\ge0$ such that $i_{n,*}$
is surjective for every $n\ge N$?

A positive answer means that each based continuous $S^k$-family
of orientation-preserving homeomorphisms of $X_n$ is based-homotopic,
through homeomorphism families, to one consisting of diffeomorphisms
and continuous in the smooth topology. The number of stabilizations
may depend on $X$ and $k$, but must work for every class at each
of the indicated later stages.

This is eventual surjectivity at each stabilized manifold, a
stronger requirement than smoothing one chosen family after a
family-dependent stabilization. No injectivity assertion is made,
and no map between diffeomorphism groups of different closed
connected sums is needed to state the question.
\subsection{Short English statement}
After sufficiently many S²×S² stabilizations, is every positive-degree family of homeomorphisms represented up to homotopy by a family of diffeomorphisms?
