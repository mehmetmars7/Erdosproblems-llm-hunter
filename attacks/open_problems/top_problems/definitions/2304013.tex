\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For $P(z)=\sum_{k=1}^n\varepsilon_kz^k$ with $\varepsilon_k\in\{-1,1\}$, is there an absolute $A>0$ such that
\[\max_{|z|=1}|P(z)|>(1+A)\sqrt n\]
for all such polynomials? This asks for a uniform gap above the square-root scale.
