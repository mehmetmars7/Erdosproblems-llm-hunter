\section*{Uniform finiteness of planar polynomial limit cycles}\label{30007174}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every integer $d>=1$, does there exist a finite B(d) such that every real planar polynomial vector field $x-prime=P(x,y)$, $y-prime=Q(x,y)$, with max(deg P,deg $Q)<=d$, has at most B(d) geometrically distinct isolated periodic orbits throughout the plane?
