\section*{Entropy-rate dimension formula for all real self-similar measures}\label{30007172}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For a finite nonempty indexed family $\phi _i(x)=r_i$ $x+t_i$ with real $t_i$, $0<|r_i|<1$ and strictly positive weights $p_i$ summing to one, let $\mu =sum_i$ $p_i(\phi _i)_*\mu $. Let $G_n$ be the random complete affine map $\phi _I1$ composed with ... composed with $\phi _In$ for independent letters with law p, $h_RW=lim_n$ $H(G_n)/n$ and $chi=-sum_i$ $p_i$ $\log |r_i|$, using the same logarithmic base. Is the lower Hausdorff dimension of mu always $min(1,h_RW/chi)$, allowing signed unequal ratios and arbitrary exact overlaps?
