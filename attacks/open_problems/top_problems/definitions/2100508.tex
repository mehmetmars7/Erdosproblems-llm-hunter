\section{Open Problems in Integrable Systems — Nijenhuis operators: singular points and global properties}\label{2100508}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2100508 · Dynamical Systems}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


A smooth endomorphism field $L:TM\to TM$ is Nijenhuis if, for all smooth vector fields $X,Y$, its torsion
\[N_L(X,Y)=L^2[X,Y]+[LX,LY]-L[LX,Y]-L[X,LY]\]
vanishes identically.\par An endomorphism $A$ of an $n$-dimensional real vector space is algebraically regular if its centralizer $\{B:AB=BA\}$ has dimension $n$, the minimum possible dimension. Equivalently, over $\mathbb C$ there is one Jordan block for each eigenvalue. Eigenvalues need not be real or distinct. A closed manifold is compact and has no boundary.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Describe precisely the closed smooth manifolds $M$ which admit a smooth Nijenhuis endomorphism field $L$ such that $L_x:T_xM\to T_xM$ is algebraically regular at every $x\in M$. \sref{2100508}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Which closed manifolds admit a Nijenhuis tensor that is algebraically regular everywhere, allowing both nontrivial Jordan blocks and complex eigenvalues?
