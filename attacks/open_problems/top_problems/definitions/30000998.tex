\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For a complete Riemannian manifold satisfying the Ma--Trudinger--Wang curvature condition for squared distance, must every tangent injectivity locus be convex? At $x$, this locus consists of tangent vectors for which the radial geodesic remains minimizing up to its endpoint before the cut locus; convexity is in the vector space $T_xM$.
