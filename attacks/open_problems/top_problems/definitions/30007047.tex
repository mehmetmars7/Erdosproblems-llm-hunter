\section*{Topological right-triviality of constant-Milnor surface families}\label{30007047}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let F(x,t) be holomorphic near ${0}×\Delta $ in $C^{3}×\Delta $, with $f_t(0)=0$ and an isolated critical point at zero in each fiber, and with constant Milnor number. After shrinking representatives and the disk, must there be a jointly continuous homeomorphism germ $\Phi (x,t)=(\varphi _t(x),t)$, with continuous inverse, $\varphi _t(0)=0$ and $\varphi _{0}=id$, satisfying $f_t(\varphi _t(x))=f_{0}(x)$?
