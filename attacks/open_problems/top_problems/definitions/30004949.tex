\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.


\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $\mu$ be a log-concave probability measure on $\mathbb R^d$. Write $h(\mu)$ for the Cheeger infimum of boundary measure divided by the smaller mass of a set and its complement, and $h_{\mathrm{half}}(\mu)$ for that infimum restricted to half-spaces. Is there a universal $c>0$ for which $h(\mu)\geq c\,h_{\mathrm{half}}(\mu)$ for all $d$ and $\mu$?
