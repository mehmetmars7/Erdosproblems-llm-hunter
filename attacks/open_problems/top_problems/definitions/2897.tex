\section*{Kirby Problem 4.21}\label{2897}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2897 · KP-4.21 · Topology · Kirby's Problems in Low-Dimensional Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $M$ be a closed connected topological four-manifold. A codimension-zero submanifold with boundary is understood to be locally flat along its boundary, so that it has a collar on either side inside $M$. A compact manifold $Y$ is smoothable if its given topology admits a smooth atlas, including compatible boundary charts.

Is there always a decomposition
\[
M=Y\cup_\Sigma Z,\qquad
Y\cap Z=\partial Y=\partial Z=\Sigma,
\]
where $Y,Z$ are compact connected topological four-manifolds, $Y$ is smoothable, $Z$ is integrally acyclic, and $\Sigma$ is an integral homology three-sphere? Integrally acyclic means $H_0(Z;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z$ and $H_i(Z;\mathbb Z)=0$ for every $i>0$; the boundary condition means $H_*(\Sigma;\mathbb Z)\cong H_*(S^3;\mathbb Z)$.

Neither a smoothing nor simple connectivity of $Z$ is required. In particular, acyclicity must not be replaced by contractibility, which would impose a stronger restriction on the discarded piece. The separating homology sphere and the smoothing of $Y$ may both depend on $M$.

The proposed conclusion localizes the obstruction to smoothing a closed manifold inside one acyclic submanifold, without asking that the smooth structure extend across the gluing. The global hypothesis that $M$ is closed is part of the question; a relative version for manifolds with a prescribed outer boundary is not automatically included. No assumption on the fundamental group of $M$ is made.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can every closed topological four-manifold be split into a smoothable piece and an acyclic piece along a homology sphere?
