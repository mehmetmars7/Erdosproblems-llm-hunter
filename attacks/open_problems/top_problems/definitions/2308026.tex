\section{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 8.26}\label{2308026}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2308026 · Analysis}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $\mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}$ and $\mathbb T=\partial\mathbb D$. Let $A$ be the boundary restrictions to $\mathbb T$ of the disc algebra: functions continuous on $\overline{\mathbb D}$ and analytic in $\mathbb D$. Let $\operatorname{Re}A=\{\operatorname{Re}f:f\in A\}\subset C(\mathbb T;\mathbb R)$, and for a circle homeomorphism $\psi$ put $(\operatorname{Re}A)\circ\psi=\{u\circ\psi:u\in\operatorname{Re}A\}$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Characterize all homeomorphisms $\psi:\mathbb T\to\mathbb T$ satisfying \[\operatorname{Re}A=(\operatorname{Re}A)\circ\psi.\] The source reports absolute continuity as necessary and $C^{1+\varepsilon}$ regularity as sufficient. \sref{2308026}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Characterize the circle homeomorphisms that preserve the real parts of disc-algebra functions as a class.
