\section*{Kirby Problem 4.72}\label{2948}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2948 · KP-4.72 · Topology · Kirby's Problems in Low-Dimensional Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $X$ be a closed connected orientable topological $4$-manifold
whose fundamental group is finite. No smoothing is assumed.
Give $H=\operatorname{Homeo}(X)$, the full homeomorphism group,
the compact-open topology. For $k\ge1$, let $\pi_k(H)$ be its
homotopy group based at the identity. For $k=0$, use the group
of path components, so $\pi_0(H)$ is the topological mapping
class group under composition.

Part (a) asks whether $\pi_k(H)$ is finitely generated for every
integer $k\ge0$, for every $X$ satisfying these hypotheses.
Finite generation in degree zero has the ordinary possibly
nonabelian group meaning; in positive degrees it is finite generation
of the corresponding homotopy group.

Let $BH$ be a classifying space for the topological group $H$,
constructed as the quotient of a contractible free $H$-space in
the usual universal-bundle model. Part (b) asks whether
$H_k(BH;\mathbb Z)$ is a finitely generated abelian group for
every $k\ge0$ and every such $X$.

The group $H$ retains its topology when $BH$ is formed; discrete
group cohomology would be a different invariant. No uniform bound
on the number of generators as $k$ or $X$ varies is requested.
The hypothesis permits any finite fundamental group, not only
the trivial group. Both assertions are universal finiteness
statements, rather than requests for just one favorable example.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For a closed orientable topological four-manifold with finite fundamental group, are all homotopy groups of its homeomorphism group and all integral homology groups of its classifying space finitely generated?
