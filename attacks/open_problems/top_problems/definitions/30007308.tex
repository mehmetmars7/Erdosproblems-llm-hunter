\section*{Small spatial embeddings from one-way near-inclusions}\label{30007308}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Does arbitrarily small one-way unit-ball distance from a unital von Neumann algebra M to N, acting on the same Hilbert space, force a unitary u arbitrarily close to 1 with $uMu*\subseteq N$? The claim concerns small implementing unitaries, not the existence of an unrestricted spatial embedding.
