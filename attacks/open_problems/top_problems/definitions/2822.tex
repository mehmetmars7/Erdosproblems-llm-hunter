\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Suppose a word-hyperbolic group has Gromov boundary homeomorphic to $S^2$. Must it act as a cocompact Kleinian group? More generally, if its boundary is planar, must a finite-index subgroup be a convex-cocompact Kleinian group? A Kleinian group is a discrete group of isometries of hyperbolic three-space.
