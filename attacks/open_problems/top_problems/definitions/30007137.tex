\section*{Smooth isometric immersion of closed surfaces into four-space}\label{30007137}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For each compact smooth surface M without boundary and each smooth Riemannian metric g on M, is there a smooth map $F:M\to R^{4}$ with injective differential at every point and $F*\delta =g$? The map may have self-intersections, and M may be nonorientable.
