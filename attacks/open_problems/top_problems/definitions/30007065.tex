\section*{Invariant anticanonical sections with nonzero Euler characteristic}\label{30007065}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let X be smooth connected projective complex with smoothly semipositive $-K_X$ and $\chi (X,O_X)\neq 0$. For any algebraic torus T acting on X with the natural anticanonical linearization, must some positive anticanonical multiple have a nonzero T-invariant global section?
