\section{Open Problems on Billiards and Geometric Optics}\label{5200012}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 5200012 · Dynamical Systems}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Newtonian aerodynamics models a freely moving body interacting by elastic boundary reflection with a rarefied medium of infinitesimal particles. In the stated one-dimensional special case the body is a point of mass $1$ and the medium consists of identical infinitesimal particles. Here $(x,v)\in\mathbb R^2$ is particle position and velocity, $\mu_t$ their distribution measure, and $X(t),P(t)=X\prime(t)$ are the position and velocity of the massive point. The request includes formulating the dynamics, rather than assuming a preselected evolution equation.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
A body moves freely in a rarefied medium in $\mathbb{R}^n$, $n\geq1$, under Newtonian aerodynamics. Determine the equations of motion and prove existence and uniqueness. In the one-dimensional case, formulate the motion using a measure $\mu_t$ on particle phase space together with the massive particle's position $X(t)$ and velocity $P(t)=X'(t)$. \sref{5200012}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Formulate the free-body dynamics in a rarefied particle medium in every positive dimension and prove existence and uniqueness, including the one-dimensional measure-valued particle model coupled to a point of mass one.
