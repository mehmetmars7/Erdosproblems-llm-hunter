\section*{Terminal numerical-dimension-one abundance in positive characteristic}\label{30007071}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For a normal projective Q-factorial terminal threefold over an algebraically closed field of characteristic $p>3$, if $K_X$ is nef with $\nu (K_X)=1$, must some positive Cartier multiple have at least two independent sections, so that $\kappa (K_X)=1$ and $K_X$ is semiample?
