\section*{Wall D(2) problem with a finite target model}\label{30007511}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $X$ be a finite connected three-dimensional CW complex and $\widetilde X$ its universal cover. If $H_i(\widetilde X;\mathbb Z)=0$ for $i>2$, and $H^3(X;M)=0$ for every $\mathbb Z[\pi_1X]$-module $M$, must $X$ be homotopy equivalent to a finite CW complex of dimension at most two?
\subsection{Short English statement}
