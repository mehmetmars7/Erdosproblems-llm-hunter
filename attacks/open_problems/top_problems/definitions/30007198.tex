\section*{Coefficient-preserving solutions of nonsingular group-equation systems}\label{30007198}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every group G and finite list $w_{1},\dots ,w_m\in G*F_n,1\leq m\leq n$, whose exponent-sum matrix has row rank m over Q, is $G\to (G*F_n)/\langle \langle w_{1},\dots ,w_m\rangle \rangle $ injective; equivalently, are the equations simultaneously soluble in an overgroup containing G?
