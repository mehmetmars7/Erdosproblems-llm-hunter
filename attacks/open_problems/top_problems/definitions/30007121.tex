\section*{Sharp entropy lower bound for log-concave densities}\label{30007121}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every log-concave probability density f on $R^{m}$, must its differential entropy $h(f)=-\int f$ logf satisfy $h(f)\geq m+\tfrac12 \log $ det Cov(f)? Is equality equivalent, up to almost-everywhere change, to f being the density of $M(E_{1},\dots ,E_m)+b$ with M invertible and independent standard exponential $E_i$ supported on $[0,\infty )$? No symmetry or smoothness is assumed.
