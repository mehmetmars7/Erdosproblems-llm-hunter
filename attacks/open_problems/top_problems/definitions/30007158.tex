\section*{Least volume of nongeodesic minimal hypersurfaces in spheres}\label{30007158}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For each integer $m\geq 2$, must a closed connected smooth m-manifold minimally immersed in the unit $S^{m+1}$, with image not totally geodesic, have induced volume at least the least volume among the Clifford hypersurfaces $S^p(\surd (p/m))×S^{m-p}(\surd ((m-p)/m))$, $1\leq p<m$?
