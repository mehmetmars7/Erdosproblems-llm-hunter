\section{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 6.75}\label{2306075}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2306075 · Analysis}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Write $\mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}$ and $\mathbb T=\partial\mathbb D$. For $n\in\mathbb N$, let $\mathcal P_n$ be the holomorphic injective polynomials $p(z)=z+\sum_{m=2}^n a_mz^m$ on $\mathbb D$ (degree at most $n$). Set $a_1=1$ and $A_m(n)=\max_{p\in\mathcal P_n}|a_m|$ for $1\le m\le n$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every fixed $n$, is there an index $n_0\in\{1,\ldots,n\}$ with \[A_1(n)<\cdots<A_{n_0}(n)>A_{n_0+1}(n)>\cdots>A_n(n),\] allowing either side to be empty? Also determine the least $n_1\in\{1,\ldots,n\}$ for which $A_m(n)\le1$ whenever $n_1\le m\le n$. \sref{2306075}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Are the sharp polynomial coefficient bounds strictly unimodal, and where does their final tail first stay at or below one?
