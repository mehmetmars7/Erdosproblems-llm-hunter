\section*{Analytic path selection at several exponential ends}\label{30007133}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Consider finitely many real holomorphic functions of real lengths $L_{1},\dots ,L_m$ and parameters z near a finite real $z_{0}$. Require bounded holomorphic extension to products Re $L>A$, |Im $L|<c(Re$ $L)^{2}$; recursively discrete nonnegative exponential-power expansions in each length with finite polynomial factors, strictly better exponential remainders at every cutoff, common tail holomorphy domains for all coefficients, and locally finite grouped weights on positive linear-rate specializations. If a finite Boolean combination of sign/equality conditions holds on a sequence with every $L_i\to \infty $ and $z\to z_{0}$, must it hold eventually on one real analytic path with the same limits and eventually monotone or constant coordinates? Additional finite lists may also be required monotone and prescribed finite function limits retained.
