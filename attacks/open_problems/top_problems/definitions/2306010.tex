{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2306010 · Analysis}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $\mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}$, $\mathbb T=\partial\mathbb D$, and $\mathbb D^*=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|>1\}$. Let $\Sigma$ be the class of analytic injective functions on $\mathbb D^*$ with Laurent expansion $F(z)=z+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}b_nz^{-n}$. Call $F\in\Sigma$ convex if the omitted compact set $K_F=\mathbb C\setminus F(\mathbb D^*)$ is convex. For convex $F,G\in\Sigma$ and $0<\lambda<1$, put $H=\lambda F+(1-\lambda)G$. The source records that $H\in\Sigma$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Is $K_H=\mathbb C\setminus H(\mathbb D^*)$ necessarily convex? \sref{2306010}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does a convex combination of two exterior convex univalent functions retain a convex omitted set?
