\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.


\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Can polynomials of degree less than $d$ over $\mathbb F_p$ be multiplied in $O(d\log p\log(d\log p))$ bit operations uniformly in $d$ and $p$, including $p=2$? The companion integer-multiplication question asks whether the cost $M(n)$ of multiplying $n$-bit integers must be $\Omega(n\log n)$. Both the uniform finite-field upper bound and the integer lower bound belong to this problem.
