\section*{What is the homotopy type of the group of diffeomorphisms of the 4-sphere?}\label{3417}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 3417 · OPG-37159 · Topology · OpenGarden}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $D^4=\{x\in\mathbb R^4:\|x\|\le1\}$, and let
$\mathcal D=\operatorname{Diff}(D^4\operatorname{ rel}\partial D^4)$
be the group of smooth diffeomorphisms fixing the boundary pointwise.
Give it the $C^\infty$ topology: convergence means uniform convergence
in local charts of every derivative on compact sets, and use the
identity diffeomorphism as base point. This is the boundary-fixing
convention in the source; collar-fixing models must be compared
explicitly when used.

The computational targets are
\[
                 \pi_j(\mathcal D,\mathrm{id})\quad(j\ge1),
       \qquad \pi_0(\mathcal D),\qquad H_j(\mathcal D;\mathbb Z).
\]
Here $\pi_j$ consists of based homotopy classes of maps from $S^j$,
$\pi_0$ records isotopy components, and $H_j$ denotes singular homology.
The source asks for further determinations of these groups as steps
toward the homotopy type of $\operatorname{Diff}(S^4)$, through its
stated relation to $O(5)\times\mathcal D$, with $O(5)$ the orthogonal
matrix group.

This is a computation program, rather than a claim that every group
displayed above vanishes. Specify the degree, coefficients, and whether
a calculation concerns the identity component or the whole space.
Finding the homotopy type would organize all these invariants; an
isolated nonzero class alone would not determine the complete type.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Determine further homotopy and homology groups of the boundary-fixing diffeomorphism group of the four-ball, and hence understand the diffeomorphism space of the four-sphere.
