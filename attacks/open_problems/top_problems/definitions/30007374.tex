\section*{The thermodynamic pressure of every bounded Gaussian Ising perceptron activation}\label{30007374}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For $M=\lfloor\alpha N\rfloor$ independent identically distributed standard Gaussian constraint rows $G_j$ and uniform Ising spins $\sigma$, and every $\alpha>0$ and bounded Borel function $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$, does
\[
N^{-1}\log\mathbb{E}_{\mathrm{uniform\text{-}spin}}
\exp\left(\sum_{j\leq M}f\left(\frac{G_j\cdot\sigma}{\sqrt N}\right)\right)
\]
converge in mean and probability to the hierarchical variational value
\[
\inf_q\{\alpha V_f(q)+S_I(q)\},
\]
with $V_f$ and $S_I$ the explicitly defined cavity and Ising entropy functionals? No activation symmetry, concavity or smallness is assumed.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For every $\alpha>0$ and bounded Borel $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$, with $M=\lfloor\alpha N\rfloor$ independent identically distributed standard Gaussian constraint rows $G_j$ and uniform Ising spins $\sigma$, does $N^{-1}\log\mathbb{E}_{\mathrm{uniform\text{-}spin}}\exp(\sum_{j\leq M}f(G_j\cdot\sigma/\sqrt N))$ converge in mean and probability to $\inf_q\{\alpha V_f(q)+S_I(q)\}$, where $V_f$ and $S_I$ are the explicitly defined cavity and Ising entropy functionals, without assuming activation symmetry, concavity or smallness?
