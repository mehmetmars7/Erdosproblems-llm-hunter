\section{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 5.31}\label{2305031}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2305031 · Analysis}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Write $\mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}$ and $\mathbb T=\partial\mathbb D$. All Taylor coefficients below are taken at $0$. Let $S$ be the class of holomorphic injective functions $g:\mathbb D\to\mathbb C$ with $g(0)=0$ and $g'(0)=1$. Let $\mathcal B$ be the Bloch space of holomorphic functions $h$ with
\[\|h\|_{\mathcal B}=|h(0)|+\sup_{z\in\mathbb D}(1-|z|^2)|h'(z)|<\infty.
\]
For $g\in S$, take the unique holomorphic logarithm $h=\log g'$ with $h(0)=0$, and let $\mathcal B_S=\{\log g':g\in S\}$. Let $\mathcal B_Q=\{\log g':g\in S\text{ admits a quasiconformal extension to }\widehat{\mathbb C}\}$. Such an extension is an orientation-preserving homeomorphism of the Riemann sphere that is locally in $W^{1,2}$ in charts and satisfies $|\partial_{\bar z}G|\le k|\partial_zG|$ almost everywhere for some $k<1$.For $h\in\mathcal B$ with $h(0)=0$, let $g_h(z)=\int_0^z e^{h(\zeta)}\,d\zeta$; it is holomorphic, locally univalent, and normalized. Set
\[R_Q=\sup\{R>0:h(0)=0,\ \|h\|_{\mathcal B}<R\ \Longrightarrow\ h\in\mathcal B_Q\text{ for every }h\in\mathcal B\}.
\]
The source records the Becker inclusion for $R=1$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Is $R_Q=1$? In addition, for $h=\log g'\in\mathcal B_S$, does
\[\limsup_{|z|\to1}(1-|z|^2)|h'(z)|<1\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad h\in\mathcal B_Q
\]
hold without further assumptions on the image domain $g(\mathbb D)$? \sref{2305031}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Is the unit Bloch ball the largest universally quasiconformally extendible logarithmic-derivative ball, and is the corresponding boundary-smallness condition alone sufficient for a univalent map?
