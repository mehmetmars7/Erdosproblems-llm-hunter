\section*{Bounded-metric anticanonical nonvanishing for klt pairs}\label{30007015}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let (W,D) be projective Q-factorial klt with effective rational boundary and nef rational Cartier $P=-(K_W+D)$. If on a smooth projective resolution $\rho $ the pullback $\rho *P$ has a semipositive singular metric with locally bounded weights and $\chi (O_{Wtilde})\neq 0$, must some positive Cartier multiple mP have a nonzero global section?
