\section{Primitive-Root Density Formulas for Rank-One Tori}\label{30000797}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 30000797 · Number Theory}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.


\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $K$ be a number field, let $T$ be a rank-one torus over $K$, and let $A\in T(K)$ be nontorsion. For each prime $p$, put $B_p=\langle T(K),\sqrt[p]{A},Z_p\rangle\subset T(\overline K)$, where $Z_p$ is a nontrivial $p$-torsion point, let $B_\infty$ be generated by all $B_p$, let $E$ be the finite abelian cokernel of $\operatorname{Gal}(K(B_\infty)/K)\to\operatorname{Aut}_{T(K)}(B_\infty)$, and put $A_p=\operatorname{Aut}_{T(K)}(B_p)$. Is the density of primes $\mathfrak q$ of $K$ for which the reduction of $A$ generates $T(\mathbb F_{\mathfrak q})$ equal to $C_{T,A}\prod_p(1-1/\#A_p)$, where $C_{T,A}=\sum_{\chi\in E^\vee}\prod_{p:\,\chi(A_p)\neq1}-1/(\#A_p-1)$?
