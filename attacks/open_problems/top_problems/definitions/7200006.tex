\section{Fröberg conjecture on the Hilbert functions of a set of forms}\label{7200006}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 7200006 · Algebraic Geometry}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Work over $\mathbb C$, as in the source's convention. Fix $n,r\geq1$ and degrees $d_1,\ldots,d_r\geq1$. Let $f_i\in\mathbb C[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$ be generic homogeneous forms of degrees $d_i$: the coefficients are chosen in a suitable nonempty Zariski open subset of the product of coefficient spaces. Set $R=\mathbb C[x_1,\ldots,x_n]/(f_1,\ldots,f_r)$ and $H_R(t)=\sum_{j\geq0}\dim_{\mathbb C}(R_j)t^j$, where $R_j$ is the homogeneous degree-$j$ part. For a series $A(t)=\sum_{j\geq0}a_jt^j$, define $[A(t)]_+=\sum_{j\geq0}b_jt^j$, where $b_j=a_j$ if $a_i\geq0$ for every $i\leq j$, and $b_j=0$ otherwise. Thus truncation starts at the first negative coefficient, if there is one.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every such choice of $n,r,d_1,\ldots,d_r$, do generic forms satisfy
\[H_R(t)=\left[\frac{\prod_{i=1}^r(1-t^{d_i})}{(1-t)^n}\right]_+?\] \sref{7200006}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does the quotient by generic forms have the Hilbert series predicted by the product formula, truncated from its first negative coefficient onward?
