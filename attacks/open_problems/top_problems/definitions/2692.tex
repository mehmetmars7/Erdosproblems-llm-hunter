\section{Kirby Problem 1.33}\label{2692}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2692 · Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


All knots and links below are smooth in the oriented $3$-sphere, up to ambient isotopy, unless another ambient manifold is specified. A link has KR-parity if its triply graded HOMFLYPT Khovanov--Rozansky homology is supported in homological degrees of a single parity: there exists $\epsilon\in\{0,1\}$ such that $H^{i,j,k}=0$ whenever $i\not\equiv\epsilon\pmod2$. A positive link has an all-positive oriented crossing diagram. A quasipositive link is the closure of a braid that is a product of conjugates of positive Artin generators. An algebraic link is the oriented intersection of a reduced complex plane-curve singularity with a sufficiently small $3$-sphere.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Find topological necessary or sufficient criteria for KR-parity. In particular, (a) does KR-parity imply positivity, and does it imply quasipositivity; (b) does every algebraic link have KR-parity? \sref{2692}{1}\sref{2692}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Characterize the single-parity support of HOMFLYPT homology and test its implications for positivity and quasipositivity, as well as its occurrence for algebraic links.
