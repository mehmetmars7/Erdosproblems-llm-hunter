\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.


\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $S=k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$, let $F=(f_1,\ldots,f_r)$ be a homogeneous regular sequence with $2\leq e_1\leq\cdots\leq e_r$, and let $P=(x_1^{e_1},\ldots,x_r^{e_r})$. For every homogeneous ideal $I\supseteq F$, does there exist a unique lex-plus-$P$ ideal $L$ with the same Hilbert function, with $b_{i,j}(S/L)\geq b_{i,j}(S/I)$ for all $i,j$?
