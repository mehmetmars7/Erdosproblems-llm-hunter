\section*{Saturation factor one for even Spin groups}\label{30007004}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For $G=Spin(2n)$ over C, $n\geq 2$, and dominant integral weights $\lambda ,\mu ,\nu $ whose sum belongs to the root lattice, does the existence of a G-invariant in $V(N\lambda )\otimes V(N\mu )\otimes V(N\nu )$ for some integer $N\geq 1$ imply the existence of one in $V(\lambda )\otimes V(\mu )\otimes V(\nu )$?
