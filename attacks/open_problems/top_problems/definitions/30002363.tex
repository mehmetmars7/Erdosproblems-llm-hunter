\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For CM-type abelian varieties, the Lefschetz-class property that all Tate classes on all powers of a reduction, respectively all Hodge classes on all powers over the complex numbers, are generated by degree-one classes is a sufficient condition for the Tate, respectively Hodge, conjecture.
