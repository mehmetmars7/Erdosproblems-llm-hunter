\section*{Uniformly flat real Littlewood polynomials}\label{30007433}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every epsilon in (0,1), is there N0 such that for every integer $N>=N0$ one can choose N real coefficients, each +1 or -1, so that the modulus of their polynomial is between (1-epsilon)sqrt(N) and (1+epsilon)sqrt(N) at every point of the unit circle?
