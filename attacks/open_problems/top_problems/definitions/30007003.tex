\section*{Every irreducible occurs in the staircase tensor square}\label{30007003}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every $m\geq 1$, put $N=m(m+1)/2$ and $\rho =(m,m-1,\dots ,1)$. Does the tensor square of the complex Specht representation $S^\rho $ contain every irreducible representation of $S_N$; equivalently, is $g(\rho ,\rho ,\lambda )>0$ for every partition $\lambda $ of N?
