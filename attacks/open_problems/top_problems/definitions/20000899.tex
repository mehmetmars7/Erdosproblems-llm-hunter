\section*{A variational energy core for stronger BSG}\label{20000899}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 20000899 · AIM-COMBINATORICS-0024 · Combinatorics · AIM Workshop Problem Lists}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $G$ be an abelian group and let $A\subset G$ be finite and nonempty.
For any finite $S\subseteq G$, write
\[
 E(S)=|\{(a,b,c,d)\in S^4:a+b=c+d\}|,
 \qquad e(S)=E(S)/|S|^3
\]
when $S\ne\varnothing$.
All tuples are ordered and repeated entries are allowed.
The sumset $S+S$ consists of distinct sums, so its cardinality measures doubling, whereas $E(S)$ measures their repeated representations.

Given a real parameter $K\geq1$ with $E(A)\geq |A|^3/K$, must there exist a nonempty subset $A'\subseteq A$ satisfying all three inequalities
\[
 |A'|\geq |A|/K^{100},\qquad
 |A'+A'|\leq K^{100}|A'|,\qquad e(A')\geq e(A)?
\]
The workshop further asks whether the same size and doubling conclusions can hold together with the stronger energy gain
\[
 e(A')\geq e(A)\left(\frac{|A|}{|A'|}\right)^{1/100}.
\]
These are universal assertions over $G,A,K$; the subset may depend on all of them.
The first question asks that passing to a structured subset never decrease normalized additive energy.
The second demands a quantified increase when the retained subset is small.
The number $100$ and the exponent $1/100$ are retained from the stated formulation, rather than silently replaced by unspecified constants.
The ambient group need not be finite or torsion-free, since all counts involve only the given finite sets.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can the Balog--Szemerédi--Gowers subset be chosen without losing normalized additive energy, and even with a specified energy gain?
