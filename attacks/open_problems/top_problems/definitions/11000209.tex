{\small UnsolvedMath ID 11000209 · Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.


\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For a planar polygonal billiard, does there always exist a regular periodic trajectory, meaning one that avoids all vertices? If so, does some such trajectory persist under small deformations of the polygon? Determine the asymptotic number of periodic trajectories of bounded length. Describe generic trajectories in generic polygons and decide whether the billiard flow is ergodic. For triangles, genericity is with respect to Lebesgue measure on the open angle simplex.
