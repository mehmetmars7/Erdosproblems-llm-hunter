\section*{\texorpdfstring{Inverse Galois Problem}{Inverse Galois Problem}}\label{20}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A finite extension $L/{\mathbb{Q}}$ is Galois when it is normal and separable. Its Galois group consists of field automorphisms of $L$ fixing ${\mathbb{Q}}$. The inverse problem is
\[
 \forall G\text{ finite}\ \exists L/{\mathbb{Q}}\text{ finite Galois}:\qquad
 \operatorname{Gal}(L/{\mathbb{Q}})\simeq G.
\]
Only abstract group realization is demanded, not a uniform algorithm, a prescribed polynomial degree, or a regular realization over a rational-function field.
\par\smallskip\textit{Formulation and concept references:} \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.08708}{[S1]}.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Does every finite group occur as the symmetry group of some finite Galois extension of the rationals?
