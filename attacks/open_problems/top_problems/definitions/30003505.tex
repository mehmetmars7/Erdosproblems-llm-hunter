\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.


\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $S$ be a Shimura variety and $V\subseteq S$ a subvariety. Call a subvariety $W\subseteq V$ optimal in $V$ if every subvariety $Y$ with $W\subsetneq Y\subseteq V$ has larger defect $\delta(Y)=\dim\langle Y\rangle-\dim Y$, where $\langle Y\rangle$ is the smallest special subvariety containing $Y$. Does $V$ contain only finitely many points that are optimal in $V$?
