\section*{Independent-set hardness with a three-colorability promise}\label{30007487}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every fixed $\delta \in (0,1/3)$, can 3SAT be reduced in deterministic polynomial time to a nonempty finite simple unweighted graph so that satisfiable formulas yield three-colorable graphs and unsatisfiable formulas yield graphs with independence number $<\delta $ times the vertex count?
