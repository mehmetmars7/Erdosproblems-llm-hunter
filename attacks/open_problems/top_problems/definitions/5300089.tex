\section{Critically finite maps with hyperbolic postcritical complement}\label{5300089}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 5300089 · Dynamical Systems}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $\mathbb P^n$ be complex projective $n$-space, with $n>1$. The source calls a rational self-map $f$ critically finite if there are possibly reducible hypersurfaces $V\subset W\subset\mathbb P^n$ for which
\[f:\mathbb P^n\setminus W\longrightarrow\mathbb P^n\setminus V\]
is a covering map. A complex manifold is Kobayashi hyperbolic when its Kobayashi pseudodistance separates distinct points.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Are there nontrivial examples of such critically finite rational maps for which $\mathbb P^n\setminus V$ is Kobayashi hyperbolic? If so, describe their dynamics. \sref{5300089}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Find nontrivial critically finite projective maps with a Kobayashi-hyperbolic postcritical complement, and describe their dynamics.
