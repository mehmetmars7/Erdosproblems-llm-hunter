\section{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 8.11}\label{2308011}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2308011 · Analysis}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $\mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}$ and $\mathbb T=\partial\mathbb D$. The Dirichlet space $\mathcal D$ consists of analytic functions on $\mathbb D$ with \[\|h\|_{\mathcal D}^2=|h(0)|^2+\int_{\mathbb D}|h^{\prime}(z)|^2\,dA(z)<\infty.\] For $h\in\mathcal D$, let $\mathcal Ph=\{ph:p\in\mathbb C[z]\}$, and say $h$ is cyclic if its closure in this norm is all of $\mathcal D$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
If $g\in\mathcal D$ is cyclic, $f\in\mathcal D$, and $|f(z)|\ge|g(z)|$ for every $z\in\mathbb D$, must $f$ also be cyclic? \sref{2308011}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does pointwise modulus domination of a cyclic Dirichlet function preserve cyclicity?
