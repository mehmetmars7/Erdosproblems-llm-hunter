\section*{Kadison–Kaplansky projection conjecture}\label{30007302}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every torsion-free discrete group G, are 0 and 1 the only projections in the scalar reduced group C*-algebra C*r(G)? This concerns the completed complex C*-algebra, rather than the algebraic group ring or only matrix projections.
