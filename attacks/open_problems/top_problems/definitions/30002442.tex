\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Must every smooth Fano contact manifold be homogeneous? Fano means that the anticanonical bundle is ample; a contact structure is a maximally nonintegrable holomorphic hyperplane distribution. Homogeneous means that a complex algebraic group acts transitively.
