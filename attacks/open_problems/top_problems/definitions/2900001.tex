\section{Betti Posets and the Stanley Depth}\label{2900001}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2900001 · Combinatorics}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Fix a field $K$. Let $S=K[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$ and $S'=K[y_1,\ldots,y_m]$, with their fine multigradings, and let $I\subset S$ and $I'\subset S'$ be nonzero proper monomial ideals. For a multigraded module $M$, a Stanley space is a subspace $uK[Z]\subset M$ generated by a homogeneous $u$ and free as a module over the polynomial ring on a subset $Z$ of the variables. A Stanley decomposition is a finite direct sum $M=\bigoplus_j u_jK[Z_j]$ as a $K$-vector space. Define $\operatorname{sdepth}_S M=\max_{\mathcal D}\min_j|Z_j|$ and $\operatorname{spdim}_S M=n-\operatorname{sdepth}_S M$. The Betti poset $\mathcal B(I)$ consists of the monomial multidegrees with nonzero $\operatorname{Tor}_i^S(S/I,K)$ for some $i\ge1$, ordered by monomial divisibility; equivalently these are the positive homological degrees of the minimal fine-graded free resolution. Use the analogous definitions over $S'$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
If $\mathcal B(I)\cong\mathcal B(I')$ as posets, must both equalities hold? \[\operatorname{spdim}_S(S/I)=\operatorname{spdim}_{S'}(S'/I'),\qquad \operatorname{spdim}_S I=\operatorname{spdim}_{S'}I'.\] \sref{2900001}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does the Betti poset determine the Stanley projective dimensions of both a monomial ideal and its quotient ring?
