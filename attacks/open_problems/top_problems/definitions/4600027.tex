\section*{Classify group shifts}\label{4600027}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 4600027 · AMR-045-0027 · Dynamical Systems · AMR Open Problem Lists}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Fix $d\ge1$. A group shift is a closed shift-invariant subgroup
$X\le G^{\mathbb Z^d}$ for some finite group $G$, with the product
topology and coordinate shifts $\sigma^v$. Different finite alphabet
groups and subgroup presentations are allowed. Two group shifts $X,Y$
are topologically conjugate when there is a homeomorphism
\[
 \phi:X\to Y,\qquad \phi\sigma^v=\sigma^v\phi
       \quad(v\in\mathbb Z^d).
\]
The homeomorphism need not preserve multiplication. By compactness,
such conjugacies and their inverses are described by finite local
coding rules on the configurations.

The problem is to classify group shifts up to this equivalence: find
invariants or structural data that agree exactly when such a conjugacy
exists, with a corresponding realization theory. The particularly
emphasized case consists of abelian group shifts with completely positive
entropy. For this condition use normalized Haar measure: every
nontrivial measurable factor must have positive measure entropy.
A factor is trivial when its probability space is a single point modulo
null sets.

The desired equivalence is topological, not merely measure-theoretic or
algebraic. In particular, having equal entropy or mutually factoring
through continuous homomorphisms is not stipulated to imply conjugacy.
The dimension $d$ is part of the action and is held fixed in each
classification problem. Known one-dimensional descriptions motivate the
question, but do not themselves classify higher-rank lattice actions.
The complete problem includes groups without the completely-positive-entropy
restriction as well as the highlighted subclass.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Classify topological conjugacy of group shifts, especially abelian shifts with completely positive entropy.
