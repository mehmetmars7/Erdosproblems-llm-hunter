\section*{Kirby Problem 1.57}\label{2716}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2716 · KP-1.57 · Topology · Kirby's Problems in Low-Dimensional Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $K\subset S^3$ be an oriented smooth knot. Its zero-trace
$X_0(K)=B^4\cup_K(D^2\times D^2)$ is obtained by attaching a two-handle with
the zero framing given by the preferred longitude. Its second integral homology
is infinite cyclic, generated by the two-handle core capped by a Seifert surface
pushed into $B^4$.
The zero-shake genus is the least genus of a closed connected oriented embedded
surface representing a generator of $H_2(X_0(K);\mathbb Z)$. Use smooth
embeddings for $g^0_{\rm sh}(K)$ and locally flat topological embeddings for
$g^{0,\mathrm{TOP}}_{\rm sh}(K)$. A knot is zero-shake slice when the relevant
minimum is zero.

Ask separately in both categories: does zero-shake sliceness imply that $K$
bounds a disk in $B^4$ in that category? The additional question is whether some
knot satisfies
\[
g^{0,\mathrm{TOP}}_{\rm sh}(K)<g^{\mathrm{TOP}}_4(K),
\]
where $g^{\mathrm{TOP}}_4$ minimizes genus of locally flat oriented surfaces
bounded by $K$ in $B^4$. The representative of the trace generator is not
required to meet the two-handle cocore in exactly one point.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does a generator sphere in a knot's zero-trace force a slice disk, and can topological zero-shake genus be smaller than topological slice genus?
