\section*{Worst-case symmetric translative covering density}\label{30007129}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Are there constants $c>0$ and $n_{0}$ such that, for every $n\geq n_{0}$, some origin-symmetric convex body $K_n\subset R^{n}$ has translative covering density greater than c n log n? Translative covering density is the infimum of the asymptotic covering density over arbitrary coverings by translates of $K_n$, with multiplicities counted.
