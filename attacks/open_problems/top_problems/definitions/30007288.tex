\section*{Unrestricted odd two-adic pro-modularity over Q}\label{30007288}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every continuous absolutely irreducible odd representation r of Gal(Qbar/Q) in GL2(E), where E is a finite extension of Q2, unramified outside finitely many finite primes, is there an odd level N containing every odd ramified prime and a continuous Z2-algebra map from the full prime-to-2N, all-weight level-Gamma1(N) Hecke algebra to the integers of E that sends $T_l$ to $\operatorname{tr}(r(Frob_l))$ and l $S_l$ to $det(r(Frob_l))$ at every l not dividing 2N? No de Rham condition is imposed.
