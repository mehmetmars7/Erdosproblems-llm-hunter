\section{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 6.12}\label{2306012}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2306012 · Analysis}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $\mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}$, $\mathbb T=\partial\mathbb D$, and $\mathbb D^*=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|>1\}$. Let $S$ be the class of analytic injective functions $f:\mathbb D\to\mathbb C$ normalized by $f(0)=0$, $f^{\prime}(0)=1$, with expansion $f(z)=z+\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}a_nz^n$ and $a_1=1$. Let the nonzero Taylor terms after $z$ occur at strictly increasing integer exponents $n_k\ge2$. The source gives the following implications: \[\liminf_{k\to\infty}\frac{n_{k+1}}{n_k}>1\ \Longrightarrow\ a_n=o(n^{-1}),\qquad \liminf_{k\to\infty}(n_{k+1}-n_k)>4\ \Longrightarrow\ a_n=o(n^{-1/2}).\] Coefficients outside the support are zero.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Are there intermediate conditions on the exponent gaps that yield coefficient-decay estimates between the two displayed implications? Find such conditions and estimates. \sref{2306012}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Find gap conditions between additive separation and Hadamard separation that give intermediate coefficient-decay estimates for normalized univalent functions.
