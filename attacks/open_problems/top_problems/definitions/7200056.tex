\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For a finite planar point set, a $k$-set is a subset of $k$ points separable from the others by a line. Determine matching upper and lower bounds for the maximum number of $k$-sets, and for the number of halving lines that split the remaining points equally.
