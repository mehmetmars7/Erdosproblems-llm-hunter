\section*{Recovery of both metric and U(2) connection from one boundary patch}\label{30007330}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For a compact connected smooth manifold M of dimension $n\geq 3$ with smooth boundary and one nonempty open patch Gamma, do equal zero-energy connection-Laplacian Dirichlet-to-Neumann pairings on compactly supported Gamma data determine smooth g and smooth u(2)-valued connection A up to a diffeomorphism Phi and a smooth unitary gauge U fixing Gamma? Explicitly ask $g_2=Phi^*g_1$ and $A_2=U^{-1}(Phi^*A_1)U+U^{-1}dU$, with $Phi|\Gamma =id$ and $U|\Gamma =I$.
