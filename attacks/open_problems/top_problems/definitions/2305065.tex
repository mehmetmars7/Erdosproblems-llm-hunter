{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2305065 · Analysis}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $\mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}$ and $\mathbb T=\partial\mathbb D$. The disc algebra $A(\overline{\mathbb D})$ consists of the functions continuous on $\overline{\mathbb D}$ and analytic in $\mathbb D$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Does there exist a nonconstant $f\in A(\overline{\mathbb D})$ such that $f(e^{i\theta})\in f(\mathbb D)$ for Lebesgue-almost every $\theta\in[0,2\pi)$? The source update reports affirmative constructions, including one by Gol\textquotesingle dberg. \sref{2305065}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can a nonconstant disc-algebra function take almost every boundary value also at an interior point? The original update reports affirmative constructions.
