\section*{Unique multiplicity-one tangents at first surface-flow singularities}\label{30007141}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For a smooth compact connected embedded surface without boundary in $R^{3}$, must the full family of parabolic rescalings at every singular point of the first maximal smooth mean-curvature-flow time converge, in the original coordinates and locally smoothly with multiplicity one at all negative times, to one smooth properly embedded self-shrinker flow?
