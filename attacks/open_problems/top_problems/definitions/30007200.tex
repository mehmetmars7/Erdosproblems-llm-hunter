\section*{Strong Ulam stability characterizes amenability}\label{30007200}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For a countable discrete group G, does amenability follow from this property: for every $\varepsilon >0$ there exists $\delta >0$ such that, on every complex Hilbert space H, each normalized map $\mu :G\to U(H)$ with $sup_{g,h}\Vert \mu (gh)-\mu (g)\mu (h)\Vert \leq \delta $ is within $\varepsilon $ uniformly on G of a genuine unitary representation on that same H?
