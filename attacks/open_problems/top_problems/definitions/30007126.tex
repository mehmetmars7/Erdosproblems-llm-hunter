\section*{Finite cylinder approximation of ruled sets}\label{30007126}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $D\subset\mathbb{R}^2$ be compact, let $V$ be $C^1$ near $D$, and let $L,h>0$. If $\operatorname{tr}DV=0$ and $-L^{-2}<\det DV\leq0$ on $D$, can
\[
E=\{(s,p+sV(p)):p\in D,\ |s|\leq L\}
\]
be covered, for each $\varepsilon>0$, by finitely many cylinders
\[
\operatorname{Cyl}(T_i,g_i)=\{(s,\xi):\xi\in T_i+sg_i\},
\]
with compact nondegenerate square tiles $T_i$, such that
\[
\sum_i\frac{|T_i|}{\sqrt{1+g_{i,1}^2+h^2g_{i,2}^2}}
\leq\int_D(1+V_1^2+h^2V_2^2)^{-1/2}+\varepsilon?
\]
Under $(s,\xi_1,\xi_2)\mapsto(s,\xi_1,h\xi_2)$, this is physical perpendicular-base cost divided by $h$.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For every compact $D\subset\mathbb{R}^2$, every $C^1$ field $V$ near $D$, and $L,h>0$ satisfying $\operatorname{tr}DV=0$ and $-L^{-2}<\det DV\leq0$ on $D$, can $\{(s,p+sV(p)):p\in D,|s|\leq L\}$, for each $\varepsilon>0$, be covered by finitely many $\operatorname{Cyl}(T_i,g_i)=\{(s,\xi):\xi\in T_i+sg_i\}$ with compact nondegenerate square tiles $T_i$ and cost $\sum_i|T_i|/\sqrt{1+g_{i,1}^2+h^2g_{i,2}^2}\leq\int_D(1+V_1^2+h^2V_2^2)^{-1/2}+\varepsilon$? This cost is the physical perpendicular-base cost divided by $h$ under $(s,\xi_1,\xi_2)\mapsto(s,\xi_1,h\xi_2)$.
