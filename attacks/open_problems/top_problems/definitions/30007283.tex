\section*{Many primes with power-smooth predecessors}\label{30007283}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For each fixed delta in (0,1/4), does the number of primes p in (2x,5x] whose predecessor p-1 has no prime factor exceeding $x^\delta $ grow at least as $x^(1-o(1))$ as x tends to infinity? The error exponent may depend on delta.
