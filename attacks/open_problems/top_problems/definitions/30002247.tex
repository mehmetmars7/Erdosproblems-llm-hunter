\section{Variance Conjecture for Log-Concave Random Vectors}\label{30002247}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 30002247 · Probability}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.


\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Does there exist an absolute constant $C$ such that every centered log-concave random vector $X\in\mathbb R^n$ satisfies $\operatorname{Var}(|X|^2)\leq C\lambda_X^2\mathbb E|X|^2$, where $\lambda_X$ is the largest eigenvalue of its covariance matrix?
