\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every nonempty finite lattice $\Lambda$, do there exist a finite group $G$ and $H\leq G$ such that $\Lambda$ is isomorphic to the inclusion-ordered lattice of all subgroups between $H$ and $G$?
