\section{Kirby Problem 4.78}\label{2954}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2954 · Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $X$ be a closed simply connected smooth four-manifold, choose a smooth embedded four-ball, and put $X^{\circ}=X\setminus\operatorname{int}(D^4)$. For $N\ge0$ set
\[
M_N=X^{\circ}\#N(S^2\times S^2),\qquad G_N=\operatorname{Diff}_{\partial}(M_N).
\]
Here $\operatorname{Diff}_{\partial}$ has the smooth topology and consists of diffeomorphisms that are the identity near the boundary. Form each connected sum in a newly attached boundary collar, so extension by the identity defines a stabilization homomorphism $s_N:G_N\to G_{N+1}$. Let $BG_N$ be the classifying space, and let $H_k(-;\mathbb Q)$ denote singular homology with rational coefficients.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every $X$ as above and every $k\ge0$, does there exist $N_0=N_0(X,k)$ such that for every $N\ge N_0$ the stabilization map induces an isomorphism
\[
(Bs_N)_*:H_k(BG_N;\mathbb Q)\longrightarrow H_k(BG_{N+1};\mathbb Q)?
\]
The original source specifies the boundary-fixed group of the punctured manifold; this is the intended meaning of its homological-stability question. \sref{2954}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Do the rational homology groups of the classifying spaces of boundary-fixed diffeomorphisms eventually stabilize when a punctured simply connected four-manifold is repeatedly summed with the product of two spheres?
