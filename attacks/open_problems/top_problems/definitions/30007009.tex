\section*{All ordinary Kähler klt fourfold programs terminate}\label{30007009}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For a normal globally Weil-Q-factorial compact Kähler fourfold with effective rational klt boundary and rational Cartier canonical adjoint, does every maximal ordinary negative-ray program terminate on the original input, retaining klt and global Weil Q-factoriality, with projective contractions of relative Bott–Chern dimension one? A pseudo-effective input must end nef; otherwise the endpoint must have a projective lower-dimensional Mori fiber contraction. Global strong Q-factoriality, if imposed, must persist.
