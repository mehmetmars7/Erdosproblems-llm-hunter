\section*{Zero-entropy probability system with no smooth positive-volume model}\label{30007175}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Does there exist an invertible ergodic measure-preserving transformation on a standard nonatomic probability space, with zero Kolmogorov-Sinai entropy, which is not measure-theoretically isomorphic to any C-infinity diffeomorphism preserving a strictly positive smooth probability density on any finite-dimensional compact smooth manifold, including nonorientable manifolds and manifolds with boundary?
