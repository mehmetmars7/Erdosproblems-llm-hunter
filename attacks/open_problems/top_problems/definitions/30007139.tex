\section*{Conditional L² Einstein gap for nonnegative curvature}\label{30007139}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Assume that every connected smooth closed Riemannian four-manifold (N,h) with $Ric_h=3h$ and $sec_h\geq 0$ has universal Riemannian cover isometric to round $S^{4}$ of curvature 1, Fubini–Study $CP^{2}$ with $Ric=3g$, or $S^{2}(1/\surd 3)×S^{2}(1/\surd 3)$. Does there then exist $\varepsilon >0$ such that every simply connected closed smooth Riemannian four-manifold (M,g) with $sec_g\geq 0$ and $\int |Ric_g-(Scal_g/4)g|^{2}dvol_g<\varepsilon $ is diffeomorphic to $S^{4}$, $CP^{2}$ or $S^{2}×S^{2}$, without a prescribed orientation?
