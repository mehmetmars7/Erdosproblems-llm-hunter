\section*{Kirby Problem 2.32}\label{2780}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2780 · KP-2.32 · Topology · Kirby's Problems in Low-Dimensional Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $F=S_g$ be a closed oriented surface of genus $g\geq2$ and let
$p:E\to B$ be a smooth oriented $F$-bundle. An Ehresmann connection is a
subbundle $\mathcal H\subset TE$ complementary to the vertical tangent bundle
$\ker(dp)$. It is flat if $\mathcal H$ is integrable, so its integral leaves
form a foliation transverse to every fiber.

Equivalently, a flat bundle is a suspension
\[
 (\widetilde B\times F)/\pi_1(B),\qquad
 \gamma\cdot(b,x)=(\gamma b,\widetilde\rho(\gamma)x),
\]
for a homomorphism $\widetilde\rho:\pi_1(B)\to\operatorname{Diff}^+(F)$.
The original bundle has monodromy
$\rho:\pi_1(B)\to\operatorname{Mod}(F)$, and flatness asks for such a
diffeomorphism-valued lift that realizes its bundle isomorphism class.

Does every oriented surface bundle over a closed oriented surface admit a
flat connection? Does the same hold when the base is a closed three-manifold?
These are existence questions for \emph{some} connection on each bundle, not
for flatness of an initially chosen connection. The higher-genus fiber setting
is essential to the mapping-class formulation; sphere and torus bundles have
additional classification issues and are not implicitly used to redefine it.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can every higher-genus surface bundle over a surface, or over a three-manifold, be equipped with a flat connection?
