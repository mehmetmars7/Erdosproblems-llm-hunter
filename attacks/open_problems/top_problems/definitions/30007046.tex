\section*{Rational effectivity modulo a controlled boundary}\label{30007046}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let Y be smooth projective complex of positive dimension and C an effective integral divisor. If $-K_Y+C$ has a singular Hermitian metric whose curvature dominates a Kähler form and whose multiplier ideal contains $O_Y(-C)$, must every pseudo-effective rational divisor M admit an effective rational correction $C_{0}$ supported on C such that $M+C_{0}$ is Q-linearly equal to an effective rational divisor?
