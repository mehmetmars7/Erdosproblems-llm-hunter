\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For a conformal map $f:\mathbb D\to\mathbb D$, let its integral-means exponent be
\[\beta_f(p)=\limsup_{r\uparrow1}\frac{\log\int_0^{2\pi}|f'(re^{i\theta})|^p\,d\theta}{\log(1/(1-r))},\qquad B(p)=\sup_f\beta_f(p).\]
Is the universal spectrum equal to $-p-1$ for $p\leq-2$, to $p^2/4$ for $-2\leq p\leq2$, and to $p-1$ for $p\geq2$?
