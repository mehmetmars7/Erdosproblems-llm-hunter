\section*{Logarithmic Kodaira additivity under stratum smoothness}\label{30007043}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $f:X\to Y$ be a connected-fiber surjective morphism of smooth connected projective complex varieties. Let E and D be reduced SNC divisors on X and Y with $Supp(f*D)\subset Supp(E)$. Assume f and every boundary stratum of E are smooth over Y minus Supp(D). For a very general fiber F with $E_F=E|F$, must $\kappa (X,K_X+E)=\kappa (Y,K_Y+D)+\kappa (F,K_F+E_F)$, with $-\infty $ absorbing in the sum?
