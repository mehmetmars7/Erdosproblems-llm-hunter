\section{Exact Connective Constants for Self-Avoiding Walks}\label{2159}

{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2159 · Combinatorics}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

fields, $[N]=\{1,\ldots,\lfloor N\rfloor\}$, and $\log$ is the natural

$f\gg g$ reverses this comparison; $f=o(g)$ means $f/g\to0$;

A walk takes unit steps $\pm e_j$ and never visits a vertex twice. Count all such length-$n$ walks from the origin, without quotienting by rotations or reflections. The constant is the exponential growth per step in a fixed lattice dimension. \sref{2159}{1} \sref{2159}{2}

\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $f(n,k)$ count the number of self-avoiding walks of $n$ steps (beginning at the origin) in $\mathbb{Z}^k$ (i.e. those walks which do not intersect themselves). Determine $ C_k=\lim_{n\to\infty}f(n,k)^{1/n}. $ \sref{2159}{1}

\subsection{Short English statement}

What is the exact exponential growth rate of the number of self-avoiding nearest-neighbour lattice walks in each dimension?
