\section{Pointwise Convergence of Polynomial Ergodic Averages}\label{30006454}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.


\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $(X,\mathcal{B},\mu)$ be a probability space with commuting invertible measure-preserving transformations $T_1,\ldots,T_m$, and let $P_1,\ldots,P_m\in\mathbb{Z}[y]$. For $f_1,\ldots,f_m\in L^\infty(X)$, do the averages $\frac{1}{N}\sum_{n=1}^N\prod_{i=1}^m f_i(T_i^{P_i(n)}x)$ converge pointwise almost everywhere as $N\to\infty$?
