\section*{Bases of projective Lagrangian fibrations}\label{30007056}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For $n\geq 1$, let X be a compact irreducible holomorphic symplectic Kähler manifold of dimension 2n and $f:X\to B$ a projective surjective holomorphic map with connected fibers to a normal projective n-fold. If every irreducible component of every fiber has dimension n and its smooth locus is isotropic for the symplectic form, must B be isomorphic to $P^n$?
