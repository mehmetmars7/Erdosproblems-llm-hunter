\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For an ample line bundle $L$ on a projective variety $X$, what tensor power $L^{\otimes\ell}$ is guaranteed to be globally generated or very ample? In particular, does Fujita's conjecture hold for a smooth $n$-dimensional projective $X$, with $\omega_X\otimes L^{\otimes\ell}$ globally generated for $\ell\geq n+1$ and very ample for $\ell\geq n+2$?
