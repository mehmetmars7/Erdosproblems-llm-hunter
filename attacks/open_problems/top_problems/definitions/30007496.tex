\section*{Zero dimension exponent for well-conditioned sampling queries}\label{30007496}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For $V\in C^{2}(R^d)$ with $V(0)=0$, $\nabla V(0)=0$ and $I\leq \nabla ^{2}V(x)\leq 2I$ everywhere, can its Gibbs law be sampled to total-variation error $\leq 1/10$ using at most $C_\varepsilon d^\varepsilon $ exact $(V,\nabla V)$ oracle queries on every execution, for each fixed $\varepsilon >0$, with unrestricted real computation between queries? Is the optimal query budget also at least c log d?
