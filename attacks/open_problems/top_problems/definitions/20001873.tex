\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Does every complete Riemannian manifold of finite volume have a nonconstant closed geodesic? Must it in fact have infinitely many geometrically distinct closed geodesics? The source additionally asks analogous questions for finite-volume Finsler metrics, distinguishing reversible metrics and the noncompact irreversible case.
