\section{Leopoldt's Conjecture}\label{1508}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 1508 · Number Theory}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let \(K\) be a number field with ring of integers \(\mathcal O_K\),
and let \(p\) be a rational prime. For each place \(v\) of \(K\)
above \(p\), write \(K_v\) for the completion and \(\log_v\)
for the \(p\)-adic logarithm on its unit group. Near \(1\), it is
defined by \(\log_v(1+z)=\sum_{j\ge1}(-1)^{j+1}z^j/j\), and
it extends to units by raising to a sufficiently large power and
dividing the resulting logarithm by that power.
The diagonal logarithmic map, extended \(\mathbb Q_p\)-linearly, is
\[
 \mathcal L_{K,p}:\mathcal O_K^\times\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb Q_p
       \longrightarrow\bigoplus_{v\mid p}K_v,
 \qquad u\otimes a\longmapsto(a\log_v u)_{v\mid p}.
\]
If \(r_1\) is the number of real embeddings and \(r_2\) the number
of conjugate pairs of complex embeddings, the domain has dimension
\(r_1+r_2-1\). Roots of unity disappear after tensoring with \(\mathbb Q_p\).
The regulator nonvanishing assertion means that the images of a
basis of units modulo torsion have this full \(\mathbb Q_p\)-rank.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every number field \(K\) and every rational prime \(p\),
is \(\mathcal L_{K,p}\) injective? Equivalently, is
\[
 \dim_{\mathbb Q_p}\operatorname{im}\mathcal L_{K,p}=r_1+r_2-1
\]
always true? \sref{1508}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Do independent global units remain independent under the diagonal local p-adic logarithm, for every number field and every prime?
