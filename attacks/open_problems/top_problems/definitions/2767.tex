\section*{Kirby Problem 2.19}\label{2767}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2767 · KP-2.19 · Topology · Kirby's Problems in Low-Dimensional Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $S$ be an orientable finite-type surface of negative Euler characteristic
and $f:S\to S$ an orientation-preserving homeomorphism. Its mapping torus is
\[
M_f=S\times[0,1]/(x,1)\sim(f(x),0).
\]
Restrict to pseudo-Anosov mapping classes: these preserve a pair of transverse
measured foliations, expanding one and contracting the other by a factor
$\lambda>1$. Their mapping tori have complete finite-volume hyperbolic
structures on their interiors. Write $M_f=\mathbb H^3/\Gamma_f$ using its
holonomy group $\Gamma_f\leq\operatorname{PSL}_2(\mathbb C)$.

A finite-volume hyperbolic $3$-manifold is arithmetic when its lattice is
commensurable, up to conjugacy, with the image of the norm-one elements of an
order in a quaternion algebra over a number field having one complex place,
ramified at every real place. Groups are commensurable if their intersection
has finite index in both.

Characterize exactly the mapping classes $[f]$ whose mapping tori are
arithmetic. The desired criteria should be expressed through monodromy or
mapping-class data, and should distinguish the arithmetic classes from other
pseudo-Anosov classes. Both closed fibers and punctured finite-type fibers
are included; conjugate mapping classes give equivalent bundle monodromies.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Which surface monodromies produce arithmetic hyperbolic mapping tori?
