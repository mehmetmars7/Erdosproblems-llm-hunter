\section*{Polynomial-bond PEPS approximation of every gapped square-grid ground vector}\label{30007224}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Fix site dimension $q\geq 2$ and bounds $J,Delta>0$. For every L-by-L square-grid Hamiltonian with site and nearest-neighbor terms of norm at most J, a unique normalized ground vector and global spectral gap at least Delta, does there exist a PEPS of maximum bond dimension at most C $L^c$ whose normalized vector is within $L^{-1}$, up to global phase, of that ground vector, with C,c depending only on q,J,Delta?
