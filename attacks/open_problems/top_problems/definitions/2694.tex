\section{Kirby Problem 1.35}\label{2694}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2694 · Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


All knots and links below are smooth in the oriented $3$-sphere, up to ambient isotopy, unless another ambient manifold is specified. The integral symplectic Khovanov group $\mathrm{Kh}_{\mathrm{symp}}(K;\mathbb Z)$ is the Seidel--Smith Floer-theoretic invariant obtained from matching Lagrangians in transverse slices to nilpotent orbits. Write $\mathrm{Kh}(K;\mathbb Z)$ for ordinary integral Khovanov homology and $\mathrm{Kh}_{\mathrm{odd}}(K;\mathbb Z)$ for the odd integral theory of Ozsv\'ath--Rasmussen--Szab\'o, whose circle-state algebra uses exterior rather than ordinary Frobenius multiplication. Grading comparisons use the conventional total grading in the symplectic construction.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
(a) Is integral symplectic Khovanov homology isomorphic to integral ordinary Khovanov homology? (b) Construct an odd symplectic Khovanov theory, a Floer-theoretic counterpart of odd Khovanov homology, as requested in the original problem. \sref{2694}{1}\sref{2694}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Compare the symplectic and combinatorial theories over the integers and build a symplectic counterpart of odd Khovanov homology.
