\section*{Weak Hessian bounds along every RCD geodesic}\label{30007155}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
In a complete separable full-support RCD(K,N) metric measure space with finite $N>1$ and measure finite on bounded sets, let F be bounded and globally Lipschitz and G bounded and continuous. If Hess $F\leq G\cdot g$ weakly against compact test gradients and nonnegative compactly supported Lipschitz weights, does each constant-speed minimizing geodesic $\gamma :[0,1]\to X$ satisfy $(F\circ \gamma )\prime\prime \leq d(\gamma (0),\gamma (1))^{2}G\circ \gamma $ in distributions?
