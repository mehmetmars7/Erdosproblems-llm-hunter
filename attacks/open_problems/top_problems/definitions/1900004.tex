\section{Geometry of Continued Fractions — Faces of sails}\label{1900004}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 1900004 · Geometry}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


For an integer $n\ge1$, a lattice $n$-simplex is the convex hull $P=\operatorname{conv}\{v_0,\ldots,v_n\}$ of $n+1$ affinely independent points in $\mathbb Z^n$. It is \emph{empty} when
\[P\cap\mathbb Z^n=\{v_0,\ldots,v_n\};\]
thus extra lattice points on the boundary, as well as in the interior, are excluded. Two such simplices are integer congruent if $P'=UP+a$ for some $U\in\operatorname{GL}_n(\mathbb Z)$ and $a\in\mathbb Z^n$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every dimension $n\ge1$, classify all empty lattice $n$-simplices up to integer congruence. \sref{1900004}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Classify lattice simplices that contain no lattice points except their vertices, allowing every dimension and identifying simplices related by an invertible integer affine change of coordinates.
