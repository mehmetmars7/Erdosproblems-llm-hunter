\section{A new-Lefschetz defect reduction for rational Hodge pairings}\label{20000420}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 20000420 · Number Theory}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.


\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $A$ be a CM abelian variety over $\overline{\mathbb Q}$ with good reduction $A_0$ in characteristic $p$, and let $c$ be an absolute Hodge class on $A$. For a complementary-dimensional Lefschetz class $x$ on $A_0$, the specialization pairing $x\cdot\overline c$ lies in $\mathbb Q_\ell$ for every $\ell\neq p$. Must it be rational and independent of $\ell$? The question also asks for the corresponding property for arbitrary abelian varieties with good reduction.
