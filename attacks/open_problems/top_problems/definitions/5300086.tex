\section{Haken-type decomposition for rational maps}\label{5300086}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 5300086 · Dynamical Systems}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


For a rational map $f:\widehat{\mathbb C}\to\widehat{\mathbb C}$ of degree at least two, $J(f)$ is the complement of its Fatou set, where the iterates form a normal family. If $C(f)$ is its set of critical points, set $P(f)=\bigcup_{n\geq1}f^n(C(f))$. In the source convention $f$ is geometrically finite when $\overline{P(f)}\cap J(f)$ is finite; parabolic cycles are allowed.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Develop an analogue, for geometrically finite rational maps, of the Haken decomposition of three-manifolds. In particular, when $J(f)$ is disconnected, can $f$ be constructed by surgery from rational maps with connected Julia sets? \sref{5300086}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Develop a Haken-type decomposition for these rational maps, including construction of maps with disconnected Julia sets by surgery from maps with connected Julia sets.
