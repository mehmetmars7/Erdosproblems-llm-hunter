\section{Algebraic Stories — Exterior algebras}\label{1500024}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 1500024 · Algebra}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $E=\bigwedge_{\mathbb C}\mathbb C^n$ and $S=\mathbb C[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$, each graded by degree. Choose generic $f,g\in E_2$ and generic $\ell_1,\ell_2\in S_1$. For $s\geq0$, put $w=n+2-2s$ and $h=n+2$. When $w\geq0$, let $a(n,s)$ count the lattice paths from $(0,0)$ to $(w,h)$ staying in $[0,w]\times[0,h]$, whose successive steps are $(1,1)$ or $(-1,1)$. Put $a(n,s)=0$ when $w<0$. For a graded algebra $A$ write $\operatorname{Hilb}_A(t)=\sum_{s\geq0}\dim_{\mathbb C}A_s\,t^s$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Are both identities
\[\operatorname{Hilb}_{E/(f,g)}(t)=\operatorname{Hilb}_{S/(x_1^2,\ldots,x_n^2,\ell_1^2,\ell_2^2)}(t)=\sum_{s\geq0}a(n,s)t^s
\]
true for every $n\geq1$? \sref{1500024}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Do the two stated generic quadratic quotients have the same Hilbert series, with every coefficient given by the specified lattice-path count?
