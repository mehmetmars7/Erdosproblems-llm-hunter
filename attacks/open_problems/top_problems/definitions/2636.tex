\section*{Kourovka Notebook Problem 21.127}\label{2636}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2636 · KOU-21.127 · Group Theory · Kourovka Notebook - New Problems, Issue 21}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $\Gamma=(V,E)$ be a finite simple graph. Its right-angled Artin group is
\[
 A_\Gamma=\langle v\ (v\in V)\mid uv=vu\text{ whenever }\{u,v\}\in E\rangle.
\]
Thus each graph vertex is a group generator, and the only defining relations are commutation relations for adjacent vertices. Put $G=A_\Gamma$ and use $[x,y]=x^{-1}y^{-1}xy$.

The commutator subgroup $[G,G]$ is generated by all commutators. For $g\in[G,G]$, its commutator length $\operatorname{cl}_G(g)$ is the least integer $r\ge0$ such that $g$ is a product of $r$ commutators of elements of $G$, with $\operatorname{cl}_G(1)=0$. Define the stable commutator length
\[
 \operatorname{scl}_G(g)=\lim_{n\to\infty}
 \frac{\operatorname{cl}_G(g^n)}{n}
 =\inf_{n\ge1}\frac{\operatorname{cl}_G(g^n)}{n}.
\]
The limit and the equality with the infimum follow from subadditivity of the numerator as a function of $n$.

Is $\operatorname{scl}_G(g)$ rational for every finite graph $\Gamma$ and every $g\in[A_\Gamma,A_\Gamma]$? The question includes zero as a rational value and asks only for rationality, with no common bound on denominators. The commutators in the definition may use arbitrary elements of the whole ambient Artin group. Stable commutator length is therefore taken in $G$, rather than intrinsically in the cyclic subgroup generated by $g$ or in a chosen smaller subgroup.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Is stable commutator length rational on the commutator subgroup of every right-angled Artin group?
