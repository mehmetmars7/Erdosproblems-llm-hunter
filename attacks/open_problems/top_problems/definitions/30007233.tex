\section*{Every extremizer of the sharp matrix Lieb–Thirring bound}\label{30007233}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Fix $m\geq1$ and $1/2<\gamma<3/2$. Let $W:\mathbb R\to M_m(\mathbb C)$ be measurable, Hermitian, and nonnegative, with $\int_{\mathbb R}\operatorname{tr}(W^{\gamma+1/2})\,dx<\infty$. Define $H=-d^2/dx^2-W$ on $L^2(\mathbb R;\mathbb C^m)$, and put
\[C_\gamma=\left(\frac{\gamma-1/2}{\gamma+1/2}\right)^{\gamma-1/2}\frac{\Gamma(\gamma+1)}{\sqrt\pi\,\Gamma(\gamma+3/2)}.\]
Does equality in $\operatorname{Tr}(H_-^\gamma)\leq C_\gamma\int_{\mathbb R}\operatorname{tr}(W^{\gamma+1/2})\,dx$ hold exactly when a constant unitary change of basis makes $W(x)$ a diagonal collection of zero channels and channels
\[(r+1)a_j^2\operatorname{sech}^2(r a_j(x-x_j)),\qquad r=(\gamma-1/2)^{-1},\quad a_j>0,\quad x_j\in\mathbb R?\]
Each nonzero scalar channel is required to have exactly one negative eigenvalue, equal to $-a_j^2$.
