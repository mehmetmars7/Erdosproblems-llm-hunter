\section*{Holomorphic curves with bounded spherical derivative}\label{3700041}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 3700041 · AMR-036-0041 · Analysis · AMR Open Problem Lists}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $n\geq2$ and let $f=[F_0:\cdots:F_n]$
be a holomorphic map from $\mathbb C$ to
$\mathbb P^n$, with entire coordinates having no
common zero. Define
\[
 \|f'\|^2=
 \frac{\sum_{i<j}|F_i'F_j-F_iF_j'|^2}
                  {(\sum_i|F_i|^2)^2}.
\]
Suppose $\|f'\|(z)\leq C|z|^\sigma$ for
$|z|\geq1$, with some $\sigma>-1$ and
finite $C$.
Let $H_1,\ldots,H_q$ be distinct hyperplanes
in general position: every at most $n+1$
of their defining linear forms are independent.
Require that $f(\mathbb C)$ is contained in
none of these hyperplanes.

Write $g_j=\ell_j(F)$ for a defining
linear form and let $n_j(t)$ count its
zeros in $|z|\leq t$ with multiplicity.
Put
\[
 N_j(r)=n_j(0)\log r+
           \int_0^r\frac{n_j(t)-n_j(0)}t\,dt,\qquad
 T(r)=\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}\log\|F(re^{it})\|\,dt
                                      -\log\|F(0)\|.
\]

Must there be constants $C',R>0$, depending
on the curve and hyperplanes, such that
\[
          \sum_{j=1}^q N_j(r)
                \geq(q+1-n)T(r)-C'r^{\sigma+1}
                       \quad(r\geq R)?
\]
No exceptional set of radii is allowed.
All intersection multiplicities are counted;
the functions are not truncated counting functions.
Linear nondegeneracy relative to every possible
hyperplane is not assumed, only avoidance of
containment in the specified targets. The
growth condition controls the spherical derivative
throughout the plane at infinity.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does a polynomial bound on the spherical derivative force the strengthened hyperplane-counting inequality with the stated error term?
