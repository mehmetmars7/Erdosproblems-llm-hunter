\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Nagata's conjecture asks whether, for $r\geq 10$ and sufficiently general points $x_1,\ldots,x_r$ in $\mathbb{P}^2$, the multipoint Seshadri constant of $\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^2}(1)$ equals $1/\sqrt r$.
