\section{The Hopf Conjectures}\label{1210}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 1210 · Geometry}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $(M,g)$ be a smooth connected closed Riemannian manifold of dimension $2m$, where $m\geq1$ and closed means compact without boundary. For a two-dimensional linear subspace $\sigma\subset T_pM$, let $K_g(\sigma)$ be its sectional curvature. Write $\chi(M)=\sum_{j=0}^{2m}(-1)^j\dim_{\mathbb Q}H_j(M;\mathbb Q)$ for the Euler characteristic.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Do the following two assertions hold for every such manifold?
\[
\bigl(K_g(\sigma)>0\text{ for every }p,\sigma\bigr)\Longrightarrow\chi(M)>0,
\]
\[
\bigl(K_g(\sigma)<0\text{ for every }p,\sigma\bigr)\Longrightarrow(-1)^m\chi(M)>0.
\]
These are respectively the positive- and negative-sectional-curvature Hopf sign questions. \sref{1210}{2}

\subsection{Short English statement}
Must positive sectional curvature force positive Euler characteristic, and must negative sectional curvature force the dimension-dependent alternating sign?
