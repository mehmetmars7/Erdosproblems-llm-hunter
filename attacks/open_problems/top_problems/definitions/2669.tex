\section*{Kirby Problem 1.10}\label{2669}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2669 · KP-1.10 · Topology · Kirby's Problems in Low-Dimensional Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $L=K_1\cup\cdots\cup K_n\subset S^3$ be a smooth framed link, $n\geq1$.
Zero framing on $K_i$ is its preferred longitude, characterized by linking number
zero with $K_i$. Simultaneous zero-framed Dehn surgery replaces every tubular
neighborhood with a solid torus whose meridian is glued to that longitude.
Suppose that the resulting manifold is
\[
S^3_0(L)\cong\mathop{\#}_{i=1}^n(S^1\times S^2).
\]
A framed handleslide replaces one component with a band sum with a parallel
copy of another, using its specified framing and transporting the framing in
the usual band sum. Framed isotopies and these slides preserve the surgery
manifold.

The Generalized Property R Conjecture asks whether a finite sequence of such
slides and isotopies always turns $L$ into the $n$-component zero-framed unlink.
An unlink bounds $n$ pairwise disjoint embedded disks in $S^3$.
The number of link components must stay $n$: adjoining auxiliary surgery
components is not one of the permitted moves in this formulation. This specifies
the unstabilized conjecture attached to the catalogue entry.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can every zero-framed link giving a connected sum of $S^1\times S^2$ be slid to the zero-framed unlink?
