\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Can every nonsingular equation $w\in\Gamma*F_n$ with coefficients in a group $\Gamma$ be solved over $\Gamma$? Also, for every nontrivial word $w\in F_2$, does there exist $n_0$ such that the word map $w:\operatorname{PU}(n)\times\operatorname{PU}(n)\to\operatorname{PU}(n)$ is surjective for every $n\geq n_0$?
