\section*{Ordinary binary affine Liouville correlations}\label{30007270}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $\Omega(n)$ count prime factors with multiplicity and put $\lambda(n)=(-1)^{\Omega(n)}$ for positive integers $n$. Fix integers $a_1,a_2\ge1$ and $b_1,b_2\ge0$ with $a_1b_2-a_2b_1\ne0$. Must
\[
 \sum_{1\le n\le X}\lambda(a_1n+b_1)\lambda(a_2n+b_2)=o(X)
 \qquad(X\to\infty)
\]
hold through all real cutoffs $X$?
\subsection{Short English statement}
Must the ordinary average of the Liouville product along every such pair of distinct integer affine forms tend to zero?
