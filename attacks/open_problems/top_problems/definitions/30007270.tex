\section*{Ordinary binary affine Liouville correlations}\label{30007270}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $\lambda (n)=(-1)^Omega(n)$, where Omega counts prime factors with multiplicity. For each fixed $a1,a2>=1$ and $b1,b2>=0$ with a1 b2-a2 b1 nonzero, must $sum_{n<=X}$ lambda(a1 n+b1)lambda(a2 n+b2) be o(X) through all real X?
