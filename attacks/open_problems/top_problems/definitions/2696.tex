\section{Kirby Problem 1.37}\label{2696}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2696 · Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


All knots and links below are smooth in the oriented $3$-sphere, up to ambient isotopy, unless another ambient manifold is specified. Fix a complex simple Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$ and color each oriented link component by a finite-dimensional irreducible representation. Let $\mathrm{RT}_{\mathfrak g}(L;q)$ be the corresponding normalized Reshetikhin--Turaev quantum link invariant. A bigraded categorification has groups $H^{i,j}$ whose graded Euler characteristic is $\sum_{i,j}(-1)^i q^j\operatorname{rank}H^{i,j}$. Functoriality requires compatible maps for colored oriented cobordisms and compatibility with identity and composition. The requested odd version is an integral variant whose reduction modulo $2$ agrees with that of the original theory, analogous to odd Khovanov homology.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
(a) For every link, every simple $\mathfrak g$ and every irreducible coloring, construct a bigraded link-homology theory functorial under link cobordisms with Euler characteristic $\mathrm{RT}_{\mathfrak g}(L;q)$. (b) For each theory construct an odd version with the same mod-$2$ reduction, or identify an obstruction to such an odd construction. \sref{2696}{1}\sref{2696}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Construct functorial colored categorifications for every simple Lie algebra, then construct odd variants or determine what prevents them.
