\section*{Full-plane Julia sets in the exponential family}\label{5300061}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 5300061 · AMR-052-0061 · Dynamical Systems · AMR Open Problem Lists}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a nonzero complex parameter $\lambda$, consider the transcendental
entire map $E_\lambda(z)=\lambda e^z$ and its iterates. Its Fatou set
$F(E_\lambda)$ is the largest open subset of $\mathbb C$ on which
$\{E_\lambda^n:n\ge0\}$ is a normal family for locally uniform
convergence in the spherical metric. Its Julia set is
$J(E_\lambda)=\mathbb C\setminus F(E_\lambda)$.

Determine completely the parameter locus
\[
 \mathcal J=\{\lambda\in\mathbb C\setminus\{0\}:
                       J(E_\lambda)=\mathbb C\}.
\]
The desired characterization should describe membership using parameter
data, the orbit of the singular value, or another explicit dynamical
criterion. Restating membership as absence of a Fatou set does not
provide the requested parameter-space description.

There are no finite critical points, because $E_\lambda'(z)\ne0$;
the finite asymptotic value zero is the singular value governing
the family. Its forward orbit begins $0,\lambda,\lambda e^\lambda,
\ldots$. Conditions on this orbit often describe important subsets
of $\mathcal J$, but a full answer must also address parameters with
bounded, recurrent, or nonconvergent singular orbits, rather than
only those lying on known escaping curves.

The equality sought is equality as sets in the complex plane. Full
Hausdorff dimension, positive or full planar measure, or a dense
Julia set is not substituted for the precise condition. The excluded
parameter $\lambda=0$ gives a constant map and is outside the
transcendental family under discussion.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Characterize exactly the nonzero exponential parameters for which every complex point lies in the Julia set.
