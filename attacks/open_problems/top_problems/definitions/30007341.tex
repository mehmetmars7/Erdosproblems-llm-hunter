\section*{Strong diffeomorphic approximation of three-dimensional homeomorphisms above exponent two}\label{30007341}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every finite $p>2$, arbitrary bounded domains $Omega,Lambda\subset R^3$ and each $W^{1,p}$ homeomorphism f:Omega$\to$Lambda, can f be approximated strongly in $W^{1,p}$ by smooth diffeomorphisms between those same domains? No inverse-map norm or quasiconformal-constant control is imposed.
