\section*{Minimal or Mori models for generalized rational lc pairs}\label{30007029}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(k\) be an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero. Let \((X,B+M)\) be a normal integral projective generalized log canonical rational pair over \(k\), with its nef rational b-Cartier data \(\mathbf M\) fixed and \(M\) the trace on \(X\). Put \(D=K_X+B+M\).

Must there exist a normal integral projective variety \(Y\) and a birational map \(\phi:X\dashrightarrow Y\), whose inverse contracts no divisors, such that \(B_Y=\phi_*B\), \(M_Y\) is the trace of the same \(\mathbf M\), and
\[
 A_{X,B+M}(E)\le A_{Y,B_Y+M_Y}(E)
\]
for every prime divisor \(E\) over the common function field, with strict inequality for each prime divisor on \(X\) contracted by \(\phi\)? Here \(A\) denotes generalized log discrepancy.

The required model must have \(K_Y+B_Y+M_Y\) nef when \(D\) is pseudo-effective. Otherwise it must admit a projective contraction \(h:Y\to T\) with connected fibres to a lower-dimensional normal projective base, satisfying
\[
 \rho(Y/T)=1,
 \qquad -(K_Y+B_Y+M_Y)\text{ is ample over }T.
\]
The nef data may not be replaced by a different b-divisor.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, does every normal integral projective generalized log canonical rational pair \((X,B+M)\), with fixed nef rational b-Cartier data, have a projective birational model \(Y\) extracting no divisor, on which generalized discrepancies do not decrease and increase strictly for contracted prime divisors on \(X\)? The adjoint on \(Y\) must be nef if the original adjoint is pseudo-effective. Otherwise \(Y\) must have a contraction with connected fibres to a lower-dimensional normal projective base, relative Picard number one, and relatively antiample adjoint. The traces on both models must come from the same fixed nef b-divisor.
