\section*{Gopalan–Servedio square-root-degree Fourier bound}\label{30007087}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Is there an absolute constant C such that every nonconstant Boolean $f:{-1,1}^n->{-1,1}$ satisfies $sum_i$ $|f_hat({i})|<=C$ sqrt(deg(f)), where deg(f) is its exact real multilinear degree?
