\section{The Standard Conjectures on Algebraic Cycles}\label{36}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 36 · Algebraic Geometry}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.


\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $X$ be a smooth projective variety over a field, and use a Weil cohomology theory with its cycle-class map. The standard conjectures ask that the cohomological operators and relations dictated by algebraic cycles actually have algebraic realizations. In particular, are the inverse hard-Lefschetz operators induced by algebraic correspondences, and are the K\"unneth projectors onto the individual cohomological degrees algebraic? The collection also includes the agreement of numerical and homological equivalence and the positivity assertion for primitive algebraic classes.
