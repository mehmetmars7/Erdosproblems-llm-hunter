\section*{Three-dimensional Kakeya maximal estimate}\label{30007430}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For delta in (0,1), define $K_\delta $ f(v) as the supremum of the averages of |f| over radius-delta, length-one tubes directed along v in $S^2$. For every $\epsilon >0$, is $||K_\delta $ $f||_L3(S2)$ $<=$ $C_\epsilon $ $\delta ^{-\epsilon }||f||_L3(R3)$, with $C_\epsilon $ independent of f and delta?
