\section*{Kirby Problem 4.71}\label{2947}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2947 · KP-4.71 · Topology · Kirby's Problems in Low-Dimensional Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $X$ be a closed orientable smooth $4$-manifold.
Its full diffeomorphism group $D=\operatorname{Diff}(X)$ is given
the $C^\infty$ topology. For $k\ge1$, take $\pi_k(D)$ based at
the identity; for $k=0$, $\pi_0(D)$ is the group of smooth
isotopy classes under composition. A group is finitely generated
if some finite set and its inverses generate every element.

Part (a) asks whether there exist such an $X$ and an integer
$k\ge0$ for which $\pi_k(D)$ is finitely generated. The group
may be trivial; no nonzero-rank requirement is imposed.

Let $BD$ denote a classifying space for the topological group $D$,
for example the base of its universal principal bundle $ED\to BD$
with contractible total space. Part (b) asks whether there exist
such an $X$ and an integer $k>0$ for which the integral singular
homology group $H_k(BD;\mathbb Z)$ is finitely generated as an
abelian group.

The manifold and degree may be chosen independently in the two
parts. Neither asks for finite generation in every degree. The
second part uses positive degree to exclude the automatic degree-zero
homology answer. The full diffeomorphism group includes any
orientation-reversing diffeomorphisms; orientability of $X$ does not
silently replace it by $\operatorname{Diff}^+(X)$. Finally, the
topological group is not being treated as a discrete group when
forming its classifying space.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can a closed orientable smooth four-manifold have a finitely generated homotopy group of its diffeomorphism group, or a positive-degree integral homology group of its classifying space?
