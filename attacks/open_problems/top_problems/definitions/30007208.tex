\section*{Single-letter classical capacity of generalized amplitude damping}\label{30007208}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every generalized amplitude-damping qubit channel $A_{\gamma ,\nu }$, $\gamma ,\nu \in [0,1]$, is its n-copy Holevo information exactly n times its one-copy value for every $n\geq 1$? Require that the one-copy optimizer is a phase-opposite equiprobable pair $\operatorname{sqrt}(1-p)|0>\pm \operatorname{sqrt}(p)|1>$, so its optimized output-entropy difference gives the unassisted classical capacity.
