\section*{Stable recovery of twisted lattice factors}\label{30007316}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For an ICC group $\Gamma $ in the class of groups abstractly commensurable with lattices in a finite nonempty product $H_i(k_i)^+$, where $k_i$ is R, C or a finite extension of $Q_p$ (with primes allowed to differ), each $H_i$ is a connected adjoint absolutely simple algebraic group, $H_i(k_i)^+$ is generated by the $k_i-points$ of unipotent radicals of proper parabolic subgroups, and each such topological group is noncompact and has property (T), an arbitrary countably infinite ICC group $\Lambda $, normalized scalar cocycles $\mu ,\omega $ and positive scales t,s, does $L_\mu (\Gamma )^t\cong L_\omega (\Lambda )^s$ hold exactly when $t=s$ and a group isomorphism $\Gamma \to \Lambda $ carries $\omega $ to $\mu $ up to a scalar coboundary, with every specified factor isomorphism implemented by that group/cochain map and an inner corner equivalence?
