\section*{Polynomial induced removal for ordered binary matrices}\label{30007097}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every fixed binary matrix H, do positive constants c,C exist such that every n-by-n binary matrix at normalized entry-edit distance at least epsilon from H-freeness contains at least C $\epsilon ^c$ $n^(rows(H)+columns(H))$ induced ordered copies of H?
