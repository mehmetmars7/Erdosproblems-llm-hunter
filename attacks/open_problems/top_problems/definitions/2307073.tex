\section{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 7.73}\label{2307073}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2307073 · Analysis}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Fix $R>0$. For a plane domain $D\subset\{z:|z|<R\}$ let $\lambda_D(z)|dz|$ be its Poincare metric, normalized to curvature $-1$: if $\pi:\mathbb D\to D$ is a covering, $\lambda_D(\pi(w))|\pi'(w)|=2/(1-|w|^2)$. For $z\in D_1\cap D_2$ use the metric of the component of the intersection containing $z$. A uniform constant applies to every pair of such domains and every point of their intersection.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Determine the sharp constant $A(R)$ in
\[\lambda_{D_1\cap D_2}(z)<A(R)
\big(\lambda_{D_1}(z)+\lambda_{D_2}(z)\big).
\]
Equivalently determine the infimum of the positive constants for which this strict inequality always holds, with value $+\infty$ if none does, and whether that infimum itself is admissible. \sref{2307073}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Find the sharp uniform comparison between the hyperbolic metric on an intersection and the sum of the two original metrics.
