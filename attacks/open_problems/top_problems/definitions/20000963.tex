\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.


\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $W(n,m)$ be the least $N$ such that every red-blue coloring of $\{1,\ldots,N\}$ has either a red $n$-term arithmetic progression or a blue $m$-term progression, and put $W(n)=W(n,n)$. Is $W(n)\leq2^{n^2}$? Also study $W^*(n)$, the least cardinality of a finite subset of $\mathbb N$ whose every two-coloring has a monochromatic $n$-term progression, including its comparison with $W(n)$.
