\section{Kirby Problem 4.128}\label{3004}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 3004 · Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


A smooth four-manifold has positive scalar curvature (PSC) if it admits a Riemannian metric $g$ with $\operatorname{Scal}_g(x)>0$ at every point. Uniform positivity means $\inf_x\operatorname{Scal}_g(x)>0$; completeness means metric completeness. Write $\overline{\mathbb{CP}}^{2}$ for the complex projective plane with reversed orientation, and $\#$ for connected sum. An empty connected sum in the displayed closed simply connected classification is $S^4$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Resolve the following four questions.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Is every closed simply connected smooth PSC four-manifold diffeomorphic to
\[\#^{a}\mathbb{CP}^2\#^{b}\overline{\mathbb{CP}}^{2}\#^{c}(S^2\times S^2)\] for some integers $a,b,c\geq0$?
\item[(b)] Characterize the closed smooth PSC four-manifolds with nontrivial fundamental group.
\item[(c)] Characterize the smooth four-manifolds with boundary admitting PSC metrics, and the boundary-condition variants described in the source: metrics that are products near the boundary, and metrics with positive outward mean curvature at the boundary.
\item[(d)] Characterize the noncompact smooth four-manifolds admitting complete metrics with uniformly positive scalar curvature.
\end{enumerate} \sref{3004}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Classify positive-scalar-curvature four-manifolds in the simply connected, non-simply connected, boundary, and complete noncompact settings.
