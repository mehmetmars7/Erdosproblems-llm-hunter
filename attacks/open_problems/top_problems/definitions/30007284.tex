\section*{Open homomorphisms of solvably closed global Galois groups}\label{30007284}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let F1,F2 be number fields and let $E_i/F_i$ be possibly infinite Galois extensions such that $E_i$ has no nontrivial abelian algebraic extension. Must every continuous homomorphism $Gal(E1/F1)->Gal(E2/F2)$ with open image be induced by a unique field embedding E2 into E1?
