\section{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 5.27}\label{2305027}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2305027 · Analysis}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $D\subset\mathbb C$ be an arbitrary connected open set supporting a nonconstant bounded holomorphic function. Write $H^\infty(D)$ for its bounded holomorphic functions.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every integer $n\ge1$, every $\delta>0$, and every $f_1,\ldots,f_n\in H^\infty(D)$ satisfying
\[\sum_{j=1}^n|f_j(z)|\ge\delta\qquad(z\in D),\]
must there exist $g_1,\ldots,g_n\in H^\infty(D)$ with $\sum_{j=1}^n f_jg_j\equiv1$ on $D$? The question is for all such plane domains, including infinitely connected ones. \sref{2305027}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does the corona equation have bounded holomorphic solutions on every plane domain that supports nonconstant bounded holomorphic functions?
