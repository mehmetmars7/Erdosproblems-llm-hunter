\section*{The Kerr–Newman area bound without electromagnetic-tail controls}\label{30007230}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Do all smooth complete maximal axisymmetric source-free electrovacuum exteriors on ${|x|\geq 1}$, with a connected outermost future MOTS minimizing area among all enclosing cuts, $g-\delta =O_4(r^{-1})$, $K=O_3(r^{-3})$, electric and magnetic fields $O_3(r^{-2})$, integrable constraint densities and finite fluxes, zero ADM linear momentum, positive ADM energy and zero total electric and magnetic charges, obey $m^2\geq A/(16\pi )+4\pi $ $J^2/A$ on $A>8\pi |J|$, with every equality datum induced by a Kerr–Newman exterior slice?
