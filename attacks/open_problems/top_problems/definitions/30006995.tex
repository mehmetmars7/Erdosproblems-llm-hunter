\section*{Higher Verlinde fiber functors in positive characteristic}\label{30006995}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every prime p, every algebraically closed field k of characteristic p and every finite symmetric tensor category C over k, do there exist $n\geq 1$ and an exact faithful k-linear symmetric tensor functor $C\to Ver_{p^n}(k)$?
