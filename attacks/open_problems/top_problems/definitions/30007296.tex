\section*{Quadratic strong-operator paving for every MASA}\label{30007296}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every MASA $A\subseteq M\subseteq B(H)$, self-adjoint $x\in M$ and $0<\varepsilon <1$, can one fix $r\leq 5\cdot 10^{8}\varepsilon ^-2$ before any strong neighborhood is chosen, so that for every finite $F\subset H$ and $\delta >0$ there are an r-partition $(p_i)$ of 1 in A, self-adjoint $a\in A$ with $||a||\leq ||x||$, and a projection $q\in M$ with $||(1-q)\xi ||<\delta $ for $\xi \in F$ and $||q(\sum p_ixp_i-a)q||\leq \varepsilon ||x||$, without an expectation or separability assumption?
