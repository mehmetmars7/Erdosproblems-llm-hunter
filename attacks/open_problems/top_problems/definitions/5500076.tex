\section{Equiprojective Polyhedra}\label{5500076}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 5500076 · Geometry}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


A polyhedron is $k$-equiprojective when orthogonal projection onto a plane gives a polygon with exactly $k$ sides for every projection direction not parallel to any face. The integer $k$ is independent of direction. For example, a cube is $6$-equiprojective.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Identify or construct all $k$-equiprojective polyhedra, for all possible integers $k$. \sref{5500076}{2}
