\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $M$ and $N$ be closed aspherical manifolds, meaning that their universal covers are contractible. If $\pi_1(M)$ and $\pi_1(N)$ are isomorphic, must $M$ and $N$ be homeomorphic? This is the topological rigidity question; no diffeomorphism is demanded.
