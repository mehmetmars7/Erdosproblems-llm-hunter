\section*{Simplex maximum for projection-body volume}\label{30007114}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every $n\geq 3$ and convex body $K\subset R^{n}$, must $|\Pi K|/|K|^{n-1}$ be at most the corresponding value for an n-simplex? The projection body $\Pi K$ has support function $h_{\Pi K}(u)=vol_{n-1}(proj_{u\perp }K)$ for unit u. No symmetry or regularity is assumed.
