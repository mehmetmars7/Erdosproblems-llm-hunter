\section{Erdős-Posa property for long directed cycles}\label{3274}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 3274 · Graph Theory}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


A directed cycle is a directed closed path with distinct vertices except for its initial and final vertex; its length is its number of arcs. Cycles are disjoint here when they have disjoint vertex sets. For a vertex set $T$, write $D-T$ for vertex deletion from the finite digraph $D$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every integer $\ell\ge2$ and every integer $n\ge0$, is there an integer $t_n(\ell)\ge0$ such that every digraph $D$ either contains $n$ pairwise vertex-disjoint directed cycles of length at least $\ell$, or has a vertex set $T$ with $|T|\le t_n(\ell)$ for which $D-T$ has no directed cycle of length at least $\ell$? \sref{3274}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can long directed cycles always be either packed in a prescribed number or hit by a bounded number of vertices?
