\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
A finite subset $X$ of Euclidean space is Ramsey if, for every number of colors $r$, some dimension $N$ forces a monochromatic congruent copy of $X$ in every coloring of $\mathbb R^N$. Is every finite set lying on a sphere Ramsey?
