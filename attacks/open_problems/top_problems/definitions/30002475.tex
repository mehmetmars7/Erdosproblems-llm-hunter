\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $A(n,d)$ be the adjacency matrix of a uniformly random $d$-regular graph, where $d>2$. Is $A(n,d)$ nonsingular with probability tending to $1$ as $n\to\infty$? What are the limiting distributions of the permanent of a random $\{\pm1\}$-matrix and the determinant of a random symmetric $\{\pm1\}$-matrix?
