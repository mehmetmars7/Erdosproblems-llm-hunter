\section*{Milnor K reconstruction at the characteristic prime}\label{30007268}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Fix a prime $p$. Let $K/k$ and $L/l$ be finitely generated extensions of algebraically closed fields of characteristic $p$, with $\operatorname{trdeg}(K/k),\operatorname{trdeg}(L/l)\ge2$. For $F=K,L$, set
\[
 V_F=F^\times/(F^\times)^p,\qquad
 R_F=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb F_p}\{[a]\otimes[1-a]:a\in F\setminus\{0,1\}\}.
\]
The class $[a]$ uses additive notation for the multiplicative quotient. The degree-two Milnor group is $(V_F\otimes_{\mathbb F_p}V_F)/R_F=K_2^M(F)/p$. Define
\[
 \operatorname{Isom}_{\mathrm M}(V_K,V_L)
 =\{\Theta:V_K\xrightarrow{\sim}V_L\text{ linear over }\mathbb F_p:
           (\Theta\otimes\Theta)(R_K)=R_L\}.
\]
The maps are required to be invertible, and to carry the relation space onto the target relation space. Let $\operatorname{Isom}(K,k;L,l)$ consist of field isomorphisms carrying $k$ onto $l$. Is the natural map
\[
 \operatorname{Isom}(K,k;L,l)\longrightarrow
 \operatorname{Isom}_{\mathrm M}(V_K,V_L)/\mathbb F_p^\times,
 \qquad\alpha\longmapsto[\alpha_*],
\]
a bijection, where $\alpha_*([a])=[\alpha(a)]$ and the quotient identifies linear isomorphisms differing by one nonzero scalar?

\subsection{Short English statement}
Do the first two Milnor groups modulo the characteristic recover these function fields and their constant-preserving isomorphisms, after identifying compatible linear isomorphisms that differ by a common nonzero scalar?
