\section*{Equal-entropy subcovers}\label{4600021}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 4600021 · AMR-045-0021 · Dynamical Systems · AMR Open Problem Lists}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Fix $d\ge2$, a nonempty $\mathbb Z^d$ shift of finite type
$X\subset A^{\mathbb Z^d}$, and a factor map
$\pi:X\to Y\subset B^{\mathbb Z^d}$. Here the alphabets are finite,
$X$ is defined by finitely many forbidden patterns, and $\pi$ is a
continuous surjection commuting with every lattice shift. Its image
$Y$ is consequently a sofic shift. A subshift is sofic when it is the
image of some finite-type shift under such a map.

For any subshift $Z$, define entropy by
\[
 h(Z)=\lim_{n\to\infty}n^{-d}
   \log\#\{z|_{\{0,\ldots,n-1\}^d}:z\in Z\}.
\]
Must there be a nonempty sofic subshift $W\subset X$ such that
\[
                 \pi(W)=Y\quad\hbox{and}\quad h(W)=h(Y)?
\]
The covering map is fixed as part of the input. Thus the question asks
to remove surplus configurations inside this particular cover, rather
than to choose an unrelated cover of $Y$. The restriction $\pi|_W$
must remain onto every point of $Y$, including exceptional configurations.

The desired $W$ must be closed and invariant under the full lattice
action, and must admit a finite-state sofic presentation. It need not
itself be of finite type. Exact entropy equality is required, rather
than a family of subcovers approaching the target entropy. Neither the
input spaces nor the chosen subcover are assumed mixing or irreducible.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Inside a given multidimensional finite-type cover, can one retain an onto sofic subcover with exactly the target entropy?
