\section{Two Smooth Integers inside Infinitely Many Prime Gaps}\label{2388}

{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2388 · Number Theory}

\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $p_k$ denote the $k$th prime. For infinitely many $r$ there are at least two integers $p_r<n<p_{r+1}$ all of whose prime factors are $<p_{r+1}-p_r$. \sref{2388}{1}

\subsection{Short English statement}

Do infinitely many gaps between successive primes contain two integers whose prime factors are all smaller than the gap length?
