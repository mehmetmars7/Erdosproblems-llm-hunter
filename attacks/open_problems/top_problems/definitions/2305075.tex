\section{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 5.75}\label{2305075}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2305075 · Analysis}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $\mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}$ and $\mathbb T=\partial\mathbb D$. If $f$ is analytic, the length of its parametrized radial image at $\zeta\in\mathbb T$ is \[\operatorname{length}(r\mapsto f(r\zeta),\ 0\le r<1)=\int_0^1|f^{\prime}(r\zeta)|\,dr\in[0,\infty].\]
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Does there exist an analytic $f$ on $\mathbb D$ with $\sup_{\mathbb D}|f|<\infty$ for which this length is infinite for every $\zeta\in\mathbb T$? \sref{2305075}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can a bounded analytic disk function have an image of infinite length along every radius?
