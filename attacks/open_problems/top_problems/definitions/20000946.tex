\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $r(C_m,K_n)$ be the least number of vertices that forces a red cycle of length $m$ or a blue clique of size $n$ in every red-blue edge-coloring of a complete graph. Is
\[r(C_m,K_n)=(m-1)(n-1)+1\]
for all $m\geq n$?
