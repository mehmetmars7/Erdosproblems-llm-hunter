\section{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 5.33}\label{2305033}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2305033 · Analysis}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Write $\mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}$ and $\mathbb T=\partial\mathbb D$. All Taylor coefficients below are taken at $0$.A regular triangular lattice has the form $L=a+b\{m+n e^{i\pi/3}:m,n\in\mathbb Z\}$, with $a,b\in\mathbb C$ and $b\ne0$. Let $f:\mathbb D\to\mathbb C\setminus L$ be a holomorphic universal covering projection, and write $f(z)=\sum_{n\ge0}a_nz^n$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every such lattice and covering projection, must $a_n\to0$ as $n\to\infty$? \sref{2305033}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Historical question: do Taylor coefficients of a universal cover of the complement of a triangular lattice tend to zero? The source records an affirmative answer.
