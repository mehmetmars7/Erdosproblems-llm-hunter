\section*{Kirby Problem 3.13}\label{2811}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2811 · KP-3.13 · Topology · Kirby's Problems in Low-Dimensional Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $M$ be a closed connected hyperbolic three-manifold. An immersion
$f:\Sigma\to M$ of a smooth surface is a smooth map whose differential is
injective at every point. Thus it is locally an embedded surface, though
different points of $\Sigma$ may have the same image. It is $\pi_1$-injective
when the induced homomorphism of fundamental groups is injective.

Does there exist, for every such $M$, a closed connected surface
$\Sigma\ne S^2$ and a $\pi_1$-injective immersion satisfying
\[
 |f^{-1}(x)|\leq2\qquad\text{for every }x\in M?
\]
This is the precise multiplicity condition meant by ``only double points.''
The source's statement does not separately demand that all double intersections
be transverse, so the cardinality bound is the defining requirement here.
Embedded surfaces qualify, since their fibers have cardinality at most one.

The exclusion of the sphere prevents a trivial local construction. No genus
bound is prescribed, and orientability of $\Sigma$ is not added to the
source's assumptions. Producing an immersed essential surface with triple
points does not meet the conclusion; those higher multiplicities must be
absent everywhere in the proposed immersion.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Must every closed hyperbolic three-manifold contain an immersed essential closed surface that never passes through any point more than twice?
