\section*{Kähler fourfold abundance after nonvanishing}\label{30007038}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For a normal connected compact Kähler fourfold with effective rational klt boundary $\Delta $, let $D=K_X+\Delta $ be Q-Cartier and analytically nef. If $\kappa (X,D)\geq 0$, must the actual adjoint have a globally generated positive Cartier multiple? When $\kappa =0$, must some positive multiple be the trivial line?
