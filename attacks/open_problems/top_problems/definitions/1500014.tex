\section{Algebraic Stories — Lefschetz properties of graded algebras}\label{1500014}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 1500014 · Algebra}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $S=\mathbb C[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$ with its usual total-degree grading. For a homogeneous quotient $R$, define
\[\operatorname{Hilb}_R(t)=\sum_{e\geq0}(\dim_{\mathbb C}R_e)t^e.
\]
A generic tuple of forms has its coefficient vector in a suitable nonempty Zariski open subset of the product of the prescribed homogeneous pieces.For a partition $\mu=(\mu_1,\ldots,\mu_q)$ of $e\geq1$, a graded algebra $A$ has the $\mu$-Lefschetz property if, for generic linear forms $\ell_1,\ldots,\ell_q$, the maps
\[A_i\xrightarrow{\ \times\ell_1^{\mu_1}\cdots\ell_q^{\mu_q}\ } A_{i+e}
\]
have rank $\min\{\dim A_i,\dim A_{i+e}\}$ for every $i\geq0$. Let $R=S/(f_1,\ldots,f_r)$, with the $f_j$ generic homogeneous forms of arbitrary prescribed positive degrees.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Does every such $R$ have the $\mu$-Lefschetz property for every partition $\mu$? \sref{1500014}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does multiplication by any generic product of powers of linear forms have maximal rank in every degree of a generic quotient?
