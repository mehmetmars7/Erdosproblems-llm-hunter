\section*{Deforming hypersymplectic triples to hyperkähler triples}\label{30007144}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let X be a closed oriented connected smooth four-manifold and let $\omega _{1},\omega _{2},\omega _{3}$ be closed smooth real two-forms such that every nonzero real linear combination is symplectic and $\int _X$ $\omega _i\wedge \omega _j=\delta _ij$. Must the triple admit a smooth path of such positive triples with each de Rham class fixed, ending at a triple of parallel self-dual forms for a hyperkähler metric?
