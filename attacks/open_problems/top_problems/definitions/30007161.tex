\section*{Codimension-one singular bound for stationary integral varifolds}\label{30007161}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For all positive m,n and every stationary integral m-varifold V in an open subset of $R^{m+n}$, is $dim_H$ $Sing(V)\leq m-1$? Sing(V) excludes points with a neighborhood on which V is a constant positive integer multiple of a smooth embedded minimal m-submanifold.
