\section{Mahler’s Volume Conjecture}\label{30004025}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 30004025 · Geometry}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.


\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every convex body $K\subseteq\mathbb R^n$, is its Mahler volume bounded by

$$\bar s(K)=\lvert K\rvert\inf_{x\in K}\lvert(K-x)^\circ\rvert\geq\frac{(n+1)^{n+1}}{(n!)^2},$$

with equality for a simplex?
