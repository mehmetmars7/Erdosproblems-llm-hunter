\section*{Riemann-profile transference for discrete entropy monotonicity}\label{20000904}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 20000904 · AIM-COMBINATORICS-0029 · Combinatorics · AIM Workshop Problem Lists}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $G$ be a torsion-free abelian group: if $mg=0$ for an integer $m\geq1$, then $g=0$.
Let $X$ have a discrete distribution of finite Shannon entropy on $G$, with countable support, and let $X_1,X_2,\ldots$ be independent copies.
Write $S_j=X_1+\cdots+X_j$ and
\[
 H(Y)=-\sum_y\mathbb P(Y=y)\log_2\mathbb P(Y=y),
 \qquad 0\log_2 0=0.
\]
The entropies of all the finite sums are finite, since $H(S_j)\leq jH(X)$.

The proposed entropy-increment inequality is the following uniform asymptotic assertion.
For every fixed integer $n\geq2$ and every $\varepsilon>0$, does there exist $h_0(n,\varepsilon)$ such that, for every such group and distribution with $H(X)\geq h_0(n,\varepsilon)$,
\[
 H(S_n)-H(S_{n-1})
 \geq\frac12\log_2\!\left(\frac n{n-1}\right)-\varepsilon?
\]
The threshold must be independent of the particular distribution and ambient group.
The asymptotic parameter is the entropy of $X$, while $n$ remains fixed; the question does not assert a uniform error as $n$ tends to infinity.
The case $n=2$ is the entropy-doubling result motivating the workshop question, and the requested extension concerns subsequent increments.
Torsion-freeness is essential to the formulation: uniform distributions on large finite subgroups would otherwise have arbitrarily large entropy but no entropy increase under addition.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Must each fixed later sum of independent copies gain at least the corresponding Gaussian entropy increment when the original discrete entropy is large?
