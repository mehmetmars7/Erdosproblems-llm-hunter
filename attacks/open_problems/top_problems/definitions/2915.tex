\section*{Kirby Problem 4.39}\label{2915}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2915 · KP-4.39 · Topology · Kirby's Problems in Low-Dimensional Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $K$ be a closed smooth surface embedded by $i:K\hookrightarrow M$
in a smooth $4$-manifold. Use the full mapping class group
$\operatorname{Mod}(K)=\pi_0\operatorname{Diff}(K)$, whose elements
are smooth-isotopy classes of self-diffeomorphisms, with composition
as multiplication. Orientability of $K$ is not required. If oriented
versions are considered, all maps can instead be restricted consistently
to preserve the specified orientations.

Define the extendable subgroup
\[
 E(M,i)=\{[h]\in\operatorname{Mod}(K):
    \exists F\in\operatorname{Diff}(M),\ F\circ i=i\circ h\}.
\]
Here one representative $h$ of the mapping class must admit the
extension. Isotopy extension ensures that the property does not depend
on that choice. The ambient diffeomorphism preserves the embedded
surface setwise but need not be isotopic to the identity.

Describe $E(M,i)$ in terms of the embedding and the topology of the
ambient manifold. Conversely, characterize the subgroups
$H\le\operatorname{Mod}(K)$ for which there exist some smooth
$4$-manifold $M$ and embedding $i$ with $E(M,i)=H$.

The realization question asks for equality with the entire extendable
subgroup. Realizing each element of $H$ separately without excluding
other extendable classes is insufficient. Likewise extensions need
not form a chosen homomorphic section into $\operatorname{Diff}(M)$;
the requirement is individual extendability. No compactness or
simple-connectivity condition on $M$ is added to the source statement.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Which mapping classes of an embedded closed surface extend over its ambient four-manifold, and which subgroups can arise this way?
