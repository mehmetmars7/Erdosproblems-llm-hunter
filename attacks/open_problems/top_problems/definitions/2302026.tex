\section{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 2.26}\label{2302026}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2302026 · Analysis}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Write $\mathcal P,\mathcal E,\mathcal R,\mathcal M$ for, respectively, the complex polynomials, entire functions, rational functions, and meromorphic functions on $\mathbb C$. For an integer $N\ge1$ and a class $\mathcal C$, let $g_{\mathcal C}(N)$ be the least $k$ for which every $f\in\mathcal C$ can be written as $f=\sum_{\nu=1}^k f_\nu^N$ with all $f_\nu\in\mathcal C$.

\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Determine $k(N)=g_{\mathcal E}(N)$, or obtain sharp estimates for it. In the extension explicitly posed in Update 2.26, determine or estimate $g_{\mathcal C}(N)$ for each $\mathcal C\in\{\mathcal P,\mathcal E,\mathcal R,\mathcal M\}$ and $N\ge2$.
The same update asks about the associated identity
\[1=\sum_{\nu=1}^k f_\nu^N\]
with the summands $f_\nu^N$ linearly independent over $\mathbb C$. In particular, do there exist nonconstant meromorphic functions $f,g,h$ with $f^n+g^n+h^n=1$ for $n=7$ or $n=8$? \sref{2302026}{2}
The original entire-function problem has $k(N)\le N$ and $k(N)=N$ for $N=1,2,3$. The 2018 update explicitly broadens the classes and identifies the cases $n=7,8$ in the three-term equation. \sref{2302026}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Find how many powers are needed to express arbitrary functions in four analytic classes, and settle the specified three-term meromorphic Fermat equations.
