\section*{A nonlinear-capacity criterion for four-state reconstruction on arbitrary trees}\label{30007360}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every deterministic infinite rooted tree with uniformly bounded number of children and $0<\lambda <1$, is four-state reconstruction equivalent to positivity of $sup_\theta $ |theta| over nonnegative root-to-infinity flows satisfying $sup_ray$ $sum_(e$ on $ray)(\lambda ^{-2|e|}\theta (e))^2\leq 1$? Reconstruction means a positive limiting expected total-variation distance of the root posterior from the uniform four-state law.
