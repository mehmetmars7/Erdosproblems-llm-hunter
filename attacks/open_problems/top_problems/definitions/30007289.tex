\section*{Weak inhomogeneous Duffin-Schaeffer}\label{30007289}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Fix a real shift gamma and a finite-valued nonnegative function psi on positive integers. If $sum_q$ phi(q)psi(q)/q diverges, must almost every real x satisfy $||qx-\gamma ||<\psi (q)$ for infinitely many q, with no coprimality restriction on the approximating numerator?
