\section*{Hodge support on a specified K3 quadratic locus}\label{30007033}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let S be a smooth projective complex K3 surface whose transcendental quadratic space, with its cup form, embeds isometrically over Q in $U_Q\oplus U_Q\oplus \langle -1\rangle ^{4}$. For every $m\geq 1$, are all rational (p,p) classes on $S^m$ algebraic, and is every rational Hodge substructure with Hodge coniveau at least r supported on an algebraic subset of codimension at least r? U denotes the hyperbolic plane.
