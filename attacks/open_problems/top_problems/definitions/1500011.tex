\section{Algebraic Stories — Hilbert series of other classes of ideals}\label{1500011}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 1500011 · Algebra}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $S=\mathbb C[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$ with its usual total-degree grading. For a homogeneous quotient $R$, define
\[\operatorname{Hilb}_R(t)=\sum_{e\geq0}(\dim_{\mathbb C}R_e)t^e.
\]
A generic tuple of forms has its coefficient vector in a suitable nonempty Zariski open subset of the product of the prescribed homogeneous pieces.Let $n,r,k\geq1$ and $d>1$. Choose generic $g_1,\ldots,g_r\in S_d$, and independently choose generic $f_1,\ldots,f_r\in S_{dk}$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Is
\[\operatorname{Hilb}_{S/(g_1^k,\ldots,g_r^k)}(t)=\operatorname{Hilb}_{S/(f_1,\ldots,f_r)}(t)
\]
for all these parameters? \sref{1500011}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does taking powers of generic forms of degree at least two preserve the Hilbert series of fully generic generators of the resulting degree?
