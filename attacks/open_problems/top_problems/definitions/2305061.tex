\section{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 5.61}\label{2305061}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2305061 · Analysis}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $\mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}$ and $\mathbb T=\partial\mathbb D$. Let $w$ be analytic on $\mathbb D$ with $w(0)=0$. For $n\ge1$ define the differential operator \[P_nw(z)=\sum_{j=0}^{n}z^jw^{(j)}(z),\qquad w^{(0)}=w.\]
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every integer $n\ge1$, does $|P_nw(z)|<1$ for all $z\in\mathbb D$ imply $|w(z)|<1$ for all $z\in\mathbb D$? The source update reports an affirmative proof by Goldstein, Hall, Sheil-Small and Smith. \sref{2305061}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Record the higher-order differential-subordination question: does the stated differential sum mapping into the disk force w itself to map into the disk? The source reports that it was proved.
