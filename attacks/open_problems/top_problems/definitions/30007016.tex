\section*{Bounded denominators in low-dimensional log Calabi–Yau fibrations}\label{30007016}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Fix $1\leq d\leq 4$ and finite rational $\Phi \subset [0,1]$. Do positive $p_{0}|p$ and a rational DCC set B depend only on $d,\Phi $ such that every projective lc d-fold $(X,B_X)$ with coefficients in $\Phi $ and contraction $f:X\to Z$ to a positive-dimensional base, with $K_X+B_X$ Q-linearly equal to f*D, has an exact presentation $K_X+B_X+(1/p_{0})\operatorname{div}(\psi )=f*(K_Z+B_Z+M_Z)$? Here $B_Z$ must be effective with coefficients in B, the resulting generalized pair lc (klt for klt input), and its moduli b-divisor b-nef with p times it b-Cartier.
