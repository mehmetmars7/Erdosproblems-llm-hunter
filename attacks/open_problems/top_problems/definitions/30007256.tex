\section*{Corrected binary Elliott for ordinary averages}\label{30007256}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let f1,f2 be multiplicative functions of modulus at most one. Suppose at least one f satisfies, for every fixed Dirichlet character chi, $inf_{|t|<=N}$ $sum_{p<=N}$ (1-Re(f(p) $conjugate(chi(p)p^{it})))/p$ $->infinity$. For distinct fixed nonnegative h1,h2, must (1/N) $sum_{n<=N}$ f1(n+h1)f2(n+h2) tend to zero?
