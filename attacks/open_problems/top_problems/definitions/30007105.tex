\section*{Talagrand discrete convexity conjecture}\label{30007105}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Does a universal integer $k$ exist such that, for every finite ground set $X$, every $p\in(0,1)$, and every family $D\subseteq 2^X$ with $p$-biased measure at least $1-1/k$, the family
\[
E_k(D)=\{S\subseteq X:S\text{ is not contained in any union of }k\text{ members of }D\}
\]
has a cover $G$ satisfying
\[
\sum_{I\in G}p^{|I|}\leq\frac12,
\]
where a cover means that every $S\in E_k(D)$ contains some $I\in G$?
\subsection{Short English statement}
Is there a universal integer $k$ such that, for every finite $X$, every $p\in(0,1)$, and every $D\subseteq2^X$ of $p$-biased measure at least $1-1/k$, the sets $S\subseteq X$ that are not contained in any union of $k$ members of $D$ have a cover $G$ with $\sum_{I\in G}p^{|I|}\leq1/2$, meaning that each such $S$ contains a member of $G$?
