\section*{Universal-cover products for integrable tangent summands}\label{30007068}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let X be a connected compact Kähler manifold with $T_X=V_{1}\oplus V_{2}$ a decomposition into holomorphic integrable subbundles. Must its universal cover split biholomorphically as $Y_{1}×Y_{2}$ so that the lifted $V_i$ is the tangent bundle along the corresponding factor?
\subsection{Short English statement}
Let X be a connected compact Kähler manifold with $T_X=V_{1}\oplus V_{2}$ a decomposition into holomorphic integrable subbundles. Must its universal cover split biholomorphically as $Y_{1}×Y_{2}$ so that the lifted $V_i$ is the tangent bundle along the corresponding factor?
