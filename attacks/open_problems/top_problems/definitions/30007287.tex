\section*{Unramified generic Ramanujan over function fields}\label{30007287}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Over every global function field F, let G be split connected adjoint absolutely simple and pi a complex cuspidal automorphic representation. If pi has a generic unramified component at even one place, must its component be tempered at every unramified place?
