\section*{\texorpdfstring{Moonshine Uniqueness Conjecture (Strongly Rational Holomorphic VOA Form)}{Moonshine Uniqueness Conjecture (Strongly Rational Holomorphic VOA Form)}}\label{30006817}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A vertex operator algebra has a graded vector space, vacuum and conformal vectors, and vertex operators $Y(a,z)=\sum_{m\in{\mathbb{Z}}}a_mz^{-m-1}$ satisfying the usual vacuum, translation and Jacobi axioms. Here CFT type means $V=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}V_n$ and $V_0={\mathbb{C}}\mathbf1$. The subspace $C_2(V)$ is spanned by $a_{-2}b$; $C_2$-cofiniteness means $V/C_2(V)$ is finite-dimensional. Strong rationality also requires simplicity, self-contragredience and complete reducibility of admissible modules. Holomorphic means $V$ is its only irreducible module. The target is
\[
 c(V)=24,\quad V_1=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad V\cong V^{\natural}
\]
for every VOA satisfying those hypotheses, where $V^{\natural}$ is the standard moonshine VOA. Matching its character alone is not the requested isomorphism.
\par\smallskip\textit{Formulation and concept references:} \href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.18166}{[S1]}.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Is the moonshine vertex operator algebra uniquely determined by strong rationality, holomorphicity, central charge twenty-four, and the absence of weight-one states?
