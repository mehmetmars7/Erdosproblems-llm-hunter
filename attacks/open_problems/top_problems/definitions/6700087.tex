\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Prove that there is a dimension-dependent constant $c_n$ such that every compact Riemannian $n$-manifold $X$ with $\operatorname{Sc}(X)\geq-\sigma^2$ satisfies $\lVert X\rVert\leq c_n\sigma^n\operatorname{vol}(X)$, where $\lVert X\rVert$ is the simplicial volume.
