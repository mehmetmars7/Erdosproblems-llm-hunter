\section*{\texorpdfstring{Smooth 4-dimensional Schoenflies problem}{Smooth 4-dimensional Schoenflies problem}}\label{3410}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A smooth embedding $e:S^3\hookrightarrow S^4$ has two complementary components; write $W_+$ and $W_-$ for their closures, regarded as smooth manifolds with boundary using collars along $e(S^3)$. The selected Schoenflies statement asks whether
\[
 W_+\cong_{\mathrm{diff}}B^4\quad\text{and}\quad W_-\cong_{\mathrm{diff}}B^4
\]
for every such embedding. Here $B^4=\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^4:\|x\|\le1\}$ has its standard smooth structure. The equivalence to smooth unknottedness is the one in the cited statement. Replacing diffeomorphism by homeomorphism, or assuming an extension of the boundary embedding in advance, changes the problem.
\par\smallskip\textit{Formulation and concept references:} \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.02003}{[S1]}.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Does every smoothly embedded three-sphere in the standard four-sphere bound a standard smooth four-ball on each side?
