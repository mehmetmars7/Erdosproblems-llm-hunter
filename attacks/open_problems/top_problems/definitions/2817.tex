\section*{Kirby Problem 3.19}\label{2817}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2817 · KP-3.19 · Topology · Kirby's Problems in Low-Dimensional Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $M$ be a closed hyperbolic three-manifold and $X$ a smooth nowhere-zero
vector field on $M$. Its complete flow lifts to a flow $\widetilde\Phi_t$
on the universal cover $\widetilde M\cong\mathbb H^3$. A lifted flow line
is proper if the map $t\mapsto\widetilde\Phi_t(x)$ from $\mathbb R$ to
$\widetilde M$ is proper: the inverse image of every compact set is compact.
In particular it escapes every compact subset in both time directions.

The orbit space $L_X$ is the quotient of $\widetilde M$ by belonging to the
same flow line, with its quotient topology. The deck group $\pi_1(M)$ acts
on this space, since deck transformations commute with the lifted flow.
The problem asks successively:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Can $X$ always be chosen so that every lifted flow line is proper?
\item Can it be chosen so that, additionally, $L_X$ is Hausdorff?
\item In the resulting plane orbit-space setting, can its deck-group action
be extended to a closed disk compactification?
\end{enumerate}
The last request means a homeomorphism $L_X\cong\operatorname{int}(D^2)$
under which every deck transformation extends to a homeomorphism of $D^2$,
and these extensions form a group action. Properness of individual orbits
does not itself guarantee a Hausdorff quotient or such a boundary action.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can every closed hyperbolic three-manifold support a flow whose lifted trajectories have a well-behaved plane of orbits and an invariant circle boundary?
