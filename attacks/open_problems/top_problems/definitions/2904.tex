\section*{Kirby Problem 4.28}\label{2904}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2904 · KP-4.28 · Topology · Kirby's Problems in Low-Dimensional Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a closed connected oriented three-manifold $M$, define $s_{\mathrm{sm}}(M)$ as the least $n\ge0$ for which $M$ embeds smoothly in $\#_n(S^2\times S^2)$. Define $s_{\mathrm{top}}$ by locally flat topological embeddings instead. Local flatness means that the pair is locally modeled on $(\mathbb R^4,\mathbb R^3)$. Let $E=S^2\widetilde\times S^2$ be the nontrivial oriented sphere bundle over the sphere, diffeomorphic to $\mathbb{CP}^2\#\overline{\mathbb{CP}}^{2}$. Replacing $S^2\times S^2$ by $E$ defines $\widetilde s_{\mathrm{sm}}$ and $\widetilde s_{\mathrm{top}}$. An empty connected sum means $S^4$.

Compute these functions for substantial classes of three-manifolds, distinguishing the two categories and the two ambient families. Specifically:

(a) Determine their values for lens spaces and Brieskorn homology spheres. Use $L(p,q)=S^3/((z_1,z_2)\sim(\zeta z_1,\zeta^qz_2))$, where $\zeta^p=1$ and $\gcd(p,q)=1$; a Brieskorn sphere is the link of $z_1^a+z_2^b+z_3^c=0$ for pairwise coprime integers $a,b,c\ge2$.

(b) Is there an integral homology sphere $M$ with $s_{\mathrm{sm}}(M)=1$? This requires a smooth embedding in $S^2\times S^2$ and no smooth embedding in $S^4$.

(c) Are the four functions algorithmically computable from a finite triangulation of $M$? A computation must halt and return the minimum, not merely find an embedding providing an upper bound. No particular time bound is required.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Compute the least number of sphere-bundle summands needed to embed a three-manifold, smoothly or locally flatly.
