\section{An Alternating Series Involving the nth Prime}\label{1885}

{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 1885 · Number Theory}

\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Is it true that $ \sum_{n=1}^\infty(-1)^n\frac{n}{p_n} $ converges, where $p_n$ is the sequence of primes? \sref{1885}{1}

\subsection{Short English statement}

Does alternating the signs make the series of the ratios of prime indices to their corresponding primes converge?
