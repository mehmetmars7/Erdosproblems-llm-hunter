\section*{Loewner's conjecture}\label{4700025}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 4700025 · AMR-046-0025 · Dynamical Systems · AMR Open Problem Lists}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Fix an integer $n>1$. Let $f$ be a real-valued real-analytic function on
a neighborhood of $(0,0)$ with $f(0,0)=0$. Put $z=x+iy$ and use the
Wirtinger operator
\[
 \partial_{\bar z}=\tfrac12(\partial_x+i\partial_y).
\]
Define the real planar vector field
\[
 X_f=2^n\bigl(\operatorname{Re}\partial_{\bar z}^{n}f,
                 \operatorname{Im}\partial_{\bar z}^{n}f\bigr),
\]
and assume $X_f(0)=0$ and that this zero is isolated. Choose a sufficiently
small $r>0$ so that $X_f$ has no zeros on $|z|=r$. Its index at the origin
is the topological degree of the map
$z\mapsto X_f(z)/\|X_f(z)\|$ from that positively oriented circle to
the unit circle. The index is independent of the sufficiently small radius.

The question asks to prove
\[
 \operatorname{ind}_0(X_f)\le n.
\]
It concerns every analytic germ satisfying the isolated-zero condition,
without assuming nondegeneracy of its Jacobian. For $n=2$ the field is
$(f_{xx}-f_{yy},2f_{xy})$, illustrating the restriction imposed by being
derived from one scalar function. An arbitrary analytic vector field need
not satisfy the claimed bound. The source records established low-order
cases; the target is the full analytic statement for general $n$.
Replacing real analyticity by finite differentiability is a related
extension, but is not an added requirement of this definition.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Bound the index of the isolated zero of an $n$th conjugate-complex derivative field by $n$.
