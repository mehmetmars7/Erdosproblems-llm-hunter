\section*{Global Arthur enhancements of occurring excursion parameters}\label{30007263}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Assume the orbit-indexed Ramanujan-Arthur decomposition of cuspidal functions at full finite level. For a split connected semisimple group over a finite field and a cuspidal excursion parameter sigma on the Weil group of the unramified open curve, does there exist a dual-group SL2 homomorphism phi and a continuous homomorphism tau to its full centralizer, both over one finite $Q_\ell $ extension, such that tau has reductive Zariski closure and all Frobenius eigenvalues have modulus one under every complex embedding, while after a single global conjugation $\sigma (w)=\tau (w)$ $\phi (\operatorname{diag}(a^deg(w),a^-deg(w)))$ for every Weil element w, including inertia, with a matching choice of square root of q?
