\section*{Prescribed large-volume stability charges on threefolds}\label{30007055}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let X be any smooth projective complex threefold with $K_X$ trivial. For each real $B_*$ and ample $H_*$ in $N^{1}(X)$, do neighborhoods V,W and one $T>0$ exist such that for all $B\in V,H\in W,t\geq T$, the exact central charge $-\int _X$ $\exp (-B-itH)ch(E)\operatorname{sqrt}(td(X))$ occurs in a geometric numerical Bridgeland stability condition with support on the full numerical K group?
