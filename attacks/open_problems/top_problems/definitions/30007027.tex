\section*{Generalized Hodge support on CM abelian varieties}\label{30007027}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let A be any complex CM abelian variety and let $W\subset H^k(A,Q)$ be a rational Hodge substructure. If W(r) is effective for $r\geq 0$, must W vanish on the complement of some closed algebraic subset of A of codimension at least r? Products and powers of CM abelian varieties are included.
