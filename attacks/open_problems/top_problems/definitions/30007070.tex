\section*{Tate classes on finite-field abelian varieties}\label{30007070}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $A/\mathbb F_q$ be an abelian variety, let $r\geq0$, and let $\ell$ be a prime different from the characteristic. Is the cycle-class map
\[\operatorname{CH}^r(A)\otimes\mathbb Q_\ell\longrightarrow H_{\mathrm{et}}^{2r}(A_{\overline{\mathbb F}_q},\mathbb Q_\ell(r))^{\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb F}_q/\mathbb F_q)}\]
surjective? For algebraic cycles with rational coefficients on $A_{\overline{\mathbb F}_q}$, do numerical equivalence and $\ell$-adic homological equivalence coincide?
