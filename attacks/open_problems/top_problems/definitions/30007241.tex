\section*{The universal radius containing all but m electrons of neutral atoms}\label{30007241}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every choice of normalized full Coulomb neutral-atom ground states $Psi_Z$, let $R_m(Psi_Z)$ be the radius at which the expected number of electrons outside the ball is m. Do $m^{1/3}\limsup _{Z\to infinity,Z>m}$ $R_m$ and $m^{1/3}\liminf _{Z\to infinity,Z>m}$ $R_m$ both tend to $(81\pi ^2/2)^{1/3}$ as integer m$\to$infinity? The inner limits keep m fixed.
