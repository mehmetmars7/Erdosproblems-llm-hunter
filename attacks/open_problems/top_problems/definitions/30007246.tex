\section*{Full support of every zero-temperature SK minimizing order parameter}\label{30007246}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
If gamma minimizes the stated zero-temperature SK Parisi functional over its admissible class, must its Stieltjes measure have support [0,1), with gamma strictly increasing on every subinterval? Require the associated Parisi diffusion identities $E[u_\gamma (t,X_t)^2]=t$ and $E[Phi_{\gamma ,xx}(t,X_t)^2]=1$ for each $0\leq t<1$. The question does not assert minimizer existence.
