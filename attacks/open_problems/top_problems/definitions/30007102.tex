\section*{Quasipolynomial density decay for progression-free sets}\label{30007102}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every fixed integer $k>=3$, do constants $C_k,c_k,\epsilon _k>0$ exist such that every subset A of \{1,...,N\} containing no nonconstant k-term arithmetic progression has $|A|<=C_k$ N $\exp (-c_k$ (log $N)^\epsilon _k)$ for every $N>=2$?
