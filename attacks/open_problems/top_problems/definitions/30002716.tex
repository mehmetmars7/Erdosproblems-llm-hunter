Let $K/k$ and $L/l$ be function fields of transcendence degree at least two over algebraically closed fields of characteristic different from $\ell$. Does the natural map from field isomorphisms, modulo Frobenius in positive characteristic, to Milnor-$K$-theory-preserving isomorphisms $K^*/(K^*)^\ell\to L^*/(L^*)^\ell$ give a bijection?
