\section*{Euclidean comparison for integer-growth harmonic dimensions}\label{30007145}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every complete smooth nonnegative-Ricci n-manifold and integer $k\geq 0$, must the real vector space of harmonic functions bounded in absolute value by some function-dependent constant times (1+distance from a fixed $point)^k$ have dimension at most that space on Euclidean $R^{n}$?
