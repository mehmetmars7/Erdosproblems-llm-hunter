\section*{Subpolynomial nonlinear dimension reduction in Lp}\label{30007122}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For each fixed $1<p<\infty $, $p\neq 2$, and $D>1$, does a constant $C_{p,D}$ exist such that every n-point subset of any real Lp space embeds, with freely chosen positive scale and distortion at most D, into $\ell p^d$ for $d\leq \exp (C_{p,D}(\log $ $n)^\gamma )$, where $\gamma =2-p$ for $p<2$ and $\gamma =1-2/p$ for $p>2$? Maps need not be linear, and $n\geq 2$.
