\section*{Weak pinned planar distance assertion}\label{30007108}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every $\epsilon >0$ and $\delta >0$, does $N_0$ exist such that every planar set P of $N>=N_0$ distinct points has at most delta N points x with fewer than $N^{1-\epsilon }$ distinct positive distances to the other points of P?
