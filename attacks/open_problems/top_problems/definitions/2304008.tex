\section{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 4.8}\label{2304008}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2304008 · Analysis}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $n\ge1$ and let $f(z)=\prod_{j=1}^n(z-z_j)$ be a monic polynomial of degree $n$, with zeros repeated according to multiplicity. Its filled unit lemniscate is the compact set
\[E_f=\{z\in\mathbb C:|f(z)|\le1\}.
\]
The monic normalization is the one used in the source's Problem 4.7 and its following lemniscate questions.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
If $E_f$ is connected, is it true that
\[\max_{z\in E_f}|f'(z)|\le\tfrac12n^2?\]
The source's Update 4.8 explains that this historical bound is false and gives the sharp replacement
\[\max_{z\in E_f}|f'(z)|\le 2^{1/n-1}n^2.
\]
The original bound and its corrected sharp version concern the same monic connected-lemniscate class. \sref{2304008}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Bound the derivative of a monic polynomial on its connected unit lemniscate. The originally proposed constant is false; the source records the stated sharp correction.
