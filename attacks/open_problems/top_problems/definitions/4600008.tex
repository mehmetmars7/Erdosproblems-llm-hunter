\section*{Factor maps between sofic shifts}\label{4600008}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 4600008 · AMR-045-0008 · Dynamical Systems · AMR Open Problem Lists}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $X\subset A^{\mathbb Z}$ and $Y\subset B^{\mathbb Z}$ be nonempty
two-sided sofic shifts. Here a sofic shift consists of the label sequences
of bi-infinite paths in a finite directed graph with edges labelled by a
finite alphabet. The spaces have the product topology and left shifts.
Write $\mathcal L_n(X)$ for their occurring words of length $n$, and set
\[
 h(X)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac1n\log|\mathcal L_n(X)|,
\]
using natural logarithms. Define $h(Y)$ analogously and assume $h(X)\ge h(Y)$.

Give necessary and sufficient conditions, stated in terms of the two sofic
systems or their finite labelled graph presentations, for a factor map
$\pi:X\to Y$ to exist. A factor map is a continuous surjection with
$\pi\sigma=\sigma\pi$. Equivalently, there must be a radius $r\ge0$ and
a rule on the allowed $(2r+1)$-letter words of $X$ whose coordinatewise
application has image exactly $Y$.

The problem includes equal-entropy pairs and systems which are not transitive;
the radius is not specified in advance. It asks for an actual classification
of when such a code exists, rather than the necessary entropy inequality
alone. Boyle's update records a solution of the transitive, aperiodic-target,
strictly unequal-entropy case. The target here retains the general factor-map
problem beyond that case, as in the catalogue.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Determine exactly when one sofic shift can continuously encode another while commuting with the shift.
