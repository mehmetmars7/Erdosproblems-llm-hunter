\section{Algebraic Stories — Lefschetz properties of graded algebras}\label{1500012}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 1500012 · Algebra}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $S=\mathbb C[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$ with its usual total-degree grading. For a homogeneous quotient $R$, define
\[\operatorname{Hilb}_R(t)=\sum_{e\geq0}(\dim_{\mathbb C}R_e)t^e.
\]
A generic tuple of forms has its coefficient vector in a suitable nonempty Zariski open subset of the product of the prescribed homogeneous pieces.For a graded algebra $A=\bigoplus_{i\geq0}A_i$, multiplication by a generic linear form $\ell\in A_1$ has maximal rank if its rank is the minimum of the dimensions of its domain and codomain. The weak Lefschetz property (WLP) requires maximal rank for $\ell:A_i\to A_{i+1}$ for all $i\geq0$. The strong Lefschetz property (SLP) requires maximal rank for $\ell^e:A_i\to A_{i+e}$ for every $i\geq0$ and $e\geq1$.Fix positive degrees $d_1,\ldots,d_r$ and generic $f_j\in S_{d_j}$, with $r>n$, and put $R=S/(f_1,\ldots,f_r)$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Does $R$ have both the WLP and the SLP for every choice of $n,r$ and the generator degrees? \sref{1500012}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Do all quotients by more generic forms than variables have both weak and strong Lefschetz properties?
