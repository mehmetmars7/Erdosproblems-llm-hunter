\section{Kirby Problem 3.61}\label{2859}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2859 · Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


A balanced sutured manifold $(M,\gamma)$ is a compact oriented $3$-manifold without closed components, with oriented sutures in annular neighborhoods $\gamma\subset\partial M$, whose complement is $R_+(\gamma)\sqcup R_-(\gamma)$, with compatible boundary orientations, $\chi(R_+)=\chi(R_-)$, and every boundary component meeting $\gamma$. The group $\operatorname{SFH}(M,\gamma;k)$ is sutured Heegaard Floer homology, the homology of its admissible sutured Heegaard chain complex. The group $\operatorname{SHI}(M,\gamma;\mathbb C)$ is sutured instanton homology: the distinguished surface-eigenspace of instanton Floer homology of an admissible closure of the sutured manifold. Their isomorphism classes are independent of diagram and closure choices. For a closed genus-two surface $\Sigma\subset\operatorname{int}M$, let $\iota$ be its hyperelliptic involution, the involution with quotient $S^2$ and six branch points. The genus-two mutant $M_\Sigma$ is obtained by cutting along $\Sigma$ and regluing by $\iota$, leaving boundary sutures unchanged. For a knot disjoint from $\Sigma$, $K_\Sigma$ denotes its image in the mutant. All dimensions below are total, ungraded dimensions over $\mathbb F_2=\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Is genus-two mutation dimension preserving for Heegaard Floer homology? In the broad sutured formulation specified by the source, is
\[
\dim_{\mathbb F_2}\operatorname{SFH}(M,\gamma;\mathbb F_2)
=\dim_{\mathbb F_2}\operatorname{SFH}(M_\Sigma,\gamma;\mathbb F_2)
\]
for every balanced $(M,\gamma)$ and every such embedded $\Sigma$? In particular, ask the same equality for total $\widehat{\operatorname{HF}}$ of closed $3$-manifolds and total $\widehat{\operatorname{HFK}}$ of knots disjoint from the mutation surface. Equality in each spin$^c$, Alexander or other grading is not required. \sref{2859}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does genus-two mutation preserve the total dimension of sutured Heegaard Floer homology, and hence the total hat Floer dimensions of closed three-manifolds and knots?
