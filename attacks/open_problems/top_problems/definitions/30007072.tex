\section*{Uniform Cartier-section thresholds for Fano-type contractions}\label{30007072}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every $d>0$ and real $\varepsilon >0$, does $\tau (d,\varepsilon )>0$ exist such that each $\varepsilon -lc$ rational d-dimensional pair (X,B), Fano-type contraction $f:X\to Z$ of normal integral quasi-projective varieties with dim $Z>0$ and relatively nef $-(K_X+B)$, and closed $z\in Z$ admits a neighborhood U and nonzero effective Cartier D on U through z with $(f^-1(U),B+\tau $ f*D) lc? Fields are algebraically closed of characteristic zero.
