\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Is every group good in the sense of four-dimensional topological surgery? Goodness is the group-theoretic condition that permits the disk-embedding theorem with that fundamental group: algebraically suitable immersed disks in a topological four-manifold can be replaced by embedded disks with the required boundaries and dual spheres. The question quantifies over all groups, rather than only groups of a given growth type.
