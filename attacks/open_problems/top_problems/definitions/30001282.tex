\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
If $k$ is a number field or a finite extension of $\mathbb{Q}_p$ and $X$ is a smooth geometrically connected hyperbolic curve over $k$ whose boundary has no $k$-rational point, does $a\mapsto s_a$ biject $X(k)$ with conjugacy classes of sections of $\pi_1(X/k)$?
