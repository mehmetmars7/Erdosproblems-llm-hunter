\section*{Multiple ergodic averages — Problem 26}\label{4800026}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 4800026 · AMR-047-0026 · Dynamical Systems · AMR Open Problem Lists}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A Hardy field is a field of germs at $+\infty$
of real-valued differentiable functions on half-lines,
closed under differentiation. Two functions represent
the same germ when they eventually agree. Seek
a function $a$ belonging to some Hardy field with
\[
                 a(t)/t^k\longrightarrow+\infty
                          \quad\text{for every integer }k\ge1.
\]
Choose an eventual representative and set
$b(n)=\lfloor a(n)\rfloor$ for all sufficiently
large $n$; its finitely many earlier terms may
be assigned positive integers.

The required sequence must have both properties
below for every $\ell\ge1$ and every invertible
measure-preserving $T$ on a probability space.
For all integer $k_1,\ldots,k_\ell$ and bounded
functions $f_i$, the averages
\[
           \frac1N\sum_{n=1}^N\prod_{i=1}^\ell
                           f_i\circ T^{k_i b(n)}
\]
must converge in $L^2(\mu)$. For all nonzero
integers $k_i$ and all $A$ with $\mu(A)>0$,
some $n$ with $b(n)\ne0$ must satisfy
$\mu(A\cap T^{k_1b(n)}A\cap\cdots\cap T^{k_\ell b(n)}A)>0$.

The problem is to construct one such Hardy-field
function and prove these universal assertions.
Membership in a Hardy field is an additional
regularity requirement beyond rapid growth of an
arbitrary sequence. No ergodicity or pointwise
convergence is required. Convergence must hold for
all integer powers, and recurrence for all nonzero
powers, independently of the chosen system.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Find a super-polynomial Hardy-field sequence that supports multiple recurrence and mean convergence along every finite collection of powers.
