\section*{Full finite-level Ramanujan-Arthur decomposition}\label{30007262}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let X/Fq be smooth projective geometrically connected, G/Fq split connected semisimple, and D any effective divisor. Consider compactly supported cuspidal functions on $G(F)\backslash G(A_F)$ at full congruence level D. For each nilpotent orbit O of the dual group choose its SL2 homomorphism phi, and at a good degree-d place require the unramified Satake class to lie in $\phi (\operatorname{diag}(q^{d/2},q^{-d/2}))$ times a semisimple element of the centralizer whose eigenvalues in every algebraic representation have modulus one under every complex embedding. Must the associated orbit-indexed cuspidal subspaces give a direct-sum decomposition of the whole space, both over Q and over $Qbar_\ell $, with no restriction on D or characteristic?
