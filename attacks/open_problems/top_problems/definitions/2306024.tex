\section{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 6.24}\label{2306024}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2306024 · Analysis}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Write $\mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}$ and $\mathbb T=\partial\mathbb D$.For a nonconstant holomorphic function $f$, let $n_f(w)$ be the number of solutions of $f(z)=w$ in $\mathbb D$, counted with multiplicity and allowed to be infinite. It is areally mean $p$-valent, for $p>0$, if
\[\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^R n_f(\rho e^{it})\rho\,d\rho\,dt\le\pi pR^2\qquad(R>0).\]
Constants in coefficient asymptotics may depend on the particular function. Coefficients are not normalized unless stated.Let $S(1)$ be the class of mean-univalent holomorphic functions with $\max(|a_0|,|a_1|)=1$. Here further require $f(z)=z+\sum_{n\ge2}a_nz^n$. For $0<r<1$, derivative and logarithmic-derivative bounds are pointwise bounds on $|z|=r$; a zero at $z\ne0$ makes $|f'/f|$ infinite there.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Prove or disprove $|f(z)|\le r/(1-r)^2$ for every such $f$ and every $|z|=r<1$. Also determine the sharp bounds for $|f'(z)|$ and $|f'(z)|/|f(z)|$ in this class. The source records the covering and lower-distortion conclusion $|f(z)|\ge r/(1+r)^2$ as background. \sref{2306024}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Establish the proposed upper distortion bound for normalized mean-univalent functions and determine their sharp derivative and logarithmic-derivative bounds.
