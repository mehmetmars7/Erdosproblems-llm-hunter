\section*{B-semiampleness for compact Kähler log-smooth fibrations}\label{30007039}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $f:Y\to X$ be surjective with connected fibers between smooth connected compact Kähler manifolds. Let $\Delta $ be effective rational SNC with coefficients in [0,1] and $K_Y+\Delta $ Q-linearly equal to f*L for $L\in Pic(X)\otimes Q$. Is the canonical-bundle-formula moduli rational line b-semiample: on some smooth Kähler modification $S\to X$, does it become a globally generated line after a positive integral multiple, and pull back as the same rational line on every subsequent smooth modification?
