\section*{Triangular-cylinder approximation of aligned radial sweeps}\label{30007109}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
In a Euclidean plane $P\subset R^{3}$ with unit normal e, take independent $a,b\in P$, $J=[\alpha ,\beta ]$, continuous $R:J\to [0,\infty )$, $C^{1}$ $w:J\to P$, $p_{0}\in P$ and $L>0$. If $w\prime (t)=\lambda (t)(a+tb)$ with continuous $\lambda $, does $S={p_{0}+\rho (a+tb)+s(e+w(t)):t\in J,0\leq \rho \leq R(t),|s|\leq L}$ admit, for every tagged partition of J, one compact nondegenerate triangular-base cylinder per interval with axis e+w(tag), so that the total perpendicular-base area tends uniformly in partitions and tags to $|det_P(a,b)|\int _J$ $R(t)^{2}/[2\surd (1+|w(t)|^{2})]dt$ as mesh tends to zero?
