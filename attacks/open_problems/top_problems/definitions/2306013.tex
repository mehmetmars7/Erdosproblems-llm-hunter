\section{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 6.13}\label{2306013}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2306013 · Analysis}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $\mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}$, $\mathbb T=\partial\mathbb D$, and $\mathbb D^*=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|>1\}$. Let $S$ be the class of analytic injective functions $f:\mathbb D\to\mathbb C$ normalized by $f(0)=0$, $f^{\prime}(0)=1$, with expansion $f(z)=z+\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}a_nz^n$ and $a_1=1$. Fix positive integers $k,m,n$. Coefficients $c_0,\ldots,c_m\in\mathbb C$ may depend on $f,k,m,n$, and must satisfy $c_0=1$ and $|c_m|\ge4^{-m}$. For an exponent $\beta_m$, the desired estimates are \[\left|\sum_{i=0}^mc_i a_{n+j+i}\right|\le K n^{\beta_m},\qquad 0\le j\le k,\] with a constant $K$ allowed to depend on $f,k,m$ but independent of $n$. A function in $S$ is starlike if its image is starlike about $0$: $tw\in f(\mathbb D)$ for every $w\in f(\mathbb D)$ and $0\le t\le1$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For each $m$, determine the best possible exponents $\beta_m$ for which these estimates hold for every $f\in S$ and every $k$, with the stated quantifiers on $K$ and the $c_i$. Preserve the proposed refinements $\beta_2=-1/3$, estimates with $\beta_3=-1/2+\varepsilon$ for every $\varepsilon>0$, and an admissible $\beta_m<-1/2$ for $m>4$. For the starlike subclass the source gives the sharp exponent $-1+2/(m+1)$; the update provides an improved general bound approaching $-1/2$. \sref{2306013}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Determine the optimal decay exponents of short coefficient combinations of univalent functions, retaining the coefficient-normalization constraints and the proposed small-order refinements.
