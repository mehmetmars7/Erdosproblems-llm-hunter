\section*{Kourovka Notebook Problem 21.54}\label{2563}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2563 · KOU-21.54 · Group Theory · Kourovka Notebook - New Problems, Issue 21}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $G$ be a finite soluble group, meaning its iterated commutator
subgroups eventually become trivial. Suppose a subgroup
$S\le\operatorname{Aut}(G)$ is isomorphic to $S_3$, with generators
$\sigma,\rho$ satisfying $\sigma^2=\rho^3=1$ and
$\sigma\rho\sigma=\rho^{-1}$. Write $g^s=s(g)$ and
$[g,s]=g^{-1}g^s$ for $g\in G,s\in S$.
Define
\[
 M(G)=\{[g,\sigma]:g\in G\}.
\]
The triality assumption is the identity
$m\,m^\rho m^{\rho^2}=1$ for every $m\in M(G)$, in that order.
The set $M(G)$ need not be a subgroup.

Assume also $G=[G,S]$, where $[G,S]$ is generated by all $[g,s]$.
For positive integers $d,n$, suppose there exist
$m_1,\ldots,m_d\in M(G)$ such that
\[
 G=\langle m_i^s:1\le i\le d,\ s\in S\rangle,
 \qquad m^n=1\quad\text{for every }m\in M(G).
\]
The exponent condition is imposed on all members of $M(G)$, not merely
the selected generators.

For a finite soluble group, its Fitting height is the least length
of a normal series with nilpotent factors; a group is nilpotent when
its lower central series terminates at $1$.
Is there a function $f(d,n)$ such that $h(G)\le f(d,n)$ for every
triality group satisfying these conditions? The bound must be independent
of $|G|$ and of the particular automorphisms realizing triality.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For finite soluble triality groups generated by $d$ triality elements and their images, does bounded exponent on those elements bound Fitting height in terms of $d$ and $n$?
