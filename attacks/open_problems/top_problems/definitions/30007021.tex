\section*{The cohomological Hikita isomorphism for quivers}\label{30007021}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For any finite oriented quiver, allowing loops and multiple arrows, put $G=\prod GL(V_i)$ and $N=\oplus _{i\to j}Hom(V_i,V_j)\oplus _i$ $Hom(V_i,W_i)$. Let F be any linear flavor torus commuting with G, and choose a character $\nu $ for which stable equals semistable in the zero cotangent moment fiber and G acts freely. Set $X=\mu ^-1(0)^{\nu -stable}/G$. For the commutative flavor-deformed BFN Coulomb algebra $A_F$ without loop rotation, let $A_F^\nu $ denote the quotient by its nonzero $\nu$-weights, without reduction. Must the natural coefficient maps from $R=H*_{G×F}(point;C)$ onto $H*_F(X;C)$ and $A_F^\nu $ be surjective with identical kernels, giving a graded R-algebra isomorphism? Empty X with zero ring is included.
