\section*{Good Freiman models from polynomial coset covers}\label{20000902}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 20000902 · AIM-COMBINATORICS-0027 · Combinatorics · AIM Workshop Problem Lists}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $G$ be an abelian group, let $A\subset G$ be finite and nonempty, and suppose $|A+A|\leq K|A|$ for a real $K\geq1$.
A Freiman isomorphism of order two from $A'\subseteq A$ into an abelian group $H$ is an injective map $\phi:A'\to H$ such that, for every $a,b,c,d\in A'$,
\[
 a+b=c+d\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \phi(a)+\phi(b)=\phi(c)+\phi(d).
\]
Thus $\phi$ is a bijection onto its image and preserves both additive quadruple relations and their failure; it need not extend to a group homomorphism.

The question asks whether there are absolute constants $C\geq1$ and $c>0$ such that every such $G,A,K$ has a nonempty subset $A'$ and a finite abelian group $H$ with
\[
 |A'|\geq cK^{-C}|A|,\qquad
 |H|\leq CK^C|A|,
\]
and a Freiman isomorphism of order two from $A'$ into $H$.
The constants must be independent of the original group's torsion, rank, and size.
The model is permitted to change both the ambient group and the retained subset, but only a polynomial loss in $K$ is allowed.
The upper bound measures the entire model group, not merely the image of $A'$.
This formulation makes explicit the absolute constants hidden in the source's $K^{O(1)}$ notation and isolates its question for arbitrary abelian groups.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does every finite set of small doubling contain a polynomially large subset with a comparably small finite additive model?
