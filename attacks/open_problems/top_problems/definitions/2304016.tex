\section{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 4.16}\label{2304016}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2304016 · Analysis}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Write $\mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}$ and $\mathbb T=\partial\mathbb D$.Use the family $P_\epsilon(z)=\sum_{k=1}^n\epsilon_kz^k$, $\epsilon\in\{-1,1\}^n$, consisting of $2^n$ polynomials. Put
\[m(P_\epsilon)=\min_{|z|=1}|P_\epsilon(z)|.
\]
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Does
\[2^{-n}\#\{\epsilon\in\{-1,1\}^n:m(P_\epsilon)\ge1\}\longrightarrow0\quad(n\to\infty)?\]
If this is false, determine the correct typical asymptotic behaviour of the minimum modulus, including its scale or limiting bounds for all but $o(2^n)$ sign choices. \sref{2304016}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does almost every plus-or-minus-one polynomial have modulus below one somewhere on the unit circle? If not, determine the correct typical minimum behaviour.
