\section*{Kirby Problem 4.54}\label{2930}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2930 · KP-4.54 · Topology · Kirby's Problems in Low-Dimensional Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For an oriented connected four-manifold $M$, let $\pi=\pi_1(M)$ and $A=\pi_2(M)$, viewed as a left $\mathbb Z[\pi]$-module by deck transformations. The homotopy two-type has a Postnikov class $\kappa\in H^3(\pi;A)$, specifying its $K(A,2)$ fibration over $K(\pi,1)$.

The equivariant intersection form is the sesquilinear pairing $\lambda_M:A\times A\to\mathbb Z[\pi]$ obtained by intersecting lifts of sphere representatives with translates in the universal cover. With compatible conventions,
\[
\lambda_M(a,b)=\sum_{g\in\pi}(a\cdot gb)g,
\qquad \lambda_M(b,a)=\overline{\lambda_M(a,b)},
\]
where the involution sends $g$ to $g^{-1}$. Compact representatives make the sum finite. The source's quadratic two-type is the quadruple $(\pi,A,\lambda_M,\kappa)$; no additional unspecified quadratic refinement is included in this terminology.

Which algebraic quadruples occur for closed oriented topological four-manifolds? Which occur for closed oriented smooth four-manifolds? Seek intrinsic necessary and sufficient realization conditions, including results for prescribed families of fundamental groups such as finite noncyclic groups.

Two quadruples are identified by a group isomorphism and a compatible module isomorphism preserving the form and Postnikov class. Realizing the module and class by a CW complex alone is insufficient: the requested realization is a genuine closed manifold with the specified intersection pairing. Smooth and topological realizability are distinct questions, and stabilization by extra hyperbolic summands does not by itself realize the original quadruple.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Which fundamental-group, second-homotopy, intersection-form, and Postnikov data arise from closed topological or smooth four-manifolds?
