\section*{Conditional abelianity for special half-weight fourfold pairs}\label{30007024}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Assume the manifold abelianity conjecture for special compact Kähler manifolds in the precise form Assumption ass:manifold of the cited paper. If X is a smooth connected projective complex fourfold and D a nonempty smooth connected reduced divisor whose square-root stack sqrt[2](X,D) is special, must $\pi _{1}(X$ minus D) modulo the normal closure of the square of a positive meridian around D be virtually abelian?
