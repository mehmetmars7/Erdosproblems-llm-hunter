\section{KLS Isoperimetric Coefficient Bound for Log-Concave Measures}\label{30004947}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 30004947 · Probability}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.


\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For a log-concave probability measure $\mu$ on $\mathbb R^d$, let its Cheeger coefficient be the infimum of $\mu^+(A)/\min(\mu(A),1-\mu(A))$ over measurable sets, where $\mu^+(A)$ is the outer boundary measure. Must this coefficient be at least an absolute positive constant times the same infimum restricted to half-spaces? The constant is required to be independent of the dimension and of $\mu$.
