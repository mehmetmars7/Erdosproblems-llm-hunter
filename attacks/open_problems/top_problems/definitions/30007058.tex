\section*{P=W for fixed-determinant trace-free coprime moduli}\label{30007058}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For a smooth connected projective complex curve of genus at least two, any composite rank $r\geq 4$ and degree d coprime to r, fix a degree-d determinant L. Under nonabelian Hodge correspondence between stable trace-free L-determinant Higgs moduli and its twisted $SL_r$ character variety, does the Hitchin perverse filtration $P_k$ equal $W_{2k}=W_{2k+1}$ on rational cohomology in every degree?
