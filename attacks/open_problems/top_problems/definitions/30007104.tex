\section*{Maker winning strategy for Snaky on the infinite square lattice}\label{30007104}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\[\{(0,0),(1,0),(2,0),(3,0),(3,1),(4,1)\}\]
up to translation, rotation or reflection against every legal Breaker strategy?
