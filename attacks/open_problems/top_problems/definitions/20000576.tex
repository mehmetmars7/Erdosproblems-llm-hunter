\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Does the pair $(L^2,L^1)$ have the Lipschitz extension property? Explicitly, is there a constant $C<\infty$ such that every Lipschitz map from every subset of the Hilbert space $L^2$ into $L^1$ extends to the whole $L^2$ with Lipschitz constant at most $C$ times the original constant?
