\section*{Bounded local klt complements for Fano contractions}\label{30007014}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every $d>0$ and positive rational $\varepsilon $, does an integer $N(d,\varepsilon )>0$ work for each normal integral $\varepsilon -lc$ Q-Gorenstein d-fold over an algebraically closed characteristic-zero field with a projective connected-fiber contraction $f:X\to Z$ and relatively ample $-K_X$, so that near each closed base point there is effective rational B with (X,B) klt and $N(K_X+B)$ integral Cartier and linearly trivial?
