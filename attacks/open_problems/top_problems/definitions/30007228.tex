\section*{A spherical distribution of the low-temperature total quantum spin}\label{30007228}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every positive half-integer spin S and every sufficiently large fixed finite beta, do zero-field even periodic $Z^3$ Heisenberg cubes have volume-normalized total spin whose moment generating function tends to sinh(m|t|)/(m|t|) for some $m>0$ and every $t\in R^3$? Require the averaged finite-volume spin-dot-spin sum divided by volume squared to tend to $m^2$.
