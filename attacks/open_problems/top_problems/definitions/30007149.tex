\section*{Bounded coordinates for negatively pinched Kähler manifolds}\label{30007149}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Must a contractible complex manifold with a complete smooth Kähler metric satisfying $-B\leq sec\leq -A<0$ for finite positive $A\leq B$ admit a bounded holomorphic map into $C^{n}$ with everywhere nonzero complex Jacobian, where n is its complex dimension?
