\section{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 6.40}\label{2306040}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2306040 · Analysis}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Write $\mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}$ and $\mathbb T=\partial\mathbb D$. Let $S$ consist of holomorphic injective functions $f(z)=z+\sum_{n\ge2}a_nz^n$ on $\mathbb D$; set $a_0=0$, $a_1=1$. A function in $S$ is starlike if its image is starlike about $0$, and convex if its image is a convex domain.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every $f\in S$ and every integer $n\ge1$, must $1+\sum_{j=2}^{n}|a_{2j-1}|\ge|a_n|^2$? For real coefficients the source records the related signed inequality $1+\sum_{j=2}^{n}a_{2j-1}\ge a_n^2$; the question is the displayed modulus version for the full complex-coefficient class. \sref{2306040}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does the sum of odd coefficient moduli up to degree $2n-1$ bound the square of the nth coefficient modulus?
