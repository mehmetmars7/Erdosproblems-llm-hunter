\section*{Harmonic dimension comparison in dimension three}\label{30007146}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every complete smooth metric g on $R^{3}$ with $Ric_g\geq 0$ and each integer $k\geq 0$, must the space of real harmonic functions of growth at most degree k have dimension $\leq (k+1)^{2}$, the Euclidean value? Each harmonic function may have its own growth constant.
