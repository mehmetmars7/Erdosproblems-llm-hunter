\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Does every simple separable unital nuclear $C^*$-algebra have uniform property $\Gamma$; that is, for every $k\geq2$, does its central sequence algebra contain $k$ pairwise orthogonal positive contractions whose trace is $1/k$ for every limit trace?
