\section*{Public pensions and longevity risk}\label{30007761}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Consider a retirement cohort with privately observed type \(\theta\sim F\), initial wealth \(w_\theta\), survival probabilities \(q_\theta(t)\), time preference \(\beta_\theta\in(0,1)\), consumption utility \(u_\theta\), and bequest utility \(v_\theta\). A pension rule \(b_t(x)\geq0\) depends only on observable characteristics \(x\). Retirees can save at a specified return and purchase available private annuities at contracts and prices determined by an explicitly specified market; borrowing and payout restrictions must be given. Let \(c_{\theta t}(b)\) and estate \(A_\theta(b)\) be optimal choices and outcomes, incorporating survival uncertainty. With fixed nonnegative welfare weights \(\omega_\theta\), budget \(R\geq0\), and convergent welfare and cost expressions, characterize measurable feasible rules maximizing
\[\int \omega_\theta\mathbb E_\theta\!\left[\sum_{t\geq0}\beta_\theta^t\mathbf1_{\{\text{alive at }t\}}u_\theta(c_{\theta t}(b))+v_\theta(A_\theta(b))\right]dF(\theta)\]
subject to \(\int\sum_{t\geq0}q_\theta(t)b_t(x_\theta)/(1+r)^t\,dF(\theta)\leq R\), where \(r>-1\) is the government's discount rate, and \(q_\theta(t)=\Pr_\theta(\text{alive at }t)\). Specify incentive restrictions if observable pension categories are manipulable. Determine when an optimal rule retains a deferral interval followed by level payments, when heterogeneity or private-market responses overturn that shape, and which linked mortality and asset data identify the welfare comparison. A representative-retiree deferred-annuity result is a starting point, not evidence that this richer specification is a source-authored open conjecture.
