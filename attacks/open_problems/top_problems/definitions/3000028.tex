\section*{Decomposition of oriented k-partition-connected digraphs}\label{3000028}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 3000028 · AMR-029-0028 · Combinatorics · AMR Open Problem Lists}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $D=(V,A)$ be a finite loopless digraph, with a vertex $r$ of
indegree zero, and let $k\ge1$. Distinct parallel arcs remain distinct
edges when directions are forgotten. Say that the underlying multigraph
is $k$-partition-connected when every partition $\mathcal P$ of $V$
into nonempty classes satisfies
\[
  |\{a\in A:\text{the endpoints of }a\text{ lie in different classes}\}|
                                       \ge k(|\mathcal P|-1).
\]
Assume this condition and $d_D^-(v)\ge k$ for each $v\ne r$.

Must $A$ have a partition $A_1\sqcup\cdots\sqcup A_k$ such that every
spanning subdigraph $(V,A_i)$ is weakly connected and
\[
                           d^-_{(V,A_i)}(v)\ge1
                                  \quad(v\ne r,\ 1\le i\le k)?
\]
Weak connectivity means connectivity after discarding arc directions.
The vertex set is retained in every part, including vertices that would
otherwise be isolated.

The conclusion is not a decomposition into rooted arborescences:
directed cycles are permitted and reachability from $r$ is not separately
required. The incoming-degree and undirected connectivity conditions
must nevertheless hold in the same parts. Splitting the arcs once to
satisfy degrees and a second time to obtain connectivity would not
establish a common decomposition satisfying both requirements.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Do the natural partition-connectivity and indegree conditions
guarantee a decomposition into weakly connected spanning digraphs with positive indegree away from the root?
