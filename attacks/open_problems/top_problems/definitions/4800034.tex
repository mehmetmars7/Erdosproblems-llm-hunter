\section{Multiple ergodic averages — Problem 34}\label{4800034}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 4800034 · Dynamical Systems}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $(X,\mathcal X,\mu)$ be a probability space. For each positive integer $q$, let $T_q$ be an invertible measure-preserving transformation, with $T_1=\operatorname{id}$ and $T_mT_n=T_{mn}$ for all $m,n\ge1$. This is a system with multiplicative structure. The harmless convention $T_0=\operatorname{id}$ is also used in the source, although no zero index occurs below. For a measurable set $A$, $T_qA$ denotes its image.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every such system and every $A\in\mathcal X$ with $\mu(A)>0$, are there positive integers $m>n$ such that \[\mu\bigl(T_{2mn}A\cap T_{(m-n)(m+n)}A\bigr)>0?\] \sref{4800034}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does every positive-measure set recur under two multiplicative transformations whose indices form the legs of a Pythagorean triple?
