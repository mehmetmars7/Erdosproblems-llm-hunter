\section*{The exact weak-recovery threshold for the symmetric three-community SBM}\label{30007401}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For the sparse equal-size three-community stochastic block model with within-community probability a/n and between-community probability b/n, is weak recovery possible exactly when $(a-b)^2>3(a+2b)$? Above this threshold require an O(n log n) algorithm achieving overlap at least 1/3+epsilon with probability tending to one; at or below it require every graph-based randomized estimator’s expected overlap to tend to 1/3.
