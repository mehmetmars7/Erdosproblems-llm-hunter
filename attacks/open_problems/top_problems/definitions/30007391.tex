\section*{A size-independent Gibbs Poincaré constant throughout high-temperature SK}\label{30007391}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every fixed $0\leq \beta <1$ in Gaussian zero-field SK, is there $C_\beta <infinity$ such that, with probability tending to one over disorder, $Var_\mu (f)\leq C_\beta $ D(f) simultaneously for every spin function? Equivalently, the single-site discrete heat-bath spectral gap is at least $1/(C_\beta $ n).
