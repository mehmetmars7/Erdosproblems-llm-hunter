\section*{Power-free values of irreducible polynomials of degrees four to eight}\label{30007274}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For irreducible f in Z[x] of degree d in \{4,5,6,7,8\}, set $k=d-2$ and suppose no prime p has $p^k$ dividing every value f(n). Does the count of positive $n<=X$ for which f(n) has no prime-kth-power divisor equal $c_{f,k}$ X+o(X), where $c_{f,k}$ is the positive product over primes of 1 minus the proportion of roots of f modulo $p^k$?
