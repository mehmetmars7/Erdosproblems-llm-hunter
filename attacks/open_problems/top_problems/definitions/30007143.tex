\section*{Exact ball-packing criterion in symplectic dimensions at least six}\label{30007143}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For $n\geq 3$ and positive capacities $R,R_{1},\dots ,R_k$, do neighborhood-defined symplectic embeddings of the disjoint closed standard 2n-balls of capacities $R_i$ into the interior of the standard ball of capacity R exist exactly when $\Sigma _i$ $R_i^{n}<R^{n}$ and $R_i+R_j<R$ for every distinct pair i,j? Capacity is $\pi $ times squared Euclidean radius.
