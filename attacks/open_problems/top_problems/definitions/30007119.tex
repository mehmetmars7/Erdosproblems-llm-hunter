\section*{Gaussian propeller partition bound}\label{30007119}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every $d,k\geq 1$ and measurable partition $A_{1},\dots ,A_k$ of $R^{d}$, must $\Sigma _i\Vert \int _{A_i}x$ $d\gamma _d(x)\Vert ^{2}\leq 9/(8\pi )$, where $\gamma _d$ is standard Gaussian probability? Empty cells and arbitrary cell masses are allowed. For $d\geq 2,k\geq 3$, is the bound attained by three planar sectors of angle $2\pi /3$ times their orthogonal complement, with remaining cells empty?
