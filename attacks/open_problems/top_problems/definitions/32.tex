\section{The Kakeya Conjecture}\label{32}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 32 · Geometry}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.


\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every integer $n\geq2$, let $E\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ contain a line segment of length one parallel to each direction. Must its Hausdorff dimension be $n$? A set with this segment property is called a Kakeya set.
\par\smallskip\textit{Formulation references:} \sref{32}{1}, \sref{32}{2}.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For every integer $n\geq2$, let $E\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ contain a line segment of length one parallel to each direction. Must its Hausdorff dimension be $n$? A set with this segment property is called a Kakeya set.
