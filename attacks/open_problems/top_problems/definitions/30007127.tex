\section*{Single-lattice covering density of order n log n}\label{30007127}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Does an absolute $C>0$ exist such that, for every $n\geq 2$ and convex body $K\subset R^{n}$, some full-rank lattice $\Lambda $ satisfies $K+\Lambda =R^{n}$ and $vol_n(K)/det\Lambda \leq Cn$ log n? No symmetry, smoothness or normalization of K is assumed.
\subsection{Short English statement}
