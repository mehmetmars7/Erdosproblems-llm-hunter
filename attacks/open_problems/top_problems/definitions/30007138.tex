\section*{Uniform bi-Hölder transport under weak MTW}\label{30007138}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Fix a compact connected smooth boundaryless Riemannian manifold of dimension at least two satisfying weak MTW for half-squared distance, and positive numbers $\lambda \leq \Lambda $. Do constants $\alpha >0$ and $C<\infty $ depending only on that metric and $\lambda ,\Lambda $ guarantee that for every pair of probability densities between $\lambda $ and $\Lambda $ almost everywhere, the optimal map has a homeomorphic representative and it and its inverse are globally $\alpha$-Hölder with constant C?
