\section{Rank-One Equivalences for Elliptic Curves}\label{30003878}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 30003878 · Number Theory}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.


\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For an elliptic curve $E/\mathbb{Q}$ and $r\in\{0,1\}$, the following are conjectured equivalent: $\operatorname{rank}E(\mathbb{Q})=r$ and the Tate–Shafarevich group $\operatorname{Sha}(E/\mathbb{Q})$ is finite; $\operatorname{corank}_{\mathbb{Z}_p}\operatorname{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/\mathbb{Q})=r$; and $\operatorname{ord}_{s=1}L(s,E/\mathbb{Q})=r$.
\par\smallskip\textit{Formulation references:} \sref{30003878}{1}, \sref{30003878}{2}.
