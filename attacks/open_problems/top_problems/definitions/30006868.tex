\section*{\texorpdfstring{Dixmier Conjecture}{Dixmier Conjecture}}\label{30006868}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a characteristic-zero field $K$, the first Weyl algebra is
\[
 A_1(K)=K\langle x,\partial\rangle/(\partial x-x\partial-1),
\]
the associative algebra of polynomial differential operators in one variable. The selected Dixmier conjecture asks whether every unital $K$-algebra homomorphism $\phi:A_1(K)\to A_1(K)$ is bijective, and hence an automorphism. An endomorphism is specified by a pair $P=\phi(x)$, $Q=\phi(\partial)$ satisfying $QP-PQ=1$; the assertion is that such a pair generates all of $A_1(K)$ and yields an invertible substitution. This entry is specifically the first Weyl algebra in characteristic zero. The proof-claim title in the supplied bibliography is not treated as proof of this assertion.
\par\smallskip\textit{Formulation and concept references:} \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.06959}{[S1]}.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Must every endomorphism of the one-variable Weyl algebra over a characteristic-zero field be invertible?
