\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For a convex body $K\subseteq\mathbb R^d$, $d\geq3$, let $\Pi K$ be its projection body. Is the affine-invariant ratio $|\Pi K|/|K|^{d-1}$ minimized exactly by ellipsoids? The support function of $\Pi K$ in a direction is the $(d-1)$-volume of the orthogonal projection of $K$ onto its perpendicular hyperplane.
