\section*{An exponential threshold-repetition bound for arbitrary finite entangled games}\label{30007219}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every finite two-player one-round Boolean-acceptance game G with finite-dimensional entangled value $v<1$, is there universal $\kappa _0>0$ such that any finite-dimensional joint k-repetition strategy wins at least ceil((v+delta)k) coordinates with probability at most $\exp (-\kappa _0$ $\delta ^5$ k/(1+log d)), for $0<\delta <1-v$ and d the source answer-alphabet parameter? Correlated question distributions are allowed.
