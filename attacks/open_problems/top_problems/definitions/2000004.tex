\section{Some Open Problems in Elasticity — Lavrentiev phenomena}\label{2000004}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2000004 · Partial Differential Equations}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $\Omega\subset\mathbb R^3$ be a bounded Lipschitz domain. Write $\partial\Omega=\Gamma_D\cup\Gamma_N\cup N$, where $\Gamma_D,\Gamma_N$ are disjoint relatively open subsets, $\mathcal H^2(N)=0$, and $\mathcal H^2(\Gamma_D)>0$. Here $\mathcal H^2$ is two-dimensional Hausdorff measure. A deformation $y:\Omega\to\mathbb R^3$ belongs to the Sobolev space $W^{1,1}(\Omega;\mathbb R^3)$, so its weak derivative $Dy$ is defined almost everywhere. Prescribe $y=\bar y$ on $\Gamma_D$ in the trace sense and zero applied traction on $\Gamma_N$.
Let $\mathbb M_+^{3\times3}=\{A\in\mathbb R^{3\times3}:\det A>0\}$. The homogeneous stored-energy density $W:\mathbb M_+^{3\times3}\to[0,\infty)$ is $C^1$, satisfies frame indifference $W(RA)=W(A)$ for $R\in\operatorname{SO}(3)$, and satisfies
\[
 W(A)\longrightarrow+\infty\quad\text{as}\quad\det A\longrightarrow0^+.
\]
Extend $W$ by $+\infty$ when $\det A\le0$. Put
\[
 I(y)=\int_\Omega W(Dy(x))\,dx,\qquad
 \mathcal A=\{y\in W^{1,1}(\Omega;\mathbb R^3):I(y)<\infty,\ y|_{\Gamma_D}=\bar y\}.
\]
Assume $\mathcal A\ne\varnothing$. For a matrix $A$, $\operatorname{cof}A$ denotes its cofactor matrix and $|A|$ its Frobenius norm.
Let $\mathcal A_{\mathrm{Lip}}=\mathcal A\cap W^{1,\infty}(\Omega;\mathbb R^3)$, using the same prescribed trace, and require both compared classes to be nonempty with finite energy infima. A \emph{Lavrentiev gap} between the full energy class and Lipschitz deformations is the strict inequality
\[
 \inf_{y\in\mathcal A} I(y)<\inf_{y\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{Lip}}} I(y).
\]
More generally the same term denotes different infima after restricting to Sobolev classes $\mathcal A\cap W^{1,q}$ with larger integrability exponent $q$. Require growth conditions on $W$ under which every finite-energy deformation has a continuous representative in $\Omega$. For example, coercivity $W(A)\ge c|A|^p-C$ with $p>3$ ensures this by Sobolev embedding; this example does not restrict the question to that particular growth condition.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Can such a gap occur for elastostatics while every finite-energy deformation is continuous? Include the comparison between the unrestricted finite-energy class and more regular deformation classes, under the same displacement boundary conditions. \sref{2000004}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can the least elastic energy fall strictly when less regular deformations are allowed, even though growth of the stored energy makes every finite-energy deformation continuous?
