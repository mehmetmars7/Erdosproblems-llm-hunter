\section*{Orientation-compatible w-vertex cover}\label{3000062}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 3000062 · AMR-029-0062 · Combinatorics · AMR Open Problem Lists}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $D=(V,A)$ be a finite loopless digraph. Each arc $a=uv$ has a
nonnegative even integer weight $w_a$, given in binary. A $w$-vertex
cover is an integer vector $x\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}^V$ satisfying
\[
                         x_u+x_v\ge w_{uv}\quad(uv\in A).
\]
The inequality treats the endpoints symmetrically, even though the
extra requirement below depends on the direction of the arc. Parallel
arcs may carry different weights and must each be respected.

Find, in time polynomial in the encoded input size, such a cover with
\[
 x_v>0\ \Longrightarrow\
       \text{there exists }uv\in A\text{ with }x_u+x_v=w_{uv}
                     \qquad(v\in V).
\]
Every positive coordinate must therefore be supported by a tight
incoming arc. In particular, a vertex with no incoming arc must have
coordinate zero. A zero coordinate requires no witness.

The cited source proves that a cover satisfying these conditions
exists for every permissible instance; the unresolved task is its
polynomial-time construction. There is no objective of minimizing
$\sum_v x_v$, and an arbitrary minimum vertex cover need not satisfy
the directional witness condition. Fractional covers are not valid
outputs: integrality and the even-weight hypothesis belong to the
formulation. A running time polynomial in the numerical value of the
largest weight alone would be pseudopolynomial, not the requested
bound in its binary input length.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Find an integer weighted vertex cover in polynomial time so that every positive coordinate has a tight incoming arc.
