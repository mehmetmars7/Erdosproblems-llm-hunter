\section{Multidimensional Szemerédi Theorem Bounds}\label{108}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 108 · Combinatorics}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


For a fixed dimension $d\ge1$ and a finite pattern $F\subset\mathbb Z^d$ with at least two points, a \emph{nontrivial homothetic copy} is $x+rF=\{x+rv:v\in F\}$, where $x\in\mathbb Z^d$ and $r\in\mathbb N$. Put
\[
r_F(N)=\max\{|A|:A\subset\{1,\ldots,N\}^d,\ A\text{ contains no nontrivial copy of }F\}.
\]
Define $\log_1 t=\log t$ and $\log_{m+1}t=\log(\log_m t)$ for sufficiently large $t$. A density threshold of \emph{finite logarithmic type} has the form $C/\log_m M$ with a fixed integer $m\ge1$, a constant $C>0$, and $M$ the cardinality of the ambient finite structure. This is the minimum quantitative scale proposed in the source.
For a prime $p$, let $\mathbb F_p$ be the field with $p$ elements and $G_n=(\mathbb F_p)^n$. A square in $G_n^2$ is
\[
(x,y),\ (x+h,y),\ (x,y+h),\ (x+h,y+h),\qquad h\in G_n\setminus\{0\}.
\]
A three-dimensional corner in $G_n^3$ is
\[
(x,y,z),\ (x+h,y,z),\ (x,y+h,z),\ (x,y,z+h),\qquad h\ne0.
\]
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Find explicit quantitative density bounds of finite logarithmic type for instances of the multidimensional Szemerédi theorem. In the integer-grid formulation, the target is: for each fixed $d,F$, find $m,C$ such that
\[
r_F(N)\le\frac{C N^d}{\log_m(N^d)}
\]
for all sufficiently large $N$. The source specifically asks for such bounds for square-free subsets of $G_n^2$ and three-dimensional-corner-free subsets of $G_n^3$, with $m,C$ independent of $n$ (and allowed to depend on the fixed prime $p$ and the configuration). Here the ambient sizes are $p^{2n}$ and $p^{3n}$ respectively. The request concerns general instances of the theorem as well as these particular configurations. \sref{108}{1}\sref{108}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Obtain density thresholds controlled by a fixed finite number of logarithms for multidimensional arithmetic configurations, including squares and three-dimensional corners over finite fields, and fixed homothetic patterns in integer grids.
