\section*{A uniform bound on excess electrons in full Coulomb molecules}\label{30007235}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For the full nonrelativistic Coulomb Hamiltonian with two electron spin states, M fixed nuclei of arbitrary positions and charges $Z_j\geq 1$ and total $Z=sum$ $Z_j$, is strict n-electron binding possible only for $n\leq Z+CM$ for one universal constant C independent of charges and positions? Also ask that adding electrons above that threshold does not lower the ground energy.
