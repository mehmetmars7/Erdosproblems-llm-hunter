\section*{Arithmetic Stein-degree bounds for log Calabi–Yau boundaries}\label{30007013}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For each $d\geq 1$ and real $t>0$, is there a finite integer N(d,t) bounding $dim_k$ $H^{0}(S^\nu ,O_{S^\nu })$ whenever (X,B) is a normal integral projective lc rational pair over a characteristic-zero field k with dim $X=d$, $H^{0}(X,O_X)=k$ and $K_X+B$ Q-linearly trivial, and S is a prime boundary component of coefficient at least t? This must also bound the Stein degree of $S\to Spec$ k.
