\section{Volume Conjecture}\label{1333}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 1333 · Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $K\subset S^3$ be a knot whose complement admits a complete finite-volume hyperbolic metric of sectional curvature $-1$. Write $\operatorname{Vol}(S^3\setminus K)$ for its volume. Let $J_N(K;q)$ be the $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ colored Jones polynomial for the $N$-dimensional irreducible representation, in the normalization $J_N(U;q)=1$ for the unknot $U$; the zero-framing normalization is understood. Put $q_N=\exp(2\pi i/N)$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every hyperbolic knot $K\subset S^3$, does the limit exist and satisfy
\[
 \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{2\pi}{N}\log\bigl|J_N(K;q_N)\bigr|
 =\operatorname{Vol}(S^3\setminus K)?
\]
Here $\log$ is the natural logarithm, with $\log 0=-\infty$ when interpreting the sequence. \sref{1333}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Do the exponential growth rates of normalized colored Jones evaluations at the specified roots of unity recover the volumes of all hyperbolic knot complements?
