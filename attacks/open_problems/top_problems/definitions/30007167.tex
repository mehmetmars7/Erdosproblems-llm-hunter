\section*{Pointwise convergence of fourfold ergodic averages under mixing}\label{30007167}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every invertible mixing probability-preserving transformation T and bounded measurable $f_1,f_2,f_3,f_4$, do $(1/N)sum_{n=1}^N$ $f_1(T^n$ $x)f_2(T^{2n}x)f_3(T^{3n}x)f_4(T^{4n}x)$ converge almost everywhere to the product of the four integrals?
