\section{Slicing Axes-Parallel Rectangles}\label{5500074}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 5500074 · Combinatorics}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


An axis-parallel rectangle is a product of intervals on the $x$- and $y$-axes. Two rectangles are independent when their projections onto both axes are disjoint. A family is independent when every pair is. For $m\ge2$, define $f(m)$ as the least uniform number of horizontal and vertical lines sufficient to intersect every rectangle of any finite family with no independent subfamily of $m$ rectangles.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Determine $f(m)$. The source reports the bound $f(m)\le2m$ and calls it a substantial resolution of the original problem; the question recorded here asks for the minimum uniform number. \sref{5500074}{2}
