\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $G$ be a hyperbolic group with boundary homeomorphic to $S^2$. Can one prove that $G$ admits an action with unbounded orbits on a median space, equivalently that $G$ does not have Kazhdan's property $(T)$? More generally, is every medianized hyperbolic group cubulable, or can one at least prove that it is residually finite?
