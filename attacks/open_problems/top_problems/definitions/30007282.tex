\section*{Restricted geometric Langlands in the stated positive-characteristic regimes}\label{30007282}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let k be algebraically closed of characteristic $p>0$, ell different from p, X a smooth projective curve and G connected reductive. Assume that the Lie algebra admits an invariant nondegenerate symmetric form nondegenerate on every Levi center, Chevalley restriction is an isomorphism for every Levi, semisimple Lie-element scheme centralizers are Levi subgroups, and every nilpotent Lie element over every extension belongs to the Lie algebra of the unipotent radical of an extension-defined parabolic. Assume either descent of k,X,G from a finite field with k its algebraic closure, or that p is very good for G and does not divide its Weyl-group order. Must the existing primed restricted geometric Langlands functor have full spectral support, giving a QCoh-linear equivalence from ell-adic sheaves on $Bun_G$ with nilpotent singular support to IndCoh with nilpotent support on the entire restricted dual-group local-system prestack?
