\section*{Hodge classes on generic abelian-cover Jacobian powers}\label{30007034}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Fix a connected topological finite abelian cover of a compact oriented surface, allowing arbitrary base genus and branching with nontrivial branch meridians. Vary the full marked complex base structure and all branch positions. At a parameter s where each rational even tensor in marked $H^{1}$ that becomes a Hodge tensor was already Hodge throughout this variation, must every rational Hodge class on $(Jac(C_s)×M)^N$ be algebraic for every CM abelian variety M and every $N\geq 1$? The same s must work for all M.
