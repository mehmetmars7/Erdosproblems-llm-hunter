\section*{Integral Bloch statement for surfaces of geometric genus zero}\label{30007012}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every smooth connected projective complex surface S with $p_g(S)=0$, is the integral Albanese homomorphism $CH_{0}(S)^0\to Alb(S)(C)$ an isomorphism? In particular, if also $q(S)=0$, must degree give $CH_{0}(S)\cong Z$?
\subsection{Short English statement}
