\section*{The length exponent of critical honeycomb chords with fixed endpoints}\label{30007370}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For critical honeycomb chords weighted per visited vertex in convex pure lattice hexagons of diameter R converging to a regular hexagon, with prescribed side-interior boundary endpoints separated macroscopically, does length lie in $[R^{4/3-\epsilon },R^{4/3+\epsilon }]$ with probability tending to one for every $\epsilon >0$? Ask the same in the specified nondegenerate half-plane, comparable-width strip and pi/3 or 2pi/3 wedge geometries, uniformly on compact families, with partition mass at least $cR^{-5/4}$.
