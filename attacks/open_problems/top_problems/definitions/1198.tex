\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For a connected reductive group over a global field, describe the constituents and multiplicities of the discrete automorphic spectrum by global Arthur parameters. The desired description must agree with the local parameterizations at each place and with the associated automorphic $L$-functions.
