\section*{Amenable Cantor actions}\label{4600037}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 4600037 · AMR-045-0037 · Dynamical Systems · AMR Open Problem Lists}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $G$ be a countable discrete amenable group. Here amenability can be
expressed by a Følner sequence of finite nonempty sets $F_n\subset G$
with $|gF_n\mathbin{\triangle} F_n|/|F_n|\to0$ for every $g\in G$.
Let $G$ act by homeomorphisms on a Cantor space $X$, a nonempty compact
metrizable perfect totally disconnected space. Assume the action is
minimal: every orbit $Gx$ is dense in $X$.

The question is whether there exists a homeomorphism $T:X\to X$ whose
integer iterates have exactly the same orbit sets:
\[
 \{T^n x:n\in\mathbb Z\}=\{gx:g\in G\}
                \qquad(x\in X).
\]
Equivalently, is the given action topologically orbit equivalent to a
minimal $\mathbb Z$-action on a Cantor space, through a homeomorphism
carrying each orbit onto an orbit? Using $X$ itself loses no generality,
since such a homeomorphism can transport the proposed $\mathbb Z$-action.

The original group action is not assumed free. Orbit equivalence
compares orbit sets and does not require a group isomorphism or
continuous orbit cocycles. Conversely a merely measurable or Borel
orbit equivalence does not answer the topological question. Minimality
would automatically hold for $T$ once its orbits agree with the given
dense orbits. The problem concerns all countable amenable groups, beyond
the special lattice actions for which stronger classification results
are already available.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can every minimal amenable-group action on a Cantor space be generated, at the level of orbit sets, by one homeomorphism?
