\section{Kirby Problem 1.61}\label{2720}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2720 · Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


An integer homology 3-sphere $Y$ satisfies $H_*(Y;\mathbb Z)\cong H_*(S^3;\mathbb Z)$. Its Freedman filling $\Delta$ is a compact contractible topological 4-manifold with boundary $Y$. An integral homology cobordism $W$ between $Y$ and $S^3$ has boundary $Y\sqcup(-S^3)$ and homology-isomorphism boundary inclusions. Knot or link concordance in $W$ means disjoint locally flat embedded annuli with the prescribed boundary components. A homology 4-ball has the integral homology of $B^4$. A semi-free action of $\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z$ has stabilizer either trivial or the entire group. Arbitrary topological knot embeddings and disks may be wild; no local-linearity assumption is imposed on the actions below.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Resolve all of the following topological questions.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Is each locally flat knot in every integer homology 3-sphere concordant to a knot in $S^3$ in an integral homology cobordism? Can the cobordism also be simply connected?
\item Does every topologically embedded knot in $Y$ bound an embedded disk in its Freedman filling $\Delta$, without requiring local flatness? Does such a disk at least exist in some homology 4-ball bounded by $Y$?
\item Can a link in $Y$ have every component individually concordant to a knot in $S^3$, yet fail to be concordant as a link to any link in $S^3$?
\item If a semi-free action of $\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z$ on $Y$ fixes precisely a knot $K$, does it extend to a semi-free action on $\Delta$?
\end{enumerate} \sref{2720}{2}

\subsection{Short English statement}
Ask whether knots and links in homology spheres admit the stated topological concordances or disk fillings, including wild disks, and whether semi-free cyclic actions extend over the contractible filling.
