\section*{Unramified de Rham quantum Langlands at irrational level}\label{30007076}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every smooth connected projective complex curve X and connected simple complex group G with specified full root datum, lacing number r and Langlands dual $G\vee $, fix dual pinnings and a theta characteristic. Define $D_c(Bun_G)$ as the presentable derived category of all D-modules twisted by the adjoint determinant line to exponent $(c-h\vee )/(2h\vee )$. For each $c\in C$ outside Q, is there a C-linear DG equivalence $D_c(Bun_G(X))\simeq D_(-1/(rc))(Bun_{G\vee }(X))$, using all connected components and compatible with localization–Whittaker comparison, moving-point collisions and the corresponding central-torsor/transgression actions?
