\section{Representative transcendental entire dynamics}\label{5300059}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 5300059 · Dynamical Systems}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


A transcendental entire map is a holomorphic function $f:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C$ that is not a polynomial. Its dynamics means the iterates $f^n$, their orbit behaviour, and the stable (Fatou) and Julia sets: the Fatou set is the largest open set where the iterates form a normal family, and its complement is $J(f)$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Find a collection of representative transcendental entire maps whose dynamics can be understood and can model general phenomena. \sref{5300059}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Develop representative examples that make the dynamics of transcendental entire functions understandable.
