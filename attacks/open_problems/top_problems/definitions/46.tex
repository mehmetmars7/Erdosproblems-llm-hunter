\section{Hilbert's 16th Problem: Topology of Algebraic Curves and Limit Cycles}\label{46}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 46 · Geometry}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.


\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For a planar polynomial differential equation $\dot x=P(x,y)$, $\dot y=Q(x,y)$ with $\deg P,\deg Q\leq n$, determine the largest possible number of isolated periodic orbits, called limit cycles, and their possible relative positions. The other part of the problem asks for the topology and arrangements of connected components of real algebraic curves and surfaces of prescribed degree. The degree bound is fixed while the coefficients vary.
\par\smallskip\textit{Formulation references:} \sref{46}{1}, \sref{46}{2}.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Determine the topology of real algebraic curves and the maximum number of polynomial-flow limit cycles at each fixed degree.
