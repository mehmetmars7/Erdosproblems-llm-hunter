\section*{Positive lower density of arbitrarily large normalized prime gaps}\label{30007273}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
If $p_n$ is the nth prime, must the set of indices n with $p_{n+1}-p_n>C$ log $p_n$ have positive lower natural density for every fixed $C>0$? The positive density may depend on C.
