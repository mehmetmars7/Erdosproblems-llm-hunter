\section*{All rational-boundary projective lc fourfold programs terminate}\label{30007010}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For a projective lc fourfold with effective rational boundary over an algebraically closed characteristic-zero field, without assumed Q-factoriality or pseudo-effectivity, do all permitted negative extremal-ray programs have the required contractions and flips and finitely many birational steps, regardless of choices? Must every maximal program end with nef adjoint or a lower-dimensional relative-Picard-one Mori fiber contraction?
