\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Establish the Fourier restriction conjecture and Kakeya dimension conjecture in their full dimensional ranges. For restriction, determine the expected bounds for restricting Fourier transforms to curved hypersurfaces. For Kakeya, must a set in $\mathbb R^n$ containing a unit line segment in each direction have dimension $n$? The source poses these as an incidence-theoretic program, including the quantitative incidence bounds that underpin the two conjectures.
