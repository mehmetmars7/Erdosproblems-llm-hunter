\section*{Quenched critical Ising interfaces under weak bond disorder}\label{30007411}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every bond-disorder law rho satisfying the manuscript stationarity, boundedness, finite-dependence and symmetry conditions, does sufficiently small fixed disorder $\epsilon >0$ produce a quenched Dobrushin critical interface law converging in bounded-Lipschitz distance, in probability over disorder, to chordal $SLE_3$ in every marked bounded Jordan domain and every admissible deterministic lattice approximation? Require a disorder threshold independent of the domain and $\beta _c^\rho (\epsilon )=\log (1+\operatorname{sqrt}(2))/2+O_\rho (\epsilon ^2)$.
