\section*{Entropy bounds at perfect-matching marginals}\label{30007474}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let G be a loopless multigraph on 2m vertices and x in its perfect-matching polytope. Is the maximum entropy H(x) of a distribution of perfect matchings having edge marginals x between $F(x)-(2-2/m)B(x)$ and F(x), where $F(x)=-\sum _e$ $x_e$ $\log (x_e)$ and B is the sum of $-(1-x_e)\log (1-x_e)$?
