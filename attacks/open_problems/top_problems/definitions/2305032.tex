\section{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 5.32}\label{2305032}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2305032 · Analysis}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Write $\mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}$ and $\mathbb T=\partial\mathbb D$. All Taylor coefficients below are taken at $0$. Let $S$ be the class of holomorphic injective functions $g:\mathbb D\to\mathbb C$ with $g(0)=0$ and $g'(0)=1$. Let $\mathcal B$ be the Bloch space of holomorphic functions $h$ with
\[\|h\|_{\mathcal B}=|h(0)|+\sup_{z\in\mathbb D}(1-|z|^2)|h'(z)|<\infty.
\]
For $g\in S$, take the unique holomorphic logarithm $h=\log g'$ with $h(0)=0$, and let $\mathcal B_S=\{\log g':g\in S\}$. Let $\mathcal B_Q=\{\log g':g\in S\text{ admits a quasiconformal extension to }\widehat{\mathbb C}\}$. Such an extension is an orientation-preserving homeomorphism of the Riemann sphere that is locally in $W^{1,2}$ in charts and satisfies $|\partial_{\bar z}G|\le k|\partial_zG|$ almost everywhere for some $k<1$. Let $h_n=\log g_n'\in\mathcal B_S$ and $h=\log g'\in\mathcal B_S$, with the logarithms normalized at $0$. Their Bloch-norm difference is $\|h_n-h\|_{\mathcal B}=\sup_{z\in\mathbb D}(1-|z|^2)|h_n'(z)-h'(z)|$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Give a geometric characterization, in terms of the normalized conformal maps $g_n,g$ and their image domains, of the convergence $\|h_n-h\|_{\mathcal B}\to0$. Find conditions that are both necessary and sufficient, rather than only compact-local convergence of $g_n$. \sref{2305032}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Describe geometrically what convergence of logarithmic derivatives in the Bloch norm means for the corresponding normalized univalent maps.
