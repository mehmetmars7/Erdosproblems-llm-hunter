\section{The Heilbronn Triangle Problem}\label{1215}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 1215 · Geometry}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.


\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For an $n$-point subset $P$ of the unit square, take the least area of a triangle with three distinct vertices in $P$. Determine the supremum of this least area over all choices of $P$, including its asymptotic order as $n$ grows.
