\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For an integer n with 0<n<d and n not equal to d-1, if E is n-Ahlfors regular and all Riesz transforms of singularity n are bounded on $L^2(E)$, is E necessarily n-uniformly rectifiable?
\par\smallskip\textit{Formulation references:} \sref{30001856}{1}, \sref{30001856}{2}.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Let $0<n<d$ be integers with $n\neq d-1$, and let $E\subseteq\mathbb R^d$ be $n$-Ahlfors regular. If its $n$-dimensional Riesz transforms are bounded on $L^2(E)$, must $E$ be uniformly $n$-rectifiable? Ahlfors regularity means that its $n$-dimensional measure in balls is comparable to the $n$th power of their radius; uniform rectifiability requires quantitative large pieces of Lipschitz images.
