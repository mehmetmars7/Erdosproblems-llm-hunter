\section*{Height-averaged logarithmic bridge-length windows for critical honeycomb self-avoiding walks}\label{30007421}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
On the honeycomb lattice with critical weight $X^length$, $X=(2+\operatorname{sqrt}(2))^{-1/2}$, use self-avoiding strict port-to-port bridges of integer height H and a fixed start per sum, with center-count length. Do there exist $c,C,h_0>0$ such that for all real $h>=h_0$ the summed mass, over $1<=H<=2h(\log $ $h)^{1/64}$, of bridges with length between $h^{4/3}(\log $ $h)^{-1/8}$ and $2h^{4/3}(\log $ $h)^{1/2}$ is at least c $h^{3/4}(\log $ $h)^{-C}$?
