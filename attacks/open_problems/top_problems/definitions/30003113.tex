\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For $\lambda\in(0,1)$, let $\mu_\lambda$ be the distribution of $\sum_{n\geq0}A_n\lambda^n$, where the $A_n$ are independent unbiased random variables taking values $\pm1$. Determine the set of parameters $\lambda$ for which $\mu_\lambda$ is absolutely continuous.
