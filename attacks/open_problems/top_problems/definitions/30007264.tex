\section*{Goldfeld analytic rank density over Q}\label{30007264}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Fix any elliptic curve E over Q, and order its quadratic twists by signed squarefree integer parameters d with $0<|d|<=X$. Must twists of analytic rank zero and twists of analytic rank one each have natural proportion tending to one half, with all higher analytic ranks having density zero?
