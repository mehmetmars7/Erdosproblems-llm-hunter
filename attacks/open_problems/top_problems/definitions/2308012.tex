\section{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 8.12}\label{2308012}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2308012 · Analysis}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $\mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}$ and $\mathbb T=\partial\mathbb D$. Let $A$ be the boundary restrictions to $\mathbb T$ of the disc algebra: functions continuous on $\overline{\mathbb D}$ and analytic in $\mathbb D$. A sense-preserving circle homeomorphism is one preserving circular orientation. Define \[\widetilde A=\{f\circ\phi:f\in A,\ \phi:\mathbb T\to\mathbb T\text{ a sense-preserving homeomorphism}\},\quad q(e^{i\theta})=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}2^{-n}e^{-i2^n\theta}.\] The series for $q$ is uniformly convergent.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Characterize membership in $\widetilde A$. Also, do there exist $h\in\widetilde A$ and a measurable $E\subset\mathbb T$ of positive arc length such that $h(\zeta)=q(\zeta)$ for every $\zeta\in E$? The source conjectures that the latter answer is negative. \sref{2308012}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Characterize circle reparametrizations of disc-algebra functions and decide whether one can agree with the stated lacunary series on a set of positive length.
