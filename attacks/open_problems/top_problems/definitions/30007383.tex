\section*{A limiting density threshold for every fixed random clause size}\label{30007383}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For each fixed $k\geq 3$, do independent uniformly signed k-clauses using k distinct variables, sampled with replacement on n variables, have a constant $\alpha _k\in (0,infinity)$ such that satisfiability probability at floor(cn) clauses tends to one for every $c<\alpha _k$ and to zero for every $c>\alpha _k$?
