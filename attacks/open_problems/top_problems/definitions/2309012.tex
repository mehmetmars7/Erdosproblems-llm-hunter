\section{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 9.12}\label{2309012}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2309012 · Analysis}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $\Gamma$ be the boundary of a square, scaled so its logarithmic capacity is $1$. For a compact planar set $K$, capacity is \[\operatorname{cap}(K)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\max_{z_1,\ldots,z_n\in K}\left(\prod_{1\le i<j\le n}|z_i-z_j|\right)^{2/(n(n-1))}.\] An $n$-point Fekete configuration on $\Gamma$ maximizes this product; take one for each $n\ge2$ and put $U_n(z)=n^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^n\log|z-z_j|$. Let $\phi$ map the unbounded component of $\widehat{\mathbb C}\setminus\Gamma$ conformally onto $\{|w|>1\}$, fixing infinity. Define $U(z)=\log|\phi(z)|$ outside the square and $U(z)=0$ inside.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Prove the estimate $U_n(z)-U(z)=O(n^{-1})$ away from $\Gamma$: for every compact $L\subset\mathbb C\setminus\Gamma$, show that there is a constant $C_L$ with \[\sup_{z\in L}|U_n(z)-U(z)|\le C_L/n\quad(n\ge2).\] \sref{2309012}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Prove first-order convergence of Fekete-point logarithmic potentials to the equilibrium potential for a capacity-one square, uniformly away from its boundary.
