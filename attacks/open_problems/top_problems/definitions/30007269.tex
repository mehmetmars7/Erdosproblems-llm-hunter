\section*{Tame eigensheaves at several marked points}\label{30007269}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $n>=2$ and $p>n$. Let X be a smooth projective geometrically connected genus-at-least-two curve over an algebraic closure of a finite field, with at least two distinct finite-field rational marked points. For every Zariski-dense geometric PGLn parameter on the punctured curve over a finite extension of $Q_\ell $, killing wild inertia and having tame $\exp (t_\ell $ $N_x)$ monodromy for an arbitrary nilpotent $N_x$ at each mark, must there be a nonzero locally constructible perverse sheaf on SLn-bundles with Borel reduction at all marks, parabolic global-nilpotent singular support, and coherent all-leg Hecke eigenisomorphisms with collision fusion? Zero residues are allowed and no Frobenius descent is required.
