\section*{Zilber-Pink for abelian varieties over Qbar}\label{30007254}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every abelian variety over a number field and every irreducible subvariety X over Qbar, must there be only finitely many maximal atypical subvarieties of X? Atypical intersections are components with dimension greater than the expected dimension of intersection with torsion translates of abelian subgroups, measured inside the smallest such special subvariety containing X.
