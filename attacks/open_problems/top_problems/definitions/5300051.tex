\section{Dynamics on a Siegel-disk boundary}\label{5300051}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 5300051 · Dynamical Systems}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


A Siegel disk $S$ for a holomorphic map $f$ is an invariant domain on which $f$ is holomorphically conjugate to an irrational rotation of the unit disk. Consider its boundary dynamics wherever, as in the source's holomorphic setting, $f$ extends to a neighbourhood of $\overline S$. A periodic boundary point satisfies $f^p(x)=x$ for some $p\geq1$. The lower Lyapunov exponent at $x$ is $\underline\chi(x)=\liminf_{n\to\infty}n^{-1}\log\|(f^n)'(x)\|$, with derivative norm in a smooth spherical metric; positivity is independent of a smooth change of metric on the compact invariant boundary. For a continuous map $T$ on a compact metric space, its topological entropy is $h_{\rm top}(T)=\lim_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}\limsup_{n\to\infty}n^{-1}\log s_n(\varepsilon)$, where $s_n(\varepsilon)$ is the largest size of a set whose distinct points are separated by more than $\varepsilon$ in $\max_{0\leq j<n}d(T^jx,T^jy)$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Can $\partial S$ contain periodic points? Can it contain points of positive Lyapunov exponent? Is $h_{\rm top}(f|_{\partial S})=0$ always true? \sref{5300051}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can a Siegel-disk boundary contain periodic points or points with positive derivative growth, and must its boundary dynamics always have zero entropy?
