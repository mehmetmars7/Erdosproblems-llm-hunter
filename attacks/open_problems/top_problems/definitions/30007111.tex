\section*{Linear coefficient of minimum spherical logarithmic energy}\label{30007111}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For ordered-pair logarithmic energy $E_{\log} (N)=min_{x_{1},\dots ,x_N\in S^{2}}$ $-\Sigma _{i\neq j}\log \Vert x_i-x_j\Vert $, is $E_{\log} (N)=(\tfrac12 -\log 2)N^{2}-\tfrac12 N$ logN+C N+o(N), where $C=2\log 2+\tfrac12 \log (2/3)+3\log (\surd \pi /\Gamma (1/3))$, as integers N tend to infinity? Collisions have infinite energy and distances are ambient Euclidean chords.
