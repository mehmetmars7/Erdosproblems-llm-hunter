\section*{Projected Lax conjecture}\label{30007125}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Does every closed hyperbolicity cone $K(p,e)$ of a real homogeneous polynomial hyperbolic in direction $e$ admit a finite exact semidefinite lift? This means
\[
K(p,e)=\left\{x:\text{there exists }u\in\mathbb{R}^a\text{ with }A_0+\sum_i x_iA_i+\sum_j u_jB_j\succeq0\right\}
\]
for some finite $a$ and finite real symmetric matrices, where $\succeq0$ means positive semidefinite; no closure of the projection is allowed.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For every real homogeneous polynomial hyperbolic in direction $e$, is its closed cone $K(p,e)$ exactly the set of $x$ for which some $u\in\mathbb{R}^a$ makes $A_0+\sum_i x_iA_i+\sum_j u_jB_j$ positive semidefinite, for some finite $a$ and finite real symmetric matrices, without taking the closure of the projection?
