\section*{All-pairs diamond distortion in tree-segment Banach spaces}\label{30007189}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let J be the real Banach-space completion of finite real arrays on the disjoint countably branching finite-height forest for the supremum $\ell _2$ norm of sums over pairwise disjoint tree segments, and $X=J*$. Let $B_infinity$ be the closed coordinate span in the dual of the analogous infinite-tree completion. Let $D_k$ be the depth-k countably branching normalized diamond, each edge replaced by countably many two-edge paths of the same total length. For every $k>=0$, every positive scale s, and every metric embedding into either X or $B_infinity$ satisfying s $d_k(u,v)<=norm(f(u)-f(v))<=D$ s $d_k(u,v)$ for all pairs, must $D^2>=1+k/4$?
