\section{Kirby Problem 1.84}\label{2743}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2743 · Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


For a classical knot $K\subset S^3$, the smooth slice genus $g_4^{\mathrm{sm}}(K)$ is the least genus of a connected smooth oriented surface properly embedded in $B^4$ with boundary $K$; $g_4^{\mathrm{top}}$ uses locally flat topological embeddings. A virtual knot is represented by an embedding in $\Sigma\times[0,1]$, for a compact oriented surface $\Sigma$, with representations identified under isotopy and addition or removal of handles away from the knot. Its virtual slice genus $g_v$ is the least genus of a connected oriented properly embedded surface $F\subset M\times[0,1]$ with boundary the represented knot in $\partial M\times[0,1]$, where $M$ ranges over compact oriented 3-manifolds with $\partial M=\Sigma$, and representatives range over the virtual equivalence class. Again distinguish smooth and locally flat versions. A classical knot gives a virtual knot by viewing it in a thickened sphere.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every classical knot $K$, do both equalities hold?
\[g_v^{\mathrm{sm}}(K)=g_4^{\mathrm{sm}}(K),\qquad g_v^{\mathrm{top}}(K)=g_4^{\mathrm{top}}(K).\]
The two categories are separate versions of the question. \sref{2743}{2}

\subsection{Short English statement}
Can allowing virtual ambient surfaces lower the slice genus of a classical knot, either smoothly or in the locally flat topological category?
