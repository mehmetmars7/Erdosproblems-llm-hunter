\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $\Gamma$ be a type $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor with faithful trace $\tau$, and let $T\in\Gamma$ have Brown measure $\mu_T=\delta_0$. Must there exist a projection $P\in\Gamma$, $0<P<1$, whose range is invariant under $T$, equivalently $(1-P)TP=0$?
