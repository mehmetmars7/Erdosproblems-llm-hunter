\section*{Lifting an adjoint section from its reduced support}\label{30007069}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let (V,C) be a normal projective Q-factorial dlt pair in characteristic zero with effective rational C, and put $A=K_V+C$. Suppose a nonzero effective Cartier divisor G is linearly equal to qA for a sufficiently divisible positive q and $Supp(G)\subset Supp(\lfloor C\rfloor )$. If $O_V(G)$ restricted to $G_red$ is semiample, must $\kappa (V,A)>0$?
