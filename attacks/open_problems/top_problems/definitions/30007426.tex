\section*{Avoiding every dyadic affine sequence in a large-measure set}\label{30007426}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every $\eta\in(0,1)$, is there a compact $E\subset[0,1]$ of Lebesgue measure greater than $1-\eta$ containing no set
\[
\{a+b2^{-n}:n\geq1\}
\]
for real $a$ and nonzero real $b$?
\subsection{Short English statement}
For every $\eta\in(0,1)$, does some compact $E\subset[0,1]$ of Lebesgue measure greater than $1-\eta$ avoid containing $\{a+b2^{-n}:n\geq1\}$ for every real $a$ and every nonzero real $b$?
