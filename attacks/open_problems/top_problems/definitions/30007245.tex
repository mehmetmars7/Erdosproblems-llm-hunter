\section*{The Riemannian Penrose bound with minimizing frontiers in every dimension}\label{30007245}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
In every $n\geq 3$, let a complete connected one-ended Euclidean exterior have smooth metric through a compact outer-minimizing locally perimeter-minimizing frontier, whose regular part is minimal and whose singular set has dimension at most $n-8$. Assume nonnegative integrable scalar curvature decaying as $O(r^{-n-\beta })$, $\beta >0$, and $g-\delta =O_2(r^{-\tau })$, $\tau >(n-2)/2$. Is $m_ADM\geq (1/2)(H^{n-1}(frontier)/\omega _{n-1})^((n-2)/(n-1))$?
