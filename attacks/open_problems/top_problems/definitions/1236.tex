\section{Ramsey Number $R(5,5)$}\label{1236}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 1236 · Combinatorics}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $K_N$ denote the complete simple graph on $N$ vertices. Define $R(5,5)$ to be the least positive integer $N$ such that every assignment of one of two colors, red and blue, to each edge of $K_N$ contains five vertices all of whose ten connecting edges have the same color. Equivalently, every simple graph on $N$ vertices contains either a five-vertex clique or a five-vertex independent set.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Determine the exact integer $R(5,5)$. \sref{1236}{2}

\subsection{Short English statement}
What is the smallest number of vertices that forces a monochromatic complete graph on five vertices in every two-coloring?
