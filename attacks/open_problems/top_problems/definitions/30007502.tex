\section*{Filtered chromatic splitting at generic primes}\label{30007502}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For $n\geq1$ and prime $p>n+1$, does $L_{n-1}L_{K(n)}S_p^\wedge$ admit an ordered $2^n$-stage cofiber filtration indexed by $I\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\}$, with initial cofiber $L_{n-1}S$ and later cofibers
\[
\Sigma^{-\sum_{i\in I}(2i-1)}L_{n-\max I}S,
\]
first-stage map the canonical localization unit, and one compatible family $y_i$ in degree $1-2i$ whose products give the corresponding $K(t)$-local sphere bases for every $1\leq t<n$? Here all local spheres use the derived $p$-complete sphere.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For every $n\geq1$ and prime $p>n+1$, does $L_{n-1}L_{K(n)}S_p^\wedge$ have an ordered cofiber filtration of $2^n$ stages indexed by $I\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\}$, starting with cofiber $L_{n-1}S$ and the canonical localization unit as its first-stage map, with later cofibers $\Sigma^{-\sum_{i\in I}(2i-1)}L_{n-\max I}S$, and a single compatible family $y_i$ of degrees $1-2i$ whose products give the corresponding $K(t)$-local sphere bases for all $1\leq t<n$, using the derived $p$-complete sphere for every local sphere?
