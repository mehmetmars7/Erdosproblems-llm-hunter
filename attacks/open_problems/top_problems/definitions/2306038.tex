\section{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 6.38}\label{2306038}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2306038 · Analysis}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Write $\mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}$ and $\mathbb T=\partial\mathbb D$. Let $S$ consist of holomorphic injective functions $f(z)=z+\sum_{n\ge2}a_nz^n$ on $\mathbb D$; set $a_0=0$, $a_1=1$. A function in $S$ is starlike if its image is starlike about $0$, and convex if its image is a convex domain. Suppose $f$ is odd: $f(z)=z+\sum_{n\ge1}c_{2n+1}z^{2n+1}$, with $c_1=1$. Put $d_n=|c_{2n+1}|-|c_{2n-1}|$ for $n\ge1$; this is a signed real difference. An assertion $d_n=O(n^{-s})$ means $|d_n|\le C_f n^{-s}$ for all sufficiently large $n$.Let $\beta=(\sqrt2-1)^2>0$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Does $\sum_{n\ge1}n^{-\beta}d_n^2<\infty$ hold for every odd $f\in S$? \sref{2306038}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Historical question: is the weighted squared difference sequence summable with the specified positive exponent? The source update gives a stronger result.
