\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
If a bounded solution of
$$\Delta u+(1-u^2)u=0\quad\text{in }\mathbb R^N$$
satisfies $\partial_{x_N}u>0$, must all level sets of $u$ be hyperplanes when $N\leq8$?
