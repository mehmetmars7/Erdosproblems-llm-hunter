\section*{A charged enclosing-area bound for weakly asymptotically Euclidean exteriors}\label{30007216}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For smooth complete one-ended three-dimensional initial-data exteriors with compact boundary, $g-\delta =O_2(r^{-q})$ and $K=O_1(r^{-1-q})$ for $q>1/2$, electric and magnetic fields $O_1(r^{-2})$, integrable constraint densities, finite ADM and charge limits, the charged matter dominant-energy condition, and source-free exterior electric and magnetic fields, does a weakly trapped condition of either fixed sign on each boundary component imply positive ADM rest mass m, $m\geq Q$ and $r_A\leq m+\operatorname{sqrt}(m^2-Q^2)$? Here Q is the norm of total electric and magnetic charges and $r_A=\operatorname{sqrt}(A_min/(4\pi ))$, with $A_min$ the full enclosing-cut infimum. For connected nondegenerate outermost outer-minimizing horizons, ask for the stated original-data Reissner–Nordström equality rigidity.
