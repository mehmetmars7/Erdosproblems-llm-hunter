\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $n,d$ be integers with $1\le n<d$, and let $\mu$ be an $n$-dimensional AD-regular measure in $\mathbb R^d$. Is $\mu$ uniformly $n$-rectifiable if and only if its maximal $n$-Riesz transform $R_\mu^*$ is bounded on $L^2(\mu)$?
