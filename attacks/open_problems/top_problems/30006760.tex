\section*{\texorpdfstring{Manins Conjecture on the Asymptotic Density of Rational Points on Fano Varieties}{Manins Conjecture on the Asymptotic Density of Rational Points on Fano Varieties}}\label{30006760}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A smooth projective variety $X/K$ is Fano when $-K_X$ is ample. Fix an adelic metric on this line bundle, its multiplicative height $H$, and let $\rho(X)$ denote the rank of the Picard group over $K$. For a nonempty Zariski open $U\subseteq X$, put
\[
 N(U,B)=\#\{x\in U(K):H(x)\le B\}.
\]
The catalogue requests $N(U,B)\sim cB(\log B)^{\rho(X)-1}$ on a suitable open subset, with $c>0$ when that asymptotic is intended. \textbf{Formulation warning:} this unrestricted closed-exceptional-set version has counterexamples; a primary discussion is \href{https://arxiv.org/html/2311.04138v3}{[E1]}, Introduction and Proposition 1.4. Modern formulations allow a thin exceptional set and additional hypotheses. Those are different statements, and are not silently substituted here. The original target is retained for traceability, not certified as an open universal conjecture.
\par\smallskip\textit{Formulation and concept references:} \href{https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01393829}{[S1]}, \href{https://arxiv.org/html/2311.04138v3}{[E1]}.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Does deleting a proper algebraic exceptional subset make rational-point counts on every Fano variety follow the proposed height asymptotic? The unrestricted version printed in the catalogue has counterexamples.
