\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $p$ be a prime and $ A_p = \{ k! \pmod{p} : 1\leq k<p\}. $ Is it true that $ \lvert A_p\rvert \sim (1-\tfrac{1}{e})p? $ \sref{2138}{1}

{\small\emph{Transcription note.} Only unambiguous escape/formatting damage and nonmathematical serialization debris were repaired in the displayed source statement.}

\subsection{Short English statement}

Modulo a large prime, do the nonzero factorials represent asymptotically the fraction one minus the reciprocal of e of all residue classes?
