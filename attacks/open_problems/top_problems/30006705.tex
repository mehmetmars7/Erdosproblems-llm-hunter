\section*{\texorpdfstring{Artins Conjecture on the Holomorphy of Non-Abelian Artin L-Functions}{Artins Conjecture on the Holomorphy of Non-Abelian Artin L-Functions}}\label{30006705}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
An Artin representation is a continuous complex representation $\rho:G_K\to\operatorname{GL}(V)$ with finite image. Its Euler product has local factors
\[
 L_v(s,\rho)=\det(1-\rho(\operatorname{Frob}_v)(Nv)^{-s}\mid V^{I_v})^{-1},
\]
with compatible Frobenius convention and inertia invariants. For every irreducible nontrivial such $\rho$, the conjecture says that the resulting meromorphic continuation $L(s,\rho)$ has no poles anywhere in ${\mathbb{C}}$. The trivial representation is excluded, since its Dedekind zeta function has a pole.
\par\smallskip\textit{Formulation and concept references:} \href{https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.16.8.532}{[S1]}.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Are all nontrivial irreducible Artin $L$-functions holomorphic throughout the complex plane?
